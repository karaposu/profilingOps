# Friendship BDA Principles

**What this is.** Ten operating principles for friendship — making friends, keeping them across decades, friend groups, and the slow arts of presence and return — derived from Belief-Driven Attachment (BDA). Derived fresh against the friendship frame. Each ends with its check-question.

**Why friendship needs its own derivation.** Friendship is the theory's **purest domain**: there is no contract, no exclusivity, no lock, no formal structure of any kind — a friendship exists *only* as mutual attachment, or it doesn't exist. Four structural consequences: (1) since exit is always free, **everything volunteered is real evidence** — friendship data is the cleanest reading environment the theory has; (2) the same absence of structure makes friendship **neglect-fragile in a specific way**: nothing holds the shell up while the attachment decays, but equally, real friendships are **dormancy-tolerant** — they hibernate and resume without penalty, and telling decay from dormancy is the domain's core reading problem; (3) contact runs on **long cycles** — weeks or months — so every fast-window instinct (over-reading a slow reply) misfires here; (4) the domain runs an **anti-ledger norm**: paid costs matter as much as anywhere, but *visible accounting kills the thing it measures* — the moment the books are shown, the stance-belief flips from friend to counterparty.

**Priority structure:** **[GATE]** principles multiply — a zero on one zeroes the rest; the others add. Gates win conflicts.

**Provenance:** `BDA/21` (Self-Positioning, backed displays), `BDA/20` (Coherence's longitudinal catalog, Signal Specificity), `BDA/60` (consent-gate, the probe stakes-gate), the compliance-versus-attachment finding, the glue-versus-leader finding, the backing-observables finding (cost-trail, off-stage continuity, the survives-easing pattern), the leadership-and-inexhaustibility finding. Status: structural layer, uncalibrated — a lens checked against results.

## I · The nature of the game

### 1 · Everything here is volunteered — so everything counts

No one is contractually at your birthday. Every message, every show-up, every remembered detail arrived because someone *chose* it against a free alternative — which makes friendship conduct the highest-grade attachment evidence there is, in both directions. Read your friendships by what is volunteered, and know you are read the same way. The dark side of the same fact: because nothing structural holds a friendship up, decay is **silent** — no fight, no breakup, no notice; the volunteering just quietly stops. Friendships rarely end; they evaporate. Whatever you are not actively backing is already evaporating at some rate.

**Check:** *what have I volunteered into this friendship lately — and what has quietly stopped arriving from me?*

### 2 · Presence at cost is the currency [GATE]

Everyone attends the wedding; the friendship-defining displays happen where attendance is inconvenient: the hospital visit, the moving day, the 2 a.m. call taken, showing up during their divorce when there's nothing fun about it. These are the domain's highest-tether signals — unfakeable because the cost is the content — and the theory's blunt accounting is that **one crisis-presence outweighs years of easy company**, while one crisis-absence re-prices years of it ("he was never there when it actually mattered" is a verdict on the whole archive). Easy-time friends are interchangeable by construction; cost-time friends are structural.

**Check:** *this moment is inconvenient — which is exactly why showing up now is worth fifty easy hangs. Am I going?*

### 3 · Never show the ledger [GATE]

Costs paid matter here as much as anywhere — but friendship runs on the **anti-ledger norm**: the moment accounting becomes visible ("I always host," "I came to yours twice"), the other side's stance-belief flips from *friend* to *counterparty in an exchange*, and attachment doesn't survive the reclassification — a friendship audited is a friendship demoted, even when the audit is accurate. The discipline: give without receipts, and handle real imbalance (which is real and does corrode) by **honest words about the feeling, never by tallies or tests** — "I miss you making the plan sometimes" lands; an itemized grievance ends things; and going silent *to see if they notice* is a probe the domain's stakes never justify (a detected test damages exactly what it was checking).

**Check:** *am I about to present a bill — or to say, plainly and without arithmetic, what I miss?*

### 4 · Specific memory is the charm

Friendship's charm-channel is **knowing someone in detail and proving it unprompted**: the follow-up on the thing they mentioned once ("how did the Thursday interview go?"), the link sent because it's *their* obsession, the reference to their story from two years ago. Specific memory is demonstrated attention accumulated over time — expensive to fake, impossible to template, and it does double work: it displays your attention *and* tells them they are worth archiving. Its inverse is the maintenance-performance: the generic "hey, how've you been!" check-in that could have been sent to anyone, which reads as exactly what it is — a contact-schedule being serviced.

**Check:** *does this message prove it could only have been sent to them?*

## II · The conduct

### 5 · Be the honest mirror [GATE]

The friend's unique, non-substitutable value — the thing no acquaintance, colleague, or algorithm supplies — is the **true thing said kindly**: "I think you're wrong about her," "that plan has a hole," "you were not at your best last night." Agreement-friends are commoditized by their own agreeableness (interchangeable with anyone else who nods), and flattery from a friend is worse than noise — it's a coherence debt, because they will eventually stand in the consequence of the thing you didn't say. The mirror has a manner-gate: truth delivered *for them* (private, timed, kind, and then dropped) — not truth performed for your own candor-credentials. But within that gate, the uncomfortable true thing is the single strongest friendship display the theory knows: it proves attention, courage, and investment in one act, and it's why the friend who tells you the truth becomes structural while the one who only affirms stays decorative.

**Check:** *am I about to say the agreeable thing or the true thing — and if the true one, is it aimed at their good?*

### 6 · Ask cheap, decline cheap

Friendship's consent-gate: invitations must be genuinely declinable — "no pressure, next time" said and meant — because a guilt-extracted yes produces **compliance-attendance**: the body present, the attachment absent, and a growing association between you and obligation that poisons the well each time it's pulled. The pressured friend doesn't leave loudly; they just become gradually harder to reach (the domain's silent exit). Symmetrically, decline cleanly and honestly yourself — a friendship where no's are cheap in both directions is one where every yes is real, and (principle 1) real yeses are the only data worth having. Chronic guilt-leverage is how friendships convert into dread-subscriptions that eventually get cancelled.

**Check:** *can they decline this without a cost — and did my last no come with honesty or with theater?*

*(One addition, 2026-08-26: **type your own no's out loud** — "not this month" is a different object than "not my thing," and an untyped no crystallizes in their ledger at whatever weight they guess. A declared no defends itself; make sure yours says exactly as much no as you mean. `BDA/60` §11.)*

### 7 · Trust the hibernation — but never skip a crisis

The long-cycle rule. Real friendships are **dormancy-tolerant**: they resume after six months at full warmth, no penalty, no re-audition — that resumability *is* the standing attachment, and it's the domain's cleanest backed/performed marker (a "friendship" that must be continuously performed to stay alive was maintenance-performance all along). So: don't over-read slow replies and long gaps — this is the theory's insufficient-window honesty applied to friendship's clock, where the honest read on a quiet month is *no data*. But the tolerance has one hard boundary: **dormancy through their crisis is not dormancy — it's the crisis-absence of principle 2**, and it re-prices everything. The skill of long friendship is exactly this pair: relaxed about ordinary silence, unfailingly awake for the moments that count.

**Check:** *is this quiet just the friendship breathing — or is something happening in their life right now that makes silence an absence?*

*(Scope-note, added 2026-08-31, from the charm_persistence finding — the corrected Charm row, `BDA/30` §1: hibernation is that row in daily life. A dormant friendship's beliefs — the admiration, the shared model — are kept by the record (who they are, what they've done) and by the shared model; what goes quiet is *expression*, waiting for an occasion, and resumption is revival, not re-creation. Silence itself never re-prices you. What re-prices is counter-evidence — above all the crisis-absence of principle 2 — so "telling decay from dormancy" reduces to one question: did anything happen that the belief has to answer for, or did nothing happen?)*

### 8 · Bring new things

A friendship running only on its archive — the same stories, the same bar, the retold golden age — is an exhausted source in slow decline: warm, and ending. Shared *new* experience is the domain's hope-channel and its renewal mechanism: the trip taken, the project attempted together, the new place, the introduction into something you've just discovered. New shared experience does compounding work — it refreshes the friendship's trajectory (there is still a future here, not just a past), it generates the next decade's archive, and it re-displays you as an unexhausted source (still growing, still surprising). The maintenance mode is comfortable precisely because it asks nothing; that's the tell.

**Check:** *when did this friendship last make a new memory — and what's the next one I could propose this week?*

## III · The wider structure

### 9 · Narrate the absent the way you'd narrate the present [GATE]

Every time you speak about an absent friend, present company runs the forecast: *this is how I'll be narrated when I'm the absent one.* Loyal narration — defending the absent friend's reasons, keeping their confidences, passing along the flattering true thing instead of the juicy one — is therefore a display made to *everyone listening*, and it compounds: the reputation "he doesn't trash people who aren't in the room" is among the most attachment-generating standing properties a person can hold. Gossip buys thirty minutes of resonance with the room at the price of every listener's long-term security with you — the worst trade in the domain. And the confidence-boundary is absolute: a secret leaked once re-prices you as a vault forever.

**Check:** *if they walked in mid-sentence, does my sentence survive — and what does present company now forecast about their own absent-version?*

### 10 · Be a connector, not a hub

In groups, the strongest position is the **glue role played outward**: making the introductions ("you two need to know each other"), hosting the table, building the connections between your friends — and then *letting those connections live without routing through you*. The hoarding alternative (keeping friends in separate silos so all warmth flows through you personally) feels like centrality and is actually fragility: hub-friendships cap at your personal bandwidth and evaporate when you're busy, while a network you wove holds *you* precisely because you built the thing everyone now lives in — each introduction a durable paid cost with your name on it. Groups also grant the cheapest of all friendship displays, spent or wasted constantly: **attention allocation in company** — who you greet first, whose story you make room for, who you draw into the conversation from its edge. Being the person who spends that on others, room after room, is a compounding display that costs nothing but the attention itself.

**Check:** *am I building connections that outlive my presence — and who, in the next room I enter, needs drawing in from the edge?*

## The compressed card

1. Everything is volunteered, so everything counts — and decay is silent; back what you want kept.
2. Presence at cost is the currency; one crisis outweighs years of easy company, in both directions.
3. Costs paid, ledger never shown; imbalance handled in words, never tallies or silent tests.
4. Prove the message could only have been sent to them.
5. The true thing, kindly, for their good — the mirror is the non-substitutable value.
6. Invitations declinable, declines honest; guilt buys attendance, never attachment.
7. Relaxed about ordinary silence; unfailingly awake for crises.
8. New shared memories, deliberately made — archives alone are endings in progress.
9. Speak of the absent so the present feel safe; confidences are forever.
10. Weave the network and let it live; spend your in-room attention on the edges.

When principles collide: gates (2, 3, 5, 9) beat everything.
