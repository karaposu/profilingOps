## Step 2 — Detect Boundaries (Top-Down)

Eight clusters, seven boundaries. Most are single-point cuts.

The **lowest-coupling cut** is **P1│P2**. The two refutations reach one conclusion — *this horn fails* — and share **no evidence at all**: one runs on a quoted definition and a stated criterion, the other on a two-legged counter-example pair. They could be worked by two people who never spoke.

The **highest-risk cut** is **P2│P3**. It is tempting to fuse them, because *the test says not-species* and *the species is really a name for an object* feel like one thought. They are not: P3's claim survives whichever way the test had gone, and P2's test is a mechanism P7 needs independently of P3's ontology. Fusing them would bury the mechanism inside an ontological claim and leave P7 without a stated method. Validated at Step 3.

Two merges were made and are recorded rather than silently taken:

- **P6 = the empty class + the pool.** Separately, they are *there are no members* and *there are two dozen candidates* — the finding's most surprising pair, and stating them apart risks the two sitting in the document unconnected. Together they answer one question: **what is there?**
- **P7 = the sorting + its limits.** A limit is a statement *about* a sorting; change the sorting and every limit moves. Strong coupling in both directions — the one place in this map where separation would have been the error.

### Determination-mechanism piece check *(Step 7 refinement)*

**Does the Q-tree carry a load-bearing concept whose use depends on a runtime determination?** Yes — **the two-way dissociation test**. P7 applies it to sort a pool; every item's placement depends on running it at runtime.

**Resolution: the mechanism has a piece of its own, and that piece is P2.** P2's job is not only to reach a verdict — it is to **run the test in full**, state its form, cite its three precedents, and execute both legs on one candidate. A reader finishing P2 has the mechanism, not just its output. Interface **I5** then carries the *form* to P7 and deliberately withholds the *result*.

**This is the inverse of the usual failure and worth recording as such.** The mode fires when a Q-tree presupposes a determination without addressing it. Here the determination is a **first-wave piece** and the sorting depends on it — which is why P2 is on the critical path despite P1 looking like the natural opening.

**One residual, recorded honestly:** P7 applies the test to four items and the census is out of scope by the Meaning commitment (I11). So the Q-tree provides the mechanism and a worked sample, and **explicitly does not** provide the full application. That is a stated limit rather than a missing piece — and P7's sixth criterion is what makes it stated.
