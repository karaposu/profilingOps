The genus holds; the ceremonial form is the outer frame's expected conduct, and the slot takes the situation's real object.
**Collision:** holds with the nesting made explicit — the generic frame lives *inside* situation-frames and honors them; the slot adopts the situation's real object where the situation supplies one.
**Prosecution (b):** "what never hurts dominates what sometimes helps" undersells Charm, which is always positive when backed — why no Charm-clause?
**Defense:** Clause C *is* Charm's generator (a backed offer → demonstration co-generates); the frame carries each variable's **generator**, never its **emphasis** — an emphasis-clause ("be impressive") would be display-level, the dead region. The mapping is derivable: C → Charm; A's real, moving work → Hope (trajectory); D + E → Resonance (honest self, honest affect); Fear's honest form (real selectivity) → B.
**Collision:** holds; the generator-map stated explicitly.
**Verdict: REFINE (both wording-level).**

### C2 — the premise's five clauses, especially B (P2)
**Prosecution:** "I need nothing from this exchange to be whole" (i) reads as indifference or coldness, and (ii) is *false* for real needs — the seller needs sales to eat, the lonely person needs connection — and a false premise is **performed wearing by the account's own rule**.
**Defense:** (i) the lineage defines outcome-independence precisely — attention *whose resolution doesn't depend on the counterparty's response* — a claim about the state's resolution, not about material needs; (ii) but "nothing" overclaims, and the prosecution is right that the clause must not deny wants: wanting the sale is fine; **needing this particular exchange to resolve a particular way in order to be whole** is what Supplication is, and what the clause must deny. Reword to resolution-form. And the lineage's committed genuine-need case (asking for help you actually need, transparently) is carried by Clause E — need *shown* is not Supplication; need *concealed while extracting* is.
**Collision:** the prosecution wins the wording. **Clause B becomes:** *"I don't need this to go any particular way to be whole."* The extraction-test still passes by construction (resolution-independence extracts nothing), and the contempt-twin dies more cleanly (whole ≠ cold; wants remain; only the *dependence* is denied).
**Verdict: REFINE** — the reworded Clause B is the finding's.
