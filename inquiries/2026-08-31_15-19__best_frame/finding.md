## Vocabulary

- **The generic frame** — the standing self-frame worn across the whole ambient distribution of daily receivers (strangers, service, colleagues, friends, family) when nothing custom applies; bearing's premise.
- **"Best"** — highest attachment-effectiveness *in expectation over that distribution*, under the extraction-constraint, with wearability as a constraint, never the objective (the founding fact: frame-consistency ≠ attachment).
- **Cause-level premise** — a premise that names a gate's *cause* (attention-direction, resolution-independence), never its *display* ("I'm the selector"); assuming displays is the caught path.
- **The record-slot** — the bracket in Clause A (`[work / life / thing]`) filled by whatever the wearer actually has; content universal, scope pinned per wearer (`21` §8).
- **The custom interface** — what a custom frame varies: the **object** (the slot filled or shifted), the **species** (`60` §2), and the **variable-emphasis** — never the gate-clauses.
- **The floor** — the frame's degraded mode (Clauses D + E alone) when no real work is attendable; a coherence-preserving hold, not a frame that attaches.
- **The alarm** — the frame's built-in diagnostic: when the slot is empty or the frame breaks, the retired approval-questions flood back.

## The Finding

### 1 · Why this frame is best — and why the three senses of "best" coincide

The objective is fixed by the founding fact and the receiver-side architecture: maximize expected attachment over the daily *distribution* of receivers, under the extraction-constraint. The multiplicative structure then does the work. Across the distribution, the **variables'** optimal emphasis varies (service rewards little; friends reward resonance; colleagues reward competence-charm) — but every **gate's** contribution is monotone in every context: a higher gate never hurts anywhere. So the expected-attachment-maximizing content for a frame worn everywhere is **(i) hold all three gates at their maxima** and **(ii) stay neutral in variable-emphasis while rich in variable-generators** — force no single variable (forcing any over-emphasizes it where it's wrong), but make each variable's display *available* to co-generate when a context calls it. **What never hurts dominates what sometimes helps.** Hence the invariant core (the gates) *is* the default frame (worn when nothing custom applies) *and* the optimum for the generic distribution — optimum, default, and invariant coincide.

One clarification the critique forced: the monotonicity holds at the **genus** level — *non-extractive attention* never hurts — while the lineage's committed caveat contexts (intimacy, ceremony, transparent asymmetry) are handled not by exception but by **species and slot** (the custom layer) and by **nesting** (the generic self-frame lives inside situation-frames and honors their conduct; the slot adopts the situation's real object — in condolence, the bereaved's wellbeing). The generic premise keeps "my own real work" as the *ambient default* because value-establishing contexts dominate daily life.

### 2 · The premise — the gates said in first person, at cause-level

Each clause names a gate's cause, never its display:

- **A (the SP cause):** *"My attention is on my own real [work]."* Attention-direction is upstream of the elevated posture (the reframe lineage's own causal claim); the bracket is the record-slot.
- **B (outcome-independence):** *"I don't need this to go any particular way to be whole."* Reworded under critique from "I need nothing from this exchange": the lineage defines outcome-independence as attention *whose resolution doesn't depend on the counterparty's response* — a claim about the state's resolution, not about wants. Wanting the sale is fine; **needing this exchange to resolve a particular way in order to be whole** is Supplication, and that is what the clause denies. It also **passes the extraction-test by construction** (resolution-independence extracts nothing) and manufactures Exit-Willingness — both exits real.
- **C (the backed offer):** *"I have something real to offer from it."* The standing self-claim at exactly the backed scope: the offer is what the record supports. It is Charm's generator — attention on the real thing makes specificity and demonstration arrive free.
- **D (Coherence):** *"I am the same person here as everywhere."* One story; kept forecasts follow.
- **E (Emotional Congruence):** *"and what I show is what I feel."* Affect-congruence — and the anti-performance commitment (no display without its state). It also carries the lineage's genuine-need case: need *shown* transparently is not Supplication.

**The generic frame:**

> ***"My attention is on my own real [work]. I have something real to offer from it. I don't need this to go any particular way to be whole. I am the same person here as everywhere, and what I show is what I feel."***

Five sentences, four commitments (A + B are the Self-Positioning gate). The **variable-generator map**: C → Charm (demonstration) · A's real, moving work → Hope (trajectory) · D + E → Resonance (honest self, honest affect) · B → Fear's honest form (real selectivity, never manufactured). And the check the lineage demands: from A + B the **selector-posture co-generates** — selecting/offering premise-posture, unhurried tempo, terminable close, no self-justification — the Expressed Frame's committed pole as *consequence*. The display-level premise ("I am the most important person here") is rejected twice over: it invites performing the display, and its VIP extension fails the extraction-constraint. Nothing new is admitted: the generic frame is the corpus's gates in the first person.

### 3 · What it co-generates, what it retires, how it reads

**Co-generates (the eight docs' generators, arriving free):** specificity and demonstration (A/C) · unhurried tempo and cheap exits, yours and theirs (B) · honest resonance and honest disagreement (D + E) · visible paid costs (real work costs, and the attention pays them) · trajectory (real work moves). **Retires (the deliberation-drain):** the **approval-questions** ("do they like me? did that land? am I doing OK?") — Supplication's entire content, gone from the active set; the **permission-questions** ("may I speak? do I have the right?") — pre-answered by C; the **decorum micro-questions** — pre-answered by D (dress and act as the person you are everywhere — which is why *both dressers are right at the generic level*; which is better is a custom-layer question of context-fit × Hope). **How it reads from outside** (the legibility gift, specified): *"they're here · they're not needy · they're consistent · they're real"* — four reads receivers already make on the backing channels, one per clause, with C as the reason they're worth reading.

### 4 · Scope, layering, and the custom interface

The generic frame is the **standing frame** — **bearing's premise**, the calculation program's card in premise-form (its target *is* Clause B; its practice *is* Clause A). ### 5 · Backing, wearability, the floor, the alarm

- **Backing, wearer-relative:** the premise is assumable only if the slot holds something real. **The generic frame presupposes a life; it is not a substitute for one.** Its precondition is the calculation program's cultivation target itself (the presence-path builds the record the frame references); an empty slot yields performed wearing — the busy-nothing — which the backing channels read.
- **Wearability:** five short clauses; the manufacture is **attention-redirection** — the cheapest state-manufacture there is (no emotion to conjure; a direction to hold), falling with practice. And the drop-test resolves by a striking route, graded honestly: Clause D makes the off-stage self *this same person*, so there is **nothing to take off** — the generic frame is the one frame that is not a costume — while putting it *on* still costs attention, a cost that falls along the presence-path toward bearing's asymptote (bearing is the frame you no longer wear).
- **The floor:** when no real work is attendable (grief, illness, exhaustion), the frame drops to Clauses D + E alone — a **coherence-preserving hold**, admittedly lower-attachment, whose value is that nothing performed enters the record, so nothing gets re-priced and re-entry is cheap.
- **The alarm:** the frame diagnoses its own breaks — when the slot empties or a challenge lands, the retired approval-questions **flood back** (the frame definition's flooding-signature). Attention drifting to their reaction is the signal to re-enter.

### 7 · Guards, and the frame by negation

The worst generic frame is the exact negation, clause for clause — **Supplication in premise-form**: *"my attention is on how this is going · I need this to go well · I have to prove I belong · I'll be whoever this room needs · I'll show what works."* It teaches the premise faster than the premise does, and it is the calibrating performer's frame. Two dark twins closed structurally: the **empty-slot fraud** (the premise worn over no real work — performed purpose; the alarm fires, the channels read it) and **needs-nothing as contempt** (killed by the reworded B — wants remain, only the dependence is denied — and by Clause E: contempt is a feeling, and showing it while claiming warmth breaks congruence). Two closing notes: the generic frame is also the verdict-timing doctrine worn (B removes the urgency to force verdicts; A grows the record that opens windows), and it is **descriptive before it is prescriptive** — it is the frame the people everyone attaches to already wear, unnamed; the corpus compiled down to what its exemplars were already doing.
