### 3 · The necessity logic and the promotion

Formation's milestones (attachment consolidating) require the belief-side risen **and** the gates settled — the architecture multiplies, and a collapsed gate zeroes the product regardless of the variables. So each factor is *necessary*, and governing a necessary factor governs the milestone's timing:

- **Leg 1** (the parent's evidence-delivery derivation): the triple governs belief-rise → low triple delays milestones.
- **Leg 2** (this dive): the triple governs standing-gate-settling → low triple delays milestones.

Each leg **independently entails** the extension's conclusion. This is not component-splitting weakening the claim — it is two independent derivations of one conclusion through two necessary channels; if either leg's premises fell, the other still carries the claim. **The promoted wording** — for the register, in the siblings' exact label shape: *"independently derived twice — the belief-side evidence-delivery route and the gate-side sampling route; not validated."* Two inquiries; disjoint premise sets; two falsifiers.

**The absorbed objection (the run's hardest):** gate-verdicts can settle *without* evidence-sampling — **default crystallization** absorbs calm conduct into the standing label (the untested bond consolidates with thin gate evidence). Pressed to depth, this strengthened the result: the default path *is frame-settling machinery* — per-cycle fit-selection, breadth- and perturbation-sensitive — i.e., **already triple-governed by the base claim's settling leg**. The gate-verdict thus has **two settling paths, both triple-governed**, with different products: the evidence path yields *tested* verdicts (durable under stress); the default path yields **scope-capped** verdicts (settled within the sampled scope; re-pricing at first exposure beyond it). One **jurisdiction sentence is mandatory wherever this lands:** the default path belongs to the base claim's settling leg — it is cited to show the bypass also fails to escape the triple, never claimed as leg 2's content (no double-counting).

**Two consequences worth keeping:**
- **Scope-capped gate-verdicts** (a flagged gloss) unify three committed cases: the parent's untested bond (calm-capped), the workplace colleague (context-capped — predicting the coworker-surprise at first outside-work exposure), the parasocial read (record-capped, gate-starved).
