## Vocabulary

- **Stance-bundle** — label + expected conduct + claim-scope (the committed form, from the pair-frame's definition).
- **The frame-family** — stance-bundles indexed by *object*: the **situation-frame** ("what this occasion is" — frame-consent, context-nulls), the **pair-frame** ("what we are"), and the **worn self-frame** ("who I am here") — with the **Expressed Frame** as the pole-vocabulary of the self-frame's readable edge (premise-posture is its per-move signature).
- **The worn self-frame (the user's sense)** — a context-scoped premise about who-I-am-here **plus the manufactured state that premise implies, worn as one unit**.
- **Assumability** — the ability to manufacture the implied state on demand (`60` §5's committed craft); trainable; *not* memorization.
- **Co-generation** — the state-side function: a real state produces words, timing, posture, and choices for free.
- **Question-retirement** — the premise-side function: the frame keeps its covered questions *no longer live* (the liveness axis); its observable is the **flooding-signature** — when a frame breaks mid-scene, its retired questions re-open all at once ("suddenly I didn't know how to stand").
- **The two wearing-modes** — manufactured (backed-by-presence) vs performed (premise held coldly, state lagging — leakage; arc-priced).
- **The compile-relation** *(metaphor-flagged)* — principles are training-time; the cards consult-time; the frame runtime. Compilation, never replacement.
- **The drop-test** — can you take the frame off at will? Bearing is *wanted* permanence; frame-servitude is *trapped* permanence (Exit-Willingness toward one's own frame).

## The Finding

### 1 · The family: one definition covers every corpus use

The corpus already used "frame" four ways, and they unify: **a frame is a stance-bundle held at an object** — the situation, the pair, or oneself-in-context. The Expressed Frame ("I'm the selector" vs "please like me") is not a fourth object but the self-frame's **display-face**: the pole-vocabulary of what others read off your moves. The family also **nests and interlocks**: the coffee-shop self-frame presupposes a situation-frame ("a place where I am served"); and two worn self-frames **co-author the pair-frame through conduct** — each frame's expected-conduct bundle includes a slot for the counterpart (the VIP premise contains a server-role), each wearer's frame-generated conduct is the evidence-stream the other's pair-frame settles on. Frame-collisions (two premises claiming the same room) are **claim-collisions** at the frame layer — already-committed machinery; the field's "frame control" doctrine stays outside.

### 2 · The ontology: not knowledge — a generator (assumability earned)

Tested against the user's own criterion (assumable; generates action without deliberation):

- **Compact knowledge fails** — knowing the principles is the founding complaint: knowledge needs runtime interpretation, which *is* the computer-mode.
- **Compressed policy fails** at the same margin — rules need per-situation application-judgment.
- **The premise + manufactured-state compound passes:** assume the premise ("I am the selector here") → manufacture the state it implies (the actor's committed craft, `60` §5) → and the state does the rest, because **real states co-generate their conduct for free**. The frame does not *summarize* the principles — **it installs a generator that produces the conduct the principles would have prescribed.** The compression is *generative, not informational* — which is exactly what "it's not just information" was reaching for.

The frame has **two functions on committed machinery**: the state-side **co-generates** (production without deliberation) and the premise-side **retires questions** (the sharp-dresser's "do I dress up?" is *no longer a question* inside the frame — context-scoped, and reversible by challenge, which is what separates this from mere habit). Its distinct, falsifiable observable: **the flooding-signature** — break the frame and the retired questions re-open at once. And the premise-only mode (belief held coldly, state lagging) is the **performed wearing** the corpus already prices: the wobble leaks; the arc runs.

### 3 · The lifecycle (and the scope answer)

1. **Assume** — choose the premise, manufacture its state; the cost falls with practice (the presence-path).
2. **Wear** — conduct co-generates at zero deliberation-cost; **premise-posture** is what others read, in seconds; every worn hour **grains both ledgers** (wearing is claim-formation in progress). "Submitting" to one's own frame is *self-governance* — a self-issued covering claim over in-context choices (the outfit decided by the frame, not re-litigated by comfort) — the opposite of Supplication.
3. **Maintain** — the coherence machinery runs on the worn frame ("try this frame to be not broken"); **challenges arrive as verdict-moments** (the barista's pushback; the peer's test), and a frame held calmly is the claim-read's "defended, not qualified." **Three break-modes, one machinery:** self-contradiction · challenge-loss · load-drop (the performed mode's fast lane). A break **re-prices retroactively** — the conduct the frame generated gets re-read as staging.
4. **Resolve** — frames are *droppable* (contexts end; shedding is part of honest wearing — off-time recovers the manufacture-effort), *breakable* (priced), or they **wear in**: repeated assumption lowers manufacture-cost until the state is standing — **a frame worn in becomes bearing** (a sufficiency-path, not an identity: bearing can also form by life's accumulation). The frame is the presence-path's unit.

**Scope (layered):** standing frames (bearing; the formed self-claim) underneath; context-assumed frames on top (the coffee-shop VIP, for this visit); in-context choices are frame-*expressions*.

### 4 · The relation to BDA and to us: the compile-relation

The frame is **BDA's runtime object**. The relation runs on three time-scales *(the "compile" word is presentation, flagged)*: **training-time** — the principles and the practice, where frames get designed and their states get built; **consult-time** — the compressed cards (checklists); **runtime** — the frame, assumed and worn. **Compilation, never replacement:** the docs are the source a frame compiles from, and a frame without the training behind it is just the performed mode. The relation to us: the calculation program already runs this architecture (bearing, the card, the one move, state-cultivation) — the user's field-observation ("people submit to a frame… it leads them to correct actions") **re-derives the program's own posture-answer from the outside**; this finding names the program's runtime layer. And one scoping the account owes plainly: **a frame guarantees frame-consistent conduct at low cost — never attachment.** Whether the generated conduct attaches is the frame's *content-quality* — exactly the deferred best-frame question.

### 6 · Guards, and the successor's interface

- **Chosen vs default:** frameless operation doesn't exist — "no frame" is either the exhausting deliberation-mode or the *unexamined habitual frame*. The real choice is **chosen-vs-default**.
- **The automation-trap:** a badly-chosen frame runs bad conduct at the same zero cost — which is why the best-frame question matters and is deferred, not dissolved.
- **The fraud-frame, priced:** a factually-false premise worn by state-manufacture is **occurrently backed** (it feels real in the room — the Stanislavski limit) while its implied **standing claims are unbacked** — the standing channels (cost-trail, off-stage, records) catch exactly this. Presence is not standing truth.
- **The drop-test:** a frame you cannot take off owns you — bearing is wanted permanence, servitude is trapped permanence.
- **The legibility gift:** a clear frame is read in seconds and lowers everyone's reading-cost — the frame is also a gift of predictability to the room.

**The successor's interface** (what the deferred best-frame inquiry will work over): **four parameters** — *content* (which premise) · *scope* (context-assumed vs standing) · *backing-requirement* (which premises the wearer's record and state-craft can actually manufacture — a frame too far from the standing frame can only be performed) · *context-fit* — **under one constraint** — *the extraction-test* (does the bundle's conduct generate or extract — the entitled edge's lesson) — with the founding fact that frame-consistency is not attachment.
