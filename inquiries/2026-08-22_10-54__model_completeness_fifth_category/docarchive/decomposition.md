### Determination-mechanism piece check *(Step 7 refinement)*

**Does the Q-tree carry a load-bearing concept whose use depends on a runtime determination?** Yes — **placement itself**. P5 recommends a region, and using that recommendation on any future candidate requires deciding *is this an input, an update-mechanism, or a pre-condition?* The Q-tree would presuppose that determination if no piece addressed it.

**Resolution: it is addressed, as a criterion inside P5 rather than as a piece of its own.** Two reasons. First, a general placement procedure is a **Process**-layer artefact and the framing document scopes Process out — a piece for it would exceed the declared layer, which is the failure I12 exists to prevent. Second, the determination the *finding* needs is single-instance: *does this candidate fit?* — which P5 answers directly. What P5 owes, and what its seventh criterion requires, is either a stated criterion for the region **or** an explicit statement that none is offered and why. That converts a presupposition into a declared limit.

**Note the asymmetry this exposes:** P3's residual needs **no** determination mechanism at all, because nothing is admitted to a remainder. P5's region does. That asymmetry is itself a result and belongs in the finding.
