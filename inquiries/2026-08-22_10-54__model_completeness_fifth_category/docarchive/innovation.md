## Phase 2 — Generate

Three variations per mechanism (generic / focused / contrarian), all seven mechanisms.

### 1 · Lens Shifting *(Framer)*

**L-a (generic) — the conditions under which a fifth category would be right.** A new architectural category is the correct move when two conditions hold: the theory has a **stated inventory** of categories, and the candidate **fits none of them** by their own criteria. Neither holds. There is no stated inventory outside the passage's source, and the candidate's own advocate calls it a property, which needs no category.

**L-b (focused) — from taxonomy to bookkeeping.** Shift the frame from *what kind of thing is this* to *where is this written down*. Under a bookkeeping lens the fifth-category question dissolves into a register question: the theory has no register of things outside the formula, so proposing a category is really proposing to start one. That is a much smaller and much more tractable act.

**L-c (contrarian) — the application lens.** The surrounding project reads people from conversations. Under a lens where the only question is *does this change a prediction*, model-completeness changes exactly one: whether engagement extinguishes. The whole category discussion is idle from that vantage — the factor is already doing its only job, and it does not need a home to do it.

### 2 · Combination *(Generator)*

**C-a (generic) — completeness × the cumulative belief state.** `f` is *a cumulative belief state*. Model-completeness is a statement about how far that accumulation has gone. Connecting them puts the concept inside the theory's own vocabulary without adding to the formula.

**C-b (focused) — completeness × Signal Specificity: a two-dimensional evidence account.** Specificity is *"the evidence-quality measure for belief-update"* — a property of **one signal**. Completeness is a property of **the accumulated set**. Together they are two orthogonal axes of the same thing: *how good is this evidence* × *how much of the space has evidence*. The corpus has the first axis fully worked, with a threshold (θ) and a magnitude role. It has nothing on the second. **The pair is a structure neither concept has alone.**

**C-c (contrarian) — the residual as a pipeline, not a pile.** *Directly attacks the run's strongest anchor.* Sensemaking says the residual's four occupants are heterogeneous — degrade, gate, output, extinguish — and that heterogeneity is what makes it a residual rather than a category. But arrange them **temporally around the formula** and the heterogeneity disappears: **receiver availability** gates whether the process starts; **model-completeness** governs whether it continues; **Vitality** degrades the signal while it runs; **coerced behaviour with zero attachment** is the exit state where output exists without the process. Entry · continuation · degradation · exit. That is not a pile. It is the lifecycle around `f × g`, and it would make the space a genuine architectural region with a stated shape.

### 3 · Inversion *(Framer)* — depth-check + multi-axis check

**I-a (generic, level 1 — component).** *"Model-completeness is not a category member"* → *"it is one."* Component-level: it just relitigates the same slot. **Invert again.**

**I-b (focused, level 2 — system, identity-axis).** *"The theory has architectural categories"* → **what does an architectural category consist of here?** It consists of **a shared structural role plus an admission test**. Apply that: *variables* have a role (additive term in `f`) and a test (can generate with the others at zero). *Modulators* have a role (multiplicative gate) and a test (eight criteria). *Sub-flavours* have a test but are a decomposition inside one variable. The residual has neither. **So the theory has exactly two architectural categories, not four and not five** — and the passage's list is a two-member set with two non-members appended.

**I-c (contrarian, level 2 — system, existence-axis).** *Could the count be **zero**?* If *variable* means "additive term" and *modulator* means "multiplicative gate," those are not categories at all — they are **positions in an expression**. Nothing is *admitted* to being an addend; a thing either appears in the sum or it does not. Under that reading the theory has **zero architectural categories, one formula, and a set of notes about things outside it** — and every categorisation dispute in this corpus's history has been a dispute about the formula's shape wearing taxonomic clothes. *(Two competing system-level statements, I-b and I-c, along different axes; the multi-axis check exists to surface exactly this.)*

### 6 · Domain Transfer *(Generator)* — native source mandatory

**D-a (NATIVE source — formal modelling: boundary conditions).** A physical or engineering model has **state variables** and **boundary conditions** — quantities not in the equations that determine whether, where, and for how long the equations apply. Initial conditions say where the system starts; validity ranges say where the model holds; stopping conditions say when integration ends. Map it: **receiver availability = an initial condition. Vitality = a validity range. Model-completeness = a stopping condition** (pursuit integrates until the model closes). And boundary conditions are emphatically **not a fifth kind of state variable** — they are *a different layer of the specification*. This is the native-domain source the guard requires, and it is the closest structural match found in the run.

**D-b (deliberately different — legal drafting).** Statutes have operative provisions, definitions, **scope provisions**, and savings clauses. A savings clause is not a fifth kind of statute; it is a provision *about the statute's reach*. Model-completeness is scope-talk about `f`, not a provision in it.

**D-c (contrarian transfer — clinical staging).** Staging is not a disease. It is how far the disease has progressed — an **index over** the same object, orthogonal to what the object is. Nobody asks whether staging is a fifth category of illness. On this reading model-completeness is an **index over the belief state**, not an item beside it — which is sharper than *"a property of the receiver's model"* because an index has a direction and a terminus, and the terminus is what ends pursuit.

**E-c (contrarian) — the category-proposal trend.** This is the **third consecutive inquiry** in which a correctly-perceived phenomenon was proposed for a structural slot and turned out to belong somewhere already existing: compliance → *signature-of*, not a species; the persistence layer → a **property**, not a layer; model-completeness → a residual placement / an existing region, not a fifth category. > ### ASM-1 — The answer is a ledger, and the placement is its fourth line.
>

**Defense.** **(1)** Every item on the ledger is a correction of *a document*, not of the user — the user quoted assistant output, and three of the four errors originate in that output rather than in anything they believed.
