## Phase 4 *(protocol Phase 3)* — Ambiguity Collapse

### Ambiguity A1 — Is the fifth-category claim the corpus's, or the passage's?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** it is the corpus's. The source finding says the factor is *"a genuinely different kind of thing from the four the theory currently recognises,"* and *a different kind of thing from four kinds* just **is** a fifth kind. The passage compresses; it does not invent.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the source's very next sentence issues the operative instruction, and it is incompatible with the compression: *"It should be documented **alongside Vitality**, in the space the theory reserves for factors that are real and consequential but do not qualify as modulators."* Alongside Vitality is **inside** an existing space. Its action item repeats it. A claim that something is a *new category* and an instruction to *put it in an existing slot* cannot both be the source's position; the instruction is the one the source acted on, and it is the one carried into its Next Actions. Additionally, *"different in kind from members of a set"* does not entail *"a new set of the same rank"* — Vitality is also different in kind from the four and did not become a category.

**Confidence: HIGH.**

**Resolution:** the escalation is the passage's, not the corpus's. **Fixed:** the concept is premise; the escalation is a claim under test. **No longer allowed:** treating *"a fifth architectural category"* as an inherited commitment. **Depends on this:** the whole placement verdict.

### Ambiguity A2 — Are the passage's four things four peers?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** they are, well enough. Each names a kind of element the theory contains, and a list does not have to be a formal partition to be a useful inventory.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the four sit at three different structural levels and the differences are load-bearing, not pedantic. *Variable* and *modulator* are the two values of one axis — additive term in `f` versus multiplicative gate — and a thing is one or the other by a single stated test. *Sub-flavour* is not on that axis at all: it is a decomposition **within** one variable, and the corpus specifies it as a **basis, not a partition**, so a Hope-instance is a linear combination of sub-flavours simultaneously. Something can be a variable *and* have sub-flavours; nothing can be a variable *and* a modulator. And *degradation factor* is a **space**, not a kind: `compliance_versus_attachment` states placement there is *"a residual placement rather than a category assignment."* Counting a fifth "alongside" these four requires them to be commensurable, and they are not.

**Confidence: HIGH.**

**Resolution:** the list is one partition's two members, plus an intra-variable decomposition, plus a residual space. **Fixed:** there is no four-member set to add a fifth to. **No longer allowed:** the arithmetic of *four → five*.

### Ambiguity A3 — Is the residual space a category?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** it is. It has a name (*Degradation Factors*), a section, members, and `apt_modulator_landscape` calls Vitality's placement *"Different architectural category"* in as many words.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** three things, none of which is precedent-citing. **(i) Its occupants have no shared structure.** Vitality is a threshold-switching physiological factor; receiver availability is a meta-belief about self; behaviour-under-threat-with-zero-attachment is a behavioural remainder; model-completeness is an epistemic property. A category's members share a structural role — the three modulators share multiplicative gating, dual character, distinct failure signatures and orthogonality. These four share only *real, and not in the formula*, which is a negative. **(ii) It has no gate.** Every category the corpus treats as one states criteria — eight for modulators, one named test for sub-flavours. This has none, and the absence is not an oversight: nothing is *admitted* to a remainder. **(iii) The corpus itself says so**, in its most recent and most explicit formulation: *"a residual placement rather than a category assignment."* And the *"Different architectural category"* phrase does dismissal-work, not taxonomy-work — it appears three times, each time disposing of a candidate, never enumerating a set.

**Confidence: HIGH.**

**Resolution:** it is a **residual**, defined negatively. **Fixed:** placement there is not admission and needs no criteria. **No longer allowed:** arguing that model-completeness must "qualify" for the space. **What changed:** the question *which category does it belong to* has no well-formed answer, because the only available home is not a category.

### Ambiguity A4 — Then is there anywhere it positively belongs?

**Strongest counter-interpretation:** no — and that is fine. A residual placement is the whole answer; looking for a positive home is the same over-reach the fifth-category move made, one step quieter.

**Why the counter only partly holds:** it is right that a residual placement **suffices** and wrong that no positive home exists. Iteration-3.4's belief-frame partitions the material by **role relative to the formula**: belief-categories (the formula's inputs), **belief-update mechanisms**, and pre-conditions. That middle region already holds two members with stated roles — Specificity as *"the evidence-quality measure for belief-update"* and θ(context) as *"the channel-specific cutoff below which evidence does not count for update."* And `f` is, verbatim and preserved across two iterations, *a cumulative belief state*. Model-completeness is a statement about **how far that accumulation has gone**. That is the same region, on the same axis, in the corpus's own vocabulary.

**The strongest structural objection to that home, tested:** *model-completeness is just prior-strength, which the theory already has* — iteration-3.4's Prediction 2, re-grounding iteration-3.3's `f_prior`. If so it is not a new anything. **This fails on the corpus's own case.** The deep teacher is *perfectly consistent yet inexhaustible*: a maximally settled, maximally strong prior that is nonetheless nowhere near complete. Strength and coverage come apart in the theory's own worked example. Prior-strength is *how firmly you hold what you believe*; model-completeness is *how much of the person your beliefs reach* — and `leadership_and_inexhaustibility` supplies its ceiling-setter, the **attention-object**, which is a fact about extent and not about confidence.

**Confidence: MEDIUM.** The region is the corpus's own and the two-members reading is quoted; but neither existing member is a property of the accumulated state — both are properties of incoming evidence. Model-completeness would be the region's first state-side member, and that widening is inference, not quotation.

**Resolution:** a positive home exists and it is a **region the corpus already named**, not a new one: the belief-frame's belief-update side, alongside Specificity and θ(context), as the extent-of-accumulation counterpart to prior-strength's firmness. **Fixed:** the answer is a *re-grounding*, not an addition. **No longer allowed:** claiming the theory has nowhere to put this. **Depends on this:** whether the finding's recommendation is *place it* or *ground it*.

**Confidence: HIGH.**
