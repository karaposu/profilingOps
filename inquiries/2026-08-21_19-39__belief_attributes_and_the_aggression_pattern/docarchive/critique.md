## Phase 2/3 — Adversarial Evaluation and Verdicts

### C1 · **The cross-layer mapping and its triple corroboration** *(Assembly 1)*

**Prosecution.** Is *costly to display* true of decisiveness? A reckless person decides constantly at no cost. If one of the three corroborating lenses fails on one attribute, the triple corroboration is a double.

**Defense.** The corpus's costly-signal frame reads the **visible** cost, not the internal one. What deciding publicly costs is **exposure** — the decider is now attributable if it goes wrong — and that cost is visible whether or not the decider feels it. The reckless case is not a counterexample; it is a case where the cost is paid and not felt.

**Collision.** Defense holds. And the three lenses partition the list identically while arriving from timing, cost and uncertainty — none of which derives from the component-assignment that produced the mapping.

> **Verdict — SURVIVE.** No caveats on critical dimensions.

### C2 · **K3 — the theory predicts two attributes the user did not name** *(D14's first test)*

**Prosecution — derivation axis, run step by step.** The inference is: *five signals exist; the user's list claims three; therefore two are unclaimed leader-relevant attributes.* The third step does not follow — being a signal does not make something a **leader-relevant attribute**. And checking the two against the corpus shows they differ sharply.

**Rhythm-Comfort** is defined as *"comfort with silence / pauses"* — straightforwardly leader-relevant, and Innovation's gloss (*unhurried under pressure*) was loose but directionally right.

**Withholding-Signal is the problem.** Iteration-3.3's Refinement 3 states that *"**Withholding-mode g₁ produces no evidence**"* for the belief that this person will attend to me specifically, and that this yields the receiver's *"not for me"* read. So Withholding **raises `g₁` while suppressing Attention-Hope** — it is double-edged by the corpus's own committed coupling rule, not a straightforwardly missing leader attribute.

**Defense.** The derivation's *conclusion* survives in modified form, and the modification is more interesting than the original claim: **the user did not name Withholding because for a leader it is genuinely ambivalent**, and the corpus explains the ambivalence. A leader who withholds reads as high-standing and as not-for-you at once.

**Collision.** Prosecution wins against the stated derivation; defense wins a better result. *Purpose-fitness:* left as stated, the finding would present an ambivalent signal as a missing virtue — misleading. *Frame absorbs the fix?* Yes.

> **Verdict — REFINE.** **Target:** state the two separately. Rhythm-Comfort is an unnamed leader-relevant attribute. Withholding is **double-edged by an existing coupling rule**, which is why a folk list omits it. Cite iteration-3.3's Refinement 3.

### C3 · **K9 — the folk-vocabulary bridge already exists twice** *(D14's second test)*

**Prosecution — direction axis, and it is decisive.** Check the 2×2's structure rather than its labels. `self_focus_generalization/finding.md:188` gives it as *"vertical axis: Self-Focus level; horizontal: Investment-Asymmetry"* with cells *Confident Selector / Respected Expert / Disengaged-Avoidant / Try-Hard-Supplicating*. **The axes are mechanism terms and the cells are folk labels.** So the 2×2 runs **mechanism → folk label**. That is a *naming* device.

The bridge this inquiry needs runs the other way: a user arrives with folk trait-language and needs it assigned to components. **The 2×2 is the wrong direction.**

**Defense.** The **species-context-fit table** does run the needed direction — lay contexts (*cause-driven leadership*, *first-contact dating*) mapped to species. One genuine instance survives.

**Collision.** Prosecution kills the "twice" claim; defense preserves one instance — and the collision produces something neither had: **the corpus has bridges in both directions and has never distinguished them.**

> **Verdict — REFINE, and the refinement is a better finding.** **Target:** one folk→mechanism instance (species-context-fit), one mechanism→folk instance (the 2×2), and the observation that the corpus carries both directions unnamed. P2's intervention becomes *a second instance of the folk→mechanism direction*, not a third instance of one undifferentiated form.

**Defense — precedent, verbatim.** The convention is stated at `hope_sub_flavors/finding.md:91`: *"Vocabulary-only anchor … APT does not commit to any specific tradition's full apparatus."* Four precedents exist (Bowlby, Signal Detection Theory, Bayesian update, factor analysis). ### C5 · **K8 — the user's parenthetical as evidence for the architecture**

**Prosecution.** Circularity, and beyond it: the observation is a **folk report**, unmeasured and unsystematic. Calling it evidence dresses an anecdote as confirmation.

**Defense.** Not circular — the no-outcome-term choice was made in earlier iterations for reasons unrelated to leadership decisiveness, so an independent observation matching it is ordinary consilience.

**Collision.** Defense holds on circularity; prosecution wins on **strength**. This is weak consilience — one unmeasured lay observation.

> **Verdict — SURVIVE, refined in strength.** **Target:** state it as *weak* consilience explicitly. The point is rhetorically attractive and must not be allowed to carry more weight than one folk observation supports.

### C6 · **K11 — commander's-intent legibility fills the open residual** *(the handoff's fifth concern, and it was justified)*

**Prosecution — generate the competition whose absence made the acceptance suspect.** The corpus's own adjacent-mechanism list contains **social proof** — *"existing devotees evidence target's value."* That is an in-corpus candidate for the gathering capacity, requiring **no import at all**, and Innovation never considered it. **Defense.** Legibility explains something social proof does not: why followers **cohere with each other** rather than merely accumulating. Social proof produces a crowd; the Resonance network needs a shared, graspable object.

**Collision.** Both survive, addressing different halves. Legibility explains coherence; social proof explains accumulation. **Neither alone fills the residual.**

> **Verdict — REFINE.** **Target:** the residual stays open with **two candidates**, and the in-corpus one (social proof) must be named alongside the imported one. The handoff's worry was correct — a single candidate had been accepted for lack of competition, and generating competition changed the verdict.

### C7 · **P3's layering — genuine Self-Focus under displayed Mission-Focus**

**Prosecution.** The visibility principle says internal state without observable manifestation produces no modulator effect. If the narcissist's *genuine* Self-Focus is internal, it does no work — so the layering collapses into "displayed Mission-Focus" alone.

**Defense.** Self-Focus is **also displayed** — iteration-3 names its signals: *"talking about self, selective engagement, not optimizing for approval in the moment."* Both layers are displayed. What separates them is not visibility but whether the display is **backed**: the Self-Focus display is backed by attention that really is there; the Mission display is not backed by the stated object.

**Collision.** Defense holds and sharpens the claim.

> **Verdict — SURVIVE, refined in statement.** **Target:** both species are displayed; the genuine/mimicked distinction is about **backing**, not visibility. Stating it as internal-versus-displayed would contradict the visibility principle.
