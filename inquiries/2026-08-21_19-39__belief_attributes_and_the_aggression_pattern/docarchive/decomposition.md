### I5 — the display-reading contract *(the coupling this decomposition nearly missed)*

**The problem.** P4 and P5 both make claims about how a **display register** is read into a modulator value. P4: a soft display reads as Supplication, hence `g(low)`. P5: a masculine-coded display reads as non-extraction, hence `g(high)`. Neither needs the other's *content* — but if each builds its own account of display-reading, the two can silently disagree, and the finding would carry two incompatible theories of the same mechanism.

This was not visible in the element-level propagation analysis. It surfaced during the **bottom-up atom check**, when Supplication's signature and the expression-register turned out to concern one axis from opposite ends.

**The removal.** Make it a one-way consistency contract:

> **P4 establishes the display→modulator-reading account as part of its own answer. P5 reuses that account rather than constructing a second one.**

P5 gains an ordering dependency it did not have; the finding gains one theory of display-reading instead of two.
