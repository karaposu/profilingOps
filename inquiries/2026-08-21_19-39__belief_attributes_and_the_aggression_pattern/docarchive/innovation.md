**Generic — the follower's evidence lens.** What evidence does a follower actually have? Not outcomes — too slow, too noisy, too confounded — but **display**, continuously. → **L-a: `g` is the fast channel and `f` the slow one, and leadership-belief runs mostly on the fast channel.** The corpus already states the ratio: *"g operates at instantaneous bandwidth via observable display signals … f develops over longer time-horizons."*

**Focused — the uncertainty lens.** Look at what the five attributes have in common. Deciding without certainty. Aggressing toward a contested goal. Reversing when wrong. → **L-b: they are all readings of how someone behaves when outcomes are unknown — which is exactly when there is no outcome to read.**

**Contrarian — the sender's-cost lens.** Flip to what each display *costs*. Deciding publicly costs exposure; aggression costs relationships; bailing out costs face. → **L-c: the attributes may be a costly-signal set rather than a capability set.**

**Multi-axis — identity axis.** What is *believing in* made of? Either (i) a prediction about future outcomes, or (ii) a state in which predictions are no longer demanded. → **I-c: the second, which is exactly why success is irrelevant.**
