### 1 · The precisified claim: one ledger, one clock, three readouts

- **The clock.** The surfacing pass caught a real discrepancy: the backing finding's §5 says reading-power grows with *"relationship duration, context-breadth, and perturbation-rate"*, while the registered claim (and the `BDA/02` registry row) says *contact-frequency*. Adjudicated from machinery rather than prose: the backing account's channels read at **occasions** (a live interaction; a perturbation; a forecast-check "at the next contact"), and its own discount rule makes same-channel repetition nearly worthless — elapsed time with no diverse occasions grows nothing. The reading window in `BDA/41` §7 is denominated in **contact-cycles** explicitly, and the settling leg says its dynamics run *"normally in cycles."* The parent finding derived duration-as-container independently. Four committed texts against one summary sentence: **the ledger's clock is the contact-cycle.** The register's reconciliation must quote both texts and resolve by machinery — an honesty duty, not a silent correctness claim (the two legs are finalized findings; corrections land forward).
- **What the ledger is.** The identity claim's object is **the pair's accumulated observation** — a *pre-doxastic* record (what has been observed, weighted and correlated), not the belief built from it. The beliefs (the f-side variables), the gate-reads, the frame-label, and the backing-verdict are all *processed readouts* of that one record. This wording survived its own strongest attack — "if formation just *is* the ledger, the extension is trivially true" — precisely by the pre-doxastic split: formation could in principle have had its own clock and inputs; the claim's content is that it doesn't.
- **The anatomy (proposal-grade).** The parent's five factors sort into three roles: **occasions** = the count; **specificity and visible cost** = per-signal evidence-weights (`BDA/10` §4's committed multiplicative scaling; the costly-signal weight); **independence** = the correlation structure of what's accumulated — with **context-breadth a driver of independence** (different contexts de-correlate evidence) rather than its whole (third-party sources and channel diversity also drive it). The registered triple's membership is untouched; the sorting is an explanatory layer above it.
- **Per-side ledgers.** Two parties hold ledgers over the *same occasions* with *different weights* (what is specific to me isn't to you). One consequence lands as a link: **asymmetric formation from identical history is lawful** — the unrequited shape parameterized by weight-asymmetry, converging with the corpus's window-position mismatch. (Development belongs to the parent map's pair-frame route.)

### 2 · The forced composition — eight elements, three derivations

What committed machinery already fixes about how formation evidence composes (each element with its source):

1. **The θ-floor** — sub-threshold occasions deliver nothing (`10` §4's hard cutoff).
2. **Multiplicative specificity** — effective magnitude = nominal × specificity (`10` §4, a committed piece of functional form).
3. **Concavity in correlated occasions** — same-shape repeats are discounted (`10` §3; `41` §7); accumulation decelerates to a plateau (the settling leg's three-phase shelf-life is this curve).
4. **Path-dependence** — pre-loading starts the ledger nonzero; prior-strength makes the same occasion move a weak belief more than a strong one (`10` §3).
5. **Duration-as-container** — calendar time enters only as the holder of cycles (the parent, now clock-grounded).
6. **Longitudinal floors** — some components can't be delivered in one occasion: Coherence "cannot be read from one message" (`20`); consistency-accrual needs repetitions (`41` §7). **Substitutability is bounded**: no single occasion, however intense, buys out repetition on the consistency-carried components — which is exactly why the short regime leans on pre-loading and third-party continuation (imported cycles).
7. **Gates outside** — the gates multiply outside f and read in seconds (`21` §6); the composition is belief-side, with one registered crossing (perturbation also delivers gate evidence early — the parent's hardship accelerator).
8. **The moving floor** *(new — two committed sources joined)* — θ is not fixed: `10` §4 orders it by relationship state (cold-stranger high … existing relationship low) and `41` §7 says it *falls with contact history*. The composition is doubly cumulative: evidence accumulates *and* the admission threshold drops — early "wasted" sub-θ occasions become admissible content later. Open, deliberately: whether the fall is band-jumps (channel classes) or continuous.

Three results then follow as **derivations** — shape-orderings only, no quantities, every premise named:

- **The S-shape ordering.** The settling leg's co-growth is a *ledger-side* fact: accumulation grows the pair's contact-surface ("more contexts… more third parties… contact-surface grows with the same accumulation"), which raises frequency and breadth — the triple's levels are partly **endogenous**. Joined with the discount's plateau: formation runs slow → accelerating → plateau. (The transplant objection — "co-growth was derived for the window's forcing, not formation" — was pressed and answered from the quoted text: co-growth is about the pair's accumulation itself, upstream of any readout.)
- **The plateau theorem.** Without de-correlation (no new contexts, no perturbation), formation is bounded — the fortieth same-shaped lunch adds nothing. Escapes: raise the weights (deeper occasions) or endogenous breadth (the relationship creating new contexts). The vendor-zone and the coffee-shop regular are this theorem's standing cases; in **breadth-saturated** pairs (everything already shared), perturbation is the remaining de-correlator — which is why long marriages' formation runs on life events and unglamorous moments, as the marriage doc already prices.
- **The regime-flip.** Occasion-count is the only thing that grows the ledger at all; the weights multiply what each occasion adds; the discount voids marginal repetition. So at **low count the weights dominate** — the pitch's one occasion must max specificity and visible cost, with pre-loading doing the rest — and at **high count de-correlation dominates** — frequency alone stops paying and breadth/perturbation carry the growth. The parent's "the regimes are the composition at two settings" now has its mechanism, and mere-exposure's kept point (raw density matters) lands where it belongs: early, at θ-crossing and availability, before the discount bites.

What is genuinely **free** — declared, not smuggled: the weights' relative magnitudes (calibration territory, B5); exact substitutability limits beyond the Coherence floor; the moving floor's shape; and one live structural question promoted to its own frontier — **does perturbation act through the weights?** (Its two jobs — raising per-occasion evidential quality under stress, and de-correlating the stream — both reduce to the two weights; if breadth does too, the triple reduces to count × weights, and the registered triple re-words. Posed with its test-edge: artificially independent-but-calm evidence streams reproducing, or failing to reproduce, their effects.)
