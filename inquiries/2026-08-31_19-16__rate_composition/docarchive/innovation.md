## Phase 2 — Generate (7 × 3)

### 1 · Lens Shifting
- **(generic) The readout-family lens.** `31`'s inference pipeline consumes the same accumulated observation — PRAGMA-reading a person is a *fourth* readout. The identity claim reframes as an **interface**: "the ledger drives readouts" invites future readouts (profiling; trust-calibration; the no-ledger) to attach without new machinery. → the readout registry idea (feeds P5/Absence).
- **(focused) The season's lens.** The walk-in season is a field of pairs at semi-controlled triple-levels (weekly cycles; single context; demo-perturbations). The co-movement observation can ride the *already-committed* M-form watches (T9 insistence, M10 charm, M11 spine) — **one window, four watches**, same contact-cycles feeding all. → P5's M-form shape.
- **(contrarian) The pair lens.** Each party holds their own ledger over the *same occasions* with *different weights* (what's specific to me isn't to you). Prediction-grade link: **weight-asymmetry makes asymmetric formation lawful from identical history** — the unrequited shape parameterized by weights, not by different contact; converges with window-position mismatch (canon). → P1's anatomy dividend.

### 2 · Combination
- **(generic) The forced list × threshold-crossing.** `41` §7 commits "θ falls with contact history"; `10` §4 commits θ's ordering by relationship state (cold-stranger high … existing relationship low). Joined: **the moving floor** — the admission threshold is itself ledger-dependent, so the composition is doubly cumulative: evidence accumulates AND the floor drops (early sub-θ occasions that "did nothing" become admissible content later). A missing **eighth forced element** for P2, from two committed sources.
- **(focused) Pre-loading × the extension's test.** Pre-loading is a **ledger-transplant** — third-party cycles imported. The discount applies to borrowed evidence (correlated with its source): fame-formation is broad-but-shallow (high f, thin own-ledger) and **re-prices on first own-cycles** (meeting your hero). → the borrowed-ledger clause for P1/P3 wording.
- **(contrarian) Duration × the non-update clause.** If the clock is cycles, zero-cycle stretches change nothing: **pause-invariance** — formation resumes at prior-strength (the school-summer test: autumn resumes at the spring level, not reset). A crisp falsifiable face of cycle-clock. → P1/P3.

### 3 · Inversion (depth-checked)
- **(generic, iterated).** L1: "the triple governs the rate" → "the rate governs the triple" (formed pairs seek contact — component-level feedback). L2 (system): **the triple's levels are endogenous** — accumulation grows capacity and contact-surface (window_close's committed co-growth), which raises frequency and breadth. Consequences: formation curves are **S-shaped structurally** (slow start → co-growth acceleration → discount plateau — both legs committed); the co-movement test must *measure* levels, not assume them fixed. → P2 derivation + P3 caveat.
- **(focused) Invert "weights held comparable."** What if weights co-vary with the triple by construction (perturbation raises tether/quality — K5)? Then comparability is the test's standing **confound**: stratify by occasion-type or the co-movement observation is spurious either way. → P3's confound clause; P4 gains its stakes.
- **(contrarian) Invert "formation is a readout."** "Formation IS the ledger" — if f's rise just *is* accumulation, the extension is trivially true and predicts nothing. Tested: fails, with a sharpening — the ledger is **pre-doxastic** (the accumulated observation record); f (beliefs), the gate-reads, the frame-label, and the backing-verdict are all *processed* readouts of it. The extension stays contentful (formation could have had its own clock; the claim says it doesn't) — and P1's wording improves: the ledger is the observation-accumulation, not the belief.

### 4 · Constraint Manipulation (both directions)
- **(ADD, generic) Zero breadth + zero perturbation** (the coffee-shop-regular regime): fast early rise (θ-crossing; availability) → hard plateau (correlation) — **the plateau theorem**: without de-correlation, formation is bounded; the only escapes are weight-raising or *endogenous* breadth (the relationship creating new contexts). Matches the vendor-zone/regular-zone. → P2 corollary.
- **(REMOVE, focused) Remove B7** (suppose the correlated-evidence discount false): deceleration fails, settling never plateaus, the independence advantage dies, the regime-flip dies, mere-exposure wins. **B7 is the composition's keystone** — most of the forced structure is forced *given the discount*; its validation (`31` §6's classifier) is the field's highest-leverage empirical item. → P2's boundary honesty + P5's note sharpened.
- **(ADD, contrarian) Assume the reduction true** (K5: breadth/perturbation act through the weights): the register's triple re-words to "count × weights"; the identity claim simplifies; the consequence-map (what re-words where) makes P4 actionable-if-triggered rather than idle. → P4.

### 5 · Absence Recognition (both levels, both directions)
- **(patch-level missing):** the claim's registered wordings never name the **clock** (cycle); no M-item watches co-movement; `02`'s row names two readouts only; the comparability criterion existed nowhere until P3.
- **(redesign-level, missing):** designed from scratch, FR2 would carry a **readout registry** — one entry defining the ledger (object; growth parameters; weights; correlation) plus a registry of readouts (each: conditions, component-needs). → P5's shape alternative.
- **(redesign-level, already present):** the corpus already runs partial ledger-instruments — the conduct-ledger, the no-ledger, the evening review's sequence question, forecast-check logging. The interface exists in fragments; P5 should point, not invent.

### 6 · Domain Transfer (vocabulary-only)
- **(native)** the corpus's own M-form/stamped-prediction style — the observation shape for co-movement follows M10/M11 precedent.
- **(different)** signal processing's *effective sample size* (correlated samples count as fewer) — precedent for the independence advantage; pharmacology's loading-dose/dosing-interval/threshold vocabulary — presentation-only precedent for pre-loading/cycle/θ. Flagged, never apparatus.
- **(contrarian)** epidemiology's own critique of cumulative-exposure products (pack-years mis-summing nonlinear risk) — precedent *against* naive product-form; supports threshold/path-dependent shapes. Vocabulary-only.

### 7 · Extrapolation
- **(generic) Late regime.** As pairs saturate contexts (all breadth shared), only perturbation still de-correlates — **long-standing pairs' formation runs on perturbation** (life events; the unglamorous moments the marriage doc already prices). → P2 sub-line.
- **(focused) Weight-interventions vs triple-interventions.** Parasocial: records raise weights and pre-load while the interactive triple stays near zero — formation without detection. Not a refutation: the co-movement claim binds **triple-interventions**; weight-interventions may move readouts differentially (each readout's component-needs differ). → P3's test grammar gains intervention-typing.
- **(contrarian) Event-repricing asymmetry.** Extend pause-invariance to its boundary: cycle-gaps preserve (non-update), but **verdict-events re-price** — after a gate-collapse or declared no, the ledger's evidence re-reads under the new label (the recording/gating machinery). Formation resumes at prior-strength after a *pause*, not after an *event*. → P1/P3 corollary pair.
