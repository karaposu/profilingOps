### MQA — Meta-question alignment

**Two reconciles and one surface.**

**Reconcile 1 — joint axis `what "form of" means`.** MQ1's `adjudicate-subsumption`, MQ2's stance sub-axis on species-versus-composition, and MQ3's `produce-a-taxonomic-verdict` span one question: **which relation is being asked about?** *Species-of* says compliance is a kind of attachment, the way a robin is a kind of bird. *Made-of-the-same-stuff* says both are built from the same components at different settings, which is compatible with their being distinct kinds. The statement's *"form of"* is natural in both senses, and they have different answers.

**Reconcile 2 — joint axis `is the layer under test`.** MQ1's `test-the-taxonomy`, MQ2's verdict sub-axis on the layer's authority, MQ3's `produce-a-restructure`, and MQ4's restructuring-scope ambiguity span one question: is the engagement-state layer the **frame** the answer works inside, or the **object** the answer evaluates?

**Surface — irreducible overlap.** MQ1's `reveal-a-third-option` and `check-the-prior-answer` share territory but resist a joint axis. Is *"or they are more complex involved?"* (i) an invitation to build a **richer taxonomy** than the yes/no the hypothesis offers, or (ii) a courteous way of registering **doubt about the quoted claim** — that *"was never attachment"* sounded too absolute?
