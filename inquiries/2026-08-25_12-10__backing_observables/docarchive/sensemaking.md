## Perspective Checking

- **Receiver's perspective:** wants an instrument usable mid-interaction — but every in-moment signal has a **false-positive profile**: anxious-but-backed displayers check reception (reassurance-seeking mimics monitoring-leakage); polish mimics performance (a much-repeated true statement sounds rehearsed *because it is* — rehearsed ≠ unbacked); neurodivergent and cross-cultural display rules break affect-congruence without any performance present. Consequence: no channel reads alone; convergence is mandatory, and the instrument needs a hazard-list as part of itself.
- **Performer's / first-person perspective:** the calibrating performer often self-describes as sincere — motivated blindness makes introspection ("do I mean it?") the *worst* instrument. The first-person form must run on the same conduct-channels turned inward (do I do this off-stage? did I pay the advertised costs? does my content vary by target or replay a template?). This satisfies the no-interior-access bound in first person too — an unexpected convergence.
- **Theory's perspective:** each observable must name its corpus home (A7) or declare itself a new *signal* under an existing element; the account's output shape is a reading-layer artifact (a `31`-adjacent instrument), not new machinery.
- **Adversary's perspective (the skilled performer who knows the list):** channel-by-channel defeats exist — attention discipline suppresses leakage; long-cons pre-pay costs; Stanislavski manufactures the in-moment state. But defeating **all channels durably** requires generating the state, paying the costs, maintaining conduct unobserved, and accruing the lived history — at which point the performance has *become* the disposition (the decay arc's own endpoint, run in reverse). The distinction is adversarially robust in the limit only because the limit collapses the distinction. This is the honest outer boundary.

## Sense Versions

**SV1 — the sign-list reading (unstable).** "List the telltale signs of faking." Fails immediately: sign-lists are exactly what skilled performers train against, what `31` forbids reading with certainty, and what false-positive profiles poison. Rejected as a *format*, retained as raw material.

**SV2 — the root asymmetry.** All the seeds share one cause: a backed display is **state-generated** — the occupied state co-produces words, affect, timing, and conduct for free. A performed display is **controller-generated** — a deliberate process must *compose* what the state would have *emitted*. Composition has costs the state doesn't pay: limited bandwidth, needed feedback, prepared material, ongoing effort, audience-scope, unpaid real costs, no lived history. Every observable is a place where one of those costs surfaces. The sign-list becomes a **channel-list with a common mechanism** — and the why-observable question is answered: performance is a production problem, and production leaves production-marks.

**SV3 — channels by reading-condition.** The marks sort by *when and where they can be read*:

| # | Channel | Reads | Corpus home | Reading-condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Congruence/alignment** — channels agreeing; affect matching content; reaction-latency (computed responses arrive a beat late) | in-moment | EC Affect-Content Congruence; Coherence-Alignment | any live interaction |
| 2 | **Monitoring-leakage** — attention flicking to reception; content steering by audience reaction | in-moment | narcissist finding (new named signal) | audience present; observer watches the watcher |
| 3 | **Specificity / off-script survival** — target-generated vs template content; conduct when the interaction leaves the script | in-moment | Signal Specificity | a perturbation occurs or is introduced |
| 4 | **Decay / load-lapse** — the display sagging under length, fatigue, cognitive load | over-time | mimicry decay arc | sustained or repeated observation |
| 5 | **Off-stage continuity** — the conduct persisting where the performer believes no audience exists | cross-context | Coherence longitudinal | access to unstaged contexts or third-party report |
| 6 | **Cost-trail** — the advertised state's real costs actually paid (time, money, risk, forgone options), verifiable in artifacts | artifact | SP costly-signal ground | records, third parties, observable sacrifices |
| 7 | **Sequence-shape / account-consistency** — the lived history having the shape the display claims; retellings staying consistent | retrospective | insistence finding; Coherence story-consistency | history examinable; repeated accounts |

Reading rule inherited from the material: channels 1–3 read the *now*; 4–7 read the *standing pattern*; **confidence lives only in convergence** — channels fail independently for different reasons, so agreement across reading-conditions is the graded verdict. Per-instance certainty is not on offer.

**SV4 — the Stanislavski limit forces a split (the account's structural product).** Test warm's presence-frame against the actor: the manufactured state is *real* — channels 1–3 pass, and rightly, because the shown state **is present**. But channels 4–7 fail — off-stage it is dropped by design, no cost-trail, no lived sequence. The channel-set is telling us something the binary hides: "the shown state is actually there" has **two readable senses**:

- **Occurrent backing** — the state is present *now*, in the display (read by channels 1–3).
- **Standing backing** — the state is a durable property of the person beyond the display context (read by channels 4–7).

The Stanislavski actor is occurrently backed, standing-unbacked. The calibrating performer is typically both-unbacked (leakage betrays even the occurrent claim). The much-rehearsed founder who means it is both-backed (polish notwithstanding). This is **not a redefinition** of A1 — the definition "the shown state is actually there" stands; the split lives in the *reading layer*: which sense a given channel reads. What a display *claims* is usually the standing sense ("I care" claims a disposition, not a moment), so re-pricing (A3) operates on standing backing, with occurrent backing as its evidence.

One more piece falls out: **frame-consent.** Theater deceives no one — the audience knows the frame. A performance is a *problem* only when it claims backedness it lacks (frame-violating). This dissolves the rehearsed-forms worry at its root: **preparation crosses into performance where the shown state is absent, not where the form was practiced.** A practiced opener delivered with the state present is a polished backed display. A practiced opener run while the state is absent — attention shown but not paid — is performance, and channels 1–3 will have something to read.

**SV5 — hazards and the adversarial boundary.** Fold the perspective checks in: (i) each channel carries a **false-positive profile** — reassurance-seeking mimics leakage; polish mimics performance; display-rule variation breaks congruence; a chaotic life breaks sequence-shape without any performance; (ii) the **adversarial limit** — every channel is individually beatable, but beating all of them durably requires becoming the thing displayed; the long-con converges on the real. Consequences: the instrument is convergence-only, hazard-annotated, and honest about its limit (it detects *cheap* performance well, *expensive* performance progressively worse, and *complete* performance not at all — because complete performance is backing).

**SV6 — the stable account (saturated).**

1. **Why observable:** performance is production; the controller pays composition-costs the state never pays; the costs surface as marks. (SV2)
2. **What to read:** seven graded channels across four reading-conditions, each with a corpus home and a false-positive profile; verdicts only by convergence. (SV3 + SV5)
3. **What "backed" reads as:** occurrent (channels 1–3) vs standing (channels 4–7); displays typically claim the standing sense; re-pricing targets it. (SV4)
4. **Boundary zone adjudicated:** practiced-form-with-state-present = backed and polished; practiced-form-with-state-absent = performance; state-manufacture = occurrently backed, standing-open; frame-consented performance is not deception. (SV4)
5. **Status verdict:** upgraded from "currently unfalsifiable in practice" to **structurally falsifiable in expectation** — specified channels, specified reading-conditions, graded convergence — while per-instance certainty remains unavailable and empirical calibration remains undone. (SV5's limit honored)
6. **Consumers:** narcissist genuine/mimicked ← the full second-person instrument (leakage + off-stage + decay were already its material); calc-program self-check ← the first-person conduct-form (off-stage, cost, template, decay questions); rehearsed-forms zone ← adjudication in (4); deployed-exit vs leaving-to-be-chased ← the exit read through channels 2, 5, 7 (does the exit monitor for pursuit; does it complete off-stage; does its sequence-shape hinge on being chased).
