## Phase 2–3 — Adversarial Evaluation & Verdicts

### C1 — the root-asymmetry mechanism (P1)
**Prosecution:** over-unification. Cost-trail is not a *composition* cost — paying money is the state's consequence, not the display's production. "One root" is rhetoric covering two mechanisms.
**Defense:** both reduce to one sentence: **an absent state causes nothing itself; the controller must cause everything, and controllers have finite capacity** — the display's stream (production) and the state's footprint (consequences) alike.
**Collision:** the defense's abstraction holds, but the prosecution's two-family structure is real and *useful* — production-marks (bandwidth, monitoring, template, effort, audience-scope) read in-moment; consequence-marks (cost, off-stage conduct, lived history) read in continuity. And the families map exactly onto the occurrent/standing partition, giving C3's split a mechanism ground instead of a mere channel-observation.
**Verdict: REFINE** — state the root once, then the two mark-families explicitly; adopt as the account's spine.

### C2 — the assembled instrument (P2)
**Prosecution:** (a) unusable complexity — 7 channels × 4 annotations × 3 modes × scale × discount × order; a mid-conversation reader will over-read single signals anyway, the exact `31` hazard. (b) The false-positive profiles gut channels 1–3 (anxious reassurance-seeking mimics leakage; polish mimics performance), and the strong channels (5–7) need access most readers lack — the instrument mostly returns "defer." (c) The discount rule leans on iteration-3.4, whose multi-source prediction is itself flagged unvalidated (the insistence route-map's route 14).
**Defense:** (a) the full form is the canonical spec; context-cuts are part of it (walk-in form = channels 1–3 + mandatory defer; relationship form = full set). (b) "Mostly defer in short windows" is not a bug — it *is* the possibility-verdict: graded and access-dependent; the old admission survives in short windows, the upgrade is real in long ones. (c) The discount enters as structural probability (same-source evidence is correlated), not as the contested empirical prediction; the dependency gets flagged.
**Collision:** prosecution's usability point partially stands — an undifferentiated table would be a `31`-hazard in canonical form.
**Verdict: REFINE** — publish with the two context-cut forms and the iteration-3.4 dependency flag. Core survives.

### C3 — the occurrent/standing split + frame-consent (P3, the flagged joint)

**Verdict: REFINE** — claim-scope wording + role-fold. The split survives; with C1's mark-families it is now mechanism-grounded.
