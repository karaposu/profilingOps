- **(generic) The discount rule.** Combine the convergence rule with iteration-3.4's correlated-evidence discount: repeated sightings of the *same* channel are correlated evidence and update less; convergence counts only across *independent* channels/reading-conditions. Three alignment-lapses < one lapse + one off-stage report + one cost-gap. → P2's convergence rule gains its inner arithmetic. **SURVIVES.**
- **(focused) Hinge-test inheritance.** The deployed-exit consumer needs no new instrument: the insistence finding's hinge-test transfers — does the exit-conduct hinge on pursuit appearing? Pursuit absent + exit completes → deployed. Non-pursuit produces slowing, reachability-maintenance, re-approach → performed (leaving-to-be-chased). Channels 2/5/7 in the existing test's clothes. → P5. **SURVIVES.**
- **(contrarian) The flat-audience probe.** The performer's monitoring-target is the receiver's reception — so the receiver can *withhold* it (go flat, delay reactions): a backed display continues unchanged; a performed one adjusts, chases the missing feedback, or stalls. Detection by feedback-starvation. Generalizes: the reader can **create** reading-conditions, not just wait for them. **SURVIVES** with a hazard: a detected probe reads as a test and damages trust (probe sparingly, cheaply, deniably).

### G2 — Absence recognition
- **(generic) The positive mark.** The channel-list reads performance-marks; absent is a mark whose *presence* indicates backing: **fluency-on-the-unrehearsable** — instant, specific response where composition would need time (real knowledge answers immediately; genuine enthusiasm interrupts). Latency cuts both ways: too-slow = composing; too-fluent-on-the-novel = backed. → fold into channel 1. **SURVIVES.**
- **(focused) The forecast-check.** The channels read past and present; absent is the future: displays imply conduct-forecasts, and backing = forecast-accuracy over contact-cycles. This is the priced path (A3) operationalized as a reading procedure — log the implicit prediction, check at the next cycle. → P2 gains temporal closure; the cheapest high-tether read available to anyone. **SURVIVES.**
- **(contrarian) Detection-cost and window-ecology.** Absent is who can afford to read: channels 4–7 need time and access; short-window contexts (walk-in, first date, con) offer only 1–3 — the weakest, least-tethered channels. **Reading-power grows with relationship duration and context-breadth.** This explains con-artist ecology structurally: cheap performance *selects* short-window hunting grounds; the decay arc is why it can't migrate to long windows. → P4. **SURVIVES.**
