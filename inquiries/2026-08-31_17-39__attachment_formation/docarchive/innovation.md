## Phase 2 — Generate (7 × 3)

### 1 · Lens Shifting
- **(generic) The object's lens.** Objects have lifecycles — a game gets old, an exam ends, a startup keeps producing. Evaluate the pair's formation as tracking the *object's generativity curve*: the object's exhaustion schedule sets when the pair must convert (to Resonance or to a new object) or plateau. → frontier: *the object lifecycle as the pair's clock.*
- **(focused) The sender's lens (the founder).** The walk-in season is a one-object, low-frequency regime; the object (the product on their case) must carry all the specificity, and "return only with something new" is *keeping the object generative between contacts*. → frontier: *object novelty as the sender's lever in low-frequency regimes.*
- **(contrarian) The absence lens.** Much of formation happens between occasions — the receiver thinks about the other, anticipates, retells to third parties. The belief-frame updates only on evidence; but re-encountering the *record* is an occasion (charm_persistence's row-note), and retelling is a third-party channel. → frontier: *between-occasion consolidation* — do beliefs strengthen by re-encounter and retelling without new evidence from the sender?

### 2 · Combination
- **(generic) The five insistence mechanisms × the receiver's side.** Threshold-crossing (repeated co-presence lowers θ), availability-resampling (a better hour), evidence-delivery (the activity), consistency-accrual (the same schedule — every break), conviction-display (choosing each other again) — re-read for the *receiver's* formation under shared objects. → frontier: *the five mechanisms are two-sided* — they describe any sequence, not only the returning sender.
- **(focused) FR2's triple × "perturbation reveals backing."** Shared hardship is high-perturbation, and perturbation is when the gates are read *hard* — Coherence and Composure under stress. Formation under hardship front-loads gate evidence (who holds, who cracks). → frontier: *hardship as a gate-test accelerator* — hardship bonds are fast because the *gates* settle fast, not only because f rises.
- **(contrarian) Reciprocity-Hope's past-conditioning × the ledger.** The person helped in bad times who "doesn't care" afterwards may not weight the past low — they may be *avoiding an unpayable debt* (a ledger they cannot close), devaluing the helper to escape it. → frontier: *debt-avoidance devaluation* — a third mechanism for the decay profile, beside mix and bearing.

### 3 · Inversion (depth-checked)
- **(generic, iterated).** L1: "attachment forms *through* shared objects" → "objects become shared *through* attachment." L2: "the object is not prior to the pair; the pair makes it shared by co-attending." System-level: **co-attention is the primitive; the object is whatever co-attention lands on.** "Find a shared object" is backwards — the move is *attend together, and an object appears* (the kids do not choose the game first; the game is where attention landed). Existence-axis: zero objects? Pure co-presence — the object is the co-presence itself (Rhythm-Comfort's comfortable silence): a null-object bond. Identity-axis: a shared object *is* a sustained co-attention target. → reframes the Sid-function as attention-manufacture and the sales case as "get their attention onto what yours is on."
- **(focused) Invert "the direct route trips the gate."** "The direct route is the *strongest* route once the gate is open": declarations between already-attached parties (the vow; "I love you") are direct and work. Directness is a *later-stage* move — the object route forms, the direct move **seals** (the confrontation finding's verdict-forcing; crystallization). → frontier: *direct moves as sealing, not forming* — a sequencing rule.
- **(contrarian) Invert the spoon.** "More per exposure forms more" → "less per exposure forms more": many small, frequent, low-cost occasions may out-form one intense block because (i) they are more *independent* (multi-source updates faster than correlated evidence), (ii) consistency-accrual needs repetitions, (iii) intensity risks the display path. The marriage doc already says small daily costs outweigh grand gestures. → frontier: *the independence advantage of many small occasions.*

### 4 · Constraint Manipulation (both directions)
- **(ADD, generic) "Assume zero perturbation."** A calm life: gates never tested hard; Coherence read only longitudinally; formation slow and Exchange/Resonance-heavy; the backing finding implies such bonds carry *unverified* gate evidence. → frontier: *untested bonds* — attachments whose gate evidence is thin, and their failure mode (the first crisis re-prices everything — the friendship doc's crisis-absence).
- **(REMOVE, focused) Remove "beliefs are about the sender."** A theory of *pair* formation appears — the pair-frame ("what we are", the frame-definition finding), symmetric costs, the shared model as a joint object. The removal shows the corpus already has a pair-level object that formation could be predicated of. → frontier: *the pair-frame's formation* — how "what we are" crystallizes (default crystallization; declarations).
- **(ADD, contrarian) "Assume one occasion only."** The pitch; the approach. All delivery in one encounter: specificity and cost maxed; pre-loading (social proof) does the rest; independence only via third parties (delegated persistence). → frontier: *single-occasion formation* — the limit the sales doc's principles are the solution to.

### 5 · Absence Recognition (both levels, both directions)
- **(patch-level) What's missing:** a receiver-side formation account; a dyad-scale object statement; a canonical definition of "occasion" (charm_persistence's row-note has one); a persistence dimension in attachment bearings; a fast-bonding-under-hardship case.
- **(redesign-level, missing) If formation were specified today,** each variable would carry a **delivery profile** — which occasions feed it (Charm: the person at their work · Hope: exchanges and promises kept · Fear: the sender's capacity shown · Resonance: co-attention) — and the theory would carry a **conversion table** — what turns one variable's evidence into another's over time (Exchange-Hope → Resonance through a generative object; need-Hope → Fear under dependency; Charm → cost-of-loss under irreplaceability). FR3 (Transactional → Bonded) generalized. → frontier: *the conversion table.*
- **(redesign-level, already present):** the docs' formation principles *are* delivery profiles per domain (presence at cost; small daily costs; bring new things; promises kept); the redesign articulates them by variable.

### 6 · Domain Transfer (native + different; vocabulary-only)
- **(native — learning vocabulary)** *spaced repetition* — spaced occasions consolidate better than massed ones; precedent for the independence advantage; never apparatus.
- **(different — team formation; initiation rites)** teams form around the *task*, not around liking (forming–storming–norming around work); hazing manufactures adversity. Precedent for the object's double job and for the manufactured line.
- **(contrarian — economics: relationship-specific investment / hold-up)** investments specific to a relationship (learning each other's ways; the notes shared; the shared jokes) are worthless outside it — they create attachment (specific capital = the shared model) and dependency (cost-of-loss) *at once*. → frontier: *relationship-specific investment* — what the military month builds: capital that is both Resonance's shared model and the fear inquiry's stake.

### 7 · Extrapolation
- **(generic) Extend the delivery rate to continuous availability.** Phones raise occasion frequency toward continuous at near-zero specificity — many correlated, low-content occasions. Prediction: continuous low-specificity contact *maintains* (H_c) but forms little; formation still needs high-specificity occasions. → frontier: *continuous availability's formation ceiling.*
- **(focused) Extend the object lifecycle.** Pairs that outlive an object's exhaustion have converted (Resonance) or re-objected; the long friendship is a *sequence of objects* (school → games → work → children). Prediction: bonds with a chain of shared objects persist; one-object bonds end with the object. → frontier: *object succession.*
- **(contrarian) Extend perturbation.** A bond formed entirely under hardship may carry a mix that does not transfer to calm — the war buddies with nothing to say in peacetime: Resonance around the object of survival, exhausted in peace; Safety-Hope with no occasion. → frontier: *the route's signature outliving its route.*
