## Phase 1 — Seed

**Seed type:** Gap + **Dissatisfaction**. The gap is a category question; the dissatisfaction is that three attempts to close it produced three rewrites and no execution.

**Direction (valuation).** What matters is not producing a better-argued fourth version. Sensemaking said outright that this will *be* a fourth version and can fail the way the previous three did. What matters is whether the run can find a way for it to fail **that has not already been tried** — and, if it cannot, whether the honest answer is that there is nothing here to name.

**Mode:** Production-task. The seed is Decomposition's 7-piece question tree.

### Methodology-Mode Consideration

- **(a) Inherited mode:** **Standard default.** The seed is a committed piece-list; its language is *state · apply · show · render*. Balanced 4 Generators + 3 Framers.
- **(b) Alternative named:** **Contrarian-rethink (Framer-weighted).**
- **(c) What follows under the alternative:** the candidate space concentrates on attacking the upstream verdicts rather than developing them — challenges to the two-relation split, to the signature category, to the drift diagnosis, and to whether the object needs a category at all.
- **(d) Decision: `Seed-time-methodology-mode-switch: Contrarian-rethink (Framer-weighted); reason: the inherited mode's predictable output IS this inquiry's named failure mode.`**

  Sensemaking's Risk perspective established that this finding will be the **fourth** version of an action item whose three predecessors were each written confidently and each superseded — and that the difference claimed for this one is structural rather than confidential. A Standard run elaborates the signature verdict across seven pieces and produces exactly the artefact that description predicts: a confident fourth version. The one property that would distinguish it is having **genuinely tried to break it**, and Framer-weighting is the mode that does that. This is a case where the inherited mode's output is predictable *in the specific way the inquiry has already identified as its risk*, which is the spec's stated switch condition.

  **What the switch costs, recorded:** the four Generators run lighter. Coverage minimum is still met — all four fire — but Combination, Absence Recognition, Domain Transfer and Extrapolation get one pass each rather than depth, and the Framers get the depth-check and multi-axis treatment.
