### G1 — Combination
- **(generic) Audience-drag and the ledger-order rule.** Combine the three-ledger structure with the reopening split: her ledger may re-raise via new-context evidence while the **audience ledger stays settled and keeps feeding back** ("aren't you two just friends?") — continuous third-party re-recording that drags reopening. Consequence, stated as mechanism-fact: successful reopenings disproportionately involve **leaving the shared context** (the folk "it happened when we both moved away"), because relocation suspends the audience's enforcement. → P5 gains audience-drag; the isolation-guard (F2-contrarian) must travel with it. **SURVIVES.**
- **(focused) The liveness-meter.** Combine liveness-decay with `60` §11's capacity-gauge: the gauge's readiness-signals (their clarifying probes; contexts they create) are exactly **liveness reads**. The gauge therefore doubles as a window-position instrument: signals present = open; once-present-now-absent = closing; long-absent = likely settled. → P4 annotation; the asker gets a position-read without asking. **SURVIVES.**
- **(contrarian) Functional nulls.** Combine settling with its *protective* face: strong context-nulls are **infrastructure** — the settled "colleague" frame is what lets mixed workplaces function; organizations *rely* on strong nulls, and weakening them has costs. Settling is not merely the asker's hazard; it is the social fabric's stabilizer. → P6's de-pathologizing extended to design-level. **SURVIVES.**

### G2 — Absence recognition
- **(generic) The honest twin of engineered liveness.** Absent: what keeps a question live *legitimately*? **Real joint novelty** — a pair whose shared contexts genuinely keep growing (new projects, new terrain) keeps the frame young without manipulation. The discriminator against breadcrumbing: real novelty is **jointly produced and cost-bearing**; engineered liveness is cheap, timed, and unilateral. → P6 gains the dual's honest twin. **SURVIVES.**
- **(focused) Window-position mismatch.** Absent: the settling-speed asymmetry between parties. His question is live (he's planning to ask); hers settled two months ago — and the silent threshold means **the mismatch has no signal until an ask samples it**. Name it as the standard tragedy of the domain, and note the mitigation is exactly the liveness-meter read periodically and lightly. → P4/P5. **SURVIVES.**
- **(contrarian) Internal settling.** Absent: the asker's own frame also settles ("she's just a friend" can settle on *him* — then *his* liveness must revive before any ask exists). One line; symmetric machinery. **SURVIVES (one line).**

### G3 — Domain transfer
- **(generic) Hysteresis.** Path-dependence vocabulary: identical conduct performed late does not do what it would have done early — the window's close is hysteretic. The mechanism is already native (recording + dead question); the term is one presentation line. **MERGED (one line).**
- **(focused) The scheduled frame-review.** Institutions formalize windows: probation periods, tenure clocks, account-review dates — **pre-registered verdict-moments that prevent silent settling**. Transfer to pairs: the implicit frame-review at natural milestones (the defer-with-a-date pattern applied to the *frame itself*) prevents both the premature ask and the silent close. → P5/doctrine: the anti-silent-close instrument. **SURVIVES.**
- **(contrarian) The incumbent-value term.** From niche-occupation: a settled frame **does work** in the receiver's life — the "best friend" label fills a role she values. Reopening doesn't just revive a question against a defense; it **puts the incumbent frame's value at risk** ("if we date and it fails, I lose my best friend" is a real cost-term, not an excuse). Reopening cost is therefore **three-legged**: liveness-revival + recording-defense + incumbent-value-at-risk — and high-value settled frames are hardest to reopen precisely because the current frame is *good*. → P5's missing leg. **SURVIVES — the strongest addition.**
