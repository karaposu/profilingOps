## Sense Versions

**SV1 — the time-as-cause reading (unstable).** "Frames settle by habit; time does it." Rejected: time does nothing in BDA — evidence does. Every settling step below is evidential; where time appears, it is a count of *cycles carrying evidence*, not duration as such.

**SV2 — category-hardening: the settling mechanism proper.** The unresolved frame is a live pair-question ("what are we?") over candidate labels. Each contact-cycle delivers conduct read against the candidates, and three committed mechanisms compound:

1. **Fit-selection (A9, A2).** The label that absorbs the cycle's conduct *without auxiliary hypotheses* gains confidence. Friendly-lunch conduct fits "friend" cleanly; it fits "courting" only with a rider ("maybe he's shy"). And the decisive move the sketch already committed: **non-asking is conduct that the default label predicts.** "He never made a move" is not absent evidence — it is *positive confirmation* of "friend," because friend-conduct predicts exactly that absence while courting-conduct predicts its opposite. This is why sustained non-asking actively closes the window rather than merely failing to open it.
2. **Deceleration (A3).** Cycles grow same-shaped; same-shaped repeats are correlated evidence and update less; the label's confidence asymptotes. Settling therefore *stabilizes itself*: a fast-moving early zone, a hardening middle, a plateau where the fortieth lunch adds nothing the thirty-ninth didn't. The shelf-life has this three-phase shape in every domain.
3. **Conduct-recording (A4).** The receiver doesn't just hold the label — she *acts on it*: confides accordingly, introduces him accordingly ("my friend"), plans around it. Each act is a **micro-declaration** entering her own consistency-ledger and the social record — the recording leg of crystallization firing in grains instead of one spoken event. Symmetrically, his non-asking conduct is *his* micro-declaration stream: he performs the friend-role into both ledgers too.

**The silent threshold (A5):** at some accumulated confidence-plus-recording the frame crosses from *open question* to *settled fact* — and nothing marks the crossing. This is why the folk case is *discovered*, not announced: the asker learns the window closed only by trying to open it.

**SV3 — the liveness rider (Hope's role adjudicated).** Is Hope-expiry a second process? Mostly no, partly yes. The possibility-belief ("this could become more") just *is* the competitor label's confidence — as "friend" hardens, "could-be-more" loses by complementarity; one competitive process, two faces. But one genuine rider remains: **liveness-decay**. A possibility held flat — neither rising nor falling — stops being *consulted* long before it is disbelieved (the trajectory-need, A7; the exhausted-source machinery's cousin). So the settled state has a texture the label-competition alone doesn't give: the question passes through three stages — **open question → closed question → no longer a question.** The end-state of default crystallization is not "she decided no"; it is "the question left her active set." This is the account's sharpest structural product, because it splits the reopening problem in two (SV6) and marks the deep difference from a declared no: the declared no is a *live-ish question with a strong defense*; the settled frame is a *dead question with a mild defense*.

**SV4 — the attractor-field and its context-dependence.** What there is to settle into: **context-supplied default labels** — every context carries a native pair-category (workplace → colleagues; commerce → vendor; venue → staff/regular; social circle → friends; a dating app → *prospects*). Consequences:

- **The same conduct settles different labels in different contexts.** Non-asking in a friend-group settles "friend"; non-asking on a dating app — where the *romantic* frame is the null — settles "dead prospect," and fast. Context-dependence = *which label is the null* × *how strongly the context enforces it* (professional norms actively police "colleagues"; a friend-group merely defaults to "friend").
- **Substance under surface:** the labels are social names; the substance is the corpus's own object — a stabilized attachment-form with a capped claim-scope (A8, A10). One line suffices; the forms-inventory is not disturbed.
- **Three ledgers, not one.** The frame is per-reader: hers, his, and the audience's. **The audience settles first** — they see only public conduct (all frame-typical; the private micro-signals that keep her question alive never reach them) — and the audience's settled label feeds back as third-party recording ("her friend," said in introductions), accelerating hers. Audience-settling is an *accelerator*, and it is why pairs embedded in dense mutual circles settle faster than isolated pairs.

**SV5 — forcing-source multiplication and the rate-factors.** The native derivation the critique asked for:

**Multiplication = co-growth.** Forcing-sources scale with the pair's **contact-surface**: as accumulation grows, the pair touches more contexts (each new context brings allocation decisions — the plus-one, the trip, the holiday, the job-in-another-city), more third parties (each needs a label to operate on the pair — every introduction is a micro-verdict-demand), and the frame's internal salience rises (the more the pair matters, the more the unresolved question costs the receiver herself — closure-pressure is her own planning need, A13). Since contact-surface grows with the same accumulation that raises capacity, **the window's opening and its forcing grow together** — the "nucleation" talk reduces to this co-growth, no physics needed. And the mechanical reason uncontrolled closes are *worse*: a third-party forcing ("so are you two together?", asked at a party) extracts her verdict **publicly, at maximum recording, with none of the asker's manner-control** — the worst conditions the confrontation account knows.

**Rate-factors (shape-level, per T11's letter):** (1) **contact-frequency** — settling proceeds per cycle (A12); high-frequency pairs settle fast in calendar time, low-frequency pairs slowly, both normally in cycles; (2) **context-breadth** — single-context pairs settle fast toward that context's null; multi-context pairs resist (competing nulls keep the question open — why "we kept meeting in new places" pairs stay unresolved longer); (3) **perturbation-rate** — frame-discrepant events reset the deceleration (a crisis, a trip, a genuinely new context re-opens the question briefly); (4) **the null's ambient strength** (app vs workplace vs friend-group); (5) **conduct's frame-typicality** — borderline conduct (readable both ways) keeps the question alive; cleanly typical conduct closes it; (6) **competing claimants** — a rival suitor keeps "could-be-more" consulted (comparison sustains liveness).

**The FR2 contribution (the two clocks resolved):** factors 1–3 are *identical* to the backing map's detection-lag parameters. That is not analogy but the frontier's actual shape: **accumulated observation is one ledger driving several state-variables at once** — reading-power rises, backing becomes legible, the frame settles, reserves deplete — the corpus's temporal quantities co-move on a single clock read through different instruments. This is the receiver-side dynamics statement FR2 has been collecting customers for.

**SV6 — the stable account: the worked case and the reopening adjudication.**

**The friend-zone, natively mechanized:** context-null "friend" (A8) + his non-asking confirming it per cycle (SV2.1 — the label *predicts* the absence) + her conduct-recording and the audience's early settlement feeding back (SV2.3, SV4) + deceleration plateauing it (SV2.2) + liveness-decay retiring the question (SV3). When his eventual declaration arrives, it fails **twice before content**: it must *re-raise a question that is no longer live*, and it arrives as frame-discrepant conduct from inside the frame — "this isn't what we are" — which is precisely the felt "it's too late / this is weird" of the folk description. The folk term dissolves into: a settled least-cost label, a dead question, and graded recording. *(Illustration discharged natively — A15 honored.)*

**Reopening, adjudicated as two ordered problems:**
1. **Re-raising** — making the question live again. In-frame conduct cannot do it (the label absorbs it); it requires **frame-discrepant evidence**, and the cleanest source is **new-context evidence** (A14): encountered genuinely outside the settled frame's home context, the person generates conduct the label's writ doesn't cover — unabsorbed evidence, the question stirs. (This is the cross-context channel from the backing account doing reopening work — same machinery, new job.)
2. **Re-answering** — the revived question then meets a **mild but real position-defense**, graded by accumulated recording (how much conduct- and third-party-recording accrued — A4, never "cheap because unspoken"; the warm flag's correction).

**The cost-structure contrast (the grading's continuation):** a *declared no* needs **question-change** (a consistency-preserving path — strong defense, live-ish question); a *settled default* needs **question-revival first** (dead question, mild defense). Two different reopening problems, not one scale. And the conduct-consequences stay under the inherited bounds: a revival attempt *is an ask* — `60` §11 governs it entirely (carried weight, cheap no, the three binds); the account names the mechanism and stops (A15's no-playbook).

**The dark dual, named:** **engineered liveness** — feeding just enough frame-discrepant conduct to keep the other's question alive while never asking and never allowing settlement — is breadcrumbing mechanized: the frame-level face of intermittent reinforcement (`50`'s adjacency list), an extraction shape the three-binds line already prohibits. Named as a boundary object so the account cannot be read as its manual.
