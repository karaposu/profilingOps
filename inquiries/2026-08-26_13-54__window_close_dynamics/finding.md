## Vocabulary

- **Frame** — the pair's mutual category ("what we are"): a context-supplied *label* on the surface ("friend," "vendor," "regular"), a receiver-side *stance-bundle* (expected conduct + claim-scope) as the substance.
- **The pair-question** — the live question "what are we?" held over candidate labels while the frame is unresolved.
- **Hardening** — a label's confidence growing until the frame is settled fact.
- **Liveness** — whether the pair-question is still *consulted* at all — a separate axis from any label's confidence level, grounded in the corpus's own no-typology (a never-registered question is a liveness-object, not a low-confidence one).
- **Micro-declaration** — conduct that acts on a label (confiding accordingly, introducing accordingly, planning around it): the recording leg of crystallization firing in grains, no spoken verdict needed. Grains are weaker than verdicts; they *sum*, never equal.
- **The null** — a context's default pair-label (workplace → colleagues; a dating app → prospects), with a context-specific enforcement strength.
- **The three ledgers** — hers, his, and the audience's: the frame settles per-reader, at different rates.
- **Window-position mismatch** — the parties holding different window-states (his question live, hers settled) with no signal until an ask samples it.
- **Incumbent value** — the standing attachment-value the settled frame already delivers, put at risk by any reopening attempt. (Grounded F1-neutrally: attachment-value-at-risk — this finding deliberately does not route it through Fear's contested definition.)
- **Engineered liveness** — feeding just enough frame-discrepant conduct to keep the other's question alive while never asking and never allowing settlement: breadcrumbing, mechanized — the frame-level face of intermittent reinforcement.

## The Finding

### 1 · The settling mechanism: how a frame hardens with no verdict-event anywhere

Time does nothing in this theory; evidence does. The close runs on three committed mechanisms compounding per contact-cycle:

1. **Fit-selection.** Each cycle's conduct is read against the candidate labels, and the label that absorbs the record *without auxiliary hypotheses* gains. The decisive fact the sketch already held, now stated precisely: **non-asking is conduct the default label predicts.** "He never made a move" is positive confirmation of "friend" — friend-conduct predicts exactly that absence, courting-conduct predicts its opposite. The confirmation is **cumulative-comparative**, not per-instance-strong: any single quiet cycle is cheap to explain away ("maybe he's shy"), but auxiliaries bill per use — ten cycles of "maybe shy" is an expensive chain, while "friend" runs auxiliary-free throughout.
2. **Deceleration.** As cycles grow same-shaped, they become correlated evidence and update less — the label's confidence asymptotes, so settling *stabilizes itself*. The shelf-life of ambiguity therefore has a three-phase shape everywhere: a fast-moving early zone, a hardening middle, a plateau where the fortieth lunch adds nothing the thirty-ninth didn't.
3. **Conduct-recording.** The receiver acts on the leading label — confides accordingly, introduces him as "my friend," plans around the friendship — and every act is a **micro-declaration** entering her consistency-ledger and the social record. Symmetrically, his non-asking conduct is *his* micro-declaration stream. The frame graduates from belief to *position* without anyone ever speaking a verdict — recorded in grains rather than one event.

**The silent threshold:** at some accumulated confidence-plus-recording the frame crosses from open question to settled fact, and *nothing marks the crossing*. This is why the close is discovered rather than announced — the asker learns the window shut only by trying to open it.

### 2 · Liveness: the question's own life, and its death

Most of what looks like a second process is the same competition seen from the other side: the possibility-belief ("this could become more") *is* the competitor label's confidence, and it loses by complementarity as "friend" hardens. But one genuine rider remains — **liveness-decay**: a possibility held flat stops being *consulted* long before it is disbelieved. The pair-question passes through readout bands: **open question → closed question → no longer a question.** The settled end-state is not "she decided no"; it is "the question left her active set" — which is the deep difference from a declared no (a live-ish question with a strong defense) and the key to reopening (§5). The rider is not new machinery: the no-typology already commits the liveness axis (a never-registered no is an unprocessed question, independent of belief-level); this account extends that axis from pursuit-openings to standing frames. One reflexive corollary: **liveness and level can dissociate in the receiver's own state** — her question can die of inattention while her sub-threshold attachment still grows ("I realized too late" is a self-read failure, not a preference change).

### 3 · The attractor-field: what there is to settle into, and whose ledger settles

- **Contexts arrive pre-labeled.** Every context supplies a null pair-category with its own enforcement strength — the workplace actively polices "colleagues"; a friend-group merely defaults to "friend"; a dating app's null is *prospects*, which is why non-asking there settles "dead prospect" in two or three cycles (the field's fastest close), while the same conduct in a friend-group settles "friend" over months. **Context-dependence = which label is the null × how strongly the context enforces it.** (Corpus ground: frame-consent already committed context-supplied roles — "role-expected professional warmth" is a null doing theory-work.)
- **Substance under surface:** the labels are social names; the settled object is a stabilized attachment-form with a capped claim-scope.
- **Three ledgers, different clocks.** The frame settles per-reader. Under frame-typical conduct — the default-crystallization case by definition — **the audience settles first** (they see only public, typical conduct; the private micro-signals that keep her question alive never reach them), and the audience's settled label feeds back as third-party recording ("her friend," said in every introduction), accelerating hers. Pairs embedded in dense mutual circles settle faster than isolated pairs. (Condition noted: bi-typical *public* conduct instead keeps audiences unsettled — the will-they-won't-they audience is reading a stall, §4.)

### 4 · The dynamics: rate-factors, multiplication, three outcomes, and the FR2 claim

**Rate-factors (shape-level):** (1) **contact-frequency** — settling proceeds per cycle, so high-frequency pairs settle fast in calendar time, low-frequency pairs slowly, both normally in cycles; (2) **context-breadth** — single-context pairs settle fast toward that context's null; multi-context pairs resist (competing nulls keep the question open); (3) **perturbation-rate** — frame-discrepant events briefly re-open the question and reset the deceleration; (4) **the null's ambient strength**; (5) **conduct's frame-typicality** — borderline conduct keeps the question alive, cleanly typical conduct closes it; (6) **competing claimants** — a rival keeps the alternative label consulted.

**Forcing-source multiplication, derived natively:** forcing-sources scale with the pair's **contact-surface**. Growth brings more contexts (each with allocation decisions — the plus-one, the trip, the job in another city: resources cannot be allocated to an unlabeled frame), more third parties (each needs a label to operate on the pair — every introduction is a micro-verdict-demand), and rising internal salience (the receiver's own planning needs the label — closure-pressure is hers, not just the world's). Since contact-surface grows with the same accumulation that raises capacity, **the window's opening and its forcing grow together** — the parent doctrine's "near the peak, the deliberate ask beats waiting" line now carries its derivation. And the mechanical reason uncontrolled closes are worse: a third-party forcing ("so are you two together?", at a party) extracts the verdict **publicly, at maximum recording, with no manner-control** — the worst conditions the parent account knows.

**Three outcomes, not one:** the dynamics **converge** (the default close), **stall** (genuinely bi-typical conduct lets neither label out-absorb the other; deceleration never sets in; the situationship as non-convergent orbit — chronic Hope plus chronic closure-pressure; real stalls usually have a **maintainer** who manages every verdict-moment with deflections, sometimes by mutual preference, often by the engineered-liveness operator of §6), or get **forced** (a verdict-moment arrives before convergence).

**The FR2 contribution, stated as its testable:** factors 1–3 are *identical* to the detection-lag parameters derived independently in the backing inquiry. The claim is **parameter-identity**: accumulated observation is one ledger driving several readouts at once — reading-power rises, backing becomes legible, the frame settles — the same three knobs governing all of them. That is the static/dynamics frontier's first concrete cross-inquiry prediction.

### 5 · Reopening: two ordered problems, three cost-legs, one direction-neutral operation

A settled frame reopens through **two ordered problems**: first **re-raise** — make the dead question live again, which in-frame conduct cannot do (the label absorbs it); it takes **frame-discrepant evidence**, and the cleanest source is **new-context evidence** (encountered genuinely outside the settled frame's home context, the person generates conduct the label's writ doesn't cover). Then **re-answer** — the revived question meets a defense whose size is the point of the grading:

- **Leg 1 — liveness-revival** (the re-raise itself);
- **Leg 2 — recording-defense**, graded by accumulated micro-declarations and third-party adoption — *never* "cheap because unspoken";
- **Leg 3 — incumbent value**: the settled frame already delivers standing attachment-value, and reopening puts it at risk — "if we date and it fails, I lose my best friend" is a real cost-term, not an excuse. High-value settled frames are hardest to reopen *because the current frame is good.* (Grounded F1-neutrally as attachment-value-at-risk.)

**The contrast that completes the parent's grading:** a *declared no* needs **question-change** against a strong defense on a live-ish question; a *settled default* needs **question-revival** against a milder defense — two different problems, not one scale. **Audience-drag** adds a fourth force: her ledger may reopen while the audience's stays settled and keeps re-recording ("aren't you two just friends?") — which is why successful reopenings disproportionately involve leaving the shared context (stated as mechanism-fact; *deliberately* pulling someone from their audience is isolation, the abuser's tool on `50`'s list, and this finding names the difference). Two more results: **raise and re-raise are one operation** — question-raising via frame-discrepant evidence, direction-neutral ("I never thought of him that way until the wedding" is the same machinery running on a never-live question, with no defense legs attached); and **the no-seeded frame** — a premature ask's declared no becomes the *seed-label* of the ensuing settlement ("the guy I turned down"), so post-no frames settle fast and hard: the parent's worst-trade claim deepens — the premature ask seeds the attractor it will later have to fight. Every revival attempt is an ask: `60` §11 governs it entirely.

### 6 · Worked cases and boundary objects

- **The friend-zone, natively mechanized:** context-null "friend" + non-asking confirming it per cycle (the label *predicts* the absence) + her conduct-recording and the audience's early settlement feeding back + deceleration plateauing it + liveness-decay retiring the question. The late declaration then fails **twice before content**: it must re-raise a no-longer-live question, and it arrives as frame-discrepant conduct from inside the frame ("this isn't what we are") — the felt "too late / weird" of the folk description. The folk term dissolves into a settled least-cost label, a dead question, and graded recording.
- **The vendor-zone (the commercial twin):** procurement's null ("just a vendor"), account-review cadence as cycles, the incumbent supplier-frame's value as leg 3 — and the strategic-partner label reachable only through frame-discrepant evidence: the insight delivered *outside the billing relationship*, the workshop that isn't a sales call. Same machinery, the user's business life.
- **Engineered liveness, named:** rationing frame-discrepant conduct to keep the other's question alive while never asking — cheap, timed, unilateral — versus its honest twin, **real joint novelty** (jointly produced, cost-bearing). Breadcrumbing is intermittent reinforcement's frame-level face; the three-binds line already prohibits it; the discriminator is now stated.
- **Functional nulls:** settling is not a pathology — strong nulls are social infrastructure (the settled "colleague" frame is what lets mixed workplaces function). The account de-pathologizes the close: the asker's regret is about *timing*, not about the receiver's error.
- **Two instruments fall out:** the **liveness-meter** (the capacity-gauge's readiness-signals double as window-position reads: present = open; once-present-now-absent = closing; long-absent = likely settled — a read, never a probe-license) and the **scheduled frame-review** (defer-with-a-date applied to the frame itself: pre-registered look-points at natural milestones, with the **still-open return** — "not settled; next look at X" — as a first-class outcome; prevents silent closes without premature sampling). Window-position **mismatch** — his question live, hers settled, no signal until sampled — is the domain's standard tragedy, and the meter is its mitigation.
