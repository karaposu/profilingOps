## Phase 2/3 — Adversarial Evaluation and Verdicts

### C1 · **K5 — inexhaustibility is generativity**

**Prosecution — substance axis, applied literally to all six species.** Run the criterion species by species and one case breaks it. **Caregiver-Focus's object is one person's wellbeing — and a person's wellbeing is never completed.** A mother's care generates endlessly. So Caregiver-Focus has a generative object, is ranked primary, and is associated with devotion **but not with followership**. Under K5 as stated, it should produce followers. It does not.

**Defense — and the defense repairs rather than deflects.** The criterion is missing a clause, and the missing clause is visible in the failing case. Completeness is a property of **the receiver's model of the sender**. Caregiver-Focus's object *is the receiver* — so watching a caregiver attend to you generates no new content **about the sender**. The object must be generative **and external to the receiver** for the receiver's model to keep filling.

**Collision.** Prosecution wins against the stated form; defense wins the repaired form, and the repair is derived from the failing case rather than bolted on. *Purpose-fitness:* left as-is, the criterion misclassifies a primary species — not sufficient. *Can the frame absorb the fix?* Yes, by one clause.

> **Verdict — REFINE.** **Target:** *an attention-object supports followership when engaging it generates further content **and** the object is external to the receiver.* Caregiver-Focus fails the second clause, which is precisely why it produces devotion without followership.

### C2 · **K4 — leadership generates a Resonance network**

**Prosecution — external-anchor axis.** `apt_modulator_landscape/finding.md:157` is strict: *"Resonance is NOT a property of either individual alone; it's a property of **the pair**."* A network is not a pair.

**Defense — external anchor, verbatim, and decisive.** Two lines settle it. Line 56: Resonance *"emerges when both parties' values/worldviews/**attention-objects** align."* Line 174: *"**When both parties are in Dialogue-Focus on the same object → Resonance emerges.**"* Substitute Mission-Focus for Dialogue-Focus and the same-object condition is satisfied for **any two followers of one cause**. Each such pair has its own Resonance, which is a property of *that pair* — exactly as the strict definition requires. Nothing is extended; the pairs simply compose.

Note also what line 56 contains: **"attention-objects."** The corpus's own Resonance definition already uses the object concept K3 claims is unwritten — which strengthens K3 as well.

**Collision.** Defense wins decisively on D11 and D2. The strict definition is not violated; it is applied.

> **Verdict — SURVIVE.** Movement-persistence has a mechanism: remove the leader-node and the follower-follower pairs, each an ordinary dyad, remain.

### C3 · **K14 — the Hope basis was never tested against non-dyadic Hope**

**Prosecution — precedent axis (D8), the strongest in the set.** One inquiry ago, an eighth-Hope-sub-flavor candidate was killed. Is this the same move re-dressed?

**Partial defense.** It is not: that candidate proposed a *member* and died for being a cross-variable modifier and for not being attachment-generating. K14 proposes no member; it observes a class-level gap. Different claim type, so the precedent does not dispose of it.

**But a second prosecution succeeds, and it is better than the candidate.** Run 3.4.1's structural unifier literally: Hope is *"the receiver's belief that the **sender's** future action, presence, or orientation will produce a specific kind of positive outcome for the receiver."* Mission-participation fails the **sender-mediated** clause — the cause's value is not produced by the sender. The leader mediates *access*, not value.

**So mission-participation is not Hope at all.** It decomposes instead into **Resonance** (the follower's world-model aligning with the cause) plus ordinary **Hope of access** toward the leader as gatekeeper. Verified against all seven sub-flavors: every one names something the *sender* does for the *receiver* — including Reciprocity-Hope, *"the receiver's belief that the **sender** will return what the receiver has already given."*

> **Verdict — REFINE, and the refinement is a better result than the candidate.** **Target:** drop the eighth-sub-flavor framing entirely. State that mission-participation is **Resonance plus access-Hope**, and that the Hope basis is dyadic by construction — which is not a defect but a scope fact worth recording. **This also corrects P3's mechanism**, which had assigned the participation component to Hope.

### C4 · **K12 — conferred position bifurcates in rather than being bounded out**

**Prosecution.** Does an appointed manager's authority produce **Fear** specifically? It might produce Hope instead — they control rewards — or nothing at all for an employee who does not care.

**Defense.** The route is not load-bearing. Whether authority supplies threat-belief or reward-expectation, it supplies `f`-side content that is gated normally, and the resulting state carries compliance's signature: it reverses when the authority is removed. Compliance is already a member of the engagement-state layer, so the phenomenon is inside the theory.

**Collision.** Defense holds; prosecution wins a precision requirement.

> **Verdict — SURVIVE, refined in statement.** **Target:** *conferred position supplies `f`-side content (threat or reward) plus institutional gating, producing compliance. What it does not supply is a generative external object — which is why it produces no followership.* Sensemaking's bound was drawn one step too wide.

### C5 · **K7 — self-as-object can be generative**

**Prosecution — and this one is sharp.** If a self treated as a project is generative, then the demotion argument gains **new** support from the opposite direction: Mission-Focus was demoted for collapsing into Self-Focus, and now Self-Focus is shown capable of doing Mission-Focus's work. The candidate that vindicates the user may also revive the argument this inquiry set out to defeat.

**Defense.** The species boundary survives on a property the prosecution does not reach: **a cause is external to both parties; a self is not.** That difference is load-bearing because it determines whether **Resonance** can form — two followers can align on a cause, but they cannot align on the leader's selfhood the same way. So self-as-project generates depth (satisfying C1's first clause and, from the receiver's view, the second) but cannot generate the Resonance network that makes a movement.

**Collision.** Defense holds and **sharpens the boundary rather than dissolving it**: self-as-object produces devoted individuals; cause-as-object produces movements.

> **Verdict — SURVIVE.** And it must be reported: the user located the property in the person, and for a real subclass they were right.

### C6 · **K8′ — inexhaustibility is observer-relative**

**Defense.** The theory is already magnitude-based rather than categorical — every variable is a scalar and every modulator a gate on a product. A tendency-level sort is the theory's native precision, not a concession.

> **Verdict — SURVIVE, refined.** **Target:** state the sort as typical and the derivation as a tendency, matching the theory's existing precision.

## Phase 3.5 — Assembly Check

With C2 and C3 admitted, the survivors reassemble into something that **reverses the mechanism's emphasis**:

> **Resonance is doing far more work in leadership than any stage recognized.** It aligns the follower with the cause (leader-follower Resonance). It aligns followers with one another (the network — which is why movements outlive founders). And **mission-participation itself is Resonance plus access-Hope, not Hope-amplification.** The April account's omission of Resonance was therefore not a minor gap: **Resonance is plausibly the primary variable in leadership, with Hope secondary** — the reverse of what Scenario 9 and every stage of this inquiry assumed.

**Prosecution of the assembly.** Is this over-reading a variable added for a different purpose? **Defense:** Resonance was admitted on the shared-niche-interest case — two strangers attaching over an unusual shared interest with Charm, Hope, and Fear at zero. A shared cause is that case at scale. The variable was built for exactly this shape.

**And the assembly explains the anti-correlation.** The species with generative *external* objects are the ones whose objects can be **shared** — and shared objects are what Resonance requires. Object-generativity produces depth; object-externality produces shareability; **followership needs both**, which is why only Mission, Dialogue, and Transcendent produce it.

> **Verdict — SURVIVE, ranked first.** It answers the inquiry's question, corrects the mechanism it inherited, and explains a structural oddity that has stood since iteration-3.
