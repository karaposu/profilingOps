### 1 · Lens Shifting *(Framer)*

**Generic — the follower's lens.** The corpus reads from the receiver's side but describes the *sender's* species. Flip it: what does a follower experience? *"There is always more here."* → **L-a: from the follower's side, inexhaustibility is experienced as never finishing — the completeness axis held permanently at its low end.**

**Focused — the object's lens.** Stop looking at people and look at the cause. Causes have properties: scale, achievability, contestedness. → **L-b: the cause's own properties modulate followership — an achievable cause exhausts (a startup that IPOs loses its mission), an unachievable one does not.**

**Contrarian — the exhaustion lens.** What happens when an inexhaustible object *becomes* exhausted — the cause is achieved, or discredited? → **L-c: cause-exhaustion as a distinct decline mode.**

### 2 · Combination *(Generator)*

**Generic — object-inexhaustibility × the completeness axis.** The prior inquiry made completeness a cross-variable epistemic modifier but never said what *sets* it. → **C-a: the attention-object is the upstream determinant of the completeness ceiling — completeness is bounded by what there is to know, which is bounded by what the person attends to.**

**Focused — Mission-Focus × Resonance × many followers.** Resonance is dyadic-emergence around a shared world-model. If many followers share one cause, each resonates with the leader **and with every other follower**. → **C-b: leadership generates a Resonance *network*, not a dyad — and a network survives the removal of a node.** That is a mechanism for movements outliving their founders.

**Contrarian — inexhaustibility × Fear.** Is there an inexhaustible-*threat* case? A leader you never finish modelling as dangerous. → **C-c: authoritarian leadership may be the Fear-side analogue — inexhaustible threat rather than inexhaustible cause — which would explain durable compliance without durable attachment.**

### 3 · Inversion *(Framer)* — depth check and multi-axis check

**Depth iteration on "followers attach because the leader's object is inexhaustible":**
- **L1 (component):** → *"followers attach because **they** have an inexhaustible object; the leader is the access point."*
- **L2 (system):** → *"leadership is not a property of a person — it is a **relation between a follower and an object, mediated by a person**."* The leader is a conduit.
- **L3 (root):** → if the leader is a conduit, then what is attached-to is the **object**, and the person is the **interface**. → **I-a: leadership may be attachment-by-proxy — the theory's first case where the attachment *target* and the attachment *object* come apart.**

**Multi-axis — existence axis.** Could the relevant count be *many* rather than *infinite*? A leader with several live finite causes never completes either. → **I-b: object-multiplicity as a rival mechanism to object-inexhaustibility.**

**Multi-axis — identity axis.** What does "inexhaustible" consist of? Not infinite content — a cause is not infinite. It is content that **produces further content**. → **I-c: inexhaustibility is generativity, not infinitude.**
