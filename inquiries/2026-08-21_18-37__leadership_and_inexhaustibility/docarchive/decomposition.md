### I4 — the Hope contract *(circularity found and removed)*

**The problem.** P3 states that Mission-Focus amplifies Hope. P4 asks *which* Hope — and its admissibility test needs P3's own claim that followers **attach** through mission-hope. Meanwhile P4's answer would refine P3's mechanism statement. Each needs the other's output.

**The removal.** Split the flow by time:

> **P3 states the mechanism with generic Hope and hands P4 the attachment claim. P4 tests the sub-flavor candidate and delivers its refinement to P6, never back to P3.**

P3 does not wait; P4 gets what it needs; the refinement lands at assembly where it belongs. The mechanism statement is therefore correct at P3-time and *more specific* at P6-time, which is an ordinary refinement rather than a revision.
