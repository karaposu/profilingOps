## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

### Ambiguity 1 — Is inexhaustibility a property of the person or an artifact of the species? *(the warm pass's surfaced overlap)*

**Strongest counter-interpretation.** It is a personal trait. Some people are simply deeper than others — better read, more experienced, more complex — and that is a fact about them, not about what they look at. Two people can both be Mission-Focused and only one of them be inexhaustible.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds).** It is asking the wrong bearer. The counter is right that people differ in depth; it is wrong that this is what the corpus's cases turn on. Test the dissociation: a person of great personal depth attending to their own standing (Self-Focus) becomes solved anyway — the dating case says exactly this, and personal depth does not prevent it. Meanwhile a person of ordinary depth attending to an inexhaustible object keeps generating new content *from the object*, not from themselves. **The receiver's model completes when the object runs out, not when the person does.** Neither horn of the original ambiguity is right: it is not the person and not the species — **it is the object the species attends to.**

**Confidence: HIGH.** The resolution is forced by testing both horns against corpus cases, not by elegance.

**Now fixed:** inexhaustibility is a property of the attention-object. **No longer allowed:** treating it as a trait, or as a bare consequence of species membership. **Depends on this:** the follower/peer derivation, the demotion rebuttal, the mimicry unification. **Changed in the model:** the user's intuition is validated and relocated one step.

### Ambiguity 2 — Does the existing leadership account survive the intervening iterations?

**Strongest counter-interpretation.** It does not. It was written when the theory had one modulator and three variables; Coherence, Emotional Composure, Resonance, and the whole belief-frame arrived afterwards. An account from that era should be re-derived, not promoted.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds).** Nothing added after April *contradicts* it. Mission-Focus is a species of the genus that survived intact; the Hope-amplification is a stance-belief amplifying a property-belief, which is exactly the shape iteration-3.3's Refinement 3 committed and had promoted. The account is **structurally compatible** with everything since. What it is, is **incomplete** — and incomplete in a precisely datable way: it omits **Resonance**, and a shared cause is definitionally a shared world-model. Resonance did not exist when it was written.

**Confidence: HIGH** on compatibility; **HIGH** on the Resonance omission.

**Now fixed:** the account is promotable with one addition, not re-derivable from scratch. **No longer allowed:** citing it as complete. **Changed in the model:** leadership's mechanism becomes Mission-Focus × (Hope-amplification + Resonance-via-shared-cause).

### Ambiguity 3 — Should Mission-Focus be promoted back to primary?

**Strongest counter-interpretation.** No. The demotion reasoning stands: attending to your chosen mission is attending to your own chosen priority. Promotion needs the orthogonality failure answered, and object-inexhaustibility is a *downstream* difference, not an orthogonality argument.

**Why the counter partially succeeds.** It correctly identifies that this inquiry has produced a **downstream-dynamics** argument, not an orthogonality argument. But the documented trigger asks for exactly that — *"distinct downstream dynamics (e.g., follower-generating vs peer-generating attachment)"* — so the argument is of the demanded type. And the demotion's own internal inconsistency (anchor D1) weakens it: its exemplar sets separate cleanly, which a real collapse would not permit.

**Where it fully succeeds:** the trigger says *"if future **empirical work** shows."* There is none, and none is reachable at this phase.

**Confidence: HIGH** that the structural case is the demanded type; **LOW** that promotion can be executed now.

**Resolution.** The structural case for promotion is **built and recorded**; the promotion is **blocked on the trigger's own empirical requirement.** Both halves stated. **Now fixed:** the argument's type and its blocker. **No longer allowed:** promoting on structural grounds alone, or dismissing the case as the wrong kind.

### Ambiguity 4 — What is followership? *(the corpus's total gap, A4)*

**Strongest counter-interpretation.** It is a fourth engagement state. Attachment, pursuit, and compliance do not obviously contain it — followers do something none of those name: they *participate*.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds).** Test followership against the layer's own discriminator, persistence under change. It is not compliance — it does not reverse on threat removal, and Gandhi's followers were not coerced. It has attachment's persistence. And it has pursuit's forward motion — but pursuit is defined as extinguishing when its gap closes, and **here the gap structurally cannot close**, because the attention-object does not run out. So followership is **attachment plus pursuit that cannot extinguish** — built from the existing two, with the non-extinguishing explained by the completeness axis. The participation the counter points to is not a state; it is the *content* of the Hope component.

**Confidence: HIGH** that no fourth state is needed. **MEDIUM** that participation is fully handled by the Hope component — see Ambiguity 5.

**Now fixed:** followership decomposes; the layer is sufficient. **No longer allowed:** proposing a fourth engagement state for this case. **Changed in the model:** the prior inquiry's layer gains its first application to a phenomenon it was not built for, and holds.

### Ambiguity 5 — Is "hope to be part of the mission" an eighth Hope sub-flavor? *(load-bearing concept test)*

**Strongest counter-interpretation.** No — it is Exchange-Hope. Joining a mission is joint activity, which is exactly what H_e names. And the immediately prior inquiry killed a proposed eighth sub-flavor on the precedent that such proposals are cross-variable specifications rather than sub-flavors.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds).** Two tests, and it fails both.

*Against H_e:* Exchange-Hope's object is **the joint activity between the parties**. Mission-hope's object is **participation in something whose value is independent of the dyad** — the cause exists and matters whether or not I join. The value's location differs, and that is a structural difference, not a shade.

*Against the prior kill:* that kill rested on the H_pleasure precedent — a candidate was rejected as *"a cross-variable affective specification, not a sub-flavor"* because pleasure attaches to whatever it modifies. And the prior inquiry's own candidate died specifically because it was **not attachment-generating**. Mission-hope fails neither test: it has its own object rather than modifying others, and Scenario 9 states outright that followers **attach** through it. **It passes the two tests the last candidate failed.**

*Nearest rival, H_g:* Generative-Hope is *"the sender's mentorship, challenge, or example will help the receiver grow,"* with receiver-primary agency. Mission-hope's outcome is not the receiver's growth; it is the cause's advancement, with the receiver as one contributor.

**Confidence: HIGH** that it does not decompose. **MEDIUM** on whether that warrants opening a taxonomy the corpus declared closed at seven — that is a judgment the next discipline should test adversarially, given how recently a sibling candidate was killed.

**Now fixed:** the candidate is admissible for testing and is not disposed of by the prior kill. **No longer allowed:** dismissing it by precedent without running the tests. **Depends on this:** whether the leadership mechanism needs new `f`-side vocabulary or reuses existing.

### Ambiguity 6 — Does leadership introduce a third object the two-party theory cannot hold?

**Strongest counter-interpretation.** Yes, and it is fatal to staying in-frame. The receiver attaches partly to the *cause*, which is not a person. A theory of attachment-to-persons has no vocabulary for that, so leadership needs its own treatment.

**Why the counter fails — but only partly.** Resonance is defined as **shared world-model, dyadic-emergence, both parties co-determining**. A shared cause *is* a shared world-model element, so the third object enters through an existing door rather than requiring a new one. That handles the *alignment* half.

**What the counter keeps:** it does not handle attachment **to the cause itself**, independent of the leader. People who stay with a movement after its founder dies are attached to something the theory does not model. That is real and unresolved.

**Confidence: MEDIUM.** The in-frame route exists for alignment; the residual is genuine.

**Resolution.** Leadership is **triadic in structure** — receiver, sender, and cause — and the theory reaches two of the three relations. The receiver-cause relation is outside it. Recorded as a bound, not resolved.

### Ambiguity 7 — Position versus followership *(reached independently by two perspectives)*

**Strongest counter-interpretation.** The distinction does not matter, because appointed leaders also generate attachment and the mechanism is the same either way.

**Why the counter fails (structural grounds).** Appointment confers **compliance-eligibility** — the ability to impose consequences — which the engagement-state layer types as compliance, reversing on threat removal. Followership persists without consequences. They are different states by the layer's own discriminator, and a manager can have the first with none of the second. That is the ordinary case in most organisations, not an edge case.

**Confidence: HIGH** that the distinction is real. **This is left explicitly OPEN** as to what the theory says about conferred position, because the answer is plausibly *nothing*, and establishing that requires work this inquiry did not do.

### SV6 — Stabilized Model

**The user's intuition is correct, and it belongs one step away from where they put it.** Inexhaustibility is not a property of the person. It is a property of **what the person's attention is on** — and the species taxonomy already sorts by it without ever saying so.

Read the six species by their objects. Self-Focus attends to one's own priorities; Caregiver-Focus to a particular person's wellbeing. Both objects can be exhausted. Dialogue-Focus attends to a shared inquiry, Mission-Focus to a cause, Transcendent-Focus to an ideal. **None of those run out.** And the three species with inexhaustible objects are exactly the three the corpus attaches to devotion — beloved teachers, charismatic leaders, moral exemplars.

That derives the distinction the corpus stated once in a parenthetical and never explained. **An exhaustible attention-object yields a completed model and a steady state — a peer. An inexhaustible one keeps producing content the observer has not yet absorbed, so the model never completes and the relation stays open — a follower.** Combined with the completeness axis from the prior inquiry, the mechanism is exact: pursuit extinguishes when its gap closes, and here the gap cannot close.

It also answers the argument that demoted Mission-Focus. The demotion said attending to a chosen mission is just attending to one's own chosen priority. **A personal priority can be completed; a cause cannot** — and the demotion's own exemplar sets separate too cleanly for a real collapse. The structural case for promotion is exactly the type the documented trigger demands. **But the trigger says *"if future empirical work shows,"* and there is none** — so the case is built and the promotion is blocked by its own terms.

**Leadership's mechanism, stated:** Mission-Focus as the attention-species, amplifying Hope, **plus Resonance through the shared cause** — the last of which the April account omits because Resonance did not exist when it was written. The account survives the intervening iterations; it is incomplete in a datable way, not wrong.

**Followership needs no new machinery.** It is attachment plus pursuit that cannot extinguish. It is not compliance, because it does not reverse when consequences are removed.

**And the pathological cases stop being a separate category.** A narcissist runs Mission-Focus as mimicry: the displayed object is a cause, the actual object is self-aggrandisement — which is exhaustible. The corpus's own costly-signal arc then predicts the observed burnout. One mechanism covers Gandhi and the cult leader, and the difference is whether the object is real.

**Two bounds, stated rather than smoothed.** The theory reaches **earned followership, not conferred position** — an appointed manager has compliance-eligibility with no followership, and that is the ordinary case in most organisations. And leadership is **triadic**: receiver, sender, cause. The theory holds the receiver-sender and the alignment relations; **attachment to the cause itself, independent of the leader, is outside it** — which is why movements survive their founders and the theory cannot yet say why.

**One live candidate, admissible but untested:** *"followers hope to be part of the mission"* may not decompose into the seven Hope sub-flavors. Its object — participation in something valuable independently of the dyad — is not exchange, attention, validation, continuity, growth, reciprocity, or protection. It passes the two tests on which the last proposed eighth sub-flavor died. Whether that warrants reopening a taxonomy declared closed is for the next discipline to attack.
