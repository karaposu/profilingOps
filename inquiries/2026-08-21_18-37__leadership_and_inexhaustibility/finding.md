## Question

Attachment & Presentation Theory (referred to below as "the theory") models how one person becomes attached to another. Four **variables** generate attachment additively — charm, hope, fear, resonance — and three **modulators** gate the result multiplicatively. One of those modulators, **Self-Positioning**, tracks *non-extractive attention*: attention not oriented toward harvesting approval from the person in front of you. It has six **species**, distinguished by where the attention goes.

The user's input announced a topic and a hunch rather than a question: leadership, and a sense that *being inexhaustible* has something to do with it. **No question was actually stated.** The question this finding answers was therefore formulated by the analysis, and that is worth knowing when reading it. The formulation was: *does the theory reach leadership, and is inexhaustibility load-bearing for it?*

Six readings were held open throughout — including the possibility that leadership needed a new domain, that the term needed defining first, and that the hunch was an appealing association rather than a structural link.

## Finding Summary

- **The hunch is correct, and it belongs one step away from where it was placed.** Inexhaustibility is not a property of the *person*. It is a property of **what the person's attention is on**.

- **The species taxonomy already sorts by it, and nobody had noticed.** Every species is defined as *"attention on [some object]"* — and those objects differ in whether they keep producing content.

- **The criterion has two clauses, and the second was found by breaking the first.** An attention-object supports followership when engaging it **generates further content** *and* **the object lies outside the receiver**. Caregiver-Focus has a generative object and produces no followers — because its object *is* the receiver.

- **This derives a distinction iteration-3 stated once and never explained.** An exhaustible object yields a completed model and a steady state — a **peer**. A generative external one keeps producing content, so the model never completes and the relation stays open — a **follower**.

- **The theory already reaches leadership.** It has a dedicated worked scenario, a coupling rule, and two context-table rows. They live in an April-dated spec file, never carried into the findings chain.

- **But that account has the mechanism backwards.** It credits Hope. Running the theory's own definition of Hope literally, *hope to be part of a mission* fails it — Hope requires the outcome be **sender-mediated**, and a cause's value is not produced by the sender. The leader mediates *access*, not value.

- **Resonance is the primary variable in leadership.** It aligns the follower with the cause, it aligns followers **with each other**, and it carries the participation component that was miscredited to Hope.

- **Which explains why movements outlive their founders.** Follower-follower Resonance is a set of ordinary dyads sharing one term. Remove the leader and the dyads remain.

- **Followership needs no new machinery.** It is attachment plus pursuit that cannot extinguish — the gap does not close because the object does not run out.

- **The pathological cases stop being separate.** A cult leader runs Mission-Focus as *mimicry*: the displayed object is a cause, the actual object is self-aggrandisement — which **is** exhaustible. The theory's existing costly-signal arc then predicts their collapse and not Gandhi's.

- **The person-located reading survives for a real subclass.** When the self is treated as a *project* rather than a *position*, it is a generative object. The theory's own respected-grandparent case is exactly this.

- **The taxonomy's existing ranking is orthogonal to all of it.** Both species ranked *primary* have non-generative objects; all four *secondary* ones are generative. The ranking measures how cleanly a species separates from its siblings, not what it produces.

- **One prediction is open and falsifiable:** the craft is a generative external object, so the account predicts that Task-Focus produces followership. The corpus has no such case.

## Finding

### Why this question arose from a phrase

A previous inquiry, resolving a different problem, described the deep teacher as *perfectly consistent yet inexhaustible* — a person whose behaviour is entirely reliable and who nonetheless never runs out of depth. The phrase was doing local work in an argument about a different topic. The user recognised something in it and connected it to leadership.

That connection turned out to be real. But the word had no standing: *"inexhaustible"* appears exactly once in the entire corpus, in that one sentence, as prose inside an example. It had never been defined, never been given signals, never been placed. The first task was therefore to find out what it names.

### 1. The property belongs to the object, not the person

Two readings presented themselves and both are wrong.

**Not a personal trait.** People do differ in depth — some are better read, more experienced, more complex. But that is not what the theory's cases turn on. A person of great personal depth whose attention is on their own standing becomes *solved* anyway; a previous inquiry established exactly this about the dating case, and personal depth does not prevent it.

**Not a bare consequence of species membership either.** Being in a particular species is not itself the mechanism.

**It is a property of the object the attention is on.** The receiver's model of someone completes when the *object* runs out, not when the *person* does. A teacher attending to a shared inquiry keeps producing new content because inquiry generates further inquiry — not because the teacher is unusually deep.

This is a genuine relocation, and it diverges from the user's own phrasing, which located the property in the person. The practical difference matters: **you do not become inexhaustible by being deep. You become inexhaustible by attaching your attention to something that does not run out.** Section 6 gives back the part of the original phrasing that survives.

### 2. The taxonomy already sorts by it

Iteration-3 defines all six species the same way — *attention on* something:

| Species | Attention is on | Object generative? | Outside the receiver? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Self-Focus** | one's own priorities | no | yes |
| **Caregiver-Focus** | one person's wellbeing | yes | **no** |
| **Mission-Focus** | a larger cause | yes | yes |
| **Task-Focus** | the craft itself | yes | yes |
| **Dialogue-Focus** | a shared inquiry | yes | yes |
| **Transcendent-Focus** | an abstract ideal | yes | yes |

The object is already the individuating slot. It has simply never been tabulated as a column. Even the theory's definition of Resonance uses the phrase — *"emerges when both parties' values/worldviews/**attention-objects** align."*

### 3. The criterion, and the case that repaired it

The first version of the criterion was one clause: an object supports followership when it is **generative** — when engaging it produces further content, rather than being merely large. A cause is not infinite; it *produces*.

Applied species by species, that version breaks on **Caregiver-Focus**. A person's wellbeing is never completed; a mother's care generates endlessly. So Caregiver-Focus has a generative object and is strongly associated with devotion — and produces **no followership**. It should, under the one-clause criterion.

The repair is visible in the failing case. Model-completeness is a property of **the receiver's model of the sender**. Caregiver-Focus's object *is the receiver* — so watching someone attend to you, however endlessly, generates no new content **about them**. The object must be outside the receiver for the receiver's model to keep filling.

> **An attention-object supports followership when engaging it generates further content, and the object lies outside the receiver.**

Caregiver-Focus fails the second clause. That is precisely why it produces devotion without followers.

### 4. What this derives

Iteration-3 says of Mission-Focus that its reliability signal has *"a distinct texture (**follower-generating rather than peer-generating**)."* One parenthetical, never explained, in the whole corpus.

It follows from the criterion. A previous inquiry established that **pursuit extinguishes when its gap closes** — when the observer has finished building their model. So:

- **Exhaustible object** → the model completes → pursuit extinguishes → what remains is attachment at a steady state. That is a **peer**.
- **Generative external object** → the model never completes → pursuit cannot extinguish → the relation stays open. That is a **follower**.

Two qualifications, both honest. The sort is **typical, not absolute** — a topic exhausted for an expert is not exhausted for a novice, so generativity is a relation between object and observer, usually dominated by the object. And the theory is magnitude-based throughout, so a tendency-level sort is its native precision rather than a concession.

### 5. Leadership's mechanism — and the correction

**The theory already reaches leadership.** `apt_layer_proof_scenarios.md` carries a dedicated scenario — *Charismatic Leadership, Mission-Focus Variant* — covering Gandhi, Mandela, King, and Jobs. `new_apt_layer.md` states a coupling rule. Two rows of a context table name leadership directly. All of it dated April, none of it carried into the findings chain.

**But the mechanism is credited to the wrong variable.** Scenario 9 says *"the cause itself becomes part of the attraction (Hope variable amplified particularly — followers hope to be part of the mission)."*

Run the theory's own definition of Hope literally. Hope is *"the receiver's belief that the **sender's** future action, presence, or orientation will produce a specific kind of positive outcome for the receiver."* Mission-participation fails the **sender-mediated** clause: the cause's value is not produced by the leader. The leader mediates *access to* the cause, not the cause's worth. Checked against all seven Hope sub-flavours, every one names something the sender does for the receiver — including reciprocity-hope, *"the receiver's belief that the **sender** will return what the receiver has already given."*

So mission-participation is **Resonance** — the follower's world-model aligning with the cause — plus ordinary **access-Hope** toward the leader as gatekeeper.

**Which reverses the ordering.** Resonance turns out to be doing three jobs in leadership: aligning the follower with the cause, aligning followers with **one another**, and carrying the participation component that Hope was credited with.

> **Leadership ≈ Mission-Focus (the species) × [Resonance-primary + access-Hope secondary]**

The April account is not wrong about the species and not wrong about the observation. It is wrong about which variable carries the weight — understandably, since Resonance did not exist when it was written.

**And this explains why movements outlive their founders.** Resonance is strictly a property of a pair. But the theory states that it *"emerges when both parties' values/worldviews/attention-objects align"* and that *"when both parties are in Dialogue-Focus on the same object → Resonance emerges."* Substitute Mission-Focus: **any two followers of one cause satisfy the same-object condition.** Each such pair holds ordinary dyadic Resonance. A movement is therefore a network of ordinary dyads sharing one term — and removing the leader-node leaves the follower-follower pairs intact. Nothing is extended; the pairs simply compose.

### 6. What following is — and where the original phrasing survives

The corpus described what leaders *do* and never what following *is*. It needs no new machinery.

A previous inquiry distinguished three engagement states by persistence under change: **attachment** persists under satisfaction, **pursuit** extinguishes when its gap closes, **compliance** reverses when the threat lifts. Followership is not compliance — Gandhi's followers were not coerced, and nothing reverses when consequences are removed. It is **attachment plus pursuit that cannot extinguish**, and the reason it cannot is section 3's criterion.

**And the user's original phrasing gets a real subclass back.** They located inexhaustibility in the person. When someone's attention-object is their own **becoming** — a project rather than a position — that object is generative and, from the receiver's side, external. The theory's own case is Scenario 15's *respected grandparent*, whose attention is on *"their own accumulated life / wisdom"* and who generates attachment.

The species boundary survives on **externality**: a cause is outside both parties and can therefore be *shared*; a self cannot. So self-as-object produces devoted **individuals**, and cause-as-object produces **movements**. The person-located reading is right for the first.

### 7. Why the pathological cases stop being separate

Every leadership passage the corpus contained before this inquiry was pathological — dictators, demagogues, cults, narcissist-followers, boss-worship. One mechanism now covers them and the admired figures together.

A cult leader runs Mission-Focus as **mimicry**: the *displayed* object is a cause; the *actual* object is self-aggrandisement — which is exhaustible. The theory's existing costly-signal arc then does the predictive work: mimicry is detected over time as the cost is seen not to be paid, and attachment decays. That arc predicts the cult leader's collapse and does **not** predict Gandhi's. The difference is whether the object is real.

### 8. Two things the account bounds, and one it does not

**Conferred position is included, not excluded** — and this corrects the analysis's own earlier reading. An appointed manager's authority supplies `f`-side content, whether threat or reward, plus institutional gating. The resulting state carries compliance's signature: it reverses when the authority is removed. Compliance is already a member of the engagement-state layer, so the phenomenon is inside the theory. What conferred position does not supply is a generative external object — **which is why it produces no followership.** That is a narrower and truer statement than "out of scope," and it covers the ordinary organisational case rather than excluding it.

**The taxonomy's ranking is orthogonal to all of this.** Both species iteration-3 ranks *primary* have non-generative objects; all four *secondary* ones are generative. That is not a contradiction — the ranking measures orthogonality, how cleanly a species separates from its siblings, not what it produces. But it explains a long-standing oddity: the corpus's most devotion-associated species have all sat in the secondary tier since iteration-3. **The correct move is to add an object axis alongside the ranking, not to re-rank.**

**What the account genuinely does not reach:** attachment to a cause with no person involved at all. The theory's variables are beliefs about a sender, and a belief about a cause has no sender. The Resonance network absorbs most of what looked unreachable, so the residual is smaller than it first appeared — but it is real.

### 9. On Mission-Focus's rank

Iteration-3 demoted Mission-Focus because *"a person deeply attending to their chosen mission IS attending to their own chosen priority, so Mission-Focus often collapses into Self-Focus."*

That reasoning fails on a property it did not consider: **a personal priority can be completed; a cause cannot.** And it is internally inconsistent — its own exemplar sets (Gandhi and Mother Teresa on one side, status-attenders on the other) separate far too cleanly for a real collapse.

Iteration-3 also records a promotion trigger: *"If future empirical work shows Mission-Focus produces distinct downstream dynamics (e.g., **follower-generating vs peer-generating attachment**), it could be promoted back to primary."* This finding produces exactly that — the demanded kind of argument, for the named distinction.

**But the trigger says *"if future empirical work shows,"* and there is none.** The structural case is built and recorded; **the promotion is blocked by the trigger's own terms.** Adding the object axis is the move that does not require it.

## Reasoning

### What survived and why

**The object-location claim survived** because both alternatives were tested against corpus cases rather than dismissed. Personal depth does not prevent a self-focused person from being solved; species membership is not itself a mechanism. What remained was the object — and the theory's own species definitions already name one apiece.

**The Resonance reversal survived** its strongest objection, which was that Resonance is strictly *"a property of the pair"* and a network is not a pair. It survives because the network here is a set of pairs sharing one term, each an ordinary dyad. The theory's own sentence — *"when both parties are in Dialogue-Focus on the same object → Resonance emerges"* — substitutes directly.

**The self-as-object subclass survived a sharp objection**: if a self treated as a project is generative, then the demotion argument gains new support from the opposite direction, since Self-Focus would be doing Mission-Focus's work. The boundary held on **externality** — a cause is outside both parties and can be shared; a self cannot — which is why one produces devoted individuals and the other produces movements.

### What was killed, dissolved, or corrected

**The eighth-Hope-sub-flavour proposal — dissolved rather than killed.** The analysis came close to proposing that *hope to be part of the mission* is an eighth sub-flavour of Hope, on the grounds that it does not decompose into the seven. It does not — but the reason is that it is not Hope. Hope's own unifier requires the outcome be sender-mediated, and a cause's value is not sender-produced. The taxonomy needs no new member; the component needed re-categorising.

This matters as precedent: a previous inquiry killed a different eighth-sub-flavour candidate. Two consecutive inquiries have now had a candidate fail at Hope's boundary, in two different ways.

**The one-clause criterion — corrected by its own failing case.** Caregiver-Focus broke it, and the break produced the fix rather than a patch.

**Object-multiplicity — killed.** Proposed as a rival: a leader with several finite causes never completes either. It is strictly weaker — multiplicity delays completion where generativity prevents it. Its residue is a note that multiplicity may modulate rate.

**Cause-exhaustion as a failure signature — killed as a signature.** No modulator collapses in the case, so it is an `f`-side trajectory rather than a signature in the modulator sense. It survives as the movements-dissolve-by-succeeding prediction.

**The conferred-position bound — corrected mid-analysis.** It was first stated as out of scope and then found to be inside, because compliance is already in the engagement-state layer. The narrower statement is both truer and more useful.

### The correction that ran against the analysis's own direction

The analysis relocated inexhaustibility from the person to the object, which is the opposite of how the user framed it. It would have been tidier to leave that relocation clean. It is not clean: **self-as-object is a real case**, corroborated by the theory's own respected-grandparent scenario. The finding reports it because leaving it out would have made the answer neater and less true.

## Open Questions

### Monitoring

- Whether the object column's generativity verdicts hold as new species or cases are added. The cell most likely to move is Task-Focus, whose object is generative and external but whose devotion case is unestablished. Observable once the Task-Focus prediction is tested.

### Blocked

- Whether Task-Focus produces followership. Blocked on the testing action under MUST. Four of this finding's core claims inherit the result, since the criterion predicts it and a failure would require narrowing.

- Whether Mission-Focus should be promoted to primary. Blocked on empirical work by the promotion trigger's own wording. The structural argument is complete and recorded.

### Research Frontiers

- **Whether Resonance-primary holds for parasocial and historical leaders.** Resonance is defined as *mutual* recognition, and a follower of a long-dead leader recognises no one who recognises them back. Either the account needs a variant for one-directional cases or Resonance is doing less work there than in live movements.

- **Whether followers who never meet hold Resonance with each other.** The network account requires follower-follower pairs, and most members of a large movement never interact. If Resonance needs actual contact, the network is smaller than the movement and the founder-survival explanation weakens.

- **What attachment to a cause consists of when no person is involved.** The theory's variables are beliefs about a sender; a cause has none. The Resonance network absorbs most of what looked unreachable, but not this.

### Refinement Triggers

- **The two-clause criterion re-opens** if a species is found with a generative external object that produces no followership, or a non-generative object that does. Task-Focus is the live test.

- **The Resonance-primary claim re-opens** if Hope's structural unifier is ever revised to drop or weaken the sender-mediated clause. That clause is the whole basis for re-categorising mission-participation; re-open on its revision, not on general reconsideration.

- **The Resonance-network account re-opens** if Resonance is ever specified to require direct interaction rather than aligned attention-objects. The current wording — *"emerges when both parties' values/worldviews/attention-objects align"* — is what licenses the network.

- **The self-as-object subclass re-opens** if self-as-project turns out to differ from self-as-position only by degree rather than in kind. That would revive the Mission-into-Self collapse argument from the opposite direction.

- **The object axis's placement re-opens** if the primary/secondary ranking is ever redefined to measure something other than orthogonality strength, since the anti-correlation finding depends on the two axes measuring different things.
