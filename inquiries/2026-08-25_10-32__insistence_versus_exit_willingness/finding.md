## Question

From the inquiry's framing: the outreach guidance had said *"Exit-Willingness — the selective consultant wins, the chaser gets commoditized. Be visibly able to leave."* The user pushed back at theory-fundamentals level: **sometimes insisting produces results. Sometimes it is irritating. Sometimes insisting resolves — maybe under confidence and charm? — and not just because people give in to get rid of the insister (explicitly not that case). Dive deep.**

The goal: a fundamentals account — the mechanism(s) behind genuine insistence-success, a discrimination of insistence-kinds and their result-types, and a verdict on whether the Exit-Willingness commitment survives as stated, needs conditioning, or splits.

### The words this finding leans on

*(BDA — Belief-Driven Attachment, this project's theory — holds that attachment is driven by what a receiver comes to believe about a person: four additive belief-ingredients — Charm "they're impressive," Hope "this promises me something," Fear, Resonance "they get my world" — multiplied by gates read off conduct, chiefly whether attention reads as genuinely on something or as fishing for approval. Approval-fishing conduct — hedging, over-explaining, permission-seeking — is called **Supplication** and collapses the gate.)*

- **Exit-Willingness** — one of the five conduct-signals observers read: risk taken *within a move* — disagreeing, being selective, closing so the other person can freely not continue ("terminable close").
- **The chaser** — the theory's negative sales exemplar (Scenario 7 of the legacy validation file): the salesperson who *"follows up aggressively, reduces price to close, elaborates value incessantly"* and gets commoditized.
- **The Coerced signature** — the committed description of pressure-won engagement: it *"reverses (relief, not attachment) on threat removal"* — the yes evaporates, with relief, the moment the pressure lifts.
- **Cumulative belief-state** — the theory's commitment that beliefs accumulate across all contact: each new interaction is another update opportunity, and repeated *identical* evidence updates far less than its count suggests.
- **θ (theta), the registration threshold** — per-channel: on cold channels most messages never register at all; the threshold falls as contact history accumulates.
- **Receiver availability** — the pre-condition outside the formula: an owner mid-lunch-rush is not processing you; a "no" given in that state was never a belief-verdict.
- **Backed vs performed** — a display is backed when the state it shows is really there; performed displays get caught, immediately or slowly.

## Finding Summary

- **The commitment and the observation never touch.** Exit-Willingness's committed content is **risk-taken-within-a-move** — the manner of each individual approach. Insistence is a property of the **sequence** of approaches. The corpus never legislated attempt-counts; the folk reading "willing-to-walk means never come back" was never in the text, and it dies here.
- **The corpus's own positive exemplars were always persistent.** The selective consultant *selects and continues* — selectivity presupposes follow-through; the willing-to-walk negotiator re-enters negotiations; the admired professor runs a decades-long research program. **Persistence toward the object was never the failing variable; extraction toward the approver was.** The chaser fails on a three-strand bundle — *aggressive* follow-up, price-dropping, incessant elaboration — never on bare persistence.
- **The verdict is a composite, and must only ever be quoted as one:** each return priced by its **manner** (composed, own-premise, terminable, specific) **×** the sequence bounded by a **content-budget** (return only with something new or changed) **×** a natural **horizon** (when you run out of new things to bring, the sequence's expected value goes negative — the account contains its own stopping rule). Quoting the manner-half alone would license the perfectly-mannered pest; the composite does not.
- **Genuine insistence-success has five mechanisms**, all corpus-native, typed by what they produce:
  1. **Availability-resampling** — the first no was an availability-no (mid-rush), never a belief-verdict; returning re-samples.
  2. **Threshold-crossing** — early attempts never *registered* (below the channel's θ); repeat contact lowers the threshold; a later attempt is simply the first one heard.
  3. **Evidence-delivery** — beliefs accumulate; a return carrying *new, specific* content is a fresh update. An unchanged repeat updates almost nothing.
  4. **Consistency-accrual** — returning as promised, the same story each time, is reliability evidence only a sequence can earn. (Supporting, never sufficient: a reliably-returning pest is a reliably-avoidable pest.)
  5. **Conviction-display** — sustained, specific, composed, terminable pursuit reads as *backed belief in the fit* — a costly signal ("they keep choosing us specifically, and they can clearly walk").
- **The user's "confidence and charms" hypothesis is the visible face of mechanisms 4 and 5** — right about the display-borne branch, incomplete as the whole: the first two mechanisms involve *no display at all* (nothing about the insister impressed anyone; the sequence just delivered contact).
- **The discriminating test operationalizes the user's own exclusion: does the yes survive the insister easing off?** Capitulation carries the Coerced signature — prompt, relief-shaped reversal when pressure lapses. Genuine success persists through the lull; its cleanest positive signature is **unprompted re-engagement** — the receiver contacts *you* after you eased. (Distinguish reversal from ordinary slow decay: reversal is prompt and relief-shaped; decay is gradual, without relief.)
- **Irritation is the failure branch's mechanics, not a mood:** an unchanged, need-shaped repeat carries no new content, so its only information is its conduct — and extraction-shaped conduct is Supplication-evidence. The gates multiply, so devaluation *compounds* with each such return. That compounding is what "irritating" is.
- **Insistence splits three ways** (on the pattern of the corpus's anger table): **deployed** (every coordinate of the conjunction: own-premise + composed + specific + new-content per move; retries only availability- and never-registered-no's; *respects a reasoned no*) · **leaked** (can't-stop pressing, unchanged repeats, need-shaped manner) · **performed** (pressure-theater: scripted closes, fake deadlines, manufactured "changed conditions" — exposed as alignment-failure when caught). A **consent-gate** sits before the table: an *invited* return ("come back in September") is not insistence at all.
- **The strongest single move the inquiry produced: ask the no its type.** *"Should I check back when the season ends — or is this a no?"* — the no offered as the easy option (which is itself Exit-Willingness displayed). The answer converts an uninvited sequence into a licensed one, dissolving the chase entirely.
- **Application (the Return Doctrine, for the walk-ins):** consent-gate first · ask-the-no · return only with new content or changed conditions, spaced · each return worth the interruption it spends · let the standing artifact insist for you (the live demo link is a zero-cost permanent return) · let third parties carry re-raises · one clean deployed exit with the door stated open · watch for unprompted re-engagement.

## Finding

### 1 · Why this question, and why it matters now

The masacan restaurant-outreach guidance told the user to be visibly able to leave. The user — about to do real walk-ins — pushed on the theory underneath: everyone has watched insistence *work*. If the theory's answer were "never insist," it would contradict observable B2B reality (follow-ups close deals) and forfeit trust; if it were "insist away," it would license the pestering the Exit-Willingness signal exists to name. The inquiry had to find which of those the theory actually says — and it turned out to say neither, because the question contained a hidden conflation.

### 2 · The verdict: two axes were hiding in one word

Read the commitment's own text. The catalog row defines Exit-Willingness as *"risk taken in the move — high: disagreement, selectivity, terminable-close; low: safety-optimized, hedged, open-ended."* The unit is **the move**. Nothing anywhere in the corpus legislates how many moves a sequence may contain.

Then read the negative exemplar to its end. The chaser of Scenario 7 *"follows up **aggressively**, reduces price to close, elaborates value incessantly"* — three strands, plus the begging premise they imply. The modifier was "aggressively" all along. And the same scenario's *positive* exemplar — the selective consultant — *selects*, which presupposes continuing with the selected: consultants follow up. The willing-to-walk negotiator of the BATNA case re-enters negotiations. The admired professor runs a lifelong research program. **Every positive exemplar in the corpus is persistent toward its object and non-extractive toward its approvers.** Persistence was never the variable that failed anyone; extraction was.

So the verdict is **absorb**: the commitment stands exactly as written; the observation is real; they live on different axes — within-move manner (owned by the catalog) and across-move persistence (previously owned by nobody).

One honest residue keeps the verdict from being a lawyer's trick, and it must always travel with it. Visible returning *does* leak information: it displays the insister's own investment — the origin spec's line that *"who's chasing whom"* is an output the receiver reads. So the composite form is binding: **manner per move × a content-budget across moves × the horizon** — and because the leak grows with the sequence, each successive return must carry *more* exit-evidence, not less. A tenth visit needs a more terminable close than a first one.

### 3 · The five mechanisms of genuine success

Why does insistence sometimes genuinely work — receiver's mind actually changed, or a real yes reached — with the give-in-to-be-rid case set aside? Five corpus-native mechanisms, in two families:

**The no-display family** (nothing about the insister impressed anyone):

1. **Availability-resampling.** The theory places receiver availability outside the formula as a pre-condition: an owner mid-service is not processing you. A "no" given in that state was an *availability-no* — no belief was consulted. Returning at a better hour re-samples the pre-condition. The insistence "worked" because the first no was never a verdict.
2. **Threshold-crossing.** On cold channels, most approaches never register at all — they fall below the channel's registration threshold. Repeat contact moves the pair down the channel ladder (cold stranger → prior contact) and the threshold falls. The fourth email that "finally worked" may simply be the first one that was ever *heard*.

**The display-borne family** (the sequence itself carries information):

3. **Evidence-delivery.** Beliefs are cumulative; every return is a fresh approach-act contributing content — *scaled by that return's specificity*. A return that brings something new and particular ("I built the demo with your actual menu") is a real update. An unchanged repeat is correlated evidence — it updates almost nothing. This is the mechanical heart of the budget rule.
4. **Consistency-accrual.** Returning when you said you would, with the same story, across weeks — that is follow-through and temporal consistency, the reliability evidence the theory's Coherence gate reads longitudinally. Only a sequence can earn it. It is **supporting, never sufficient**: consistency makes you modelable, and a modelable pest is a reliably-avoidable pest — the credit amplifies only when the manner is clean.
5. **Conviction-display.** Sustained, specific, composed, terminable pursuit of *this particular* counterparty reads as backed belief in the fit — a costly signal (the insister keeps paying real cost, keeps choosing you specifically, and visibly could walk). This is where the receiver's read becomes "they really believe in this," which is Charm-type content about the insister. (An honestly-flagged share of this credit may ride an adjacent mechanism the theory scopes outside itself — pushing past ordinary rejection-points reads as status — the exact share is an open question.)

**The user's hypothesis, placed.** "Insisting resolves under confidence and charms, maybe?" — *confidence* is the visible face of clean manner (composure under the brush-off; each move from one's own premise), and *charm* is the conviction-credit of mechanism 5. Right about the display-borne family; incomplete as the whole — the no-display family succeeds with no confidence or charm involved at all.

### 4 · The discriminating test — the user's exclusion, made into an instrument

The user excluded "they gave in to get rid of the insister." The corpus gives that excluded case an exact signature: pressure-won engagement *"reverses (relief, not attachment) on removal"* of the pressure. Invert it into a test:

> **Does the yes survive the insister easing off?**

- **Capitulation:** prompt, relief-shaped reversal the moment pressure lapses — cancellation, avoidance, visible relief.
- **Genuine success:** the engagement persists through the lull; its cleanest positive signature is **unprompted re-engagement** — the receiver comes back to *you* after you stopped pushing. (Every real pipeline administers this test for free: nobody can visit weekly forever.)
- **Caution:** distinguish reversal from ordinary decay. Attachment lawfully weakens as access recedes; that fading is gradual and relief-free. The test reads the *immediate post-easing* behavior, not eternity.

The results of insistence therefore type as: **registration-success** (mechanisms 1–2 — the belief was available all along; the sequence delivered contact), **belief-change** (mechanisms 3–5), **transaction** (a real but shallow yes that dissolves when the exchange completes — legitimate), or **capitulation** (the excluded zone — now a testable signature rather than a vibe).

### 5 · The three-case table, the no-typology, and the consent-gate

On the pattern of the corpus's anger analysis (which split one phenomenon into deployed / leaked / performed and found each discriminated by a different component), insistence splits the same three ways — with each coordinate re-derived for insistence rather than borrowed:

| | **Deployed insistence** | **Leaked insistence** | **Performed insistence** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-move manner | own-premise, composed, terminable, **specific** | need-shaped: pressing, hedging-then-pushing | polished pressure-theater |
| Content per return | something **new** or a changed condition, every time | unchanged repeats | manufactured "news," fake deadlines |
| Termination | **respects a reasoned no**; retries only availability- and never-registered-no's; exits cleanly at the horizon | can't stop; no-tolerance absent | stops when the tactic stops paying |
| Predicted result | registration-success or belief-change (survives easing) | compounding devaluation — the irritation branch | recruits short-term; alignment-failure when caught ("he's putting it on"); decays |

**The deployed column is a conjunction.** Manner alone does not qualify (the *composed pest* — perfect manner, nothing new, weekly — fails on content and, as his identical no's accumulate into a reasoned no, on termination). Content alone does not qualify (the *needy innovator* — genuinely new material, delivered desperately — fails on manner: the content accumulates while the gate collapses, the "great product, unattachable person" law replayed at sequence scale).

**The no-typology the table consumes:** a **reasoned no** (a belief-verdict after real consideration — respected; further pressing is the excluded zone), a **not-now no** (availability — retry at a better moment), a **never-registered no** (sub-threshold — the approach was never heard; a later attempt is a first attempt). The deployed insister invests in telling these apart — which yields the inquiry's strongest single move:

> **Ask the no its type.** *"Should I check back when the season ends — or is this a no?"* — with the no offered as the easy option. Offering the exit is itself Exit-Willingness displayed; and the answer **converts the sequence**: an invited return is not insistence at all.

Hence the **consent-gate before the whole table**: licensed returns ("come back in September") exit the phenomenon entirely — zero asymmetry-cost, zero tension. Two cautions ride the move: phrase it so the no really is easy (a pressured version crystallizes soft no's into hard ones), and treat polite false licenses ("sure, come back" as a brush-off) as availability-no's the budget rule will catch next round.

### 6 · Why irritation is mechanical, not mood

"Sometimes it is irritating" has a full account in committed texts. An unchanged repeat carries no new belief-content (correlated evidence), so the only information in the move is its **conduct** — and need-shaped conduct (pressing for the response) is evidence of extraction, the exact pattern the standing-gate punishes. The gates *multiply*: each such return is another datum deepening the collapse, so the devaluation **compounds** — and the pressure itself transfers load to the receiver (the "exhausting to be around" report). Irritation is what gate-collapse-in-progress feels like from the receiving side. The failure branch is as lawful as the success branches — which is what gives the discrimination its teeth.

### 7 · The Return Doctrine (the application layer, for the walk-ins)

Assembled entirely from the surviving pieces — each step delivers value, seeks consent, or exits; no step extracts:

1. **Consent-gate:** an invited return isn't insistence — get invitations when you can.
2. **Ask the no its type** at the moment of no, with the no made easy.
3. **The return-budget:** return only with something new or a changed condition ("I'm back because something is different" is the cleanest non-needy premise); spaced, composed; the budget's exhaustion is the stopping rule.
4. **Pay the toll:** each return must itself be worth the interruption — carry value in the visit, since every return spends receiver-availability that was never volunteered.
5. **The standing artifact:** the live demo link (their-own-menu when possible) insists continuously at zero display-cost — the object returns so the person doesn't have to.
6. **Delegated persistence:** let real third parties and real usage carry re-raises (the warm-intro channel, used openly).
7. **The deployed exit:** when the budget is spent, one clean close with the door stated open — and then genuinely do not monitor the silence. (Sales practice knows this move re-engages; its full discrimination from leaving-to-be-chased inherits the corpus's open backing-observables question.)
8. **The observable:** unprompted re-engagement after natural easing is the belief-change signature; a yes that needed constant pressure was the excluded kind all along.

Silence, note, is not free either: not-returning lets pre-loaded beliefs decay ("weakens as access recedes"). The doctrine prices both directions — and answers the decay-side preferentially with the zero-display channels (steps 5–6), never with more personal pressure.

### 8 · What this lands where (homing and boundaries)

Per the theory's element-ontology, insistence is a **phenomenon**, not a new element: it homes as a phenomena-index entry (`BDA/41`) plus a scope-note on the Exit-Willingness row (`BDA/21`) — "this signal prices the move, not the sequence; sequence-effects live in the cumulative belief-state and the Coherence gate's longitudinal signals." A six-signal catalog (splitting Exit-Willingness into within-move and across-move signals) was considered and rejected: return-conduct is only observable longitudinally, which would break the catalog's instantaneous character while adding nothing the note and entry don't. Boundaries kept honest: the theory adjudicates the belief- and registration-layers of "insisting produces results" — never deal-outcomes as such (no outcome term); the account is static machinery composed over a sequence, exercising (not opening) the corpus's standing dynamics frontier; and everything inherits the theory's structural-only, uncalibrated status.

## Reasoning

**What was killed, and why.** The one full kill: **splitting Exit-Willingness into two catalog signals** (within-move exit-comfort + across-move return-policy). Prosecution won on two grounds jointly: the display catalog is an *instantaneous* detection list — only the Coherence gate reads longitudinally — so a return-policy signal would break the catalog's character; and it adds nothing the row-note plus phenomena-entry don't already deliver. The extracted seed rides the homing edit: *sequence-effects live in the Coherence gate's longitudinal machinery, not in a new display signal.* Killing the named alternative is what makes the homing verdict chosen rather than defaulted.

**What was deferred.** The capitulation-bootstrap idea (a pressured yes that opens an evidence channel the product then converts) — its entry is the user-excluded shape and its machinery is adjacency-flavored; deferred with a revival gate rather than absorbed.

**What survived only in a refined form.** Five refinements were forced by prosecution and folded in: the verdict survives *only as the composite* (the perfectly-mannered infinite suitor objection); consistency-accrual was demoted to supporting-never-sufficient (the reliably-returning pest objection); the survives-easing test gained the relief-vs-decay distinction (a fading yes is not necessarily a capitulation-yes); the deployed column became an explicit *conjunction* (the composed pest and the needy innovator each pass half the coordinates and must both fail); and the "attention-toll" idea was re-worded into committed vocabulary (each return spends receiver-availability and risks Contagion-load, so it must carry value-content) — the theory has no attention-economics term and doesn't need one.

**Why the five-mechanism account over the user's hypothesis alone.** The hypothesis (confidence + charm) cannot explain follow-up success in channels where the receiver never perceived the earlier attempts — the availability and threshold mechanisms succeed with no display at all. The hypothesis was therefore *placed* (the visible face of the display-borne family) rather than accepted as the mechanism or rejected as wrong.

**The frame's own premises were prosecuted first**, with the sources quoted verbatim rather than paraphrased: the catalog row (its unit is the move), Scenario 7 (the modifier "aggressively" and the bundle), the Coerced row (the reversal-with-relief signature and its post-removal-residue reading).
