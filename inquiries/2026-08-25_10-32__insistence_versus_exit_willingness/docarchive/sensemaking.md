## Phase 1 — Cognitive Anchor Extraction

**Constraints**
- C1 · Exit-Willingness's committed content is **risk-taken-within-the-move** (disagreement, selectivity, terminable close). The row's text legislates the *manner of a move*, never attempt-count.
- C2 · The capitulation case is excluded by the user, and it has a precise corpus signature: **reverses when the pressure lifts** (the Coerced shape).
- C3 · **No outcome term**: "insisting produces results" is partly outcome-language; the theory can adjudicate only the belief-layer (what the receiver comes to believe) and the attention-layer (what registers at all) — never deal-closure as such.
- C4 · Receiver-side: every mechanism must route through what the receiver comes to believe or register.
- C5 · Confirmed-absent: insistence has **no owner** in the corpus; the account must be composed.

**Key Insights**
- KI1 · **The chaser is a bundle.** Scenario 7's negative exemplar reads, verbatim, "follows up **aggressively**, reduces price to close, elaborates value incessantly." Three strands + (implicitly) a begging premise. The corpus never condemned bare persistence — the modifier was "aggressively" all along (read-quotes-to-end honored).
- KI2 · **Chasing is an output** (origin line): visible insistence *displays the insister's own state*, and the receiver reads it. Two readings are available: attention-on-the-yes (outcome-dependent need → Supplication → devaluation) vs attention-on-the-fit (backed conviction about the object — the deployed-anger precedent shows wanting-something-under-fire can still be non-extractive when attention stays on the standard, not the approval).
- KI3 · **Every return is a fresh approach act** (iteration-3.3): each contributes f_Charm (initiation confidence) + Attention-Hope ("they keep choosing ME specifically") + a fresh g₁-read — all scaled by that return's specificity. Repeated *specific* returns accumulate positive content; repeated *generic* pressure accumulates Supplication-evidence.
- KI4 · **Evidence vs repetition** (cumulative f + the correlated-evidence discount): a return carrying *new* material is a fresh update; an *unchanged* repeat is correlated evidence — it updates almost nothing, so the only new information in the move is its conduct, and pressure-shaped conduct reads extractive.
- KI5 · **Channel-migration**: repeated contact moves the pair down the θ ladder (cold-stranger → prior-contact); early attempts may have failed *below the registration threshold*, not negatively. Insistence can succeed by finally being *heard*, with no belief reversed.
- KI6 · **Availability-resampling**: some no's are availability-no's (the pre-condition was closed — the mid-rush owner). Returning re-samples availability; the first "no" was never a belief-verdict.
- KI7 · **Coherence-accrual**: returning as promised, consistent across visits, is *commitment-follow-through* and *temporal self-consistency* evidence — the persistent one becomes reliably modelable. A g₂ credit that only a sequence can earn.
- KI8 · **The anger template plausibly transfers**: deployed / leaked / performed insistence — but the transfer must be argued per-coordinate, not assumed (anger is affect-display; insistence is conduct-over-time).
- KI9 · **The user's hypothesis decomposes** (warm frontier): "confidence" = EC-composure of each move + own-premise (g₁) manner; "charm" = conviction read off the insister as competence/status content (with a share possibly carried by the *adjacent* social-norm-violation mechanism — pushing past ordinary rejection-points as status display). The hypothesis names the visible face of intact gates, not the whole mechanism-set.
- KI10 · **Insistence-won yeses must be typed**: belief-change / transaction / compliance — and the mechanism determines the type.
- KI11 · **The asymmetry cost**: each visible return also reveals sender-side investment ("they need this more than I do") — devaluation pressure that only the move's own manner can offset. This is why the failure branch *accumulates*: gates multiply, and each needy return is another datum.

**Structural Points**
- SP1 · **Two axes hiding in one word**: within-move manner (Exit-Willingness's actual content — owned) vs across-moves persistence (a sequence-property — unowned). The chaser is low on BOTH; the question is what high-manner + persistent looks like.
- SP2 · The prospective case-table: deployed / leaked / performed insistence.
- SP3 · The mechanism inventory: availability-resampling · channel-migration · evidence-delivery · Coherence-accrual · conviction-display (+ adjacencies: norm-violation status; intermittent reinforcement — which mostly conditions the *insister*, explaining why insisters over-believe in insistence).
- SP4 · The per-mechanism result-type mapping (which mechanism yields which persistence signature).

**Foundational Principles**
- FP1 · Belief-driven: "results" = receiver belief-changes (or registration events); that layer is the adjudicable one.
- FP2 · Gates multiply: one Supplication-shaped return can collapse what content built — per-move risk is asymmetric.
- FP3 · Backed vs performed: performed exit-willingness ("I'm fine either way," said needily) is readable; performed persistence-theater decays.

**Meaning-Nodes**: insistence · exit-willingness · the chaser · conviction-vs-need · the returning founder · "does the yes survive?"

## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

#### Ambiguity A1: Does Exit-Willingness oppose insistence at all?
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** Yes — the *spirit* of the signal is outcome-independence, and returning again and again for the same yes IS outcome-dependent conduct; the "axis-split" is a technicality that lets a needy chaser rebrand as a persistent conviction-holder.
**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** (1) The corpus's own positive exemplars are persistent: the selective consultant *selects and continues* (selectivity presupposes follow-through); the BATNA negotiator re-enters; the PI runs a lifelong program. If persistence-as-such were outcome-dependence, these exemplars would fail their own signal — they don't, because outcome-independence is defined on **where attention lives during the move** (the deployed-anger precedent: wanting the standard honored, under fire, with attention on the standard, is high-g). (2) Mechanically, the receiver reads *displays*; a terminable, composed, own-premise return carries no Supplication signature to read — count is not a display; manner is. (3) Sequence-effects enter the formula only through cumulative f and longitudinal g₂ — both of which can be *positive* for returns. The counter's residue is real and kept: repeated returns *do* leak sender-side investment (KI11), so the per-move manner must carry MORE exit-evidence as the sequence lengthens, not less.
**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** **Axis-split, absorb-shaped.** Exit-Willingness (within-move risk manner) stands as written and un-conditioned. Across-move persistence is a distinct, previously-unowned axis — now characterized, not legislated against.
**Fixed:** the commitment's text; the two-axis structure. **No longer allowed:** citing Scenario 7 against bare persistence (the chaser fails on manner + genericness + price-collapse); the folk reading "one no and never return." **Depends on this:** the case-table (A5), the outreach feedback, the finding's verdict-section. **Model change:** the "tension" becomes a scope-discovery.

#### Ambiguity A2: What mechanism makes genuine insistence-success genuine?
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** The user's own — confidence-and-charm is the mechanism: insistence works when the insister is impressive and composed, full stop.
**Why the counter fails as stated (structural grounds):** two surfaced mechanisms involve **no display at all**: availability-resampling (the first no was a pre-condition no; nothing about the insister changed anyone's mind) and channel-migration/threshold (early attempts never *registered*; later ones cleared a falling θ). A third — evidence-delivery — is f-side content, not insister-quality. A display-only account cannot explain follow-up success in channels where the receiver never perceived the earlier attempts. The counter survives *partially*: for the display-borne branch, "confidence" and "charm" are exactly the visible faces of the gate-conditions (EC-composure + own-premise g₁) and the content-credit (conviction read as competence/status — with an honest flag that part of this credit may ride the *adjacent* norm-violation mechanism, outside the theory proper).
**Confidence:** HIGH on the five-mechanism set; MEDIUM on the Charm-vs-adjacency share within M5.
**Resolution:** **Five typed mechanisms**: **M1 availability-resampling** (the no was never a verdict) · **M2 threshold/channel-migration** (registration, then ordinary update) · **M3 evidence-delivery** (cumulative f; escalating returns update, unchanged repeats hit the correlated-evidence discount) · **M4 Coherence-accrual** (follow-through + temporal consistency — reliability credit only a sequence can earn) · **M5 conviction-display** (sustained, specific, composed, terminable pursuit read as *backed belief in the fit* — costly-signal logic; the user's-hypothesis branch).
**Fixed:** the mechanism inventory and its typing. **No longer allowed:** confidence/charm as the *sole* mechanism; any mechanism whose predicted result reverses-on-easing (that is the excluded zone by construction). **Depends:** result-mapping (A3), case-table (A5). **Model change:** the hypothesis is decomposed and *placed* rather than accepted or rejected.

#### Ambiguity A3: "Produces results" — which results?
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** All insistence-success is capitulation at different speeds; the "genuine" cases are just slower wear-downs.
**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** M1 and M2 successes involve **no felt pressure** — in the sub-θ case the receiver never registered the earlier attempts as a sequence at all; there is nothing to capitulate *to*. And the capitulation shape has a committed, testable signature the genuine branches lack: **it reverses when the pressure lifts** (Coerced). M3/M4/M5 predict the opposite: a belief-change that *survives the insister easing off*.
**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** **The discriminating observable: does the yes survive the easing?** Insistence-success types as: registration-success (M1/M2 — the belief was available all along), belief-change success (M3/M4/M5 — new belief content), transaction (Hope-only — dissolves on delivery, legitimate but shallow), or capitulation (excluded zone — reverses). The user's exclusion is hereby operationalized as a *test*, not a vibe.
**Fixed:** the test; the typology. **No longer allowed:** treating a pressured yes as evidence the mechanism worked. **Depends:** the finding's discrimination-rule; the outreach guidance. **Model change:** "results" stops being one thing.

#### Ambiguity A4: Is "sometimes it is irritating" a separate phenomenon?
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** Irritation is mere receiver mood — outside a belief theory.
**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the irritation branch has a full mechanical account in committed texts: an unchanged repeat carries no new f-content (correlated-evidence discount), so the only information in the move is its *conduct*; need-shaped conduct is Supplication-evidence, and the gates **multiply** — each such return deepens g₁-collapse (devaluation compounds); pressure-conduct also transfers load (Contagion-Drain's "exhausting" report is literally the irritated receiver). Irritation is the phenomenology of *gate-collapse-in-progress under repeated extraction-shaped moves*.
**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** irritation = the leaked/needy case's signature, mechanically produced; same machinery, failure branch.
**Fixed:** the failure branch's mechanism. **Depends:** the case-table's leaked column. **Model change:** the account now explains failure as well as success — the motivated-reasoning guard is satisfied structurally.

#### Ambiguity A5: "Insisting" — collapse the kinds into the case-table (per-coordinate transfer).
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** the anger table shouldn't transfer — anger is momentary affect; insistence is extended conduct; borrowing the three-way shape is analogy-abuse.
**Why the counter fails (structural grounds):** the transfer is re-derived, not borrowed: each column is built from insistence-native coordinates — (deployed) each move own-premise + terminable + composed + **specific** + carrying new content; the sequence **terminates on a reasoned no** (respects belief-verdicts; retries only availability/threshold no's); (leaked) can't-stop pressing, unchanged repeats, need-shaped manner, no-tolerance absent; (performed) pressure-theater — scripted closes, fake deadlines, manufactured scarcity — Coherence-Alignment failure when detected, decay-arc afterwards. The three-way shape survives because the *coordinates* (g₁ premise, EC, specificity, content-freshness, termination-condition) are insistence-native; the anger table supplied the template only.
**Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH (derived, unexemplified — the same status performed-anger held at its derivation).
**Resolution:** the **insistence case-table** with those columns and coordinates; plus the **no-typology** it consumes (reasoned-no / not-now-no / never-registered-no).
**Fixed:** table shape + coordinates. **No longer allowed:** treating "insist or walk" as a binary. **Depends:** the finding's discrimination deliverable; the outreach rewrite. **Model change:** kinds become configurations.

*(Load-bearing concept test: "deployed insistence" — proxy-vs-structural: the label names a real configuration (coordinates enumerated), not a vibe; discoverability: its determination runs on observable per-move signals + the termination-condition; user-language: the user says "insisting" — the phenomenon keeps their word, the configurations qualify it. ### SV6 — Stabilized Model

**Insistence is a sequence of approach-acts. The theory prices each act's manner and accumulates each act's content — and Exit-Willingness legislates the manner of each move, never the length of the sequence.**

- **The commitment survives as written** (absorb): its content was always within-move risk (terminable close, selectivity, composed disagreement). The folk reading — "willing-to-walk means don't come back" — was never in the text, and the corpus's own positive exemplars (the selective consultant who selects-and-continues, the re-entering negotiator, the lifelong PI) are all persistent-toward-the-object while non-extractive-toward-approvers. **That inversion is the deep pattern: persistence was never the failing variable; extraction was.**
- **Genuine insistence-success has five corpus-native mechanisms**, typed by result-signature: availability-resampling and threshold/channel-migration (registration-successes — no belief reversed, the sequence just delivered contact); evidence-delivery (cumulative f — escalating returns update, unchanged repeats don't); Coherence-accrual (the reliability credit only a sequence can earn); conviction-display (the costly-signal branch — sustained, specific, composed, terminable pursuit read as backed belief in the fit). **The user's "confidence and charms" is the visible face of the last two** — right about the display branch, incomplete as the whole.
- **The discriminating observable operationalizes the user's own exclusion: does the yes survive the insister easing off?** Genuine branches predict yes; capitulation (Coerced signature) predicts reversal-and-relief.
- **The failure branch is equally mechanical:** unchanged, need-shaped repeats carry no new content, so their only information is extraction-shaped conduct — and gates multiply, so devaluation *compounds* per return. That compounding is what "irritating" is.
- **The instrument:** the deployed / leaked / performed insistence case-table (per-move coordinates: premise, composure, specificity, content-freshness; sequence coordinate: termination-condition — a deployed insister retries not-now and never-registered no's and **respects reasoned no's**), consuming the no-typology.
