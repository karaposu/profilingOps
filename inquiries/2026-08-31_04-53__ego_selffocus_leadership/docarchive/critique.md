Coherence in the corpus is a sender-side modulator, not a law that a follower's self-belief *cannot* coexist with accepting cover. **Defense:** derive it from committed machinery instead: the follower's standing self-claim is a *held position*, and held positions carry **consistency-accrual (M4)** — accepting a covering claim that contradicts it is an update that must overcome the position's accrued resistance.
