**Hidden coupling found and surfaced: two.**

The first is **P5's power to invalidate P2 retroactively** — if a type is a weight vector rather than a cell, *projection* may be the wrong word for what P2 established. Left implicit this is the textbook case. The second is subtler and would have been fatal: **P2's whole argument rests on reading the row-names as glosses.** That is an assumption about *how to read the table*, not a datum in it. A reader who takes *Transactional* to carry content beyond *Hope-dominant* rejects the projection immediately. Surfacing it converts P2's first criterion from *state the mapping* into *show the trace row by row* — the argument has to be demonstrated, not asserted.
