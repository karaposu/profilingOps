## Phase 1 — Seed

**Seed type:** Question + Gap. The question is structurally settled by Sensemaking; the gaps are what the settlement leaves standing — ten unnamed regions of the combination space, three parked sub-taxonomies, four ungathered relation-shapes, and no term for *form* at all.

**Direction (valuation).** What matters is not producing more members. Sensemaking established that completeness does not apply at the level the question points at, so generating candidate members would answer a question already shown to be malformed. What matters is whether the settlement gives the asker something **usable in place of the list** — a way to get any form's identity and behaviour without having one.

**Mode:** Production-task. The seed is Decomposition's 8-piece question tree.

### Methodology-Mode Consideration

- **(a) Inherited mode:** **Standard default.** The seed is a committed piece-list with verdicts rendered upstream; its language is *state · trace · render · report*. Balanced 4 Generators + 3 Framers.
- **(b) Alternative named:** **Generator-weighted exploration.**
- **(c) What follows under the alternative:** the candidate space would fill with *proposed members* — candidate types, candidate sub-flavours, candidate relation-shapes. Breadth of members is exactly what Sensemaking showed cannot be adjudicated: there is no gate at the type level, and no frequency data to decide which regions deserve names. A generator-weighted run would produce a large set of items with no test to apply to them.
- **(d) Decision: DEFAULT (inherited mode retained), with a stated reason and one recorded partial.** The alternative optimises for the answer-shape the finding rejects. **The partial:** at one piece — the composition space — generator work genuinely carries the load, because ten regions need *characterising* rather than adjudicating. Combination and Absence Recognition are therefore weighted toward that piece specifically, without shifting the run's mode.

## Phase 2 — Generate

Seven mechanisms × three variations (generic / focused / contrarian).

### 1 · Lens Shifting *(Framer)*

**Generic — the detection lens.** Current frame: a form is something the theory *names*. Shift to: a form is something a detector could *distinguish*. The detection layer scores variables on five categorical levels, so many nearby weight-distributions are indistinguishable in practice. → **L-a: the number of forms is bounded by detection resolution, not by combinatorics** — a different answer to *how many* from any the corpus contains.

**Focused — the practitioner lens.** What does someone reading a real conversation need? Not a complete list — a **decision**: will this persist or not? Under that frame the useful partition has **two** members, and the corpus already has it: externally-grounded versus internally-maintained. → **L-b: the operationally useful count is two, and it is already shipped** — the question's answer is smaller than the question expects, not larger.

**Contrarian — the sender's lens.** From the sender's side, *forms of attachment* means the forms you can **cause**. And that list has a property the receiver-side framing hides: **you cannot supply a combination.** You supply components; the mix is an emergent property of what the receiver already believes. → **L-c: combinations are not sender-addressable**, which means the combination level is real for describing but useless for prescribing — and the corpus's whole prescriptive layer lives on the component side for exactly this reason, without saying so.

### 2 · Combination *(Generator)*

**Generic — the composition space × Signal Specificity.** Specificity scales each variable's *effective* magnitude. So which variable dominates can shift with specificity alone, at constant nominal content. → **C-a: dominance is specificity-relative** — a case can move between regions without the sender changing anything except how specifically the signal is aimed. Any crisp cell-assignment is therefore unstable in exactly the dimension the corpus already measures.

**Focused — the composition space × the persistence parameters.** The prior finding derived ground-externality × removal-abruptness. Apply them per combination rather than per row: a Charm + Fear case has **two** externally-grounded variables with **different** abruptness, so it degrades in two stages — the fear lifts abruptly, the admiration recedes gradually. A Hope + Resonance case has one removable and one not, so it falls to a floor rather than to zero. → **C-b: every combination has a *derivable* persistence profile.** The unnamed regions are not blank; they are **computable**. *(Inherits the prior finding's recorded limit: the Resonance row's active-maintenance clause is not derived by either parameter.)*

**Contrarian — forms × the null case.** Every enumeration here counts non-zero states. There are two ways to reach zero, and they are architecturally different: **`f`-zero** (nothing to attach to) and **`g`-zero** (a gate blocked it). Both give a zero product; only the second is *attachment prevented* rather than *attachment absent*. → **C-c: the space has a boundary and the boundary has two kinds of edge**, which the corpus half-carries already in its double-collapse mechanism without naming the two zeros apart.

### 3 · Inversion *(Framer)* — depth check + multi-axis check

**Depth iteration on *"the types are a projection of the variables"*:**

- **L1 (component):** → *"the variables are a projection of the types."* Backwards on its face; invert again.
- **L2 (system):** → **neither projects the other; both project a third thing.** The variables *are* belief-categories; the types are dominance-readings of those categories. → **I-a: the projection has a direction only because the corpus built variables first.**
- **L3 (root):** → if both are readings of one belief-inventory, then *"how many forms of attachment"* is *"how many attachment-generating belief-categories are there"* — and the corpus answered that in its belief-frame: **four property-beliefs**, with the closure stated as *"combinatorial, not enumerative."* → **I-b: the question's answer was already given, about a different object, and never connected.** The generator count of four is not merely equal to the property-belief count of four; it is the **same count**, since the variables *are* the property-belief categories.

**Multi-axis — existence axis.** Could the number of *levels* be **zero** — the level scheme an artifact rather than a structure? → **I-c: *form of attachment* may have exactly one legitimate referent — the property-belief categories — and the other four "levels" may be levels of *description*, not levels of *form*.** The strongest available contrarian, and it deflates Sensemaking's central organising device.

**Multi-axis — identity axis.** What does a form fundamentally consist of? Under the belief-frame, a form is a **belief-content**: Charm-attachment is attachment generated by *this person is high-status*. → **I-d: forms are beliefs**, and the enumeration question is *which beliefs generate attachment* — the belief-frame's own question.

### 4 · Constraint Manipulation *(Framer)* — both directions mandatory

**ADD — *"one sentence, no new vocabulary."*** → *"Attachment comes in four kinds because four beliefs generate it; everything else is a mixture of those, a description of one, or something outside the theory."* → **CM-a: the whole result states without the level apparatus**, which matters because Sensemaking rated the level names MEDIUM on alignment with the asker's own words.

**ADD — *"the answer must survive a fifth variable being admitted."*** Then generators become five, the dominance space becomes thirty-one non-null patterns, and the sub-flavour work becomes five taxonomies. Everything scales; nothing breaks. → **CM-b: the account is count-independent** — it is a rule, not a list — which is precisely what makes it survive the corpus's own three candidate modulators and nine adjacent mechanisms.

**REMOVE — drop *"attachment is receiver-side."*** Then forms can be **dyadic**: mutual-bonded, one-way-coerced, mismatched-transactional. The corpus already scores every variable in both directions. → **CM-c: a dyad-level form taxonomy is constructible from data the corpus already specifies** — and it is a *seventh* enumeration, at a level none of the six occupies.

### 5 · Absence Recognition *(Generator)* — both levels mandatory, redesign-level bidirectional

**Patch-level.** Missing: names for the unnamed regions · a gathering place for the four relation-shapes · sub-taxonomies for three of four variables · any term at all for *form*. → **A-p.**

**Redesign-level · direction 1 — what's missing.** If this theory were built today it would carry, for each named set, **what the set is a set of, what admits a member to it, and whether it is primitive or derived.** The corpus has six sets and states the admission rule for only some — which is precisely what this inquiry had to reconstruct from scratch. → **A-a: a list-registry.**

**Redesign-level · direction 2 — what is already present in different form.** Does the project already do this? **Yes, in two fragments.** The anger inquiry recorded that *"the corpus has no routing rules anywhere"* and that *"what is missing is an **index**"* — and noted that iteration-3 already hand-curates one for five phenomena. And the adjacent-mechanism list carries explicit scoping language about what is and is not inside the theory. → **A-b: the registry exists in two partial forms and was already identified as missing once**, so A-a is a promotion rather than an invention.

### 6 · Domain Transfer *(Generator)* — native source mandatory

**Native (the corpus itself) — the basis-not-partition decision.** The corpus faced exactly Sensemaking's categorical-versus-basis fork one level down, for Hope's sub-flavours, and decided **basis**, with a stated rationale: instances are empirically multi-member and a partition *"would lose the multi-flavor structure."* → **D-a: the fork is settleable by the corpus's own precedent rather than by fresh argument** — and the precedent's rationale transfers exactly, since concrete attachments are as multi-member as concrete hopes.

**Deliberately different — colour.** Three primaries; every colour a mixture; a few hundred *named* colours; the naming set varying by language and by use. *How many colours are there?* has the same shape as this question, and its answer separates cleanly into **three primaries, a continuum, and a use-determined naming layer.** → **D-b: the naming layer is independent of the structure layer and is sized by use, not by structure** — which maps exactly onto Sensemaking's structural/naming split and predicts that *different applications would name different regions*.

**Contrarian — chemistry.** Elements versus compounds: a fixed small set of elements, unbounded compounds — but **not every combination forms**, because valence rules exclude most. → **D-c: the corpus has a valence rule and does not know it.** Hope + Fear was tested and absorbed by Coherence — a combination that does not produce a type but collapses a gate.

**And the rule is derivable rather than merely observed.** Why *that* pair? Because *this person will benefit me* and *this person will harm me* are contradictory claims about one sender, and contradictory claims are what Model-Collapse is. Charm + Fear is not contradictory (high-status **and** dangerous describes a powerful person coherently); Fear + Resonance is not (shares my world **and** can hurt me describes an intimate). So: **combinations whose beliefs imply contradictory sender-intentions are coherence-stressed.**

**One refinement the corpus's own case forces.** Its Hope + Fear case was a **stranger** — no model in which to accommodate the contradiction. With more information a receiver *can* form a model that holds both (the mentor who could also fire you). So the rule is not *Hope + Fear never forms* but **Hope + Fear requires model-support to form, and lacks it by default.** Which means the combination space's occupancy is **gate-dependent**, not purely `f`-determined.

### 7 · Extrapolation *(Generator)*

**Generic — accretion.** If regions keep getting named as they are encountered, the type list grows the way the species list grew — by absorption, with no gate. → **E-a: the type list will accrete unless the projection is stated**, and a list that accretes without a gate is what makes a taxonomy feel arbitrary.

**Focused — what implementation forces.** Build the detector and it emits four variable scores; something must turn those into a label. That code needs a rule, and the corpus has none. → **E-b: implementation forces the naming question**, and forces it as an argmax-with-ties problem — the same blocker the prior inquiry relocated onto coefficient calibration.

**Contrarian — extrapolate the question sequence.** Compliance → forms. If forms are four beliefs, the next question is *what are the beliefs* — which is the belief-frame, shipped months ago. → **E-c: this line of questioning converges on iteration-3.4**, suggesting the belief-frame is the corpus's actual answer-layer for *what kinds* questions and has never been used as one.
