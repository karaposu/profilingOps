## Phase 2 — Generate (7 mechanisms × 3)

### 1 · Lens Shifting (Framer)
- **(generic) The reader's lens.** Evaluate the row as a *detection rule* (`31`'s side) rather than a mechanism: from outside, a Charm-dominant attachment whose expression has stopped is indistinguishable from one that has weakened. Under this lens the row is *right* — it is an observation rule for the signature column. The correction becomes: move the cause out, keep the signature.
- **(focused) The sender's lens.** Evaluate the row as the sender's question — *what must I maintain when I'm not in the room?* Three maintenance channels fall out, one per distance-effect: the **record** maintains the Charm-belief; the **exchange** maintains Hope; **presence** supplies occasions for expression. Silence starves the last two, not the first.
- **(contrarian) The scarcity lens.** Under conditions where the absence is read as *chosen* (selectivity) rather than as decline — and the record and public standing stay intact — receding access **raises** Charm (aspirational distance, scarcity of access: `50` §3). The corpus's own display-coupling supplies the receiver-side form: withholding-mode Self-Positioning with suppressed Attention-Hope reads as *"impressive but not for me"* (`10` §6) — Charm intact, H_a suppressed. The reclusive star is that coupling at population scale.

### 2 · Combination (Generator)
- **(generic) The three distance-effects × the backing finding's channel table** → **the maintenance channels of a Charm-belief at distance:** the record (consequence-marks: outputs, costs paid), third-party report (social proof keeps pre-loading f — the friend with many dates never had to be present), and the occasional occasion. A "distance-maintenance card" for the prescriptive layer.
- **(focused) The record parameter × `21` §8's standing self-claim** → the corpus already has the sender-side twin: the **distance-ecology note** (`02`/`40` §1 — the *backed* standing claim survives distance; inflation decays close-up). Charm-persistence is its receiver-side reading: what survives distance is the record-backed evaluation; what decays with proximity is the unbacked one. One mechanism, two readings.
- **(contrarian) Relative re-pricing × Coherence** → the return case has a *gate* hazard, not only a variable one: a comeback that presents a *changed* person ("they're different now") is a Coherence-Stability event (Model-Collapse), which zeroes the revived Charm regardless of the record. Revival requires the returned person to be *the same person as the record* — Clause D, in the frame's terms.

### 3 · Inversion (Framer — depth-checked)
- **(generic, iterated to system level).** L1: "Charm weakens with distance" → "Charm rises with distance" (component-level; the scarcity case). L2: "distance changes Charm's *magnitude*" → "distance changes what Charm is *about*" (the object). L3 (system-level): **at distance the Charm source *becomes* the record** — the belief re-attaches to whatever carries the evidence: Mozart → the music; the founder → the product; the mentor → the ideas. The origin's own phrase, "access to the Charm source," was never restricted to the person: the source is the evidence-carrier. Multi-axis: existence-axis — can the source-count be zero? Only when nothing carries evidence (no record, no report, no occasions) — the ex-boss limit; identity-axis — Charm consists of an *evaluation*, and evaluations attach to records as readily as to people.
- **(focused) Invert the predication.** "weakens = expression" → "weakens = the belief itself decays (forgetting)." What follows: the theory would need a decay law with a rate — which structural-only forbids and the belief-frame lacks — and the decisive case (an intact evaluation of a two-centuries-dead sender) would have to be re-explained. The inversion prices the counter: a decay law is the admission fee, and nothing in the corpus pays it.
- **(contrarian) Invert the user's premise.** "We'd still be charmed with no access to follow" → "with no access at all there is no Charm, only the *memory* of having been charmed." What follows, existence-axis: could Charm be zero at total absence? Pattern-A says Charm is a *current* belief; without evidence a belief about present standing cannot be held as present. But the identity-axis answers: the belief **re-indexes its tense** — from "is impressive" to "was / is-on-the-record impressive" — and that is still Charm, because Mozart proves past-tense admiration generates attachment-class behaviour. **The tense-shift**: total absence does not zero Charm; it moves its object into the record and its tense into the perfect.

### 4 · Constraint Manipulation (Framer — both directions)
- **(ADD, generic) "Assume no record survives."** The ex-boss limit: only occasions and third-party report maintain; the belief goes stale and re-prices freely; expression lapses. Under this constraint the original row's claim *holds* — it is the record = 0 special case of the general account.
- **(REMOVE, focused) Remove "structural-only."** Rates become statable — staleness half-lives per record type, per field. The corpus cannot supply them; the removal shows exactly what data FR2 would need: *staleness × record-availability × field-stability* observations. Recorded as the FR2 customer line, not as a claim.
- **(ADD, contrarian) "The receiver cannot read the record."** Competence-legibility (`11` §2): a record the receiver cannot evaluate maintains nothing for them. So record availability is **receiver-relative**: availability × legibility. Refines P2's determination criterion — what counts as a record is *what this receiver can read as evidence*.

### 5 · Absence Recognition (Generator — both levels, both directions)
- **(patch-level) What's missing:** (a) the belief-frame (`10` §3) has an update rule and no fall-side statement — the un-maintenance account lives in a phenomena-index entry (`41` §7) with no architectural home; (b) no glossary entry for "access" despite six usages; (c) no home for parasocial *Charm*-persistence (FR5 covers Resonance only).
- **(redesign-level) What would exist if the persistence column were designed from scratch under the belief-frame:** two columns, not one phrase — **maintenance-source** (what keeps the belief evidenced) × **removal-event** (what takes its ground away). Charm: maintained by the evidence stream — the person's conduct, the record, third-party report; removed by counter-evidence or re-pricing (gradual). Hope: maintained by the exchange's continuation; removed when it ends (abrupt). Fear: maintained by the threat / dependency; removed when it lifts (abrupt). Resonance: maintained by the receiver's own model; removed by — *unstated* — the design exposes FR1's twin (Resonance's removal event is as unwritten as its maintenance).
- **(redesign-level, already-present) What the corpus already does in a less articulated form:** the maintenance idea exists — "the maintenance answer is the zero-display channels" (`41` §7), "maintenance ≠ creation" (`70`), "decays toward whatever was actually backed" (`30` §6). The two-column table articulates what three scattered lines already say.

### 6 · Domain Transfer (Generator — native + different)
- **(native — belief/memory vocabulary, vocabulary-only per `03` §5)** memory research's *availability vs accessibility* (a stored trace vs a retrievable one): the un-maintained Charm-belief is *available, not currently accessed* — a gloss for "intact but stale," never a mechanism import.
- **(different — reputation and credit ratings)** a rating persists until new information; it *stales* rather than falls, and staleness widens the reader's uncertainty ("under review"). Transfer: what a stale Charm-belief loses first is not magnitude but the **reader's confidence** — a detection-side statement (`31`): a stale Charm reading is a lower-confidence reading, not a lower reading.
- **(contrarian — dormant brands)** brands that disappear retain equity for years and can be revived — *when the category has not moved on*; the revival literature's condition is exactly re-pricing. Vocabulary-only support for revival-with-re-pricing; no metrics imported.

### 7 · Extrapolation (Generator)
- **(generic) Extend "the record maintains Charm."** Where records are permanently retrievable (a public profile, a repository, a catalogue), distance-decay of Charm tends toward zero for anyone with a public record; what remains is re-pricing. Prediction (stampable): the absent colleague's Charm persists more in a record-rich environment than in a record-poor one — access to the *person* is not the parameter.
- **(focused) Extend the return case to cycles.** Repeated disappear/return cycles re-price each time; a record that **grows during absence** (releases, output) beats one that freezes. For the user's season: *absence with output* keeps Charm maintained while the exchange sleeps — ship while silent.
- **(contrarian) Extend re-pricing.** In fast-moving fields the record *itself* devalues (old work looks dated): Charm from a frozen record decays at the *field's* rate, not with access. So record-maintenance is bounded by **field-stability**: Mozart's field is closed and canonical; a 2015 model paper is not. The bound is about the record, never the person.
