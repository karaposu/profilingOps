## SV1 — Baseline understanding

## Phase 1 — Cognitive Anchor Extraction

- **C2 (Charm's definition):** "this person is high-status / competent / impressive — I look up to them; I want their approval" (`11` §2); Pattern-A temporality *current-positive*.

- **C5 (corpus discipline):** frozen `BASEFILES/`; finalized findings are not rewritten (corrections land in canon; consumers get a note); properties are had, not admitted (`03` §2) — re-describing a property needs no admission gate.

## Phase 2 — Perspective Checking

- **Technical / Logical.** Take the belief-frame literally: f_C is a cumulative magnitude; with no decay law, an evidence stream that *stops* leaves f_C where it was; only counter-evidence lowers it. So, as written, the frame cannot produce "Charm weakens by distance" at all — only "Charm stops rising." That is too strong (the frame is silent, not committed to non-decay), and the silence is filled by `41` §7's un-maintenance. **New anchor K15 — expression needs occasions.** Attachment is a disposition to *engage / return*; with no access there is nothing to return to; the disposition is **unexpressed, not gone**. From outside, an unexpressed disposition and a weakened one look alike — the row's "weakens" may be an *observability* fact. The Mozart admirer expresses through the output (listening, defending); the retired star's fan has no occasion until the return.
- **Human / User.** The user's phenomenology: charm survives absence and "is there" on reappearance; "we can just have access to follow them" names evidential access as fame's normal case. **New anchor:** the *comeback* is frequently a Charm *increase* — absence plus an intact record plus scarcity/aspirational distance (`50` §3) — the sign-reversal seen in the field. The user's "or is it different?" is answerable: not different in kind — the same belief, revived; different in *what was doing the weakening* meanwhile.
- **Strategic / Long-term.** For the theory: every persistence row is a dynamics claim inside a static theory (FR2); the fix must be a *property re-description*, not a mechanism admission. For the user's application: the insistence finding's Return Doctrine prices silence by this row ("not-returning lets pre-loaded beliefs decay"). Under the corrected reading, silence lets the *Hope* component lapse (the exchange stalls; H_c and access-Hope thin) and removes *occasions*, while the Charm-belief about the founder/product stays — the doctrine's decay premise re-attributes to Hope + un-maintenance, not Charm.
- **Risk / Failure.** (1) Over-correcting into "Charm never weakens": false — Charm falls by counter-evidence, **and by a third route the corpus has not named: relative re-pricing.** **New anchor K16:** Charm is graded against the receiver's *current* reference class ("impressive" is relative); a person absent while the field moves on returns to an intact record and a moved standard — Charm lowered without any counter-evidence about the person (the once-impressive skill now ordinary). (2) Mozart is Charm-*dominant*, not Charm-pure: "identifying" is Resonance-flavoured; the persistence seen there may be partly the Resonance row's — flag, don't lean on Mozart alone. (3) Deleting the row would orphan the Charm+Fear prediction; re-word instead.
- **Resource / Feasibility.** No data; what can be committed: re-worded row + predication note + an access vocabulary + a re-attribution note for the doctrine; what cannot: rates, or any claim about *how long* staleness takes. Canon edits are cheap; finalized consumers get notes.
- **Ethical / Systemic.** Parasocial attachments are one-directional and can be exploited (scarcity theater); the corpus already prices manufactured scarcity as fraud — nothing new to add here.
- **Definitional / Internal consistency.** (a) The row contradicts `11` §1's decisive case under the interactional reading. (b) `30` §3's "ground externally removable" contradicts `10` §5's own statement that Charm's ground moves by counter-evidence "later and elsewhere." (c) The row's sign contradicts `50` §3's scarcity-of-access under the interactional reading. (d) The two-family assignment (Charm externally grounded) is *right* — the ground is the sender's real standing — but the *removal event* named is wrong. Strong anchors (a decisive case; the architecture) outrank a provisional row. **And the row contradicts itself:** its column is a *signature* (outside-view behaviour, by `30` §1's own reading), yet its wording states a *cause* ("as access recedes") — a signature row asserting a mechanism.
- **Definitional / Frame-exit completeness** (gating fires: "access" will be committed across multiple values). *Existence enumeration:* interactional · evidential · none · scarcity-of-access (adjacent) · Hope's access (H_c Continuity, access-Hope, "accessible high value") · reading-access (backing) · the sender's own access to the audience (the origin's "audience shrinks" — evidential from the sender's side). *Role assessment:* Hope's access referents are outside this inquiry's frame (Charm) but **load-bearing for the fame case** — the operation "explain the observed weakening on retirement" depends on them → re-locate: that weakening belongs to the **Hope row** ("dissolves when exchange ends") applied to the mixed fan attachment; this inquiry works the Charm slice. *Verdict rigor:* strongest counter to "the fan's weakening is Hope's" — "then pure-Charm attachments never weaken": fails on K15 (unexpressed) and K16 (re-pricing), which weaken pure-Charm attachments without any Hope; and Mozart (no Hope, no weakening) is consistent, not counter. *Residual:* none new — the sender-side usage reduces to evidential.
- **Phase / Calibration-state.** No calibration exists; the correction is stated as a tendency under the same provisional flag; mechanisms, not rates; the early-stage default is *the belief persists until re-priced or contradicted; its expression waits for occasions.*
- **The uncomfortable perspective (the original authors were right).** Most admiration *does* fade when the admired person leaves your world — the ex-boss, the star of one's teens: you stop thinking of them, you don't return. Under the disposition reading this is exactly right: expression fades with occasions, the belief goes stale, the reference class moves. So the row is **right as a signature** and **wrong as a mechanism attribution** — and the fame case is the *limiting* case where the record keeps maintaining the belief.

*Meta-inspection (H1, H2, H3, H7):* H1 — of S1's three predication objects, the behavioural signature is the disposition's *outside view* (the corpus's signature-to-state relation), so the candidate set collapses to two: **the belief vs the disposition (with its signature)**. H2 — Hope's referents re-located (above). H3 — the user's question inherits the row's causal shape ("as access recedes") while denying its truth; the reframed question is *what does a Charm-dominant attachment depend on — for its belief, and for its expression?* H7 — no calibration; tendencies only.

### SV3 — Multi-perspective understanding
The row is a **signature mis-stated as a mechanism**. Distance does three things to a Charm-dominant attachment: the belief goes **un-maintained** (intact, stale); the disposition loses **occasions** to express (looks weaker from outside); and the belief is exposed to **relative re-pricing** as the reference class moves. The *ground* is removed only by counter-evidence. Where the sender's **record** stays available it maintains the belief in the person's absence — the fame case is the limiting case: evidential access without interactional access, which is what the origin's "access" meant. In mixed fan attachments the part that dissolves on retirement is **Hope**. On return the stored belief is **revived** at prior-strength, often *raised* by scarcity — unless the field moved.

## Phase 3 — Ambiguity Collapse

#### A1 · "Access" in the row — interactional, evidential, or none?
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** the draft's behaviour column ("seeks proximity to signal association") shows the authors meant *proximity* — interactional access; the receiver wants to be near the source, and the attachment weakens when they can't be.
**Why it fails (structural):** the two draft columns name different things — the behaviour column describes what the *admirer does* (seeks proximity), the persistence column names what *removes* the attachment, and its trigger is what the *source* does ("celebrity retires, audience shrinks" — the signal stream ending). Under the interactional reading the row also contradicts the theory's decisive case (Mozart, K3) and the sign of scarcity-of-access (K8). The evidential reading is consistent with all three.
**Confidence:** HIGH.
**Resolution:** in the row, "access to the Charm source" = **evidential access** — the stream of Charm-evidence available to the receiver (the source's output, conduct, standing) — never reachability of the person.
**Fixed:** the row's "access" term. **No longer allowed:** "access to the person" as the row's meaning (the paraphrase is a narrowing to be corrected). **Depends:** the access vocabulary (A3's typology); the paraphrase fix; the re-worded row. **Model change:** the user and the origin partly agree — the row was never about reaching the person.

#### A2 · What is "weakens" predicated of — the belief or the disposition?
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** the belief itself decays — people forget; f_C literally falls with time.
**Why it fails (structural):** the belief-frame has no decay law; its own accounts of the fall-side are un-maintenance (the belief persists un-refreshed) and counter-evidence; erasure-by-time is not a mechanism the theory has, and the mentor-from-afar and Mozart cases show intact evaluations after decades. The counter has *partial* merit: **staleness** is a belief-side property (its currency, not its content) — the belief is intact but un-maintained. The row's column is, by `30` §1's own reading, a *signature* — outside-view behaviour — so its "weakens" is predicated of the **disposition's expression**.
**Confidence:** HIGH (predication); the staleness note MEDIUM-HIGH.
**Resolution:** "weakens" describes the disposition's *expressed* strength; the belief is *intact-but-unmaintained* (stale in currency, unchanged in content until re-priced or contradicted).
**Fixed:** predication object = the disposition (with its signature); the belief's status = intact/stale. **No longer allowed:** reading the row as a claim that Charm-*beliefs* erode by distance. **Depends:** A3, A5, the corrected row. **Model change:** the corpus's signature-to-state relation applies here — the row is the outside view of an unexpressed disposition.

#### A3 · The mechanism of the observed weakening — ground-removal, un-maintenance, occasion-loss, or re-pricing?
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** `30` §3's committed derivation — Charm's ground *is* externally removable, because Charm is a *current* belief (Pattern A) that needs present evidence; distance is ground-removal for a present-tense belief.
**Why it fails (structural):** Pattern A's "current" is the *temporality of the belief's object* (Charm evaluates what the sender is now; Hope what they will do), not a requirement of continuous evidence — the decisive case holds a "current" Charm-belief about a dead sender on the strength of the record. Ground-removal proper is what `10` §5 names: counter-evidence ("persistent failure eventually lowers Charm"). What distance removes is (i) *maintenance* (fresh evidence), (ii) *occasions* for expression, and it *exposes* the belief to (iii) *relative re-pricing* (K16). The counter keeps one true point: because Charm is about present standing, a long absence makes the sender's *present* standing uncertain — which is (i) and (iii), not ground-removal.
**Confidence:** HIGH that distance ≠ ground-removal; MEDIUM-HIGH for the three-way decomposition (structural, untested).
**Resolution:** three distance-effects — un-maintenance · occasion-loss · re-pricing exposure — and one ground-removal route — counter-evidence.
**Fixed:** the decay taxonomy for Charm. **No longer allowed:** "Charm's ground recedes with access" as a mechanism; a decay modulator. **Depends:** the corrected row; the re-attribution of the insistence doctrine's decay premise; the Charm+Fear re-wording. **Model change:** `30` §3's parameter "ground externally removable" is *right for Hope and Fear* (events remove their ground) and *misapplied to Charm* (only re-evaluation removes it) — a refinement of the two-family reading, not its replacement.

#### A4 · The fame case — is the retirement-weakening Charm's or Hope's?
**Strongest counter-interpretation:** fans' admiration itself fades — they stop calling the star great; that is Charm falling.
**Why it fails (structural):** what fades is *salience and expression* (K15) and *relative position* (K16); the evaluation is evaluative content ("I look up to them") and returns intact when cued ("he was the best"). What genuinely *dissolves* on retirement is Hope — H_a (being seen), H_c (the sender staying reachable), access-Hope — the Hope interior is where "access" lives natively (`11` §3; the frozen spec's "accessible high value"), and the celebrity-fan case was retired as decisive precisely for an H_v residue.

**Resolution:** in mixed fan attachments, retirement dissolves the Hope component and suspends expression; the Charm-belief stays, stale, subject to re-pricing.
**Fixed:** the fame case's decomposition. **No longer allowed:** citing the fan's cooling as evidence that Charm-beliefs decay by distance. **Depends:** the FR5 hook; the doctrine re-attribution. **Model change:** the Hope row ("dissolves when exchange ends") already covers the part of the fame case the Charm row was blamed for.
