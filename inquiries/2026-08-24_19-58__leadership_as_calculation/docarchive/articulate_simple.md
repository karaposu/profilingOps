
## Itemize

**Count = 3.**

- **I1** — the possibility-and-focus question: *"how to be a good leader if you don't have human skills. Is it possible, or which human skills exactly you should develop, I guess?"* — carrying the self-description as its case material (a technical person; not into small talk; "not really alluring… like bright"; "I cannot express myself so good"; willing to learn; "social dynamics is a really huge field, and my focus should be what").
- **I2** — the feasibility question about the method: *"Do you think this is a feasible approach?"* — where the approach is: use the framework ("attention, charming, fear, self-focus") to identify the correct things to improve, and to guide conduct in three named uses — *talking with people, making decisions, maintaining posture* — such that it *"can feel like a calculation"*, usable *"in vital moments"*, compressing the *"five, ten years"* default. With the explicit license: *"I want this to be feasible, but I am asking you also to dive deep."*
- **I3** — the roadmap question: *"What should we know better in a more concrete way? What meaning gaps should we uncover first in order to make this approach a reality?"*

**Split reasoning.** Three distinct deliverable shapes: I1 wants a possibility-verdict plus a development-focus; I2 wants a graded verdict on a *method*; I3 wants an ordered gap-list. Held together, one Deconstruct tuple would carry three shapes — the suppressed-multi-item signature. Keep-together was weighed for I1+I2 (the statement fuses them: the which-skills ask is posed *as* the framework-application); the split holds because a method-verdict and a skills-focus are different artifacts, and the dependency is recorded instead of erased.

**Two dependencies recorded, not resolved.** I1 partially depends on I2 (if the framework-path fails, the which-skills answer reverts to non-framework grounds); I3 depends on I2 (the gap-list is scoped *"to make this approach a reality"* — i.e., to whatever form of the approach survives I2).

## Item I2 — is the framework-as-calculation approach feasible?

### MQ1 (verdict-axis)

**Identified-ambiguities-list:**
