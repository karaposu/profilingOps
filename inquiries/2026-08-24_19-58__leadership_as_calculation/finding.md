### The words this finding leans on

This finding is written to be readable on its own. The theory it draws on has its own vocabulary; here is all of it, in plain language, before it gets used. ("The corpus" below means this project's accumulated findings plus its specification documents.)

- **The framework (APT — Attachment & Presentation Theory).** A model of why one person becomes drawn to, and stays engaged with, another. Four ingredients *add up*: **Charm** (they seem impressive — status, competence, skill), **Hope** (being around them promises something good), **Fear** (there are consequences to losing them), and **Resonance** (the felt sense of seeing the world the same way — shared values, shared cares). On top of the ingredients sit multiplying *gates*; the one that matters here is **standing** (the theory calls it Self-Positioning): whether your attention reads as genuinely on something, or as fishing for the other person's approval. When attention reads as approval-fishing — the theory's word for that pole is **Supplication**: hedging, over-explaining, permission-seeking — the gate drops and everything the ingredients built collapses.

- **The display catalog (the five signals).** The theory's list of the five concrete behaviors observers actually read — mostly unconsciously — to judge where your attention is and what your standing is. They are: **Premise-Posture** (you decide and speak from your own premise rather than asking permission), **Exit-Willingness** (you can abandon a bad position or decision without drama), **Self-Justification-Density** (how much you defend and justify yourself unprompted — *low* is what reads well), **Rhythm-Comfort** (comfort with silence and pauses — not needing to fill them), and **Withholding** (giving slightly less than expected — a double-edged signal: it raises perceived standing but can also read as "this person isn't interested in me"). Whenever this finding says "the catalog," it means this list.

- **The shared term ("the term").** The prior finding's name for the thing a leader's attention is on and that followers can share: a cause, a mission, a product, a craft — *the third thing that you and the people around you can all care about together.* Not "term" as in terminology — think of it as the shared object. The prior finding's result was that a leader is precisely the person whose attention is visibly on such a shared object and through whom others get access to it. A person's own self cannot be this object (a self can't be shared); a project can. For this user, the term already exists: the thing they want to create.

- **Backed versus performed.** A display is **backed** when the attention it shows is really where it appears to be — you look absorbed in the work because you are. It is **performed** when it is put on. The corpus's repeated finding is that performed displays get caught: sometimes immediately (audiences read a performer who is visibly adjusting to their reactions as approval-fishing, and cool toward them), sometimes slowly (a faked posture is expensive to maintain, and observers notice the cost isn't being paid — the corpus calls this slow exposure the *costly-signal decay arc*).

- **Outcome-independence.** The theory's named cultivation target — the state where your attention stays on doing the thing well rather than on how the other person is reacting or whether you will win. It is the theory's operational, plain-behavior definition of what "genuinely on something" means. (The theory's own phrasing: "the marker of non-extractive-ness made operational" — i.e., the practical, observable form of not-approval-fishing.)

- **Attention-configurations (the theory calls them "species").** The theory's names for what your attention is habitually *on*: your own project, another person's wellbeing, a larger cause (**Mission-Focus**), the craft itself (**Task-Focus**), a shared inquiry, and so on. "Configuration" in this finding means: which of these you occupy in a given context.

- **Bearing.** Your stable, observable attention-posture across many interactions — which configuration you tend to hold, and how solidly. The corpus's own line: over time, someone's bearing "becomes part of what others call character." This is the theory-side word for what the user called "posture."

- **Legibility / legibility-work.** Making your work readable and actable by people who cannot judge the raw substance: writing it down clearly, structuring it, demonstrating it. Held at **medium confidence** in the corpus — a promising idea resting, so far, on a single source, which is why this finding uses it carefully and never as a proven claim.

- **The prescriptive layer, and the tiered self-audit.** Most of the theory *describes* how attachment works. One part of it — added in the iteration the corpus labels 3.1, in `inquiries/self_focus_dynamics/finding.md` — *advises*: it contains a table matching contexts to the attention-configuration worth cultivating in them, names outcome-independence as the thing to cultivate, points to mature practice traditions for the how (rather than inventing exercises), and includes a short self-check questionnaire the corpus calls the **tiered calibration audit**. This advisory part is "the prescriptive layer." Before this inquiry, it had never been applied to an actual person.

- **PRAGMA.** The project's conversation-analysis layer: it measures messages along dimensions such as expressed involvement, control, information density, and intent. Mentioned here because its dimensions make a good vocabulary for reviewing one's own conversations after the fact.

One more scope fact, stated up front because several verdicts rest on it: **the framework is, by its own documentation, not yet empirically validated, and its numeric weights are uncalibrated.** Everything below is structural reasoning from the theory's committed texts — the theory's own flags say so, and this finding inherits that status.

## Finding Summary

- **Yes — it is possible.** What makes people follow, on this theory's account, is not small talk, allure, or verbal brightness. The main engine is **Resonance** — the sense of sharing a worldview — and it is generated when your attention is visibly on a **shared object**: the cause, product, or mission that you and the people around you can care about together (what the prior finding calls "the term"). Charm is one additive ingredient among four, not the motor. The signals that carry standing work even **without words** (the theory's animal-training example: handlers earn deep animal trust purely through calm, non-anxious bearing). And people demonstrably follow unsuccessful and uncharming leaders. The bar the user set — "a good one, maybe not the best" — is exactly the bar this mechanism supports.

- **The deficit-audit dissolves two of the three feared deficits and relocates the third.** *Small talk:* the display catalog — the five behaviors people actually read to judge standing (listed above) — contains **Rhythm-Comfort: comfort with silence and pauses**. The list you are judged by *rewards* not filling silence; small-talk fluency appears nowhere on it. *Allure:* allure is Charm-display, and Charm is one ingredient of four; moreover the user already has Charm-content — technical competence — whose real limitation is that most people can't *read* it (a legibility problem, not an absence). *Expression:* what actually fails in the theory is a **mode** of expressing — hedging, over-explaining, asking permission (the approval-fishing pattern) — not limited eloquence; and the one place expression genuinely matters for leading is **making the work actable by others**, which is writing- and structuring-work: the user's home terrain.

- **The audit also finds the risk the user didn't name:** over-explaining and over-defending one's technical choices is exactly the catalog's **Self-Justification-Density** failure — the one display-failure a technical temperament is genuinely prone to. The audit is two-sided, which is what makes its kind verdicts credible.

- **The feasibility verdict splits in two.** The **slow half** of the user's approach — use the framework to calculate *what to improve* and *what posture to hold* — is **feasible right now, by precedent**: the theory's own prescriptive layer already does it (the context-to-configuration table; the named cultivation target; the practice pointers). The **fast half** — calculate *what to do in the moment* — survives as exactly **one modest move**: put your attention back on the work. Choosing *displays* in the moment is the theory's own documented failing pattern — its worked example is the stage performer who visibly adjusts to the audience's reactions and is read as approval-fishing, "even if technically competent."

- **The tempting shortcut gets a price tag, not a ban.** Directly engineering your displays (scripts, templates, playbooks — the natural engineer's move) does work in one direction: a displayed cause still recruits people. But performed displays get caught, in the moment or slowly, and **what persists of any following is the part that was actually backed**. Since the user's goal is durable leadership, the genuine path selects itself on the theory's own arithmetic — no moralizing needed. One honest **boundary zone** remains: rehearsed and prepared forms (is a practiced opener a performance?) cannot be adjudicated yet, because *what backing observably looks like* is an open question the corpus itself has deferred.

- **"Calculation" survives in the qualitative sense — which the user's own hedge licenses.** The theory has no calibrated numbers (its weights are on its own pending list), so a numeric conduct-calculus is dead for now; and even future calibration would revive numeric *reading of other people*, never numeric *conduct* — no quantity of numbers makes a faked display earn like a real one. What survives genuinely feels like calculation and is how expert fields handle judgment under pressure anyway: a **lookup**, a **criterion**, a **self-audit**, and **one pre-committed move**.

- **What compresses is the search, not the practice.** The framework's real gift is collapsing "social dynamics is a huge field" to a short, specific list — that kills the five-to-ten years of *unfocused wandering* the user feared. The cultivation itself still costs what practice always costs: daily, unglamorous, months-scale work.

- **The program is assembled entirely from instruments the project already had:** the cultivation target (outcome-independence), the context-to-configuration table, the five-signal catalog as the actual training list (zero small-talk items on it — it reads like an engineering temperament pointed at people), the practice pointers (Stoic dichotomy of control; performance-psychology process cues; Carl Rogers' therapeutic "congruence" — *genuine, not performing*), the tiered self-audit as a baseline measure, and an evening-review habit built from pieces the corpus already cites. Plus the one fast move — which doubles as the **recovery protocol** for the moment you catch yourself hedging or over-justifying.

- **The gap-roadmap, ordered:** first, **backing-observables** — what does a genuine display observably look like versus a performed one — because both halves of the program need it (it is your self-check *and* the boundary of the hazard) and because the corpus itself already deferred it with the admission that the genuine/faked distinction is "currently unfalsifiable in practice." It is the roadmap's **only gate**: it must be answered before anyone designs display-targeted training. Everything else — turning the five signals into practice exercises, finding a second source for the legibility idea, designing the feedback-loop instrument — merely sharpens a program that can start immediately.

- **The endpoint has a name and an external test:** sustained practice consolidates into **bearing** — the stable posture that, in the corpus's own words, "becomes part of what others call character." You'll know it's working when *other people describe you differently.*

## Finding

### 1. Why this question completes a pair

The immediately prior inquiry answered *what a leader is*: the person whose attention is visibly on a shared object — a cause, a product, a mission — and through whom others get access to it; "following" someone means staying attached to them and continuing to pursue, which that shared object keeps generating. It closed with a conditional addressed to this user: wanting to create something means the shared object already exists (it's the thing you want to create); putting your attention visibly on *it* is the configuration that generates followers; and none of that requires being the most competent person in the room.

This inquiry is that conditional's second half — the person side. Can *this specific person* occupy that configuration, what exactly should they train, and can the framework serve as the calculating instrument they want?

One discovery reframed everything early: **the theory already contains an advisory layer built for almost exactly this use** — the prescriptive layer described in the vocabulary section above, with its cultivation target, its context-to-configuration table, and its practice pointers — and in the four months since it was written, *it had never once been applied to an actual person*. This inquiry is that layer's first field test. The user's needs and the layer's unfinished parts turn out to line up, which is why the roadmap at the end serves both.

### 2. The audit: what the feared deficits turn out to be

The method: run each feared deficit through the theory's own instruments, instead of through folk judgment about what "leadership material" looks like.

**"I'm not really into small talks."** Look at what observers actually read to judge standing — the five-signal display catalog. One of the five is **Rhythm-Comfort: comfort with silence and pauses**. The list you are judged by literally rewards *not* filling silence. And the whole standing-display works **without words**: the theory's animal-training example (its cross-species validation case) shows handlers earning deep trust from animals purely through calm, grounded, non-anxious bearing — no conversation involved. Small-talk fluency appears nowhere in the mechanism. Verdict: **not load-bearing** — and its absence may even read well.

**"I am not really alluring… like bright."** "Alluring" is a Charm-display — and Charm is only one of the four additive ingredients; the prior finding already established that leading runs on attention-direction, not on out-charming anyone. More pointedly: the user *already has* Charm-content — technical competence is exactly what the theory files under Charm — and its real limitation is that most people **cannot read it**. An investor or a new hire can't directly perceive deep technical skill. That is a **legibility** problem (make the work readable) — not an absence of the quality. Verdict: **not required as folk-allure**, and partially already possessed in a form that needs translation, not creation.

**"I cannot express myself so good."** The theory is precise about what actually fails in expression, and it is a **mode, not a fluency level**: hedging, over-explaining, permission-seeking — the approval-fishing pattern that drops the standing-gate. Limited eloquence is not that pattern; a plain, slow, even awkward speaker who speaks from their own premise reads *fine*. Where expression genuinely matters for leading is one specific place: **making the work actable by others** — writing it down, structuring it, demonstrating it so people can act on it independently. That is the relocated deficit — and notice its shape: it is document-work, the user's home terrain, not party-work.

Two scope-notes keep this audit honest. First, "these traits may have social costs in daily life" is not tested here and not denied — what is refuted is the narrower claim "these traits block leading," which is a question the mechanism can actually answer. Second, the audit was also run in the *opposite* direction — not just "what does the user lack" but "what does the user have in excess that the theory prices" — and it found the risk the user did not name: **over-explaining and over-defending one's technical choices is exactly the catalog's Self-Justification-Density failure** (the signal where *low* is good and unprompted self-defense reads badly) — the one display-failure a technical temperament is genuinely prone to. It is named as a risk from the catalog, not a diagnosis of the person. (And one possible head start, stated as a possibility only: the failing modes are *acquired* habits — filler-talk, hedging, permission-talk — and someone who never picked up the small-talk habit may simply have less to unlearn.)

### 3. The possibility-verdict

**Yes.** The grounds, all previously-committed results of this corpus, spelled out:

- Following is generated primarily by **Resonance** — the shared-worldview ingredient — through a shared object the leader gives access to; not by charm.
- The display channel that carries standing **works without verbal social skill** (the pre-verbal animal-training case).
- Competence is one ingredient of four, so a ceiling on one ingredient does not cap the others.
- The corpus's folk-attributes inquiry opens with the observation that **people follow unsuccessful men** — belief in a leader is decoupled from results and from polish alike.

"Good, maybe not the best" is precisely the bar this supports: nothing here promises exceptional leadership; all of it removes the folk "human skills" as the gate.

### 4. What "calculation" can honestly mean here

The user's word "calculation" imports a mode the corpus has never used for conduct — and the user's own hedge (*"it can **feel** like a calculation"*) marks what is actually wanted: structured decision-forms that suit a technical mind. Not arithmetic.

**The numeric reading is dead, for now, and precisely why:** the framework's weights sit on its own pending-calibration list, its detection outputs are coarse categories rather than quantities, and its validation flag is unresolved — **there are no numbers to calculate with.** The revival condition is that calibration itself. And one scope-line drawn in advance: even if calibration lands someday, numbers would revive quantitative *reading of other people* (diagnosis), never quantitative *conduct* — because what a display earns depends on whether it is backed, and no quantity of numbers changes that.

**The qualitative reading survives — and it is not a consolation prize.** Structured qualitative judgment under pressure is how expert domains actually operate: aviation handles cockpit load not by computing in the moment but by simple procedures pre-committed in calm; and this very project runs on checklists, graded confidence labels, and count-everything discipline. The four calculation-feeling forms this finding hands over:

1. a **lookup** — which attention-configuration fits this context (the prescriptive layer's table);
2. a **criterion** — for any candidate skill or advice: *does it touch the five-signal catalog or the cultivation target? If not, skip it* — this single test is what shrinks the "huge field";
3. a **self-audit** — the tiered questionnaire, as a baseline and periodic check;
4. **one pre-committed move** — next section.

One more re-reading the dive-deep license requires: *"then I can make the correct decisions"* cannot mean what it says, because the framework has **no outcome term** — nowhere in it does the success or failure of a decision appear; it models what people read off you, not whether your calls turn out right. What it *can* make correct is the **posture you decide from**. And here the theory hands the user a gift: its own earlier derivation is that *deciding from your own premise is read positively regardless of whether the decision turns out well* ("they don't have to be correct" — the folk-attributes inquiry derived exactly this). Decisiveness without outcome-anxiety is itself the trainable thing, and outcome-independence is its name.

### 5. The feasibility verdict: two halves, one move

**The slow half — "calculate what to improve, and what posture to hold" — is feasible right now, by precedent.** The theory's prescriptive layer already does this, qualitatively:

- *Which configuration?* The context-to-configuration table answers per context. For cause-driven leadership its row prescribes **Mission-Focus** — attention on the cause itself, the "visionary-leader" pattern. For this user: attention visibly on the thing they want to create.
- *What to cultivate?* **Outcome-independence** — the named target: attention that stays on the work rather than on reactions and results.
- *How?* By deliberate delegation to mature practice traditions rather than invented exercises: the **Stoic dichotomy of control** — the daily discipline of separating what is up to you (your attention, actions, responses) from what is not (others' reactions, outcomes) — which the corpus itself notes "IS outcome-independence in daily discipline"; **performance-psychology** process cues and pre-performance routines; and **Carl Rogers' therapeutic conditions**, whose first condition is *congruence: the therapist is genuine, not performing*. Notice that last one: the requirement that the display be backed is stated *inside* the practice tradition itself.

**The fast half — "calculate what to do in vital moments" — survives as one modest move: put your attention back on the work.** Not on the room, not on how you're coming across — on the standard, the problem, the thing being made. Why this and only this:

- Choosing *displays* in the moment is the theory's documented failing pattern. Its worked example is the stage performer *"visibly calibrating… to audience reaction"*, whom audiences read as approval-fishing and cool toward, *"even if technically competent."* Its second example is performed anger — anger put on for effect — which observers read as *"he's putting it on."* In-the-moment display-management is precisely the thing that gets caught in the moment.
- The attention-move is different in kind: it operates *upstream* of display. Your visible signals leak from where your attention actually is; move the attention and the signals follow — so what observers read is the genuine article, because it is one.
- Its grounding is three-way: the theory's "deployed anger" result (anger aimed at a violated standard, proportionate and terminating, *earns respect* — that is attention-on-the-standard holding under fire); the Stoic dichotomy (this exact move as a daily practice, millennia old); and the theory's own architecture (the standing-gate reads attention-direction).
- Its form is aviation's: **calculate slow, act fast.** The move is pre-committed and rehearsed in calm; in the moment you don't deliberate, you fire it.
- And it **doubles as the recovery protocol**: the moment you catch yourself hedging or over-justifying mid-conversation, the answer is the same move — stop (cleanly, without drama: the theory's respect-earning anger *terminates*, it doesn't spiral), don't apologize-spiral about the slip (that would be more unprompted self-justification — the exact signal you're recovering from), and return attention to the work. One move, two doors: entry and recovery.

Its limits, stated because the license demands them: the move's logic rests on committed theory texts, but its **effectiveness in real vital moments is untested** — the feedback-loop instrument that would test it is on the roadmap. It is one move, not a system. And nothing anywhere in this project operates in real time — the theory analyzes conversations after the fact; there is no live coach and this finding does not pretend to be one.

### 6. The tempting path, priced

Could the user instead engineer the displays directly — scripts, conversational templates, playbooks? For a technical person that is the natural temptation-shape: not faking charm (they know they can't) but **script-engineering** — building the playbook. The honest answer is a price, not a prohibition.

Calculated display *works*, in one direction: a displayed cause still recruits — display alone is enough to *accumulate* people, and professional performers live on it. Instructive, though, is what the display-professionals themselves converged on: the durable acting schools (the Stanislavski lineage — "method" acting) train actors to *manufacture the real state* rather than assemble its outward signs. The profession whose product IS display voted for backing.

Then the theory's arithmetic does the rest: performed displays get caught — in the moment (the calibrating-performer pattern above) or slowly (the costly-signal decay arc: a faked posture is expensive to keep up, observers eventually notice the cost isn't being paid) — and **what persists of any following is the part that was actually backed**. A goal of *durable* leadership therefore selects the backed path by itself; no sermon is required. The manipulation worry resolves the same way: manipulation, in this theory's own terms, is display-selection aimed at extracting reactions from people — which is exactly the pattern the standing-gate punishes. The corrected form — cultivate the state, attend to the work — is non-extractive by definition. This finding is a posture-program, not a display-manual.

One **boundary zone** is marked rather than decided: rehearsed displays, practiced openers, prepared forms. Is a rehearsed introduction a performance? Nobody can adjudicate that yet, because *what backing observably looks like* — how a genuine display differs, visibly, from a well-faked one — is an open question the corpus itself has deferred (its own words: the genuine/faked distinction is "currently unfalsifiable in practice"). That question is the roadmap's first item.

### 7. The program, and what it compresses

Everything the user should actually do already existed in the corpus; this inquiry's work was finding it, auditing it, and assembling it:

| Element | What it is, plainly | How you know it's working |
|---|---|---|
| **The target** | outcome-independence — attention that stays on the work, not on reactions or results | the tiered self-audit's questions (the questionnaire lives in `inquiries/self_focus_dynamics/finding.md`; this finding points to it rather than reproducing questions it did not re-read; applying it is the roadmap's quick win and the program's baseline) |
| **The configuration** | the table-row for your context — for creating something: attention visibly on the thing being created | your attention observably on the work, not the audience |
| **The trainables** | the five-signal catalog: decide from your own premise · exit bad positions cleanly · don't defend yourself unprompted · tolerate silence · (withholding — handled with care, since it cuts both ways) | the catalog's own behavioral descriptions |
| **The practice** | the pointed-to traditions: Stoic journaling and evening review; process cues from performance psychology; Rogers' congruence | the daily practice happening, or not |
| **The review** | an evening review of the day's significant exchanges, using PRAGMA's conversation-dimensions as the checklist vocabulary (how involved was I? controlling? dense? what was my intent?) | done nightly, or not |
| **The move** | attention back on the work — both the vital-moment act and the recovery act | the evening review catches its firings |
| **The endpoint** | **bearing** — the stable posture that over time "becomes part of what others call character" | *others describe you differently* |

These compose into the **calculation card** the user was implicitly asking for — a one-page instrument: *situation → table-row → target-check (pointer to the audit) → the five signals → the one move.* Three of its four cells are fully specified in this finding; the audit-cell is a named pointer to where the questionnaire lives (deliberately not paraphrased, since this inquiry did not re-read it).

And the compression claim, split honestly: what the framework compresses is **the search**. "Improve socially" collapses from an endless field to one state, one table, five signals, one move — that is precisely the five-to-ten years of unfocused wandering dying. What it does not compress is **the practice**: the traditions' costs (daily, unglamorous, months-scale) remain in full. In the skill-acquisition field's own vocabulary: the framework supplies the *practice-target specification* — knowing exactly what to practice, which that field regards as the hard part — and leaves the repetitions to the practitioner.

A first week could look like this — one possible instantiation, entirely optional: pick the table-row for your current main arena; start the evening review; let the move fire once in a heated meeting.

### 8. The gap-roadmap: ordered, typed, runnable

Scoped to the surviving form of the approach, with one rule made explicit up front: **only one gap is a gate. Everything else sharpens a program that can start immediately.**

1. **Backing-observables** *(a meaning-gap; THE gate; runnable as its own inquiry today).* What observably separates a backed display from a performed one? This one question is demanded twice over: the program needs it as the user's *self-check* ("is my cultivated bearing actually backed, or have I built a performance?") and as the *hazard-boundary* ("when does preparation cross into performance?") — and, independently, the corpus already carries it as deferred work with a stated cost (the genuine/faked distinction "currently unfalsifiable in practice"). It **gates** exactly one thing: display-targeted training. Practicing a *display* without a backing-check is practicing a performance — so signal-drills wait; state-practice (the traditions) proceeds ungated in the meantime.
2. **The catalog as trainables** *(meaning→process; gated on 1).* For each of the five signals: what does *practicing it* actually consist of? The catalog was built for reading people, not training them; the translation to exercises is real work. (Withholding needs special care — it is the double-edged signal.)
3. **Legibility's independent ground** *(a meaning-gap; ungated).* The user's strongest lever — document-shaped legibility-work — still rests on a single imported source at medium confidence, and now three parts of this program lean on it. It needs a second, independent leg (the open-source world, where a well-documented project recruits contributors without any personal magnetism, is the named test-case) — or an honest retirement.

## Reasoning

### Why this answer over the alternatives

**The two-sided audit survived because it is derivations, not opinions.** Each feared deficit ran through a specific committed instrument — Rhythm-Comfort for small talk, the one-of-four bracketing for allure, the mode-not-fluency narrowing for expression — and the audit's credibility rests on the catch that cuts *against* comfort: the over-explanation risk, found by reading the catalog against the user's surpluses. An audit that only dissolved fears would deserve suspicion; this one also names the failure the user actually risks.

**The one-move fast half survived against both alternatives.** Against a richer "vital-moments system": nothing in this project operates in real time, in-the-moment display-selection is the documented failing pattern, and aviation's answer to cognitive load is pre-commitment — one small pre-committed move is what the evidence supports. Against offering nothing for the fast half at all: the move's three-way grounding (the respect-earning "deployed" anger form; the Stoic practice; the theory's own attention-reading architecture) is committed text, and the move's second door (recovery from a caught slip) makes it genuinely load-bearing. Its effectiveness stays honestly untested.

**The priced-path form survived its own softening history.** It began, in this inquiry's middle stages, as "display-calculation is self-defeating," met the strongest counter (professional performers succeed at exactly this), and settled as a price — display recruits now and decays toward the backed — which is both truer and more persuasive than a ban, because it lets the user's own stated goal (durable leadership) do the selecting.

**The qualitative calculation survived the bait-and-switch objection** on the user's own words: "it can *feel* like a calculation" licenses precisely what is delivered — four structured decision-forms — while the numeric dream dies with its perimeter drawn (revivable only by calibration, and then only for reading people, never for conducting oneself).

### What was killed or corrected along the way

**Making the framework sender-side — killed on architecture.** One generation-stage idea removed the theory's receiver-side constraint (it models what *observers* believe, not what actors should do). Killed: that would be a different theory. Its residue survives as a clarifying line: the corrected approach *respects* receiver-sidedness — it changes the state observers read, rather than trying to write their beliefs directly.

**"The user as the corpus's N=1 calibration source" — corrected before publication** to "evidence *toward* validation": self-reported practice is not calibration by the spec's own standards. The field-test framing survives on the plain fact that the prescriptive layer had zero worked cases.

**The calculation card's audit-cell — corrected before publication.** As first drafted, the card presented the tiered audit as a fully-specified cell; the review process caught that the audit's actual question-content was never re-read during this inquiry. The cell now ships as a named pointer to where the questionnaire lives; nothing unread is paraphrased as if quoted.

**The "first week" sketch — bounded.** A week-plan inside a finding drifts toward prescription; it survived as a three-line, explicitly-optional instantiation with no imperatives.

**The run's kindest ideas were held at their grades:** the "less to unlearn" head start stays a *may*; the legibility uses stay at medium confidence with their single origin declared; the endpoint's observable is admitted to be slow.

### Contradictions reconciled across the pipeline

The statement's numeric premise ("a calculation") met the framework's no-numbers state and resolved on the user's own hedge — the qualitative reading was licensed by *"it can feel like"*. The vital-moments desire met the calibrating-performer evidence and resolved by *object*: calculate attention, never displays. And the wanting-it-feasible hope met the dive-deep license and resolved the only honest way: a yes built from limits stated out loud — which is also why the yes can be trusted.
