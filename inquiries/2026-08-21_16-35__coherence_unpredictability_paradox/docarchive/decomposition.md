### Circularity check and its resolution

A latent circularity was found at **B|C**: if P2 opens a saturation pole while P3 independently places the axis *outside* the Suite, the two verdicts contradict — the saturation pole would itself *be* the completeness axis inside g₂.

Resolved per the spec's remedy (*"an interface needs to be defined at a contract level before either piece is built"*): **I3 is a contract, not a data flow.** P2 must emit its second-pole answer in a binary form P3 consumes without renegotiation — pole **opened** (axis claimed by g₂) or pole **declined** (axis unclaimed). With the contract in place the dependency is one-way and the cycle disappears.
