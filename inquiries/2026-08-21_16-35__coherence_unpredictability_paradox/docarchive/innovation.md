### 3 · Inversion *(Framer)* — with depth-check and multi-axis check

**Depth iteration on "Coherence gates attachment":**

**Multi-axis check — existence axis.** Could the count of engagement states be **zero** rather than three? Then pursuit is belief-*acquisition* and compliance is belief held under duress. → **I-c: the output layer isn't new machinery — it's the belief-frame extended to belief *dynamics*.**

### 5 · Absence Recognition *(Generator)* — both levels mandatory, redesign-level bidirectional

**Patch-level.** Missing in the current design: a saturation pole on Coherence; an eighth Hope sub-flavor slot; a Pursuit Index; the attachment/compliance distinction. → **A-a.**

**Redesign-level · direction 1 — what's missing.** If APT were built from scratch today it would carry a **temporal/dynamics layer**: every variable and modulator would have a trajectory, not just a level. ### 6 · Domain Transfer *(Generator)* — native source mandatory

**Native source (attachment psychology — APT's own parent field).** Bowlby and Ainsworth distinguish **attachment behavior** (proximity-seeking, activated by threat, *deactivated when the caregiver is available*) from **the attachment bond** (persists regardless of whether the behavior is active). That is precisely the pursuit-vs-attachment distinction, established for sixty years, in the field APT descends from. **iteration-3.4 imported Bowlby's "working models" and did not import the behavior/bond distinction — the very thing now needed.** → **D-a.**

**Deliberately different 1 — economics.** Attachment is a **stock**; pursuit is a **flow**. Conflating them is the classic stock-flow error, and it predicts exactly the observed confusion. → **D-b.**

**Deliberately different 2 — thermodynamics / information theory.** A gradient does work; equilibrium does none. Pursuit is driven by an information gradient; when the gradient flattens (model complete) motion stops while the system still holds energy. → **D-c.**

**Deliberately different 3 — neuroscience.** Habituation: response to a constant stimulus decays; novelty restores it. Predicts that *partial re-opacity restores pursuit* — testable, and matching the folk advice. → **D-d.**

### 7 · Extrapolation *(Generator)*

**Generic.** If APT keeps adding refinements without a dynamics layer, every future anomaly involving change-over-time will present as a modulator gap. Three iterations on → a Suite bloated with pseudo-modulators that are really trajectory effects. → **E-a.**

**Focused.** Five iterations have deferred empirical validation. The structural-only method eventually hits a wall where competing structural readings cannot be adjudicated without data. **This inquiry is arguably already there** — the residual (does completeness also erode attachment?) is exactly such a case. → **E-b.**

**Contrarian.** Extrapolate the *success* case: with a dynamics layer, APT becomes predictive rather than descriptive — and Cluster 4 may then **dissolve**, because attention-allocation-over-time *is* the dynamics layer. → **E-c: Cluster 4 may not need a substrate reframe; it may need a dynamics layer.**
