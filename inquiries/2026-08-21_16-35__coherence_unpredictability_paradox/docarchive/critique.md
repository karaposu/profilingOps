### C1 · **H_d — Discovery-Hope as an eighth Hope sub-flavor**

**Prosecution — substance axis, running 3.4.1's admission test verbatim.**

*Attachment-generating:* **fails, and fails on a verbatim kill precedent.** 3.4.1:54 records:
