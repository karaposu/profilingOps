# Finding: Anger's Three-Way Routing Reconciled

## Question

APT (Attachment & Presentation Theory) models how one person becomes attached to another. It has four **variables** that generate attachment by addition — charm, hope, fear, resonance — and three **modulators** that gate the result by multiplication. Because the modulators multiply, any one of them falling to zero collapses attachment regardless of the variables. The theory also produces two outputs: a **magnitude** (how strong) and a **TYPE** (what kind), where TYPE is read off whichever variable dominated.

The prior inquiry left an unfinished problem. The phenomenon of *people with anger problems receiving more deference* had been explained three different ways at three different points, and the explanations had never been reconciled:

- **Fear-dominant TYPE** — the output classification for engagement driven mainly by fear, which the theory labels *Coerced*.
- **Coherence** (the second modulator) — failing when an observer cannot form a stable model of the person.
- **Emotional Composure** (the third modulator) — failing when someone cannot regulate their emotional state.

The user asked to go deeper into this, noting that the conflict had been logged early in the prior inquiry's pipeline and never re-tested downstream.

**The goal** was a routing adjudication: a diagnostic analysis ending either in a rule for which component handles this phenomenon, or in a demonstration that no such rule is needed. Bounded to that region of the theory — not the full modulator set, not the whole TYPE taxonomy, not empirical validation.

Two readings were held open throughout, because the statement did not settle between them: whether "three routes" was a **premise** to work from or a **hypothesis** to test. The framing came from a prior summary rather than from the user's own analysis, and surfaced evidence settled it as hypothesis.

## Finding Summary

- **The three routes were never in conflict.** They are not rival explanations — they are **coordinates of one state**, all determined simultaneously for any given case.

- **The reason they cannot compete is structural.** A TYPE is a *read-off* of the variable mix; the modulators are *gates* on the result. Adopting one does not exclude the others — it **entails** values for them. Competition was never possible.

- **The count was wrong, and understating it mattered.** There are four discriminating coordinates, not three routes. The fourth was already written in the corpus and nobody had counted it.

- **The phenomenon is three cases, not one.** Deployed anger, leaked anger, and performed anger. Each has a distinct signature, and each of the three modulators is the decisive discriminator for exactly one of them — so none is redundant.

- **Deployed anger earns standing.** It is proportionate, aimed, and it stops. All modulators stay intact, and the fear signal is highly specific. This is the case the observation is about.

- **Leaked anger earns avoidance.** Emotional Composure collapses, producing the reports the theory already predicts — *"exhausting to be around."*

- **Performed anger earns nothing, and it is new.** Theatrical rage without genuine provocation fails Coherence's *alignment* aspect: the surface does not match the depth. This is the *"he's putting it on"* case, and finding it filled the only coordinate that had no value.

- **The real discriminator between respected and unrespected anger was already in your corpus, in its oldest file.** `new_apt_layer.md` line 58 states that fear under low Self-Positioning reads as bluster and fails to consolidate into respect. It was written before Coherence and Emotional Composure existed, and it survives intact.

- **That rule needed translating, not repairing.** "Low Self-Positioning" already means Supplication in the current architecture, so restating it in species terms is a derivation from two committed texts — not a change to either.

- **The most surprising result comes from a factor nobody had applied here.** Signal Specificity scales every variable's effective magnitude. The person angry at *everything* emits anger that could be sent to anyone — which is the theory's literal definition of a **generic** signal. That is why they earn contempt, and no modulator-based account predicts it.

- **Above the local answer sits a general one.** The corpus contains no routing rules anywhere. Thirteen iterations built components without ever needing to relate them; this is the first inquiry that did.

- **Left open, and it sits underneath everything:** Fear is defined two incompatible ways across the corpus. The routing here declares which reading it uses rather than settling the question.

## Finding

### Why this question existed at all

The prior inquiry answered a question about Coherence and, in passing, explained the anger observation three different ways — once through the Coerced TYPE, once through Coherence, once through Emotional Composure. Each explanation was locally correct. Nobody checked whether they were consistent with each other.

That is a normal thing to happen in a theory built one component at a time, and it is worth saying plainly why: **for thirteen iterations, every inquiry addressed a single component.** No inquiry ever had to say how components relate for a particular real-world case. This is the first one that did, and the confusion it exposed is a consequence of that history rather than a defect in any component.

### 1. Why the three explanations cannot compete

The decisive fact is that the three things being compared are not the same *kind* of thing.

**A TYPE is a read-off.** Iteration-3.2.1 of the theory states it directly: TYPE *"is determined by variable mix inside f … Because f is additive, TYPE reads directly from the variable-weight distribution — **no separate mechanism is needed**."* Fear-dominant TYPE is not a mechanism that could compete with anything. It is a label for the observation that fear carried the largest share.

**The modulators are gates.** Coherence and Emotional Composure multiply the result. They are a different position in the same computation.

Competition requires that adopting one explanation *excludes* another. Here, adopting one **entails** values for the others. Every case has a fear value, a charm value, a Self-Positioning value, a Coherence value, an Emotional Composure value, and a TYPE — all at once. There is nothing to arbitrate.

A simple test makes this concrete. Ban the word "route" and try to state the problem: *"for this phenomenon, fear and charm take values on the additive side; the three modulators take values; the TYPE is read off."* Nothing is lost. The rivalry was carried by the vocabulary, not by the structure.

### 2. The phenomenon is three cases

"The anger phenomenon" is not one thing, and the user's own sentence contains more than one of them — *"anger spikes **issues**"* describes one case, *"treated a lot more **respect**"* describes another.

**Deployed anger** is proportionate, aimed at a specific thing, and it terminates when its point is made. It does not spill onto bystanders.

**Leaked anger** is disproportionate, untargeted, lingering, and contagious.

**Performed anger** is theatrical — rage produced as a tactic, without genuine provocation behind it.

The third case was not in the original framing. It was found by taking a principle the theory already states about messages and applying it to affect. Iteration-3.2.1 says that writing a *"specific-seeming"* message without genuine engagement produces **Coherence failure**, because *"the structure appears engaged but lacks the [substance] that only engagement produces."* Performed anger has exactly that shape: it appears engaged and lacks the substance. That is a failure of Coherence's **alignment** aspect — surface not matching depth.

Finding it mattered for a specific reason. Coherence was the one component with no value in the account; every attempt to fill it had come up empty. Performed anger is its value.

### 3. The classification table

| | **Deployed** | **Leaked** | **Performed** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Self-Positioning** | Mission/Task-Focus — attention on the standard | **Supplication** — demanding a reaction | ambiguous |
| **Coherence (alignment)** | intact — the anger is real | intact on alignment | **FAILS** — surface ≠ depth |
| **Emotional Composure** | intact — fast recovery | **COLLAPSES** — Contagion-Drain | intact — the performer is in full control |
| **Fear × Specificity** | high — aimed at this, now | **low** if angry at everything | variable |
| **What the observer reports** | standing, deference | *"exhausting to be around"* | *"he's putting it on"* |

Read down the columns and something falls out that answers the original question better than any adjudication could: **each modulator is the decisive discriminator for exactly one case.** Emotional Composure separates leaked from the rest. Coherence's alignment aspect separates performed from the rest. Self-Positioning separates deployed from leaked. Specificity separates aimed anger from indiscriminate anger.

**No component is redundant, and none is in competition with another.** That is the reconciliation the inquiry was asked for.

One cell is honest rather than complete: Self-Positioning's value for performed anger reads *ambiguous*, because a performer's attention-direction has not been examined. It is recorded as ambiguous rather than guessed.

### 4. The rule that was already there

The single most accurate sentence in the corpus about this phenomenon is in its oldest file. `BASEFILES/profiling_data_extraction/pragma/core/new_apt_layer.md`, line 58:

> *"**Fear** under low Self-Positioning reads as **bluster** → fails to consolidate into **respect**."*

It was written in April, before Coherence and Emotional Composure existed as modulators, and it uses the observation's own word — *respect*. Read as a two-sided rule: fear plus low Self-Positioning gives bluster; fear plus intact Self-Positioning gives respect.

**It needed translating, not repairing.** "Low Self-Positioning" sounds like a magnitude, and an earlier stage of this inquiry proposed rewriting it as a claim about *species* instead — treating that as a repair of the older spec. Checking the source showed the repair was unnecessary, because iteration-3 already states the equivalence: *"**Any species of non-extractive attention produces `g(high)`** … **Supplication produces `g(low)`**."*

So "low Self-Positioning" already means Supplication. The translation is a derivation from two committed texts, not a change to either. This distinction is worth being careful about: calling it a repair would imply the older spec was wrong, and it was not.

The translated rule reads: **fear plus Supplication gives bluster; fear plus Task-Focus gives respect.**

One objection deserves answering, because it looks fatal. Is an exploding person really *supplicating*? Supplication is usually glossed as approval-harvesting, and someone demanding fear-compliance is not seeking approval. But the axis is defined more broadly than the gloss — `new_apt_layer.md` line 62 describes it as *"attention that is NOT oriented toward harvesting approval / validation / **response** from the current counterparty."* **Response** is named. An outburst that gets no reaction has failed on its own terms. It is harvesting a response, which is what makes it extractive.

### 5. Why the person angry at everything earns contempt

This is the result that no account based on the modulators predicts, and it comes from a factor already in the theory.

**Signal Specificity** scales how much of a variable actually lands. Iteration-3.2.1 states it generally: *"**Each variable's** effective magnitude is scaled by the specificity of the signal carrying it."* It defines a **generic** signal as one where *"the same content could be sent, with trivial modification, to any counterparty in the same category."*

Apply that literally to anger. A person who explodes identically at everyone emits anger that could be sent, with trivial modification, to anyone. That is a generic signal by the theory's own definition — and the theory even supplies the detection cue, noting that *"generic signals detected by high template-match probability."* Identical outbursts are maximal template-match.

So the perpetually angry person's fear signal is scaled down toward zero before any modulator is consulted. They generate little, and what they generate does not consolidate.

**This costs nothing architecturally.** Iteration-3.2.1 is explicit that *"Specificity is not a 5th attachment variable … It is a quality of how existing variables are expressed."* Nothing is being added — an existing quality-factor is being applied to a variable it was always defined to cover.

### 6. What "respect" actually denotes

The observation is stated in terms of respect, and respect is not one outcome.

The theory's TYPE taxonomy already separates them. Charm-dominant engagement is typed **Status/admiration** and weakens as access recedes. Fear-dominant engagement is typed **Coerced** and *"reverses (relief, not attachment) on threat removal."* Different persistence under change is a structural difference, not a matter of how something feels.

The respected volatile figure plausibly generates both at once — deference from fear, and admiration from charm. Charm has two paths in. One is **social-norm violation**, which iteration-3 lists as an adjacent mechanism whose effect is that *"I'm not bound by what binds ordinary people"* signals high status. The other is **competence display**: staying controlled under provocation reads as capability, and charm tracks what someone *is*.

This matters for the routing, because it means fear alone under-explains the case.

### 7. The general finding

**The corpus has no routing rules anywhere.** No finding and no spec states which component a presenting phenomenon involves. Every routing so far has been done inside individual arguments.

What is missing is not a decision procedure — the whole result of this inquiry is that no decision is needed — but an **index**: a mapping from a presenting phenomenon to the values every component takes for it.

And that index partly exists already. Iteration-3 maps five phenomena to component-sets by hand: *"Narcissist-followers (Self-Focus + intermittent reinforcement + grandiosity + trauma bonding)," "Cults," "Dictators / demagogues (Self-Focus or Mission-Focus + grandiosity + social-norm violation + arbitrary power)," "Parasocial celebrity," "Pathological boss-worship."* That is exactly the form, hand-curated, never named as one. Five cases in one region is not a general index — but it is the right shape, and generalising a shape is much cheaper than inventing one.

Building the general index is **deliberately not done here.** The corpus has no empirical calibration, and generalising from one worked case would over-reach.

### 8. The Fear reading this routing assumes

Fear is defined two incompatible ways across the corpus. Iteration-3.4 gives *"this person can harm me / poses threat."* The older spec and `apt_missing_dimension` give *"consequences of losing you. Power dynamics, secrets, dependency."*

Threat-of-harm and cost-of-loss are different beliefs, and they come apart cleanly — someone can be terrifying to lose and incapable of harming you, or the reverse.

**This routing assumes the threat-of-harm reading.** It is declared rather than settled, because settling it is a separate inquiry and doing it as a side-effect of an anger question would be the wrong place. Every verdict above is conditional on that declaration.

### 9. On the iteration label

This finding adds no variable, no modulator, and no formula element. It applies existing components to a phenomenon and promotes one existing sentence. That is clarification class.

One complication is worth flagging rather than resolving: iteration-3.4.1 designated the `3.4.x` numbering for **variable sub-taxonomies** specifically, naming `3.4.3` as the Fear sub-flavour slot. The prior inquiry also proposed `3.4.3` for itself. The numbering convention has a collision, and it should be settled deliberately rather than by whichever inquiry ships first.

Because orthogonality is theoretical, all three modulators are simultaneously determinable for one case — which is exactly what makes the classification table possible. **5. Intermittent reinforcement and trauma bonding are scoped outside APT.**
- **Source:** `inquiries/self_focus_generalization/finding.md` (iteration-3).

**Pattern note.** Three commitments confirmed outright and two confirmed-with-frame-revision. The re-test protocol notes that an all-confirmed pattern is a soft signal of unchallenged inheritance. Worth recording that real challenges did land, just not on the five: the inquiry's own inherited framing (three routes) was refuted on the count, one candidate's change-class was overturned before publication, and a third case was found that none of the five commitments anticipated.

## Reasoning

### What survived and why

**The coordinates-not-rivals verdict survived** because it is forced by the formula's structure rather than chosen. A read-off of the additive side and a multiplier on the whole product cannot compete, since adopting one entails values for the others. The strongest objection — that the user asked for reconciliation and "they were never in conflict" reads like declining the work — was answered by the classification table, which performs the reconciliation concretely rather than dissolving it.

**Signal Specificity survived and is the strongest single output.** The objection was that specificity is defined for messages and stretching it to affect displays is equivocation. It fails on the text: the scope is stated as *"each variable's"* effective magnitude, and the generic-signal definition applies to indiscriminate anger with no modification at all.

**Performed anger survived** two objections. Against the charge that it is a degenerate form of leaked anger: the two are *opposite* on Emotional Composure, since a performer is in full control and leaked anger is defined by lost control. Against the charge that it is just the calm-liar case the theory already covers: that case is composed and incoherent with no anger present, while this one is angry, composed, and incoherent — a different cell.

**The bluster rule's translation survived, with its change-class corrected.** The objection that an exploder is not *supplicating* — since supplication is glossed as approval-harvesting — fails because the axis is defined as attention harvesting *"approval / validation / response,"* and response is named explicitly.

### What was killed and why

**A fourth engagement state called "avoidance" — killed.** Proposed to name what happens when an observer leaves. It fails because the theory's Contagion-Drain signature describes attachment *discounting or collapsing*, which is the zero case, not a fourth state with its own persistence profile. The seed survives as a recommendation to state the layer's null case explicitly.

**A relational-state modulator class — killed.** Proposed so that deference could be modelled as a property of the relationship rather than of the sender. It contradicts iteration-3.4's belief-frame directly, in which every modulator is a receiver-side stance-belief *about the sender*. That commitment was made deliberately.

**"Anger operates at the receiver-state pre-condition layer" — refined rather than killed.** Pushed to its root, one line of reasoning found that anger *compels* attention, which is an effect on the precondition for attachment rather than on any variable. The strong version over-reaches, because iteration-3.3 placed that layer outside formula scope and the theory plainly does model this phenomenon through Fear. The surviving version is an observation: anger has an attention-capture effect *in addition to* its Fear contribution.

**"The discriminator is entirely on the additive side" — refined.** Emotional Composure demonstrably discriminates, so the strong claim fails. The surviving version is that discrimination is *distributed*, which is what the table shows.

### The correction that had to be made mid-inquiry

An earlier stage classified the bluster rule's translation as a **repair** of the older spec, reasoning that it converts "low Self-Positioning" from a magnitude into a species claim. Checking the source showed that the corpus already equates the two, so nothing was being changed. The classification was corrected to a **derivation** before publication.

This is small but not trivial. A repair implies the older text was wrong. It was not, and the finding would have misstated the corpus's own history.

### The claim this inquiry deliberately did not settle

Fear's two definitions are incompatible, and the routing above uses one of them. Settling it inside an anger inquiry would be the wrong place — the divergence spans files of different vintage and affects every use of the variable. The routing declares its reading instead, and the conditionality is stated in the finding rather than hidden inside it.

## Open Questions

### Monitoring

- Whether the classification table's cells hold up as real cases are classified against it. The cell most likely to move is Self-Positioning's value for performed anger, currently recorded as ambiguous. Observable after ten or more real cases have been classified.

### Blocked

- Whether performed anger is genuinely distinct from leaked anger. Blocked on the testing action listed under MUST. Until it resolves, the third column of the table is derived rather than observed, and everything that depends on the table inherits that.

- Which reading of Fear the theory should commit to — threat-of-harm or cost-of-loss. Blocked on a dedicated inquiry. This sits underneath the whole routing.

### Research Frontiers

- **Whether cross-component routing is a capability the theory should have.** Thirteen iterations built components without relating them. Naming the index artifact type is a cheap first step; whether a general capability is warranted is a separate and larger question.

- **Whether a single interaction can supply evidence about temporal properties.** Two of the three cases are distinguished partly by properties that unfold over time — whether the anger *terminates*, how fast recovery is. If one conversation cannot establish these, the criteria need a longitudinal input the project may not have.

- **Whether observers can distinguish the three cases prospectively or only in retrospect.** The distinction turns on inferred intent, and the observer usually sees the anger without seeing what provoked it. Retrospect-only would make the criteria diagnostic rather than predictive, which is a materially weaker claim.

### Refinement Triggers

- **The classification table re-opens** if performed anger fails its test against real cases, since one of its three columns would collapse.

- **The Specificity application re-opens** if iteration-3.2.1's specificity formula is ever narrowed from *"each variable's"* to a subset of variables. The specific blocking feature is the formula's general scope; re-open on its narrowing, not on general reconsideration.

- **The distributed-discrimination claim re-opens** if a fourth discriminating coordinate is found, since the claim is a count as well as a shape.

- **The adjacent-mechanism scoping re-opens** if social-norm violation is adjudicated into Charm, since anger-deference would then draw one of its two Charm paths from inside the theory rather than from the adjacent list.

- **The iteration-label question re-opens** whenever the next `3.4.x` clarification ships, since the numbering was reserved for variable sub-taxonomies and two inquiries have now proposed `3.4.3`.
