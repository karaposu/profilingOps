So Emotional Composure is not mis-assigned; it is the coordinate that **discriminates the two sub-cases**, and its signature correctly predicts that one of them earns no respect.

**Confidence: HIGH.** The resolution is forced by Emotional Composure's own aspect structure, particularly recovery speed, rather than by appeal to the spec's authority.

**Resolution.** Emotional Composure stays in the set, with a corrected role: not a rival explanation but the **sub-case discriminator**. Warm's conflict C2 is resolved, not by removing g₃, but by finding what it actually discriminates.

**Now fixed:** deployed anger leaves g₃ intact; leaked anger collapses it. **No longer allowed:** treating all anger as an Emotional Composure failure. **Depends on this:** the two-sub-case structure of the whole answer. **Changed in the model:** g₃ moves from suspect member to load-bearing discriminator.
