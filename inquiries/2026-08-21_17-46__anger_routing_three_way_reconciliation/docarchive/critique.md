## Phase 2/3 — Adversarial Evaluation and Verdicts

### C1 · The position account — *co-determined coordinates, not competing routes*

**Prosecution.** *Multi-axis, user-perspective:* the user asked for the routes to be **reconciled**. An answer of the form *"they were never in conflict"* can read as dissolving the question rather than answering it — the same shape as declining the work. *Specification-gap probe:* if they are coordinates, what tells an analyst which coordinate values a case has? Without that, the account is unusable.

**Defense.** The dissolution is forced by the formula's own structure, not chosen for convenience: iteration-3.2.1 states TYPE *"reads directly from the variable-weight distribution — no separate mechanism is needed."* A read-off and a multiplier cannot compete because adopting one **entails** values for the others. And the specification gap is answered downstream — the three-coordinate signature (K1 + K2 + K4) is exactly the missing determination mechanism.

**Collision.** Defense wins on D1, D2, D6, D11. The user-perspective objection is real but answerable: the account does not decline the reconciliation, it performs it and finds the components were never rivals — and it now delivers a usable discrimination procedure alongside.

> **Verdict — SURVIVE.** No caveats on critical dimensions.

### C2 · **K4 — part of the deference effect is Signal Specificity**

**Prosecution — substance axis, run literally.** Does 3.2.1's specificity concept actually apply to an angry outburst? Its definition is *"a signal is **specific** if it contains information only obtainable from genuine engagement with this particular counterparty; **generic** if the same content could be sent, with trivial modification, to any counterparty in the same category."* That is a definition about **messages**, and stretching it to affect displays may be equivocation.

**Defense — external anchor, verbatim.** Two lines settle it. `attachment_variable_interactions/finding.md:29` — *"**Each variable's** effective magnitude is scaled by the specificity of the signal carrying it"* — the scope is every variable, Fear included, stated generally. And the generic-signal definition applies to anger with no stretching at all: the person **angry at everything** emits anger that *could be sent, with trivial modification, to any counterparty* — literally the definition of generic. 3.2.1 even supplies the detection cue: *"generic signals detected by high template-match probability."* An identical outburst at everyone is maximal template-match.

Further, `finding.md:37`: *"Specificity is not a 5th attachment variable … It is a quality of how existing variables are expressed."* So K4 proposes **no new element** and carries **no Suite-entry burden** — it applies an existing quality-factor to a variable it was always defined to cover.

**Collision.** Defense wins decisively on D11 and D7. The equivocation charge fails because the corpus's own definition covers the case without modification.

> **Verdict — SURVIVE, and it is the strongest single output of this run.** It explains a case no gate-based account predicts — that the perpetually angry person earns contempt rather than deference — and it does so from committed text.

### C3 · **P2's claim that g₁ is *the* discriminator** *(Innovation's re-test trigger, adjudicated)*

**Prosecution.** C2 establishes a discriminating coordinate on the `f` side. FP3's prosecution establishes another on g₂. The claim that g₁ is *the* discriminator is therefore over-concentrated — it names one of at least three.

**Defense.** g₁ remains the discriminator for the specific contrast the phenomenon is usually stated as — respected volatile person versus unrespected exploder — and the corpus's only existing rule on the matter is a g₁ rule.

**Collision.** Defense holds the narrow claim; prosecution kills the strong one. *Purpose-fitness:* left as-is, would the account still do its job? Partly — it would discriminate the headline contrast but misclassify both the angry-at-everything case and the performed-anger case. That is not sufficient. *Can the frame absorb the fix?* Yes — weaken "the" to "one of."

> **Verdict — REFINE.** **Target:** state discrimination as **distributed across four coordinates**, with g₁ carrying the headline contrast. What "right" looks like on D4: every sub-case is classified by the account, not just the two originally named.

### C4 · **K1 — restating the bluster rule in Self-Positioning species terms**

**Prosecution — the intervention-shape axis (D12).** Innovation classified this as a **REPAIR** of `new_apt_layer.md:58`, on the reasoning that it *"converts 'low Self-Positioning' from a magnitude into a species claim."* That reasoning assumes the original meant a magnitude. **It did not.** `self_focus_generalization/finding.md:73` states verbatim:

> *"**Any species of non-extractive attention produces `g(high)`**, amplifying the attachment variables. **Supplication produces `g(low)`**, collapsing them."*

The corpus already equates `g(low)` with Supplication. So "low Self-Positioning" *was already* a species claim, and K1 is not changing the rule's semantics — it is **deriving** a translation from two committed texts. **Innovation's shape verdict is wrong.**

**Second prosecution — substance.** Is an exploder really *supplicating*? iteration-3's gloss is *"approval-harvesting,"* and demanding fear-compliance is not seeking approval. **Rebutted on verbatim text:** `new_apt_layer.md:62` defines the axis as *"attention that is NOT oriented toward harvesting approval / validation / **response** from the current counterparty."* **Response** is named explicitly. An outburst that gets no reaction has failed on its own terms; it harvests a response. Supplication holds.

**Defense.** The content is not merely intact — it is **better** supported than Innovation claimed, because it is a derivation from two committed texts rather than a proposed change to one.

**Collision.** Content survives and strengthens; the shape classification dies.

> **Verdict — SURVIVE on content · REFINE on shape.** **Target on D12:** reclassify from **REPAIR** to **ADD-CONTENT** — making an equivalence the corpus already commits explicit for this phenomenon. This matters beyond bookkeeping: a REPAIR would imply the older spec was wrong, and it was not.

### C6 · **The third sub-case — performed anger** *(emerged from the FP3 prosecution)*

**Collision.** Defense wins on D1, D4, D2. It also closes the gap Innovation's per-coordinate trace flagged: **g₂ was the under-worked coordinate, and this is its value.**
