### I5 — the Fear-reading contract *(latent circularity, found and removed)*

**The problem.** P2 must route a phenomenon through Fear. Fear is defined two incompatible ways across the corpus (threat-of-harm versus cost-of-loss), and the two readings give the phenomenon different weights. The resolution of that divergence lives in P5 as an OPEN item — but P5 runs last. P2 would therefore be waiting on an input that never arrives, and P5 would be reporting on a routing already performed. **That is circular.**

**The removal.** Convert the dependency into a contract fixed before P2 runs:

> **P2 declares its Fear-reading explicitly and routes under it. P5 records the routing as conditional on that declaration, and records the divergence as OPEN.**

Neither piece waits on the other. P2 gets a stated premise instead of a resolution; P5 gets a declared premise instead of a verdict to produce. The conditionality becomes visible in the finding rather than hidden inside the routing.
