## Phase 2 — Generate

### 1 · Lens Shifting *(Framer)*

**Generic — the diagnostician's lens.** A theorist asks *"which component is responsible?"* A diagnostician asks *"what do all the readings say?"* and treats the **pattern** of readings as the diagnosis. → **L-a: "which route" is a theorist's question; the practical operation is a reading-vector, and no arbitration step exists in it.**

**Focused — the sender's lens.** APT is receiver-side by construction. Viewed from the sender, deployed versus leaked is a **skill** difference — the deployed-anger person has learned to modulate. Skill is competence, and competence is what Charm tracks. → **L-b: a second Charm path — deployed anger reads as competence-display, independent of norm-violation.**

**Contrarian — anger is not a variable.** What if anger is a **delivery mode** and what varies is what is being delivered (threat, standard, status, distress)? APT has two domains — Attachment and Presentation — and delivery modes live in the second. → **L-c: anger may be a Domain-2 presentation phenomenon whose Domain-1 effect is what everyone has been trying to route.**

### 2 · Combination *(Generator)*

**Generic — deployed/leaked × the engagement-state layer.** The prior finding names attachment, pursuit, and compliance. Deployed anger produces compliance, stable while the threat stands; leaked anger produces the observer leaving. → **C-a: avoidance as a fourth engagement state.**

**Focused — the bluster rule × Self-Positioning's species taxonomy.** `new_apt_layer.md:58` says *Fear under **low** Self-Positioning reads as bluster*. But Self-Positioning is not a magnitude in the current architecture — it is a **genus with species** (Self-Focus, Caregiver-Focus, Mission/Task-Focus; negative pole Supplication). Deployed anger is attention on a standard → **Mission/Task-Focus**. Leaked anger demands a reaction → **Supplication**. → **C-b: restate the rule in species terms — Fear × Supplication = bluster; Fear × Task-Focus = respect.**

**Contrarian — anger × Signal Specificity × θ(context).** Specificity is a magnitude multiplier (`effective_magnitude = nominal × specificity`). Anger is *maximally* specific — always at a particular person about a particular thing. And the contrast case is decisive: the person who is **angry at everything** has near-zero specificity and earns contempt, not deference. → **C-c: part of anger's deference effect is a Specificity effect, not a Fear effect.**

**Depth iteration on "the modulators gate Fear":**

**Multi-axis — identity axis.** What does deference fundamentally consist of? **Behaviour-suppression in someone's presence.** That is inhibition, not approach — and APT models attachment as approach. → **I-c: deference is inhibitory; the theory's motivational direction may not fit it.**
