## Sense Versions

**SV1 — the avoidance reading (unstable).** "Confrontation is dangerous; delay all asks." Fails on contact: the corpus *commits* a deliberate forcing instrument (A6), the orbiter bound prices indefinite delay (A12), and ambiguity has its own receiver-costs (the unresolved "what is this?" discomfort). Rejected as a format; retained as the delay-pole of a two-sided discipline.

**SV2 — the mechanism: the ask as a sampling-and-conversion event.** The pre-verdict state is *additive*: the receiver accumulates property-beliefs (interesting, valuable, attractive, useful) while holding **no stance** — nothing demands one, so evidence flows unfiltered. A verdict-forcing move does three things at once:

1. **Samples.** It reads the accumulation *at this moment* — collapsing a trajectory into a point-verdict. Sub-threshold accumulation returns "no" **even on a rising curve**: the no reads "not enough yet" as "no."
2. **Converts.** It demands a stance-belief ("what will I do about this?"), and stances **gate** (A1): post-verdict, subsequent evidence arrives pre-filtered through the declared category ("someone I turned down" processes new displays differently than "someone I'm still forming a view on"). The ask converts an additive process into a gated one.
3. **Records.** Spoken aloud, the verdict enters two ledgers — self-coherence (A3) and the social record (A4) — and reversal now costs consistency on both.

And the asymmetry the user observed (no→yes hard; yes→no easy) has a clean mechanical ground: **a yes stays under test** (the relationship keeps generating evidence and keeps re-pricing), while **a no terminates the evidence-stream** — no more demos, no more conversations, nothing arrives to update it. *The no seals itself by shutting off the very input channel that could reverse it.* Crystallization is therefore mostly **evidential starvation**, secondarily consistency-defense. Every part of this is committed BDA machinery; nothing was imported.

**SV3 — what exactly is irreversible (the grading).** Three objects, three answers:

- **The pre-ask ambiguity: fully irreversible.** Once asked, "we're just talking" is dead — the question now exists for both parties and cannot be unasked. This is the true content of the user's "non reversable."
- **The declared stance: sticky, revisable only via question-change.** This *explains* the Return Doctrine rather than contradicting it: "returning with something new" works because it poses a **new question the old no never answered** — consistency is not violated, so the verdict is cheap to form fresh. Unchanged re-asks fail twice over (correlated evidence updates nothing; consistency-defense actively resists). The yes-ladder is this mechanism weaponized — consistency-pressure exploited toward an unearned macro-yes — which is exactly what the honest form (each ask carried by its own evidence) is not.
- **The frame: damaged by pressure, not by existence.** A light ask with a genuinely cheap no leaves the frame intact (the clean-exit machinery); a heavy or pressed ask converts the frame itself ("the thing where he wants something"), and every later interaction is read through it.

**SV4 — the two-sided timing doctrine.** The lever is the ask's existence, weight, and moment (A5). Its rules:

- **Carrying-capacity (A10 generalized):** ask-weight ≤ accumulated evidence. Weight = the stance demanded (a coffee < exclusivity; a Tuesday demo < a purchase; hearing the pitch < a public commitment). Under-capacity asks manufacture no's by sampling below threshold.
- **Actionability:** the ask samples *belief × actionability*. Even high accumulation returns no when a yes cannot be enacted (her standing relationship; their frozen budget; the decision-maker absent). **Asking when a yes is unactable converts "not actable now" into a declared no — the worst trade available.** This is the anchor case's exact structure, and the no's content ("I have a bf") names the actionability-blocker, not the person's value.
- **The delay bound (A12):** delay is legitimate only as *readiness-waiting* (rising trajectory + a planned verdict-moment) and is priced by the asker's own budget and horizon. Indefinite orbit is chasing with patience; and past some accumulation, *non*-asking becomes its own pressure (the unresolved frame's discomfort).
- **The sender-side deferral (A9):** the reader's rule "insufficient window → defer the verdict" mirrors exactly: insufficient accumulation → don't demand their verdict. One discipline, two directions.
- **Redirect moves (the third option):** when a verdict-moment arrives too early — incoming ask, situational forcing, the room demanding a binary — it can be *converted* rather than answered or forced: **downgrade** the question's weight ("not the whole thing — can we agree on just X?"), **split** it (partial, carried commitments), **defer with a date** (a scheduled verdict is not an evaded one), **reframe** to the question the evidence can carry. This is the user's leader-hypothesis grounded: at group scale the leader is the room's *verdict-timer* — preventing premature public binaries (A15 makes a room's declared no maximally sticky), staging commitment as capacity-carried asks, never as consistency-exploitation (the anti-yes-ladder bound, A14).
- **The terminal forcing (A6):** at pursuit-end the doctrine *inverts deliberately*: when the goal flips from protecting growth to pricing your own exit, crystallization becomes the feature — the ask-the-no forces the typed verdict precisely to free the asker's budget. The full arc: **protect ambiguity while accumulation rises → ask when capacity carries the weight and a yes is actable → force the verdict when you need your budget back.**

**SV5 — membership: BDA-internal dynamics, prescriptive doctrine.** The adjudication: (a) the mechanism is assembled *entirely* from committed machinery — property/stance beliefs, gating, thresholds, consistency-accrual, receiver-side evidence dynamics; zero imports. The phenomenon is **the temporal face of the stance-gate**: BDA already commits that stances gate; this account supplies *when stances form* and that asks (and situations) force formation. (b) The separate-tactics-layer alternative fails on a structural fact: verdict-forcing exists whether or not anyone strategizes — situations force stances with no asker present — so it is belief-dynamics, not optional tactics. (c) The prescriptive residue (when to ask) lands in `60` like all conduct guidance. Consequences: the concept enters the theory as **verdict-forcing / stance-crystallization** — dynamics and signals, no new element, no new variable; it registers as a customer of the static/dynamics frontier (FR2) — a receiver-side irreducibly-temporal account, joining the sender-side one the insistence inquiry logged. And the user's closing intuition is confirmed structurally: this *matters for attachment* because the stance-gate is one of BDA's gates — **verdict-timing is the trigger-timing of a gate**; mistimed, it closes the gate at zero before the variables ever finish accumulating.

**SV6 — the stable account, with case verdicts.**

1. **Concept:** a verdict-forcing move demands a stance now (offer / declaration / status-question; move- or situation-sourced). Pre-verdict, accumulation is additive and unfiltered.
2. **Mechanism:** the ask samples the trajectory at a point, converts additive→gated, and (spoken) records the verdict on two ledgers; the no then seals itself by evidential starvation plus consistency-defense. (SV2)
3. **Irreversibility, graded:** ambiguity unrestorable; stance revisable only through question-change (which is why the Return Doctrine works and re-asks fail); frame damaged by pressure, not existence. (SV3)
4. **Doctrine:** carrying-capacity + actionability before the ask exists; readiness-waiting not avoidance; redirect moves for premature verdict-moments; deliberate terminal forcing at pursuit-end; consent-gate untouched throughout (once asked, the no stays cheap). (SV4)
5. **Membership:** BDA-internal dynamics (the stance-gate's temporal face) + `60`-layer doctrine; FR2 customer. (SV5)
6. **Case — the bad-relationship approach:** her accumulation may be rising, but any yes is unactable, so an intent-declaration forces the worst trade (unactable-now → declared no, socially recorded, evidence-stream cut). The non-declared path — genuine conversation *within the ambient frame*, no engineered escalation — keeps accumulation additive while her situation evolves on her own clock; if her relationship ends by her own process, her question becomes askable (and her unprompted re-engagement is the corpus's own positive signal). **Principle 4's verdict: right-but-fused.** The pretext-prohibition stands absolutely. The direct-light declaration also stands *in the cold-approach context it was written for* — there, the interruption itself already demands an account ("why are you talking to me?"), so intent-clarity is frame-required and the lightest true frame is the cheapest available ask; covert cold-approaching is what creates the puzzle. The revision the account demands is a **scope-note plus a general timing principle for ambient frames** (acquaintance, colleague, regular, friend-of-friend), where no account is demanded, silence is frame-appropriate, and the declaration is a *chosen verdict-moment* governed by capacity × actionability. **The ethics line:** protecting *her* verdict-timing (not forcing a premature stance) is honest; protecting *your deniability* while extracting investment — engineered intimacy that dodges every clarifying moment she initiates, pressure on her relationship, manufactured escalation — is the covert operation the corpus already prohibits. The test: **whose verdict-timing is being protected?** And the orbiter bound stays live: his budget and horizon are finite; a patience without limits is chasing.
7. **Case — sales ask-timing:** the purchase-ask should exist when the evidence carries a purchase-stance (the demo happened, on their case; objections explored) *and* a yes is actable (budget, authority present). Until then, **communicate-not-sell is the additive mode** — showing builds capacity without demanding a stance; asking converts. The walk-in's ask-sizing is the capacity rule already half-applied: first contact earns a Tuesday-demo ask, not a purchase-verdict. "When to talk about the product" answers itself: the product *enters* whenever its entry demands no stance (a demo is additive); the *ask* is placed at capacity-peak, sized down under uncertainty — and the deflection-read (never-registered no) plus ask-the-no's exit forms already guard both ends.
8. **Leader:** the confrontation-redirect hypothesis is confirmed as the group-scale timing role — prevent premature public binaries, downgrade/split/defer-with-date/reframe, stage capacity-carried commitments, never consistency-exploitation.
