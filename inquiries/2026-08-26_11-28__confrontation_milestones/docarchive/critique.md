**Collision:** both prosecution points land as *ordering corrections*: SV2 had starvation primary ("mostly") — wrong; **gating is primary, starvation compounds it when contact ends.** And the conversion claim needs the sources-of-crystallization sentence (ask / situation / spontaneous / default).
**Verdict: REFINE** — mechanism survives with the ordering fixed and the sources stated.
