## Sense Versions

**SV1 — the folk reading (unstable).** "Confidence comes from success" / "born leaders." No mechanism, no path-structure, and the vocabulary imports the excluded literature. Rejected as format; the true fragments re-enter mechanized.

**SV2 — the reflexivity adjudication: the machinery transfers, with one disanalogy.** The self-frame question — *"am I someone whose direction others should take, in domain D?"* — is a frame-question, and both components the crystallization machinery needs already exist reflexively in the corpus: **the reader** (the first-person conduct-read — the self observing its own record, committed by the backing finding's self-check and the calculation program's introspection-distrust) and **the streams** (one's own conduct-record; others' conduct toward oneself). So formation is stance-crystallization on the self-frame:

- **Self-verdicts:** a grant is a verdict-demand on the self ("will I be this?") — acceptance adopts the claim in one recorded event; declining is a micro-declaration of "not me."
- **Micro-declarations:** everyday conduct acts on a self-label — deferring ("you decide"), taking the head of the table, how one introduces oneself — grains into one's own ledger *and* the audience's.
- **Deceleration and the silent threshold:** same-shaped evidence asymptotes; accumulation-led claims cross **silently** — people *discover* they hold the claim when a challenge samples it ("I noticed I'd stopped asking permission").
- **Default crystallization and liveness:** never claiming while occasions pass settles "not-me"; the question "could I be that?" runs open → closed → **no longer a question**. This is non-formation's mechanism — and it is formation, of the other claim.

**The one disanalogy — the bias-term:** unlike the pair-case, the reflexive reader has stakes in the verdict, and the corpus already commits the failure (motivated blindness). The self-ledger is read through a **reading-bias parameter**, and the correctives are structural: (i) conduct-anchored self-reads (*what did I do*, never *how do I feel about myself*); (ii) **the outside-stream as calibration** — grants received or withheld, deference expressed or absent, read as evidence the way one would read them about a stranger; (iii) the **scope-pin** — the claim's scope tied to the record, not the self-feeling.

**SV3 — the paths as evidence-source profiles (and the admission test run).**

- **Grant-led (event-shaped):** an external verdict-event offered; acceptance adopts the claim in one step, publicly and internally recorded. Fast — and **front-loaded**: the claim exists ahead of the personal record; the office's term-cover carries the new appointee while their ledger fills. Its failure case is *grant-without-conversion* (the promoted-beyond-backing chair-dependent claim). Its clean case includes **de-formation**: Cincinnatus adopts scoped to the crisis and genuinely releases — claims can be laid down; and *revocation* is real (the fired executive's office-dependent claim collapses with the office unless accumulation caught up).
- **Accumulation-led (gradual):** wins, roles held, deference received accrete; deceleration stabilizes; the threshold crosses silently. Slow and **self-backed by construction** (the honest-reader caveat applies). The presence-path is its committed state-layer sibling: manufacture the real state repeatedly → a standing state; do the covered work repeatedly → a standing claim.
- **Asserted-unbacked (display-first):** the claim declared before either stream supports it. Two exits: **conversion** — the assertion generates occasions (people test the loud; grants find the visible), and if occasions are *converted into record*, backing catches up (the honest edge of "fake it till you make it" exists, but only as occasion-generation, never as substitute — and the account states it as an edge, not a path to take); or **collapse** — performed importance, leakage, the arc. The fork between exits is whether display-spend converts to ledger-fill.
- **The inherited candidate — not a fourth path.** Growing up beside a claim-holder decomposes: standing family-grants issued early and often + deference-by-association accumulating + **template-availability** (watching claim-holding teaches its *form* — a capability-factor, not formation). Verdict: an *early-timing, high-density profile* of grant+accumulation — with one lawful structural note: **early grants scope which claim forms**. A childhood granted "the cultured one, the wise one" forms exactly that claim — a knowledge/peer-scope — not the covering claim. (Stated generally; the friend's case is illustration, not biography — his interior stays his.)

**SV4 — the flywheel and its four brakes.** The transition-structure: **a grant creates a role → the role generates occasions → occasions convert to record → the record attracts deference → deference invites larger grants** — each loop widening backed-scope. The brakes, each a named failure-station:

1. **Grant-without-conversion** — the ledger never fills; the claim stays office-dependent (revocation-fragile).
2. **Accumulation-without-grant** — the full ledger nobody offers to: usually a **legibility failure** (A16 — the record isn't *readable*; T5's territory: make the work visible and the deference-stream can flow).
3. **The deflationary reader (the impostor-shape)** — the ledger full, the self-read discounting it; grants get *declined* at the adoption-step; the flywheel stalls at the self-verdict. Corrective: read the outside-stream as evidence; **accept-then-convert** (take the granted role at its scope and let the ledger catch up — grant-led formation is the deflationary reader's honest exit, because it outsources the verdict their bias can't render).
4. **The inflationary reader (grandiosity's formation-story)** — thin ledger, inflated read; claims asserted beyond record at the assertion-step; the arc prices it. Corrective: the scope-pin.

The two bias-directions land as brakes at **opposite stations** — one mechanism, both pathologies, both correctives structural. (Kind by construction: the impostor gets a reading-instruction, not a pep-talk; the grandiose gets a rule, not a sermon.)
