## The whole (one paragraph — the prerequisite check)

The Fear gap has an anatomy: BDA's Fear is one *content* (a forecast that this person can bring about a dreaded outcome), two *generators* (the sender's capacity to hurt; the sender's credible withdrawal) with their own removal events and counter-moves, and one *admission condition* (dependency — the receiver cannot costlessly leave the field; the user's "compensability" from the receiver's side, the corpus's outcome-independence from the sender's). The detector was reading the admission condition all along. "Negative" is four questions with one fixed answer (Fear generates). Fear of fearing is outside scope and enters at two joints. The magnitude the theory lacks is a receiver-stakes layer, possibly shared across variables. The deliverable is a frontier field registered under F1, with fixed points marked as observations and F1 left open.
