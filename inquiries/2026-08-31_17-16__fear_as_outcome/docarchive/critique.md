## Phase 2 — Adversarial evaluation

**C1 · The Fear sub-flavor basis (content axis) + generators (mechanism axis) — A-r.**
*Prosecution (D3, user-anchor):* naming six flavors *is* creating something concrete — the very thing the user forbade. *Prosecution (D2, substance):* the two axes are not independent — abandon and deprive are withdrawal-generated, harm / expose / humiliate hurt-generated — so "two axes" is one six-valued axis grouped in two. *Defense:* the flavors are the slot's *opening question* with candidates that must each pass the sub-flavour test (`03` §1.3) later; and some flavors admit both generators (exclude: by expulsion or by the group leaving; deprive: by cutting off or by not renewing) — the axes are *partly* independent, and the cross-table is itself a frontier. *Collision:* survives if the flavors are labeled candidates and the axis-independence is posed, not asserted. **REFINE** — direction: present as *"the slot's likely skeleton — a content basis (candidates: …) × a mechanism axis — with the cross-table's independence as the first question."*

**C2 · The H_c–Fear seam — withdrawal-Fear Hope-linked, hurt-Fear Hope-independent — C-g (+ L-c).**
*Prosecution (D2, generate-alone):* if withdrawal-Fear *always* co-occurs with Continuity-Hope (the landlord you dread losing is the landlord you hope keeps housing you), then withdrawal-Fear may fail generate-alone as a separate variable — it would be Hope's risk-face, and the user's own phrase ("loss of hope") would be literally right. *Defense:* that is not an objection to the frontier but its sharpest form: the seam becomes a *test* — find a withdrawal-Fear case with zero Continuity-Hope; if none exists, withdrawal-Fear is Continuity-Hope's negation and only hurt-Fear is Fear proper. *Collision:* the frontier survives sharpened, with a RE-TEST implication for the anatomy (the two generators may differ in *variable-status*, not only mechanism). **REFINE** — direction: state the seam as the generate-alone test on withdrawal-Fear; carry the P1 implication.

**C3 · Dependency as the admission condition; compensability's role — K4 / P2.**
*Prosecution (frame-premise 2; D2 external-anchor):* the corpus's pass cites *"threat/dependency cases"* jointly; "threat without dependency never generated attachment in any corpus case" is an absence of cases, not a test. *Substance:* the mugger — fear with no dependency → flight; the hostage — dependency literal; the abusive intimate — dependency manufactured. *User-perspective:* the user said the *boss* case is fear "because he can fire," not "because I can't leave" — the user's own words locate the condition in the sender's capacity. *Defense:* "he can fire me" is dread; "I can't find another job" is dependency — the user supplies both, in different sentences (the rich employee; Turkey). *Collision:* survives at MEDIUM-HIGH, as an inference the corpus permits; its test is stated (a threat case with costless exit that nonetheless attaches would refute it). **SURVIVE** — caveat: carry the grade and the refuting case.

**C4 · The position / stakes layer — C-f, Cn-REM, D-n, E-c, Cn-ADD-g.**
