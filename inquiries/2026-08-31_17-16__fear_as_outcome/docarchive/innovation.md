## Phase 2 — Generate (7 mechanisms × 3)

### 1 · Lens Shifting (Framer)
- **(generic) The receiver's-conduct lens.** Evaluate Fear not as a belief but as what the receiver *refrains from* — the four signals are inhibitions (not challenging, not disengaging, not speaking freely, not confronting). Under this lens Fear is the one variable **detected through the receiver's inhibition** rather than through the sender's display or the receiver's engagement. → frontier: *Fear as the inhibition variable* — why its readout is structurally unlike the other three.
- **(focused) The sender's lens (the user's own seat).** From the sender's side, cost-of-loss Fear is **irreplaceability**: what the receiver cannot compensate is what you uniquely hold. Its honest source is real value — a product genuinely hard to replace — which is Charm's *record* under dependency. → frontier: *a derived Fear component*: Charm × dependency → cost-of-loss, without any threat.
- **(contrarian) The time lens.** Pattern A says Fear is "mostly future." Under the withdrawal generator the dread is about the *continuation of the present* (that the boss keeps employing me) — a present-tense fear of a future stop; under the hurt generator it is purely future. → frontier: *Fear's temporality splits by generator*, and the withdrawal half mirrors Continuity-Hope (H_c).

### 2 · Combination (Generator)
- **(generic) Dependency × the Hope nest.** Continuity-Hope (the sender staying present) and cost-of-loss Fear share an object; one is its positive face, the other its negation held by a dependent receiver. But the secret-holder (dread of *disclosure*) is a *hurt*-generator case with no Hope — so the seam runs one way: **withdrawal-Fear is Hope-linked; hurt-Fear is Hope-independent.** → frontier: *the H_c–Fear seam*, a sharpened form of "Fear beside Hope."
- **(focused) Compensability × the comparison set × need.** Three receiver-side scalers the corpus has noted separately (Charm's comparison set; the user's compensability; Hope's implicit need) compose with receiver availability into one object — **the receiver's position** (alternatives · reference class · needs · openness) that scales every variable from outside the formula. → frontier: *the position layer*.
- **(contrarian) Fear × Resonance.** The corpus's non-contradictory pair ("an intimate who can hurt you"). Resonance is internally maintained — a shared world-model has no substitute in the market — so dependency on the person who *gets you* is the least compensable stake there is. → frontier: *Resonance-dependency as the un-compensable stake* — the abusive intimate and the committed marriage differ only on P7's honest/manufactured line.

### 3 · Inversion (Framer — depth-checked)
- **(generic, iterated).** L1: "Fear is a belief about the sender" → "Fear is a property of the receiver's situation." L2: "Fear is neither alone; it is the *product* of a belief about the sender's **capacity** (to hurt / to withhold) and the receiver's dependency." System-level: **is Fear a first-class belief at all, or a capacity-belief × dependency composite?** — and is the capacity-belief Fear's own, or Charm's status content read with negative valence when one is dependent? Existence-axis: could the Fear-belief count be zero (Charm + dependency only)? Identity-axis: what Fear consists of — the negative valence attached to another's power when one cannot leave. (Tested below against the secret-holder: power without status.)
- **(focused) Invert the thesis.** "Fear is always about outcomes" → "fear is never about outcomes — it is about *agents*": the child fears the father, not the belt; fear personifies, and outcomes are how we explain it afterwards. What follows: BDA's Fear-as-a-belief-about-a-person is right *because* fear personifies. → frontier: *the direction of derivation* (outcome → person, or person → rationalized outcome).
- **(contrarian) Invert the admission condition.** "Dependency makes fear attachment-relevant" → "attention makes it relevant; dependency is not needed": the feared stranger *captures attention* (hyper-vigilance) for the encounter — engagement without any disposition to return. → frontier: *attention capture vs attachment* — Fear's unique property of occupying attention at zero attachment (the origin's "Fear changes Power Distribution, affects willingness to disengage").

### 4 · Constraint Manipulation (Framer — both directions)
- **(ADD, generic) "Assume the receiver can always leave costlessly."** Abundant alternatives everywhere. Then Fear reduces to pure avoidance; the Coerced row empties; only hurt-generator fear remains, as flight. Prediction: Fear-dominant attachments and the readout's over-report hazard should thin out in abundance contexts (the user's abundant-job-market vs Turkey contrast). → frontier: *context abundance* as the macro-form of compensability.
- **(REMOVE, focused) Remove "beliefs are about the sender."** The receiver's self-beliefs enter; fear of fearing becomes a variable; the theory becomes a theory of the receiver's state and loses its object. What the removal shows: *why* the scope rule exists — and what a **receiver-state companion layer** (availability-style, beside the formula) would hold: availability, position, assumed threat, holding-cost. → frontier.
- **(ADD, contrarian) "Assume dependency is symmetric."** Partners, co-founders: each would lose the other; cost-of-loss Fear on both sides stabilizes without coercion. → frontier: *symmetry of dependency* as the discriminator between captivity and commitment (the marriage doc's "attached, not captive"; the vow as maximal mutual crystallization).

### 5 · Absence Recognition (Generator — both levels, both directions)
- **(patch-level) What's missing:** (a) no definition of "dependency" anywhere in the canon; (b) no Fear worked case — the Coerced row is unexemplified; (c) no removal event stated for the withdrawal generator (the type table says only "threat removal"); (d) no receiver-side scaler anywhere except the day-old comparison-set note.
- **(redesign-level) What would exist if Fear were specified today the way Hope is:** a **structural unifier** (negative-valenced · sender-mediated · receiver-affecting · forward-or-continuation-oriented) and a **discriminating axis** — *the kind of dreaded outcome* — yielding a **Fear sub-flavor basis** mirroring Hope's seven: F_harm (bodily / material), F_expose (secrets, shame), F_deprive (withdrawal of provision), F_abandon (withdrawal of presence — Continuity's negation), F_exclude (the group's removal), F_humiliate (status loss). The user's thesis supplies the axis (outcomes); the generators (hurt / withdraw) become a *second* axis — how the sender brings the outcome about. → the reserved slot's likely skeleton: **content × mechanism**.
- **(redesign-level, already present in different form):** dependency already lives in three places (the origin's word; the Coerced referent; isolation as manufactured dependency); receiver-side scaling in two (availability; the comparison set). The redesign articulates what the corpus already does piecemeal.

### 6 · Domain Transfer (Generator — native + different)
- **(native — decision vocabulary, vocabulary-only per `03` §5)** *expected loss* = probability × magnitude. A Fear-belief has a **confidence face** (can he? will he?) and a **stakes face** (how bad, uncompensated) — the corpus's single Fear level conflates them; compensability is the stakes face. → frontier: *confidence vs stakes* as two parameters of one belief.
- **(different — labor economics: the outside option; switching costs)** bargaining power is one's alternatives; the willingness to push back at the boss *is* the outside option. Vocabulary-only precedent for the stakes layer's bearer (the receiver's market position) and for the sender's lever (raising switching costs — honestly by specific value, dishonestly by lock-in: the business doc's "attached, not captive").
- **(contrarian — ethology: appeasement / submission displays)** the four Fear signals are appeasement conduct, species-general, persisting while the dominant is present. Suggests the detector reads a *dominance relation* and that Coerced "attachment" may be proximity-maintenance under dominance rather than attachment. → frontier: *is Coerced attachment attachment?* (the worth-having axis, from outside); vocabulary-only.

### 7 · Extrapolation (Generator)
- **(generic) Extend the withdrawal generator.** As the sender's record grows and the receiver's alternatives shrink, cost-of-loss Fear rises with no threat at all — the irreplaceable mentor, employer, partner. Prediction: high-Charm senders in scarce contexts carry Fear components inside warm attachments; the mixed rows (Charm+Fear; Resonance+Fear) should be *common* where alternatives are scarce. → frontier: *Fear as the shadow of irreplaceability*.
- **(focused) Extend fear of fearing.** The receiver who pre-emptively avoids every high-stakes sender never forms the attachments that would give them alternatives; the holding-cost compounds into more dependency, which raises stakes, which raises fear. → frontier: *the dependency spiral* (the user's Turkey and street examples composed).
- **(contrarian) Extend compensability to its limit.** Total compensability — nothing to lose — zeroes Fear and also zeroes Hope's stakes (nothing needed): the receiver who can compensate everything attaches to no one — receiver availability's saturation seen from the stakes side. → frontier: *is the stakes layer non-monotone* — an optimum dependency for attachment ("attached, not captive" as the middle)?
