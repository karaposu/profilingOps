# Marriage BDA Principles

**What this is.** Ten operating principles for marriage and long-term partnership, derived from Belief-Driven Attachment (BDA). Derived fresh against what the long-term frame changes — this is not dating continued; it is structurally a different game. Each principle ends with its check-question.

**Why marriage needs its own derivation.** Four structural inversions from every other domain: (1) it is the **maximum-baseline, maximum-access** reading condition — your partner eventually sees everything, including off-stage, which means *no performance survives here*; only backed states do; (2) the problem inverts from **forming** attachment to **keeping it from decaying** — the ledger never closes, and yesterday's credit is always quietly re-pricing; (3) the domain's defining structural risk is the **exhausted source** — a fully-known, fully-consumed partner is the theory's decay mode, and marriage is the only domain where total knownness is the default trajectory; (4) exits are structurally expensive (children, money, entangled lives), which makes it dangerously easy to mistake **captivity for attachment** — a marriage can be held together by its locks long after the attachment is gone, and the locks hide the reading.

**Priority structure:** **[GATE]** principles multiply — a zero on one zeroes the rest; the others add. Gates win conflicts.

**Provenance:** `BDA/21` (backed vs performed displays, the priced path), `BDA/20` (Coherence's longitudinal catalog, Emotional Congruence), `BDA/60` (Return Doctrine's repair logic, the calculation program), the backing-observables finding (off-stage continuity, cost-trail, forecast-check, the decay arc), the compliance-versus-attachment finding, the leadership-and-inexhaustibility finding, the anger-routing finding (deployed / leaked / performed anger). Status: structural layer, uncalibrated — a lens checked against results.

## I · The condition of the domain

### 1 · You will be known — only backing survives [GATE]

Marriage is the one context with full access: every context, every mood, every off-stage hour, decades of baseline. Every performance decays under sustained observation, and no observation is more sustained than a spouse's. The consequence is a kind of relief: **stop managing displays entirely** — the project is unwinnable here, and the effort itself is legible (a spouse reads the managing before reading the display). The only lever that works at this access level is cultivating the actual states — attention, care, desire, respect — and letting conduct emerge from them. If a state is gone, the work is regenerating or honestly addressing *the state*, never staging its display; a staged "everything is fine" buys days at most, at compound interest.

**Check:** *am I about to manage how this looks, or attend to what this is?*

### 2 · The ledger never closes [GATE]

There is no point in a marriage where accumulated credit lets conduct stop mattering. Attachment re-prices continuously, and the wedding bought exactly nothing forward — display-credit is provisional forever, and the everyday *is* the display: how you speak at hour three of a stressful Sunday, whether the attention still lands when nothing is at stake, what your face does when they walk into the room. One entry most people miss: **narration counts as conduct.** How you speak about your spouse to others — and to yourself, in your own head — is read (it always leaks) and is a forecast of how you will treat them. Contempt rehearsed internally is a display in progress.

**Check:** *if this ordinary moment were the only evidence of the marriage, what would it show — and how am I narrating them today?*

### 3 · Attached, not captive

The locks (children, mortgage, entangled decades) will hold a dead marriage upright for years — which is precisely their danger: they hide the reading. Compliance-staying (staying because leaving is expensive) behaves like all compliance: minimal, resentful, and gone in spirit long before it's gone in fact. The honest instrument is the **re-choose test**, run on yourself, periodically and privately: *knowing everything I now know, would I choose this person today — and if the answer hesitates, what specifically would make it a yes?* That second clause is the whole point: the test isn't for judging the marriage, it's for generating its maintenance agenda. Build the marriage that would be re-chosen; never coast on the fact that it can't cheaply be left.

**Check:** *is this marriage being held by its value or by its exit-costs — and which am I currently feeding?*

### 4 · Fight backed [GATE]

Conflict is where a spouse reads you hardest, against total baseline — nothing performed survives a fight with someone who knows your real anger. The theory's anger-sorting applies directly:

- **Deployed** honest anger — owned, aimed at the issue, proportionate — is legitimate display and often *productive*: it is congruence (the content is grave and the affect matches).
- **Leaked** resentment — the sigh, the sarcasm, the cold service — is an unowned display; it delivers the anger while denying it, which is an alignment failure they read instantly.
- **Performed** anger — put on for leverage, volume as negotiation — is caught on contact ("you're not even really angry") and re-prices your genuine anger too: the next real grievance arrives pre-discounted.

And one absolute: **the exit is never a move.** Threatening to leave as leverage is a performed exit — manufactured fear that buys anxious compliance and poisons the ground. Mention leaving only if it is real, once, deployed — never brandished.

**Check:** *is this anger owned and aimed — or leaking, or staged for effect?*

## II · Against the decay mode

### 5 · Small daily costs outweigh grand gestures

Marriage's highest-tether channel is the daily paid cost: the inconvenient attention, the thing handled so they didn't have to, presence at the unglamorous moment, desire expressed on an ordinary Wednesday. Grand gestures are low-frequency and — being public and dramatic — partially discounted (anniversaries are frame-expected; the frame performs them for you). The daily small cost is the opposite: private, uncelebrated, and paid when no script demanded it, which is exactly what makes it unfakeable. A year of Wednesdays beats the anniversary.

**Check:** *what did today's conduct actually cost me for them — and would anyone but them ever know?*

### 6 · Don't become a consumed source

The exhausted source is the domain's structural killer: the fully-known partner, finished becoming, whose every story has been heard and whose next decade is predictable, stops generating attachment *no matter how good they are* — not from any failing, but because attachment needs a gradient and total knownness is flat. The counter is to **keep becoming, visibly**: your own live trajectory (learning, building, changing) run for its own sake and legible at home; reserves that keep existing because you keep growing them; the occasional capacity they didn't know you had. This is the one place the theory endorses something like mystery in marriage — not withheld information, but **unfinished development**.

**Check:** *when did my spouse last see something genuinely new in me — and is anything currently growing that will surprise them?*

### 7 · Their becoming is your job too

The mirror duty: a spouse who has finished becoming — or who believes you think they have — is decaying as a source *and knows it*, which corrodes from both ends. So carry their trajectory actively: back the risk they're hesitating over, protect the hours their development needs, and — the highest-leverage move — **hold a live picture of who they're still becoming and let them catch you believing it.** People stay most attached where they are becoming their best self, and being *seen as still in motion* by the person with total baseline is the strongest hope-display that exists. The marriage's implicit promise is mutual: neither of us is finished.

**Check:** *what is my spouse becoming right now — could I answer specifically — and when did I last visibly invest in it?*

### 8 · Re-weight the words [GATE]

Long marriages breed token-decay: "love you" flattened into punctuation, compliments on autopilot, apologies as procedure. Flat delivery of heavy content is an incongruence display — and at maximum baseline it's read precisely: they know exactly what your *meant* version sounds like, so the token version is measured against it every time. Two disciplines: **say the heavy things only when you can back them with the felt state** (an eye-contact "I love you" weekly outweighs a doorway one daily — frequency is not the metric, congruence is), and **retire and revive**: when a phrase has flattened, stop using it until it can carry weight again, and say the backed thing you *can* say meanwhile. Same law for apologies: the procedural "sorry" that changes nothing is a forecast issued and defaulted (see 10).

**Check:** *can I feel, right now, the thing this sentence claims — or am I issuing the token?*

## III · The structure that carries it

### 9 · Keep a third thing

A marriage pointed only at itself consumes itself — two people staring at each other will eventually complete the audit (principle 6's trap, jointly). The durable structure is triangular: both partners attached to a **shared third thing** — the family being raised, the home being built, the venture, the shared craft, the cause — with attachment routed *through* the marriage into it. The third thing does quiet structural work: it generates a shared trajectory (joint hope that neither must supply alone), a stream of shared costs paid side by side (the strongest ongoing co-display), and an identity for the marriage beyond mutual consumption. When the marriage strains, the third thing is also what you can both still face together while the dyad repairs.

**Check:** *what are we building that is neither of us — and when did we last actively build it together?*

### 10 · Repair forecasts are the highest-stakes promises [GATE]

After a rupture, the repair conversation issues the marriage's heaviest forecasts: "I understand now," "that won't happen again," "I'll work on it." These are priced above all other promises because they are issued at maximum attention and checked against maximum baseline — and a defaulted repair-promise doesn't just fail once; it re-prices every apology before and after it ("the sorry is just the exit from the fight"). Three rules: **promise only the specific change you will actually execute** (smaller and kept beats comprehensive and defaulted); **let them observe the change rather than announcing it** (announced reform is a display; observed reform is evidence); and — the strongest coherence move in the domain — **volunteer the miss first**: "I did the thing again on Tuesday; I caught it late; here's what I'm adjusting." Self-audited failure, surfaced by you before they raise it, is the one display a decaying marriage can't fake and a recovering one runs on.

**Check:** *is this repair-promise specific enough to be checkable — and would I bet on the me of next month keeping it unprompted?*

## The compressed card

1. Full access = no performance survives; cultivate states, never manage displays.
2. The ledger never closes; the everyday is the display; narration counts as conduct.
3. Value-held, not lock-held; run the re-choose test and feed its answer.
4. Anger owned and aimed; never leaked, never staged; the exit is never a move.
5. A year of Wednesdays beats the anniversary — daily private costs are the tether.
6. Keep becoming, visibly; unfinished development is marriage's legitimate mystery.
7. Hold a live picture of who they're becoming; let them catch you believing it.
8. Heavy words only with the felt state behind them; retire flattened tokens.
9. Build a third thing and route attachment through it.
10. Repair-promises: specific, kept, observed — and volunteer your own misses first.

When principles collide: gates (1, 2, 4, 8, 10) beat everything.
