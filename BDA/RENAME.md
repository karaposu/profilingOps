# RENAME — APT → BDA (2026-08-25)

**The theory formerly named APT (Attachment & Presentation Theory) is renamed BDA — Belief-Driven Attachment.** This is the folder's one rename note; everything else simply uses the new name.

## The name

**Belief-Driven Attachment**: attachment (the output — the receiver's disposition to engage with, persist toward, and return to a person) is *driven by* receiver-side beliefs about that person — four additive property-belief categories (Charm, Hope, Fear, Resonance) gated by three multiplicative stance-belief categories (Self-Positioning, Coherence, Emotional Composure). The name is the theory's own identity sentence (iteration-3.4's belief-frame) compressed to three words. "Driven" — rather than the corpus's internal verb "generated" — states the *continuous* dependence: attachment tracks the current belief state, and when a belief's ground is removed the attachment moves with it (weakens / dissolves / reverses, per the persistence signatures).

## Why the old name was retired

1. **"Presentation" named the theory's most eclipsed part.** Domain 2 (Content / Style / Expressed Frame) was the January framing's second half; thirteen iterations later the architecture's center is the belief-frame and the Modulator Suite, and presentation organizes nothing in the canonical layer.
2. **"Attachment & … Theory" collided with Bowlby** and said nothing about mechanism. The mechanism-qualifier in front does the distinguishing work.
3. **The corpus asked for this itself.** The frozen spec's May 2026 annotation: *"maybe we need a better name now since it is all related to belief?"* — tracked as FR10 in the open register, adjudicated 2026-08-25 by this rename.

Candidates considered and declined: *Human Attachment Theory* (worsens the Bowlby collision; contradicts the theory's own cross-species and institutional validation cases) · *Attachment & Positioning Theory* (keeps the acronym, misweights one gate of three) · *Attachment Belief Theory* (ambiguous — reads as "beliefs about attachment," the exact reading iteration-3.4 rules out) · *Belief-Generated Attachment* (accurate but heavy; "-generated" also carries an unwanted AI connotation now).

## Scope of the rename (deliberately minimal — no legacy compatibility maintained)

- **Renamed:** this folder (`APT/` → `BDA/`) and the theory-name usages inside its files.
- **Deliberately NOT renamed:**
  - The **21 findings** under `inquiries/` and everything under `BASEFILES/` — frozen and never edited. **Wherever a finding or legacy spec says "APT," read "BDA."**
  - **"APT Inference"** and **"APT Profiling"** — retained as the *proper names of the legacy PRAGMA spec artifacts* (`apt_inference.md`, `apt_profiling.md`). Any future implementation should name its components BDA Inference / BDA Profiling; the old names refer to the old specs.
  - Historical entries in `90_HISTORY.md`, which use the name that was current at each entry's time.

One mapping line is the whole compatibility story: **APT = BDA; the rename changed the name, not one claim.**
