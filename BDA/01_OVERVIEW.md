## The epistemic banner (read this first)

Everything in this folder is **structural theory, not established empirical fact**. The theory's own documentation flags: *empirical validation pending*; *coefficients uncalibrated* (there are no numbers to compute with); most worked cases are analyzed examples, not controlled observations. The theory is **receiver-side** (it models what an observer comes to believe about a person — it has no term for what anyone's results or outcomes are) and **per-dyad** (one observer, one observed; groups are handled only as compositions of pairs). Confidence grades used throughout the corpus — HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW, and "guarded" for single-origin ideas — are preserved wherever content is restated here.

---

## What BDA is

**BDA — Belief-Driven Attachment — is an account of why one person becomes drawn to, stays engaged with, and returns to another person.** Its single load-bearing move is in the name: attachment is **driven by beliefs**. Not by what the other person *is*, and not by anyone's results — by what the receiver comes to *believe* about them. BDA names the small set of belief-categories that actually generate attachment (most beliefs about a person — their height, their ZIP code — generate none) and states how those categories combine. *(Until 2026-08-25 the theory was named APT — Attachment & Presentation Theory; the findings and legacy specs use that name throughout. APT = BDA; see `RENAME.md`.)*

## The formula in one screen

```
Attachment ≈ f(Charm, Hope, Fear, Resonance) × g₁(Self-Positioning) × g₂(Coherence) × g₃(Emotional Composure)
```

- **Four ingredients that ADD** (so any one alone can generate attachment):
  - **Charm** — "this person is impressive": status, competence, skill. *Current-positive.*
  - **Hope** — "being connected to this person promises something good for me." *Future-positive.* Key origin insight: high value alone isn't enough — they must believe you'd actually share it.
  - **Fear** — the negative-valence ingredient. Carries an **unsettled fork**: "this person can harm me" vs "losing this person would cost me" — both readings live in the corpus, deliberately unresolved (see `11`).
  - **Resonance** — "this person sees the world the way I do": shared values, frameworks, cares. Unlike the other three, it **emerges from the pair** — both people's maps have to align.
- **Three gates that MULTIPLY** (so any one at zero collapses everything the ingredients built):
  - **Self-Positioning** — does your attention read as genuinely *on something*, or as fishing for this person's approval? Approval-fishing (**Supplication**) drops the gate; the result is **devaluation** ("this guy doesn't deserve this").
  - **Coherence** — can a *reliable* model of you be formed? (Reliable — not *finished*: being fully figured out is a different axis entirely.) Failure: **Model-Collapse** ("I can't figure out who they really are").
  - **Emotional Composure** — is your emotional state regulated under pressure? Failure: **Contagion-Drain** ("exhausting to be around").
- **Two outputs:** **MAGNITUDE** (how strong — the sum times the gates) and **TYPE** (what kind — read off which ingredient dominates: Status/admiration, Transactional, Coerced, Bonded, Collaborative). Each type carries a **persistence behaviour** — what happens to it when circumstances change.
- **One quality-factor:** **Signal Specificity** — every ingredient's effective strength is scaled by how *specific to this person* the carrying signal is. A message sendable to anyone carries almost nothing, whatever its nominal content.

## Glossary

Every term this folder uses, defined the way a newcomer needs. (Owner files hold the full treatments; nothing below is bare corpus shorthand.)

**Adjacent mechanisms** — nine real attachment-generating mechanisms deliberately scoped *outside* BDA (intermittent reinforcement, grandiosity signaling, social-norm violation, scarcity, progressive commitment, trauma bonding, social proof, isolation, aspirational distance). They layer on top of BDA's mechanism in extreme cases (cults, narcissist-followers). `50`.

**Attachment (two referents!)** — (1) the formula's output: the receiver's disposition to engage with, persist toward, and return to the sender; (2) one of the three engagement-state names. The corpus learned to always say which is meant; so does this folder. `10`, `30`.

**Attention-object** — the thing a person's attention is on (their own project, another's wellbeing, a cause, a craft…). The species taxonomy's real individuating slot; two properties matter: is it **generative** (keeps producing new content) and is it **outside the receiver**. `21`.

**Backed vs performed** — a display is *backed* when the attention it shows is really where it appears to be; *performed* when put on. Performed displays get caught — sometimes instantly, otherwise via the **costly-signal decay arc** (the faked posture's unpaid cost shows over time). What persists of any following is the backed part. `21`, `30`.

**Bearing** — a person's stable attention-posture across many interactions; over time it "becomes part of what others call character." (BDA covers this slice of character-perception, not character in full.) `21`.

**Belief-frame** — the meta-frame (iteration-3.4): BDA specifies *which receiver-side beliefs about another person generate attachment when held* — the identity claim the theory's name now states. Property-beliefs (what the sender IS) fill the ingredients; stance-beliefs (how the sender IS) fill the gates. Attachment is generated by beliefs, not itself a belief. `10`.

**Frame (the runtime object)** — a stance-bundle (label + expected conduct + claim-scope) held at an object: the situation, the pair, or oneself-in-context. The *worn self-frame* = a context-scoped premise about who-I-am-here + the manufactured state it implies, worn as one unit — installed by state-manufacture (assumable, not memorizable), co-generating conduct, retiring in-scope questions, coherence-maintained, and (worn in) becoming bearing. What the principles compile into; guarantees frame-consistency, never attachment. `60` §12.

**Cluster 4** — the standing, never-triggered question of whether the whole theory should be re-grounded in attention as substrate. Has written reopening conditions and a determination procedure (the census). `03`, `80`.

**Coherence** — see the formula section. Aspects: **Stability** (same person across time/contexts) and **Alignment** (surface matches depth). Its belief statement means *reliable*, never *finished*. `20`.

**Compliance** — a word doing three jobs: (1) the Fear-dominant attachment's outside view (real, nonzero); (2) behaviour under threat with *zero* attachment behind it (a gate collapsed — the null case); (3) going-along-with produced by attachment strength, no Fear involved. Say which. `30`.

**Devaluation / Model-Collapse / Contagion-Drain** — the three gates' failure signatures (what each collapse looks like from outside). Signatures are **properties** — had, not admitted. `20`.

**Display catalog (the five signals)** — what observers actually read to judge standing: **Premise-Posture** (speak from your own premise vs ask permission), **Exit-Willingness** (can abandon a position without drama), **Self-Justification-Density** (unprompted self-defence — low reads well), **Rhythm-Comfort** (comfort with silence), **Withholding** (giving slightly less than expected — double-edged: raises standing, can read "not for me"). Operationalized by 12 metrics (6 text, 6 multimodal). `21`.

**Display modes** — Self-Positioning displayed by *selective engagement* (specific attention at this person, from your own evaluation → realizes Attention-Hope) or by *withholding* (visible non-engagement → suppresses it, producing the **"not for me"** read). `21`.

**Double-Collapse** — why generic messages produce near-zero attachment: one root cause (no genuine engagement with this specific person) fails the ingredient layer (no specificity) and the gate layer (Supplication-shaped style) simultaneously. `10`.

**Engagement states** — attachment (persists when satisfied) / pursuit (ends when its gap closes) / compliance (reverses when the threat lifts). **A derived view** of the persistence property — never a free-standing taxonomy. `30`.

**Followership** — attachment **plus pursuit that cannot extinguish** — possible only when the pursued object keeps generating content and lies outside the follower (the **two-clause criterion**). `40`.

**Genus / species** — the genus is **Non-Extractive Attention** (attention not aimed at harvesting approval/validation/response from the person in front of you); the species are what the attention is *on*: **Self-Focus**, **Caregiver-Focus** (primary), **Mission-**, **Task-**, **Dialogue-**, **Transcendent-Focus** (secondary). Negative pole: **Supplication**. `21`.

**Hope's sub-flavors** — Exchange, Attention, Validation, Continuity (primary); Generative, Reciprocity, Safety (secondary) — a *basis*: real hopes are mixtures. `11`.

**Legibility** — making your work readable and actable by people who can't judge the raw substance. **Guarded**: held at medium confidence, single origin, never promoted. `40` (boundary noted wherever used).

**MAGNITUDE / TYPE** — the two outputs; TYPE is a *projection* (read off the dominant ingredient), derived-but-predictive. `10`, `30`.

**Model-completeness** — how much of a person the observer has finished figuring out. Outside the formula; ends *pursuit* when it saturates; its ceiling is set by the sender's attention-object ("you become inexhaustible by attaching your attention to something that does not run out"). `50`.

**Outcome-independence** — the operational marker of non-extraction: attention whose resolution doesn't depend on the counterparty's response. Defined in `21`; prescribed as the cultivation target in `60`.

**Pattern A** — the descriptive organization of the unilateral ingredients: Charm = current-positive, Hope = future-positive, Fear = (mostly future) negative; Resonance sits on a separate (dyadic) axis. Descriptive content, not a hidden substrate. `11`.

**Persistence under change** — each attachment type's outside-view behaviour when circumstances shift (weakens as access recedes / dissolves when exchange ends / reverses on threat removal / persists through absence / high-persistence mixed). A property of the type. `30`.

**PRAGMA** — the conversation-measurement system BDA sits inside (signals → dynamics profile → interpretation → attachment readings; the legacy specs name those readings "APT Inference / APT Profiling"). `31`, `90`.

**Receiver availability** — whether the receiver is open to attaching at all. A pre-condition outside the formula (a belief about *self*, not about the sender). `50`.

**Removal test** — the behavioural discriminator for group roles: when the person is removed, does member-member engagement persist, and does the shared term survive? `40`.

**Resonance network** — a movement modeled as many ordinary pairs sharing one term (follower-follower Resonance) — why movements outlive founders. `40`.

**Self-Positioning** — the first gate; the theory's biggest element (genus, species, levels, catalog, modes, backing). Two-level naming: *Non-Extractive Attention* theoretically, *Self-Focus* user-facing. `21`.

**Shared term ("the term")** — the shareable external object a leader's attention is visibly on and through whom others get access to it (a cause, product, mission — never a self: a self can't be shared). `40`.

**Sid-function** — co-attention manufacture: making a team out of strangers by continuously supplying shared attention-objects. Vital, member-side, and *not* leadership (its object contains the receivers). `40`.

**Signal Specificity / θ(context)** — the per-ingredient quality scaling, and its per-channel threshold below which signals don't register at all (cold DM high; warm intro lower; physical proximity lowest). `10`.

**Signature-to-state** — the corpus's named relation between an inside state and what it looks like from outside (devaluation is to a Self-Positioning collapse what compliance is to a Fear-dominant attachment). `30`.

**Species-context-fit** — the prescriptive table matching contexts to the attention-configuration worth cultivating there (value-establishing → Self-Focus; caring → Caregiver-Focus; cause-driven leadership → Mission-Focus; …). Advice, not description. `60`.

**Supplication** — attention whose resolution depends on the counterparty's response: hedging, over-explaining, permission-seeking. What collapses g₁. (Not the same thing as compliance — different party, different object.) `21`.

**Tiered calibration audit** — the gateway self-check ("Am I okay if they don't respond favorably?") plus the 7-point composite. `60`.

**Two-clause criterion** — an attention-object supports followership when engaging it **generates further content** and the object **lies outside the receiver** (partial inclusion — a group containing the receiver — still fails). `40`.

**Two-family reading** — Charm/Hope/Fear are *externally grounded* (their ground can be removed: access recedes, exchange ends, threat lifts); Resonance is *internally maintained* (persists through absence). `30`.

**Vitality** — physical aliveness/energy. Real, consequential at clinical extremes, **not** a gate (it threshold-switches rather than gating smoothly). `50`.
