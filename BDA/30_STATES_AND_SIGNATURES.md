## 1 · The type table, read correctly

| Dominant variable | TYPE | Persistence under change |
|---|---|---|
| Charm-dominant | Status / admiration | goes quiet without occasions; kept by the record; revived on return *(corrected 2026-08-31 — see the row-note)* |
| Hope-dominant | Transactional | dissolves when exchange ends |
| Fear-dominant | Coerced | **reverses** (relief, not attachment) on threat removal |
| Resonance-dominant | Bonded / deep | **persists through absence; maintained via shared model** |
| Hope + Resonance | Collaborative | high persistence; both transacts and connects |

**The table is a join of two enumerations.** Column one is a **generator** (which variable dominates), column three is a **signature** (outside-view behaviour), and column two — TYPE — is the **join key**, belonging to neither list. That is why "type" always felt like it should be the list of attachment's forms and never quite worked as one. The mixed row is the join visibly stretching (compound generator; oddest name), which is evidence *for* this reading.

**TYPE is a projection** (`10` §5): identity determined by the dominant variable; names add receiver-side content (admiration, coerced, bonded) but that content is a function of the variable; no admission gate exists *because nothing is admitted to a projection*. **Derived-but-predictive** is its status: the mix predicts trajectory that the sum cannot.

**"Forms of attachment" — the two-level answer.** At the generator grain: **four** (the variables). At the signature grain: **five** (the persistence entries) — and *compliance is one of the five*, which is the sense in which "compliance is a form of attachment" is simply correct. Counts are resolution-indexed (`03` §7); the nine adjacent mechanisms are the outside-the-theory answer at the generator level (`50`).

**The Charm row-note (replacing the former standing caution; source: the charm_persistence finding, `inquiries/2026-08-31_16-25__charm_persistence/finding.md`).** The row formerly read *"weakens as access to the Charm source recedes"* — a **signature mis-stated as a mechanism**. Its author's own gloss of "access" was the source's *evidence stream* ending ("celebrity retires, audience shrinks"), never reachability of the person; and its "weakens" was the attachment's *expression* going quiet, not the belief eroding — the canon's decisive Charm case (Mozart, `11` §1: zero access, attachment-class behaviour present) and the mentor-admired-at-a-distance-for-years case are consistent with the signature and contradict the mechanism. The mechanism, from committed material: the belief's **object** is the sender; its **evidence-carriers** are the person's conduct, the person's **record** (output, standing, third-party report — the footprint a real state leaves), and occasions. Distance **un-maintains** the belief (content intact, currency stale), removes **occasions** for the disposition to express (nothing to return to — an unexpressed disposition and a weakened one look alike from outside), and **exposes it to re-pricing** against the receiver's comparison set; its ground is removed only by **counter-evidence** (`10` §5). Where a record stands it maintains the belief absent the person; at total absence the standing **freezes** on the record. Two limits: no record (the signature at its maximum — still no belief-erosion) and fame (maximal record, zero reachability). In mixed fan attachments what dissolves on the source's retirement is the **Hope nest** (Continuity its base; Validation the residue `11` §1 recorded), not the Charm; scarcity of access (`50` §3) can raise it. Return is **revival** at prior-strength (`10` §3 — maintenance ≠ creation), boosted by scarcity, discounted by re-pricing, gated by Coherence (the return must be coherent with the record). Rows remain tendencies pending data; M1 is discharged for this row (`80`).

## 2 · Persistence is a property; the engagement states are a derived view

**Persistence-under-change is a property each type has** — in exactly the relation each modulator has to its failure signature (the corpus's twice-used, once-named **signature-to-state** relation: devaluation is to a Self-Positioning collapse what compliance is to a Fear-dominant attachment). Properties are had, not admitted (`03` §2). It is **not a layer** (nothing consumes it — terminal), and the three engagement-state names are **not sibling states**:

- **attachment** — persists when satisfied
- **pursuit** — ends when its gap closes (its closure-factor, model-completeness, sits *outside the formula* — `50`)
- **compliance** — reverses when the threat lifts

The sibling-taxonomy version was **invalidated** on four grounds: the members are tested against three different perturbations (not values of one axis — and Coerced *passes* attachment's own satisfaction test); the promotion dropped two of the five rows; the members compose (followership = attachment **plus** pursuit); and their ending-factors sit at different architectural levels. The names are retained as a **derived view** — read off dominance plus ground-externality — with all five rows carried, the two previously dropped rows recovered for free. Membership criteria for the view are *impossible in principle*, not missing.

**Determination is bounded, not solved:** deciding which signature a real case shows needs the dominant variable — but detection emits five-level categoricals (ties routine; the mixed row IS a tie category) and the coefficients are uncalibrated, so there is no weighted argmax. The gap is relocated onto the calibration roadmap item (`80`).

## 3 · What the persistence column encodes: the two-family reading

Read literally, the rows encode **two parameters**: is the variable's ground **externally removable**, and **how abruptly** does removal happen? Fear's ground can be zeroed in an instant (threat lifts → *reverses*); Hope's discharges on delivery (*dissolves*); Charm's is removed only by **re-evaluation** — counter-evidence or re-pricing — gradually (the row's *weakens*, read as its signature: expression goes quiet; corrected 2026-08-31); Resonance's is **not removable** — the fourth row names *internal maintenance*, not slow decay.

**Maintenance-source (added 2026-08-31, the charm_persistence finding; Resonance's cell derived and its removal event filled same-day — the third_leg finding).** Beside its removal event, each row has what keeps the belief evidenced: **Charm** — the evidence stream (the person's conduct, their record, third-party report); **Hope** — the exchange's continuation (removed when the exchange ends — unchanged); **Fear** — the threat or dependency persisting (removed when it lifts — unchanged); **Resonance** — the receiver's own model, **now derived**: the self-carried accumulation of registrations (`11` §5) — nothing external grounds it — and its *removal event*, formerly unstated (FR1's twin), is **contact-borne only**: **counter-registration** (fresh co-attention occasions where stances mismatch — predictions stop landing, terms diverge; accumulated, the folk "we grew apart"; in one massive stroke, what betrayal does *to this variable*, beside its other-variable events) or **discovered performance** (the stock re-read as display — the mimicry arc's Resonance face). **Never distance; never events.** The removal column thus carries three shapes: **by events** (Hope, Fear — abrupt), **by re-evaluation** (Charm — gradual), **by counter-registration in contact** (Resonance — otherwise internally maintained).

**Growth-source (added 2026-08-31, the third_leg finding — the maintenance-source clause's formation-side sibling).** Each row also has what *grows* the belief: **Charm** — record-events and demonstrations (the person seen at their work); **Hope** — exchanges realized and promises kept; **Fear** — the threat or dependency's persistence deepening *(committed-mirror wording — the Fear-side formation account awaits its own field's registration; this cell upgrades then)*; **Resonance** — **registrations** (stance-matches on co-attended objects), with conversion to the Bonded form when a **generative** object's **variety × recurrence** generalizes the model beyond its domain — the object completes, Exchange-Hope dissolves, the object-independent model remains (signature: **transferred prediction**; domain-locked histories complete as peers instead — FR3's first row, derived).

So: **Charm / Hope / Fear are externally-grounded engagement; Resonance is internally-maintained** — and compliance's nearest relatives are status-attachment and transactional attachment, not "attachment" as such. What the engagement-state view called *attachment* is the Resonance family. (The question "is compliance a form of attachment?" had two answers because it compared a member of one family against the name of the whole taxonomy.) This binary independently matches the unilateral/dyadic variable split (`11` §1) — with support honestly graded as **internal consistency**, since types are determined by variables (the "two independent derivations" claim was corrected). The mixed row is the reading's prediction, not its exception: one removable + one non-removable ground → falls to the Resonance floor, i.e. "high persistence." **The formerly-unexplained residual is discharged:** *what actively maintains* Resonance is now derived — maintained AS the shared model (self-carriage; `11` §5; the third_leg finding); the residue lives at `80` FR1 (resolved-with-residue).

## 4 · Compliance, precisely

**Composition: yes.** Same formula, Fear carrying the sum — "being a threat to navigate" is one of the belief-frame's independent generators. The inhibitory-vs-approach difference is a behavioural overlay, not a compositional one (approach/avoidance was killed as substrate).
**Species: no.** A weight configuration is not a mechanism, and the corpus's sub-kind criterion is causal mechanism-distinctness (`03` §1.4) — with the recorded qualification that no *relational* criterion exists, and whether one would flip the verdict turns on the open type-interconversion question.
**The relation: signature-to-state.** Compliance is what a Fear-dominant attachment looks like from outside.

**The word's three jobs** (the formula has exactly three relevant output states — the closure is structural):
1. **Fear-dominant, nonzero output** — a real attachment; the Coerced row's referent.
2. **Zero output, gate collapsed** — behaviour under threat with *no* attachment behind it (the original no-Coherence question's case). Correct verdict: "with a gate at zero the product is zero." The famous sentence "deference that evaporates was never attachment" was **right for this case and wrong in its citation** (it cited the nonzero row); the null case itself is placed in `50`.
3. **Going-along from attachment strength** — no Fear anywhere (the dating case's "compliance"; conferred position's "threat **or reward**").

Three inquiries used the layer without collision because each used a different job. **Fork-dependence:** under cost-of-loss Fear, the Coerced row describes *dependency*, and ordinary-language compliance may map to no type at all (`11` §4).

## 5 · The combination space

Fifteen non-null mixes (4 singles, 6 pairs, 4 triples, 1 four-way). Named: **one** (Hope+Resonance → Collaborative). Worked-but-unlabeled: **Charm+Fear** (the respected volatile figure — "deference from fear AND admiration from charm," two channels at once, degrading in two stages: fear lifts abruptly when the threat ends; admiration persists on the record and goes quiet without occasions — re-worded 2026-08-31 with the Charm row). Ruled out as a type: **Hope+Fear from a stranger** → the **coherence-stress rule** — contradictory sender-intention claims ("will benefit me" + "will harm me") are what Model-Collapse names, so the combination collapses g₂ rather than forming a type — **conditional on threat-of-harm Fear**, and softened to *stress, not exclusion* (with model-support a receiver can hold it: the mentor who could fire you). Non-contradictory pairs: Charm+Fear (a powerful person, coherently), Fear+Resonance (an intimate who can hurt you).

**Per-region decay shapes are derivable** (shape, never magnitude) from the two persistence parameters applied per-mix. **Region-assignment is unstable by design:** specificity scales per-variable, so one message can shift which variable dominates without the sender changing anything else. **Naming the regions is blocked on use, not data** — the corpus's own tiering criterion (operational frequency) is a property of an application, and nothing applies the theory yet.

## 6 · The decay arc (owner)

**Costly-signal decay:** genuine non-extractive attention pays a real cost (foregone optimization of the current exchange); observers infer reliability from the visible cost. A **performed** display doesn't pay it; over time the unpaid cost surfaces as inconsistencies; attachment decays **toward whatever was actually backed**. Two detection routes co-occur (mimicry-detection; Coherence's Model-Collapse — `20` §5). **Partial decay** is the general prediction: a mixed genuine/mimicked source contracts to its backed core rather than collapsing (the narcissist's following shrinks to the personal core; the harsh leader's coerced component evaporates on exit while cause-Resonance persists). Second decay pathway — a *sincere* leader whose cause stops generating — is deferred with a revival condition (`80`). Whether display-only Resonance is as durable as backed Resonance is assumed not, and flagged as assumption — **upgraded to a derived expectation (2026-08-31, the third_leg finding): fake registrations fail exactly where stances get forced (high-stakes co-attention extracts real takes), so a display-built stock is fragile at precisely the occasions that test it; the flag stands pending observation.**
