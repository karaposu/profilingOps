# 40 · Groups and Leadership — Followership, Roles, and the Removal Test

> **Current as of:** 2026-08-25 · **Sources:** `leadership_and_inexhaustibility` (the founding account), `belief_attributes`, `glue_versus_leader`, `leadership_as_calculation`, with the engagement vocabulary from `compliance_versus_attachment` · **Validation:** structural-only; the Task-Focus followership prediction is the account's one clean open falsification test. **Standing bound:** the theory is per-dyad — every group claim here is a composition of pairs; group-level machinery is twice-deferred (`80`).

## 1 · Followership (what following IS)

> **Followership = attachment + pursuit that cannot extinguish.**

Attachment persists when satisfied; pursuit normally ends when the observer's model of the person completes (`50`: model-completeness). Followership is the configuration where the pursuit *cannot* complete — because what is being engaged keeps producing. Not compliance (nothing reverses when consequences are removed — Gandhi's followers were not coerced); not transaction (nothing dissolves on delivery).

## 2 · The two-clause criterion (this file owns it)

> **An attention-object supports followership when (1) engaging it generates further content, and (2) the object lies outside the receiver.**

- Clause 1 (**generative**): a cause, a craft, a shared inquiry *produce*; a personal position can be completed. Generativity is a relation between object and observer, usually dominated by the object ("a topic exhausted for an expert is not exhausted for a novice") — hence the tendency-level precision.
- Clause 2 (**externality**): found by the criterion's own failing case — **Caregiver-Focus** has a generative object (a person's wellbeing is never finished) and produces devotion **without followers**, because its object *is* the receiver: watching someone attend to you generates no new content *about them*.
- **Partial-inclusion extension** (named, from the Sid case): an object that *contains* the receiver — "the group and its members" — still violates clause 2. This is what derives the glue verdict (§4).

Consequences: **peer vs follower** — an exhaustible object → model completes → pursuit extinguishes → a peer at steady state; a generative external object → the relation stays open → a follower. **Devotion vs followership** — internal objects yield belonging and devotion without following.

**The claim-cover layer (added 2026-08-31; source: the ego/self-focus finding — the standing self-claim's home is `21` §8):** person-attached following means accepting the leader's standing claim *over you* in some domain — and the follower's own standing self-claim is a held position carrying consistency-accrual. The result is the **consistency-resistance gradient**: cover-acceptance against one's own standing claim meets resistance proportional to the claim-scope gap — a *gradient*, not a law (conversions happen, at high belief-revision cost, rarely); "big egos won't follow smaller ones" is the gradient's typical outcome, stamped derived/uncalibrated. It runs on a **per-domain lattice**, never a linear ego-scale: the strategy-scoped leader leads bigger-egoed specialists whose claims live elsewhere; the *vizier* holds a counsel-scoped claim under the throne-claim; leadership teams work by complementary scoping (one covering claim per domain — colliding same-domain claims grind). **Two escape-mechanisms** let leadership exceed personal claim-cover: **term-attachment** (this section's own machinery — the follower's ego subordinates to the shared term, not the person; the leader borrows the term's bigness; *institutional terms — offices — are an instance*: the chair carries cover the occupant wears) and **domain-scoping**. The prescriptive corollary (owner `60`): *to lead people with big claims, get a bigger term, not a bigger ego* — inflation manufactures performed importance and rides the mimicry arc down. **Formation-side (added 2026-08-31; machinery at `21` §8):** claims form on the **flywheel** — grant → role → occasions → record → deference → larger grants — with four brakes (grant-without-conversion; accumulation-without-grant, a legibility failure; the deflationary and inflationary reader-brakes) and two per-domain spirals; **scope-migration** (a craft-record under a direction-grant — the Peter Principle) is grant-led formation's standing trap; and **followers co-author the leader's claim** — deference-expressions are grant-events in the leader's outside-stream, so a yes-room pumps the leader's claim past their record: cheap dissent protects the leader's *own self-read*, not only their information. Two cautions travel with the layer: the **too-proud-to-follow trap** (a claim so wide it can accept no cover forfeits all coalitions — a general of nothing), and the **distance-ecology note** (stamped prediction-grade): of the theory's readable objects, the *backed standing self-claim* is the one that survives distance (seconds-bandwidth, no baseline), while competence-evidence needs bandwidth mass audiences lack — so claim-inflation thrives at distance and decays close-up, and mass politics structurally favors the backed fool over the calibrating expert on the channels the crowd can read.

## 3 · Leadership's mechanism

> **Leadership ≈ Mission-Focus (the species) × [Resonance-primary + access-Hope secondary].**

The leader is the person whose attention is visibly on a **shareable external term** (a cause, mission, product, craft) and who is the term's **access-point**. The load-bearing correction (against April's Scenario 9): mission-participation is **not Hope** — Hope's unifier requires the outcome to be *sender-mediated*, and a cause's value is not produced by the leader; the leader mediates *access*. So the participation component is **Resonance** (the follower's world-model aligning with the cause), plus ordinary access-Hope toward the leader.

**Resonance does three jobs:** aligns follower with cause; aligns followers **with each other**; carries the participation component. **The network account:** any two followers of one cause satisfy the same-object condition → each pair holds ordinary dyadic Resonance → a movement is a network of pairs sharing one term → **remove the leader-node and the dyads remain** — why movements outlive founders. (Frontiers: one-directional cases — parasocial/dead leaders; whether follower-follower Resonance needs actual contact — `80`.)

**Shareability** is the deep boundary: a cause is outside both parties and can be *shared*; a self cannot — so self-as-object produces devoted **individuals** (the respected grandparent), cause-as-object produces **movements**. (Shareability is also the standing candidate for a genuinely species-predicated property — first in the census queue, `80`.)

**What leadership does NOT require:** being the most competent (Charm is one additive variable of four; people follow unsuccessful men); results (no outcome term — decisions "don't have to be correct"); harshness or warmth as such (`41`). **The pathological variant:** the cult leader runs Mission-Focus as **mimicry** — displayed cause over a genuine self-aggrandisement object (exhaustible) — and the decay arc predicts their collapse and not Gandhi's; layer-precision in `20` §5.

## 4 · The glue split (one word, two relations)

- **Glue(people → purpose)** — sticking people to a shared outcome through you — **is the leader-function** (shared-term access). Not an alternative to leading; it is leading.
- **Glue(person ↔ person)** — the **Sid-function**: **co-attention manufacture** — continuously supplying the shared attention-objects (talk, occasions, activity) on which member-member Resonance forms, for members who lack "common things." Run the criterion: the object ("the group and its members") fails externality by partial inclusion → **devotion and belonging, without followership**. "A vital part of the group, and not a leader" is a theorem, not an intuition.

Two mechanisms → two roles (the "us-as-term" unification fails on exactly the externality clause — the failure IS the split's content). **Glue-ness is person × group pair-predicated:** the same Sid is load-bearing in a taciturn group and redundant noise in a talkative one — how "annoying" and "vital" are both true at once. The role has real value the leader-frame hides: the glue manufactures the Resonance family's raw material — the team's durability-infrastructure (sports names and pays the "glue guy"). Pathological variant: connection-**performance** without backing — the broker who spans gaps deliberately and keeps members connected only through themselves; the mimicry apparatus transfers whole.

## 5 · The removal test (behavioural discriminator; two questions)

> **Remove the person. (1) Did member-member engagement persist? (2) Did the shared term survive?**

- Leader removed → the follower-follower network persists (the term is external and still there).
- Hub-glue removed → the team collapses to acquaintances (the connective production stops).
- **Sub-structure:** **hub** (all bonds run through them; removal collapses everything) vs **catalyst** (their manufacture seeded real member-member bonds; removal leaves residue — the chemistry reading of the user's own metaphor: adhesive stays in the bond, catalyst can leave).
- **Two-cause caution:** collapse-on-exit has two diagnoses — glue-loss (term survives, bonds don't) vs **term-loss** (the shared term lived only in the founder's head — a legibility failure, different fix entirely).

**Importance ≠ leadership.** The removal test measures load-bearing-ness; followership tracks pursuit. A glue-holder can be the group's most load-bearing member with followership at zero. Two real axes.

## 6 · The roles table (inventory; admission rule: distinct mechanism + removal signature)

| Role | Attention on | Generates | Removal signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Leader** | shareable external term | followership (Resonance-primary + access-Hope), inward and outward | network persists — the term remains |
| **Hub-glue** | the group and its members | belonging / member-side cohesion | collapse to acquaintances |
| **Catalyst-glue** | the group and its members | member-member bonds that outlive the manufacture | residue persists |
| **Broker** (pathological glue) | own position, displayed as connection | dependency on the spanning node | gap re-opens; no residue |
| **Technical founder** | the craft (Task-Focus) | predicted: deep-narrow apprentice-followership — attachment half evidenced (Scenario 12), followership half **open** | term-loss risk if the craft-vision was never made legible |

Latent candidates when a fifth is classified: captain, star.

**The required-claim note (added 2026-08-31, per role — the standing self-claim, `21` §8):** each role demands a different claim-scope — **Leader:** a direction-scoped standing claim (or the term's borrowed cover — §1's escapes); **Hub-/Catalyst-glue:** **claim-free** (hosting and bond-manufacture need no standing claim over anyone — which is why glue-people are common and person-leaders rare); **Broker:** a *performed* connection-claim (position displayed as service — consistent with its pathology); **Technical founder:** a craft-scoped claim only ("about this craft, follow me"), whose term-loss risk is exactly a legibility failure of the claim's scope. The advisor role (dyadic, `21` §8's worked case): a domain-scoped standing claim — grant-without-claim leaves the slot vacant however capable the candidate.

## 7 · Adjacent verdicts

- **"Automatically the leader" (title/investment/public face):** org-chart sense — true and empty; followership sense — **false**: conferred position supplies f-content ("threat or reward") and institutional gating but **no generative external object**, so it produces **compliance**, which reverses when the office is removed. The reachable content is real: bringing investment and being the public face is **attachment-generation in the outward receiver-population** (investors, press, public) — corpus-native at institutional scale (Brand Loyalty scenario). A different job from being followed by one's own team; the burden/responsibility component has no mechanism home (flagged).
- **Company outcomes (good CEO vs good founder):** outside the theory — no outcome term. In reach and user-serving: following doesn't require results; competence is one-of-four, so leadership never ran on being the best.
- **People-gathering:** an *effect*, not an attribute. Three candidate leader-side/member-side capacities: **legibility** (why followers cohere — guarded, single-origin, medium confidence; the open-source case is its named independent test), **social proof** (why they accumulate — an adjacent mechanism), and the **Sid-function** (member-side cohesion, cause-independent). Open with three candidates (`80`).
- **Legibility (the guarded idea, used at its grade):** making value readable by receivers who can't judge raw substance — the outward-function's possible substance, a partial delegation of leading to a legible term, and the term-loss diagnosis. Three consumers, one origin; promotion blocked pending an independent ground.
