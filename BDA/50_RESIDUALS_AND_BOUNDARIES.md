## 1 · The outside-the-formula factors (a residual, not a category)

This is a **residual space**: things are *left over* into it, with reasons; it admits nothing and states no criteria (`03` §2). Its four occupants share only "real, and not in the formula" — their structural roles are deliberately heterogeneous (a lifecycle reading was tested and failed):

| Factor | What it is | Structural role | Why not in the formula |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vitality** | physical presence / aliveness / energy | **degrades** signal | additive in the normal range (~10% tired → ~10% discount), collapsing only at clinical extremes (dissociation, severe depletion) — **threshold-switching, not smooth gating**; admitting it would open criterion-loosening without principled gating |
| **Receiver availability** | whether the receiver is open to forming attachments at all (loneliness, saturation, commitment, load) | **pre-condition** — gates whether the process runs | explained by the belief-frame: BDA models beliefs *about the sender*; availability is a meta-belief *about self* — a different belief-category by scope, and invisible to detection anyway |
| **Coerced behaviour with zero attachment** | doing-as-told under threat while a gate is collapsed (the original no-Coherence case) | an **output with no process** — the null case | the product is zero; a zero output has no type and no engagement state to name; this is compliance's job-2 (`30` §4) |
| **Model-completeness** | how much of a person the observer has finished figuring out | **ends pursuit** when it saturates | fails smooth multiplicativity (saturation ends the chase without zeroing attachment); not a Hope sub-flavour ("more to find" names no outcome — it resolves per-variable: competence→Charm, values→Resonance, regard→H_v) |

Entry-writing standard (set by the receiver-availability entry): each placement is *explained from a frame*, not stipulated. Escalation guard: a **fifth entry** re-opens the schema question; two entries sharing a structural role would be the first sign of a category forming (`80`).

## 2 · Model-completeness, in full (the one residual with real internal structure)

- **The quantity is receiver-side:** a property of the receiver's model of the sender. **The ceiling is sender-side:** set by what the sender's attention is on — *"you do not become inexhaustible by being deep; you become inexhaustible by attaching your attention to something that does not run out."* Both halves deliberately, in one place.
- **Distinct from Coherence** (the collision that discovered it): Coherence asks *"can I predict them?"* (consistency); completeness asks *"have I finished figuring them out?"* / *"should I keep looking?"*. Independent axes — the deep teacher is perfectly consistent and inexhaustible. The dating advice ("don't be predictable / solved") is a completeness fact.
- **Distinct from evidence-quality:** Specificity and θ are properties of *incoming evidence*; completeness is a property of the *accumulated state* — a distinction the belief-update machinery had not drawn (its adjacency, not membership, is the honest placement).
- **What it governs:** pursuit's extinction (`30` §2). What is **unknown**: whether saturation *also* erodes attachment itself (the contempt-in-long-relationships question — the decisive case; if yes, Coherence eventually gains a genuine second failure pole). Blocked on longitudinal data; low confidence either way (`80`).
- **Provenance note:** the origin's "information asymmetry" was **bidirectional** ("what do they know about you vs. what you know about them") and a candidate third domain; today's definition is unidirectional — the narrowing was never a recorded decision (recover-or-dismiss is queued, `80`).
- **A category caution the corpus wrote itself:** "cross-variable epistemic modifier" is a class of one; if no second member appears after two further iterations of work, fold it back into this residual without the class name.

## 4 · Hard boundaries (what the theory cannot say, by architecture)

- **No outcome term.** Nothing in the formula represents results — decision quality, work quality, company outcomes. Company-bloom claims are unanswerable, period (`40` §7). Correctness acts only via later belief-updates (Charm erosion; Coherence collapse).
- **No worth-having term.** The theory computes strength and kind, never whether an attachment is good to have — the direct cause of the Coerced over-report hazard (`31` §5). Adding one would be a genuine architectural change; revive only if the hazard is confirmed in an implementation.
- **The dyad ceiling.** Receiver-side, per-pair. Groups enter only as compositions of dyads (the network account) — which has so far sufficed (no group question has failed to yield to composition); the group-dynamics extension and dyadic-level modulators stay deferred **with a named customer now** (the glue-function).
- **Attachment to a cause with no person involved.** The variables are beliefs about a *sender*; a cause has none. The Resonance network absorbs most of what looked unreachable; the residual is real and open.
- **Static, not dynamic.** The theory consumes levels; PRAGMA measures trajectories it never uses. Every change-over-time observation keeps arriving disguised as a missing modulator — the standing structural frontier (`30` §7, `80`).
- **Character in full.** BDA covers the Self-Positioning slice of character-perception only (`21` §7).
- **Real-time operation.** Nothing anywhere in the project operates live; analysis is after-the-fact (`60` inherits this bound).
- **Cultural calibration.** What reads as productive Self-Focus in one conversational culture may read as rudeness in another; unexplored (iteration-3.4-slot territory).
- **Age / developmental / channel variants.** Unexplored; multimodal metrics need channel-specific calibration.

Two rules keep this section honest: **placement is explained, not stipulated** (the receiver-availability standard); and **exclusion is priced, not moralized** — every boundary states what would change it (a criterion passed, a hazard confirmed, data existing).
