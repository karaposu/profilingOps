# 02 · Registry — Every Named Element

> **Current as of:** 2026-08-25 · **Sources:** all 21 findings + frozen core (full read); the registry itself was proposed by `forms_of_attachment` ("what it is a set of, what admits a member, whether primitive or derived") and extended with the artifacts finding's predication address · **Validation:** structural-only; the theory is empirically unvalidated and its coefficients uncalibrated.

This is the coverage-audit backbone (design rule 7). **A claim that cannot be addressed here has no home** — the signal to either extend this registry deliberately or reject the claim. Kinds used below, per `03_METHODS.md`: **category** (has an admission test; things are argued in and out), **member** (admitted item), **property** (had, not admitted), **derived view / projection** (determined by something else; no admission gate — nothing is admitted to a projection), **space/residual** (things are left over into it; admits nothing), **instrument** (a test or procedure), **factor** (real, consequential, outside the formula).
