## 0 · Origin (January 2026)

The theory began as a chat conversation, **"Attachment domain: the framework for high-value communication"** (created 2026-01-22, exported to the repo root 2026-08-21). The user's opening framing, in their own words: how to represent yourself in chat so that high-potential conversations convert. Two domains were proposed:

- **Attachment domain** — "how do you make someone keep talking to you" — with **three** variables: how much they look up to you (→ Charm), how much they hope for benefit from you — with the observation that high value alone isn't enough, they must believe you'd share it (→ Hope, and already the accessible-high-value insight), and how much they're scared of you (→ Fear).
- **Presentation domain** — how the first domain is shown (→ Content / Style / Expressed Frame).

Also present at origin and later dropped without a recorded decision: a **bidirectional information-asymmetry** idea ("what do they know about you vs. what you know about them"), proposed as a candidate third domain. It resurfaced in August, narrowed to one direction, as model-completeness (see entry 15; the narrowing is logged in `81_ERRATA_LEGACY.md`'s adjacent-notes).

The frozen spec `core/apt_layer.md` (and its backup `old_apt_layer copy.md`) is the cleanest snapshot of this two-domain, three-variable stage, including the "How Frame Was Resolved" debate (internal frame demoted to "a tool") that iteration-1 would partly overturn.

## The PRAGMA context (March 2026)

APT was embedded as one interpretive framework inside **PRAGMA**, the conversational-act analysis system (signal layer → dynamics profile → interpretation layer → three outputs: Behavioral Profiling, APT Inference, APT Profiling). The PRAGMA core files (`pragma.md`, `apt_inference.md`, `apt_profiling.md`, `behavioral_profiling.md`, `dynamics_profile.md`, `interpretation_layer_*`) date from this period and still describe the **three-variable** APT.
