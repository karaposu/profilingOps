# 20 · The Modulator Suite — Coherence, Emotional Composure, and the Gates' Rules

> **Current as of:** 2026-08-25 · **Sources:** iteration-3.2 (`apt_modulator_landscape`) as the founding document; corrections and refinements from `coherence_unpredictability_paradox`, `anger_routing`, `model_completeness_fifth_category`, `apt_as_belief_theory` · **Validation:** structural-only; signal catalogs unvalidated; orthogonality claimed theoretically, not empirically. Self-Positioning — the Suite's first member — has its own file (`21`); this file owns the Suite as a category and the other two members in full.

## 1 · The Suite

The modulator position — between the attachment variables and presentation — is a **named architectural slot with three structurally equal members**:

| Member | Aspects | Failure signature | Detection tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Self-Positioning** (g₁) | genus/species interior — see `21` | **devaluation** — "this guy doesn't deserve this" | instantaneous (seconds) |
| **Coherence** (g₂) | Stability + Alignment | **Model-Collapse** — "I can't figure out who they really are" | **longitudinal** (cross-segment, over time) |
| **Emotional Composure** (g₃) | Physiological + Affective + Recovery | **Contagion-Drain** — "exhausting to be around" | instantaneous |

"Structurally equal at role level": all three are multiplicative gates with dual character (internal + displayed), distinct failure signatures, and pairwise orthogonality. Discovery order is historical, not architectural — Self-Positioning has no privilege for arriving first. The **temporal-detection difference** (Coherence needs longitudinal aggregation) is operational, not a role difference.

**The unifying theme is reliability** — predictable non-extractive attention; a consistent person; a regulated state — and it is deliberately a *theme*, not an element: collapsing the three into one "Reliability" modulator was rejected because the three failure signatures are distinct attributions observers actually make ("needy" vs "erratic" vs "draining") requiring different responses. Under the belief-frame, all three are **stance-beliefs** about the sender — beliefs about *how* the sender is — which is why they gate rather than add (`10` §2).

**Failure signatures are properties, not a category.** Each modulator *has* one (had, not admitted — `03` §2). The signature is the outside view of the collapse; the same signature-to-state relation recurs at the type level (`30`).

## 2 · The eight entry criteria (this file is their owner)

Conjunctive — one failure disqualifies:

1. **Multiplicative on attachment** — its collapse zeroes the product regardless of the variables.
2. **Dual character** — an internal state plus an externally observable display.
3. **Orthogonal to Self-Positioning** (dissociation case exists both ways).
4. **Orthogonal to every other member** (pairwise).
5. **Not reducible to a Self-Positioning species.**
6. **Not reducible to an adjacent mechanism.**
7. **Observable signals** — a specified detection catalog.
8. **A distinct failure signature.**

Plus the enforcement standard set by the Vitality exclusion: **smooth multiplicativity across the whole range**. Threshold-switching behaviour (fine in the normal range, collapsing only at extremes) does not qualify — "admitting it would open criterion-loosening paths (clinical depression? substance intoxication? severe sleep deprivation?) without principled gating."

Corrections on record about these criteria (`81`): **generate-alone is not among them** (it is the *variable* test — a modulator is supposed not to generate alone); Vitality was excluded on the **one** smoothness ground, not a pair. Candidates the criteria have turned away: Vitality (smoothness) · g₄(Mode) (no value independent of g₁ × context — the phenomenon became `21`'s coupling rule instead) · a decay-modulator class (criterion-loosening; revival gated on calibration data) · a relational-state modulator class (modulators are receiver-side beliefs *about the sender*, by the belief-frame). Model-completeness fails smoothness (saturation ends the chase without zeroing attachment) — with the honest note that only that one criterion was formally applied to it; running the other seven is queued (`80`).

## 3 · Coherence (g₂) — in full

**Definition.** The consistency of a person's expression across time and contexts (**Stability**) combined with the alignment between surface expression and internal state (**Alignment**). Observers build their model of a person longitudinally; when Coherence fails they cannot form a stable model, and attachment cannot consolidate no matter how favourable the variables are — there is no consistent person to attach to.

**The belief statement — repaired wording (binding):** *"I can form a **reliable** model of this person."* Never "finished." The original "stable model" phrasing invited a fatal misreading — *solved, fully-mapped* — which is a different axis entirely (**completeness**, outside the formula, `50`). Coherence is about *consistency*: is this the same person across time and situations? A person can be perfectly consistent and never run out of depth (the deep teacher); being figured out ending the chase is a completeness fact, not a Coherence failure. This one-sentence repair dissolved the "don't be predictable" dating objection entirely.

**Aspects (not species — same mechanism, different content; `03` §1.4):**
- **Stability** — same core behaviour and priorities across work/home/social/crisis and across weeks/months.
- **Alignment** — surface matches depth; what you present is what you are. (The stable hypocrite has Stability without Alignment; the fluctuating sincere person has Alignment without Stability — the corpus's own four-corner grid.)

**Failure — Model-Collapse.** Observer reports, verbatim from the founding spec: "I can't figure out who they really are" / "They're different every time I see them" / "Something doesn't add up." Note the second report: **erratic behaviour is a Stability failure** — "consistent unpredictability" is *not* a stable second-order model (that proposal was killed on the source's own next sentence).

**Signal catalog (longitudinal, 3-tier).** Inference signals: Cross-Context Stability · Value-Action Alignment · Temporal Self-Consistency · Role-Boundary Consistency · Commitment-Follow-Through. Text metrics (cross-message): value-vocabulary consistency · topic-continuity · tone consistency across audiences. Multimodal (longitudinal): posture/voice consistency over calls; presentation consistency. Detection requires cross-segment aggregation — Coherence cannot be read from one message.

**Orthogonality dissociations:** vs SP — the narcissist is Self-Focused moment-to-moment yet incoherent across contexts; the consistent caring mother is not-Self-Focused yet highly coherent. vs EC — the **consistent hothead** (coherent, dysregulated) and the **calm liar** (composed, incoherent).

**Coherence's roles elsewhere:** it absorbs contradictory-signal combinations (Hope+Fear from a stranger → manipulation wariness = Model-Collapse, not a negative f-term — with that rule's Fear-fork condition, `30` §5); performed anger fails its Alignment aspect ("he's putting it on", `41`); and it supplies the second, complementary mechanism of narcissist-follower burnout (§5).

**Cultivation references (vocabulary-only, `03` §5):** Stoic journaling · ACT values-clarification · McAdams's narrative identity · Aristotelian/MacIntyrean virtue as stable disposition · Ricoeur's narrative self · "word is your bond" integrity traditions.

## 4 · Emotional Composure (g₃) — in full

**Definition.** The capacity to regulate emotional and physiological state under pressure — staying present and functional when stressed, challenged, or disrupted.

**Aspects:** **Physiological Regulation** (body, voice, breath) · **Affective Regulation** (emotional valence stability) · **Recovery Speed** (time back to baseline after disruption).

**Failure — Contagion-Drain.** Dysregulation transfers emotional load to the observer, who feels drained or burdened by proximity. Reports: "They're exhausting to be around" / "I feel worse after talking with them" / "Too much drama." Attachment signals discount or collapse. (An observer *leaving* because of this is the zero case, not a fourth engagement state — that proposal was killed; the null case lives in `50`.)

**Signal catalog (instantaneous, 3-tier).** Inference signals: Voice Stability Under Challenge · Recovery Latency · Affect-Content Congruence · Physiological Leakage (absence of shaking/tension/rushed breath) · De-escalation Capacity. Text metrics (stress-responsive): word choice under pressure · response-latency patterns when challenged · punctuation/capitalization under stress · escalation-vs-de-escalation language. Multimodal: breath pattern · body tension · gaze regulation · pitch regulation · gesture regulation.

**Orthogonality dissociations:** vs SP — the anxious-Self-Focused person; the calm-Caregiver-Focused person. vs Coherence — the consistent hothead; the calm liar.

**Routing precision (the corrected assignment, from the anger findings):** EC is the decisive discriminator for **leaked** anger only. **Deployed** anger — proportionate, aimed, terminating — leaves EC *intact*; being in control of it is what regulation means (the respected volatile figure fails no modulator). **Performed** anger also leaves EC intact (the performer is in full control) and fails Coherence-Alignment instead. The earlier blanket "anger problems live in EC" over-assigned and is corrected on the record (`81`). Full anger table: `41`.

**Cultivation references:** Gross's process model of emotion regulation · Porges's polyvagal theory · Levine's Somatic Experiencing · Linehan's DBT modules · mindfulness · HRV biofeedback.

## 5 · The narcissism account (two mechanisms, complementary)

At moment-to-moment level, narcissistic attention **is** Self-Focus mechanically (attention on own greatness/story/image) — so the modulator amplifies, and followers accumulate; the *exaggerated* devotion comes from adjacent mechanisms layering on (`50`). The burnout has **two co-occurring mechanisms**: (1) **signaling-theory mimicry decay** — non-extractive attention is a costly signal; the narcissist displays it without paying the cost; the unpaid cost is detected over time (iteration-3); (2) **Coherence failure via Model-Collapse** — different personas across contexts; followers eventually cannot hold a stable model (iteration-3.2). The layer-resolution from the August work (`41` §narcissist): the **genuine** species is Self-Focus, the **displayed** species is Mission-Focus, the difference is **backing** — and the displayed (shareable) mission is what answers "so many followers," with **partial decay** predicted: the mimicked mission-display erodes, the genuine Self-Focus persists, the following contracts to a smaller personal core rather than collapsing.

## 6 · Epistemic status lines

- **Orthogonality is theoretical, not empirical.** Dissociation is possible at the definition level; real-world correlation is *expected* (consistent people tend to be regulated; shared developmental causes). Correlation does not collapse the dimensional distinction (height/weight analog). This expectation is exactly what made two modulators look like rivals in the anger case before the routing was reconciled.
- **Interaction terms between modulators** are not modeled (independent gates; a 3.5-slot question, `80`).
- **Modulator asymmetry** — whether Supplication collapses the product more sharply than non-extraction amplifies it — is surfaced and unanswered (`80`).
- **Single failure poles.** All three members have exactly one failure pole; whether that is a property of modulators or an accident of discovery has never been examined (`80`; the specific reopening: Coherence gains a second pole only if completeness is ever shown to erode attachment itself, not just pursuit).
- **Cluster 4 note:** both new members have attention-*aspects* (attention-consistency; somatic attention), but their load-bearing aspects are identity and physiology — the dissociations that kept Cluster 4 untriggered, flagged close-to-line (`03` §6).
