## 1 · System-level facts

**Admission.** A variable must pass **generate-alone** (`03` §1.1): attachment with everything else at zero. All four pass; Signal Specificity famously fails.

**Additivity.** f is purely additive with context-dependent, uncalibrated coefficients; no interaction terms (`10` §1 owns the argument and the killed alternatives).

**Property-beliefs.** All four are receiver-side beliefs about what the sender IS (`10` §2):
Charm — "this person is high-status / competent / impressive" · Hope — "interaction will yield future positive outcomes" · Fear — (fork, §5) · Resonance — "this person shares world-models / values / ways of seeing with me."

**Pattern A (valence × temporality)** — descriptive organization of the three *unilateral* variables; **not** a substrate (it predicts nothing the variables don't; three substrate candidates were killed — R-α perceived-value on parsimony + Resonance-non-subsumption, R-β evidence-of-consequence as a restatement of the belief-frame, R-γ approach/avoidance as a binary overlay). Cross-discipline corroboration (neuroscience's current-valuation vs future-prediction systems; Bowlby's working models; decision theory's asset-vs-discounted value) is vocabulary-only.

| Variable | Valence | Temporality |
|---|---|---|
| Charm | positive | current |
| Hope | positive | future |
| Fear | negative | mostly future |
| Resonance | — separate axis (dyadic emergence, not value-flow) | — |

**Unilateral vs dyadic.** Charm/Hope/Fear are **unilateral-evaluation** categories (the receiver evaluates the sender); Resonance is a **dyadic-emergence** category (both parties' maps co-determine it). The four-variable set has an internal type-distinction; Resonance passes orthogonality *as a different kind of variable*. This split is independently re-derived by the persistence column's ground-externality binary (`30` §3) — support: internal consistency, not independent derivations (the correction is on record).

**Pairwise orthogonality — the evidence, with the refinement:**
- **Charm without Hope — decisive.** The historical-figure case: admiration of Mozart/Napoleon with all Hope sub-flavors at zero *by physical constraint* (the sender is dead); real attachment-class behaviour follows (listening, defending, identifying). (The celebrity-fan case was retired as decisive — it carries H_v residue.)
- **Hope without Charm — robust in the Hope-dominant range; borderline at the pure extreme.** The anonymous-offer case: a concrete offer from an unknown contact produces *engagement with the offer*, but attachment-to-person may wait for the sender-as-person component. Operational guidance: don't classify pure-Hope-zero-Charm as attachment-to-person without engagement-confirmation. (Preserved-with-refinement; iteration-3.2's claim stands so qualified.)
- **Resonance alone — decisive.** The shared-niche-interest case (two strangers, medieval Ottoman financial instruments): attachment with C/H/F ≈ 0. This is also why Resonance is additive, not a modulator (× 0 = 0 would forbid it).
- **Fear alone** — threat/dependency cases generate engagement without the positive variables (its *kind* of engagement is the Coerced signature, `30`).

**Empirical closeness ≠ substrate identity.** Hope and Charm co-occur strongly in life for two stated reasons: **causal correlation** (Charm-resources — status, competence — translate into Hope-fulfillment-capacity, plus selection effects) and **Object-of-Hope inclusion** ("I hope to be charmed" is H_e-specialized + H_a, not a new flavor). The **H_v ↔ Charm boundary**: both inhabit the evaluation domain in *inverse directions* — H_v is the sender's future evaluation of the receiver; Charm is the receiver's current evaluation of the sender — co-occurring in evaluation-heavy contexts (romance, mentorship, networking), structurally distinct.

**Belief:** "this person is high-status / competent / impressive — I look up to them; I want their approval."
**Interior (named, deferred):** status / competence / high-value signals — three candidate members; the Charm sub-taxonomy slot (3.4.2's framework) has never been opened. Charm is the natural first future sub-taxonomy: three named members, and no inherited fork.
**Committed facts:** current-positive (Pattern A) · the decisive Charm-no-Hope case (above) · **competence-legibility limit** (deep competence lands only on receivers who can read it — the technical founder's bound; the legibility idea itself is guarded at medium confidence, `40`) · devaluation reading under g₁-collapse: *shiny-but-hollow* · TYPE row (corrected 2026-08-31): Status/admiration — "goes quiet without occasions; kept by the record; revived on return" (`30` §1's row-note explains the former counter-case — the mentor admired from afar for years — as the row's signature, not a failure of it) · **the comparison-set note:** Charm is comparative on its face ("high-status" is positional), so a belief left un-evidenced is exposed to *re-pricing* against the receiver's moving comparison set — a receiver-side parameter beside the legibility limit, not a formula term (the charm_persistence finding).

## 3 · Hope

**Belief:** "the sender's future action, presence, or orientation will produce a specific kind of positive outcome for me."
**The structural unifier — all four primitives required:** *forward-oriented* · *positive-valenced* · ***sender-mediated*** · *receiver-affecting*. The sender-mediated clause is load-bearing far beyond Hope's interior: it is what re-categorized mission-participation as Resonance + access-Hope (the leader mediates *access* to a cause, not the cause's value — `40`).

**The seven sub-flavors** (discriminating axis: **Object-of-Hope**; a **basis, not a partition** — a concrete hope is a weight vector; "I hope this person will love me" ≈ H_a + H_v + H_c + H_safe ± H_e, H_g):

| | Object of hope | Agent | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H_e Exchange** (primary) | future joint activity — collaborate, date, befriend | co-agent | Person A's "let's meet to collaborate" |
| **H_a Attention** (primary) | being specifically seen, and continuing to be | sender | coupled to g₁'s display mode (`21` §4); suppression = "not for me" |
| **H_v Validation** (primary) | being seen *as I want to be seen* | sender | presupposes H_a; inverse-direction boundary with Charm |
| **H_c Continuity** (primary) | the sender staying present / reachable | sender | base of the nest (estranged-parent case: H_c without H_a) |
| **H_g Generative** (secondary) | my own growth through them | **receiver**-primary, sender-as-catalyst | mentorship contexts |
| **H_r Reciprocity** (secondary) | return on my past investment | sender | uniquely past-positive-conditioned |
| **H_safe Safety** (secondary) | active protection when needed | sender | active hope, not Fear-absence; presupposes H_c |

**Nesting:** H_v ⊃ H_a ⊃ H_c; H_safe ⊃ H_c (each adds distinct positive valence; detection weights inherit the implications). **Killed candidates:** Status-Hope (→ Charm + H_e-specialized), Pleasure-Hope (cross-variable affective specification — routed to whichever variable carries the pleasure). **Recognized hybrid:** H_forgive (H_v + H_c, past-negative-conditioned). **Access-Hope** (the leadership usage) is ordinary Hope toward the leader as gatekeeper — the cause's value itself is not Hope (fails sender-mediation). Relationship-type predictions (unvalidated): collaborative → H_e+H_g; romantic → H_a+H_v+H_c; long friendship → H_r+H_c; protection-relevant → H_safe+H_c.

## 4 · Fear — the fork, first-class

**The two definitions, both live in the corpus (design rule 6 — presented, not resolved):**

- **Threat-of-harm** (iteration-3.4's belief statement): *"this person can harm me / poses threat."*
- **Cost-of-loss** (the origin spec and iteration-1): *"consequences of losing you — power dynamics, secrets, dependency."*

They come apart cleanly: someone can be terrifying to lose and incapable of harm, or the reverse.

**The instrument mismatch (the strongest evidence the fork is real):** Fear's belief statement is threat-shaped, but Fear's **detection signals** are loss-shaped — "consequences of losing this relationship: asymmetric control they don't challenge · reluctance to disengage even when disinterested · careful word choices · avoidance of confrontation." **The instrument measures a different construct from the one the definition names.**

**What each reading changes:** the anger routing *declared* threat-of-harm (all its verdicts conditional on that). The coherence-stress rule (Hope+Fear contradictory → Model-Collapse) **holds only under threat-of-harm**; under cost-of-loss the combination is mutually reinforcing and the rule evaporates. Under cost-of-loss, the **Coerced row describes dependency**, and ordinary-language compliance may map to *no* type at all. The fork has been load-bearing in **two consecutive findings** — the trigger its own records set for opening the dedicated Fear sub-taxonomy inquiry (3.4.3's reserved slot; `80`).

**Other committed Fear facts:** Specificity applies to Fear like every variable — indiscriminate anger is a generic signal, scaled toward zero *before* any modulator is consulted (the angry-at-everything → contempt result) · the **over-report hazard**: Fear's four detection signals all fire on a routine employee–manager exchange, and Specificity does not mitigate (it scales magnitude; type reads *relative* weights) — the detection consequence lives in `31` · devaluation reading under g₁-collapse: *bluster* — "fear + Supplication gives bluster; fear + Task-Focus gives respect" (the translated April rule).

## 5 · Resonance

**Belief:** "this person shares my world-models / values / ways of seeing — they get me." **Dyadic-emergent:** a property of the pair, not of either person — which is why its admission grounds differ (it entered by the generate-alone test *and* by being the wrong shape for a modulator: modulators are individual properties).

**Distinct from Dialogue-Focus:** Dialogue-Focus is one party's individual attention-species; Resonance is the pair-level emergent. When both parties hold the same attention-object (any species), Resonance emerges — the substitution that builds **follower-follower networks**: any two followers of one cause satisfy the same-object condition, so a movement is a network of ordinary dyads sharing one term, and removing the leader leaves the pairs intact (`40`).

**Committed facts:** the persistence family's core — "persists through absence; **maintained via shared model**" — **derived (2026-08-31; the third_leg finding, `inquiries/2026-08-31_21-08__third_leg/finding.md`):** the shared model = the pair's **co-registered stock** (common references; converged vocabulary; co-built content) **+ each side's confirmed model of the other's way-of-seeing** — read off this section's own signals; it grows by **registration** — stance-matches on co-attended objects (the admission document's own verb: maps "align enough for recognition to *register*"); and it persists through absence because it is the receiver's **own accumulated state** — the one variable whose ground is *constituted by* self-carried history rather than evidenced by an external condition. *(Derivation direction, explicit: premises = the composition readout + the ground-constitution contrast with the other rows; the persistence row is the conclusion derived, never a premise.)* Absence starves growth, never ground; removal is **contact-borne only** (`30` §3). Grade: derivation over committed material, **not validated**; conversion's engine and signature at `30` §3 / FR3 · **leadership's primary variable** (with access-Hope secondary; `40`) · operational signals: spontaneous agreement rate, language convergence, shared-reference comfort, non-explicit mutual understanding, co-construction patterns *(the composition's traces — each signal is a component's face)* · the frontier cases: parasocial/historical figures and followers who never meet — now posed as **one-way registration** (a real, self-carried, *one-legged* stock — registrations against public stances, lacking co-construction and confirmed mutual prediction; is it attachment's Resonance or a Resonance-like state?) — open (`80` FR5).
