# 10 · Architecture — The Formula and Its Logic

> **Current as of:** 2026-08-25 · **Sources:** iterations 1, 3.2 (`apt_modulator_landscape`), 3.2.1 (`attachment_variable_interactions`), 3.3 (`apt_context_layer`), 3.4 (`apt_as_belief_theory`); August findings `compliance_versus_attachment`, `forms_of_attachment`, `belief_attributes` · **Validation:** structural-only; coefficients uncalibrated; no element here is empirically validated.

## 1 · The formula

```
Attachment ≈ f(Charm, Hope, Fear, Resonance) × g₁(Self-Positioning) × g₂(Coherence) × g₃(Emotional Composure)
```

**f is purely additive.** `f = a·C + b·H + c·F + d·R`, with context-dependent coefficients a–d that are empirically underdetermined (on the pending-calibration list — a standing block for anything numeric). There are **no interaction terms inside f**: the variables draw on distinct evidence classes processed by distinct subsystems, and independent channels integrate additively. Three interaction structures were tested and eliminated (Resonance-as-gate — killed on the stranger-offer and celebrity cases; combinatorial magnitude bonus — reinterpreted as TYPE depth; negative terms — absorbed by the Coherence gate). Inner additivity and outer multiplicativity are compatible claims at different levels.

**The gates multiply.** Any g at zero zeroes the product regardless of f — which is why modulator failures are fatal and why the modulators were separated from the variables in the first place. Each g has a distinct failure signature (`20`).

## 2 · The belief-frame (what the formula is *about*)

BDA is structurally **a theory of which receiver-side beliefs about another person, when held, generate attachment** — the claim the name Belief-Driven Attachment states. Two disambiguations the corpus made deliberately:

- **Attachment is generated by beliefs — it is not itself a belief.** It is a behavioural disposition: the receiver's tendency to engage with, persist toward, and return to the sender. (The proposal to identify attachment with the observer's mental model was killed on this commitment.)
- **The frame is a META-frame, not a substrate.** Belief-formation theory supplies the *vocabulary* for how the categories update; it does not derive the taxonomy. The specific categories are empirical content. (Naming note: the frozen spec's own annotation asked whether "APT" still named the thing well "since it is all related to belief" — adjudicated 2026-08-25 by the rename to Belief-Driven Attachment; `RENAME.md`.)

**Property-beliefs vs stance-beliefs — why f adds and g multiplies.** The variables are *property-beliefs* (what the sender IS: impressive / promising / threatening / world-sharing) — each independently sufficient for partial attachment, so they sum. The modulators are *stance-beliefs* (how the sender IS: their agency-direction, their consistency, their regulation) — any single stance-failure destroys the receiver's ability to form a stable belief about the sender at all, so they gate. This explains structurally what iteration-3.2 had decided empirically.

**External anchor (vocabulary-only, per `03` §5):** Bayesian update, schema theory, attribution theory, Bowlby's working models — no tradition canonical.

**Word discipline:** *attachment* has **two referents** — the formula's output, and one engagement-state name (`30`). Every file in this folder says which it means. There is also a third, pre-formal sense (the origin's "why they stay") which is what everyday intuitions usually track.

## 3 · f is a cumulative belief state

f represents the receiver's **current** belief about the sender, formed from **all** available evidence: in-interaction signals, prior direct experience, third-party **social proof**, and platform/channel priors. **f does not start at zero** — the friend-with-many-dates case: his f was pre-loaded by women talking about him; his in-interaction job was to *maintain*, not create. "Cumulative" means **history-dependent** (strength accumulated from evidence over time) — it does not give f a domain to be "complete over" (that misreading was caught and killed; completeness lives outside the formula, `50`). A formal `f_prior + f_interaction` split was rejected as bookkeeping without evidence; PRAGMA's four evidence source-channels (interaction / prior-experience / social-proof / channel-prior) are an implementation pattern, not formula structure.

Three predictions of the belief-frame, with honest labels: **multi-source independence** (genuinely new — independent sources signaling the same direction update f faster than correlated sources); **prior-strength** (re-grounding — strong f_prior is slow to move); **evidence-quality threshold** (re-grounding — sub-θ evidence doesn't update at all, however frequent).

## 4 · Signal Specificity and θ(context)

**Every variable's effective magnitude is scaled by the specificity of the signal carrying it:**

```
effective_magnitude(variable_i) = nominal(variable_i) × specificity(signal)   if specificity ≥ θ(context);   else 0
```

*Specific* = contains information only obtainable from genuine engagement with this particular counterparty. *Generic* = sendable, with trivial modification, to anyone in the category (detected by template-match). Specificity is **not a fifth variable** (fails generate-alone — `03` §1.1); it scales **each variable separately**, which makes any dominant-variable assignment unstable in a dimension the theory already measures (`30` §5). Its scope is *every* variable — including Fear (the angry-at-everything person emits a generic signal; that is why they earn contempt, `41`).

**θ(context)** is per-channel, qualitative ordering committed (quantitative ranges deferred): cold-stranger DM **high** · public-figure-to-stranger **high** · warm introduction **medium** · mutual match **medium-low** · existing relationship **low** · physical proximity **very low**. Channel shifts mid-interaction recalculate θ. Per-**receiver** θ was killed (it is the receiver-state pre-condition in disguise, and PRAGMA cannot see it).

**The Double-Collapse.** Generic messages fail *twice from one cause*: non-engagement with the specific counterparty leaves nothing specific to say (f-layer: all effective magnitudes ≈ 0) *and* defaults the style to permission-seeking (g-layer: sender-g₁ reads Supplication). Both failures share the root; there is **no optimization path for templates** — a "specific-seeming" template without engagement produces a Coherence failure instead (structure appears engaged, substance is missing). This is a structural prediction, not a moral one.

**Sender-SP from message style:** in single-message contexts the message's *style* is the primary carrier of the sender's g₁ — "let me know if YOU want to" reads Supplication regardless of how little effort it took (direction-of-agency, not effort quantity, decides the reading).

## 5 · The outputs: MAGNITUDE and TYPE

**MAGNITUDE** — the scalar: f-sum × g-product. Current engagement intensity.
**TYPE** — the qualitative kind: read **directly off the variable-weight distribution — no separate mechanism**. The five named rows and their persistence behaviours live in `30`. Three architecture-level facts:

- **TYPE is a projection.** That is *why* it has no admission test (nothing is admitted to a projection — every other named set in the theory has a gate that has visibly bitten). The row-names are not the variables renamed — they describe the *receiver's* state (admiration, coerced, bonded) — but each row's identity is **determined by** its dominant variable.
- **Derived-but-predictive.** Magnitude and type are two readings of the same four numbers (the sum; the mix) — not independent dimensions. Type is not redundant: the mix predicts persistence the sum cannot (a high-magnitude Fear-dominant attachment behaves worse over time than a low-magnitude Resonance-dominant one).
- **Whether a type is a cell or a weight-vector** leans vector (the theory's own word is *distribution*; Hope's sub-flavors set the basis precedent) — held at medium confidence, open in `80`.

**No outcome term — anywhere.** Nothing in the formula represents whether anyone's decisions, work, or company succeed. Attachment is generated by beliefs about a person, never by results. Consequences: decisiveness is read independently of correctness ("they don't have to be correct" — derived, `41`); company-outcome claims are outside the theory (`40` §6); correctness acts only *later and elsewhere* (persistent failure eventually lowers Charm; a self-account that stops matching events collapses Coherence).

## 6 · The context layer (iteration-3.3's three refinements, assembled)

1. **f as cumulative belief state** (§3) — the pre-interaction scaffold made explicit.
2. **θ(context)** (§4) — what registers at all is channel-dependent.
3. **The display-mode coupling** — g₁'s display mode (selective-engagement vs withholding) determines whether Hope's **Attention-Hope (H_a)** component is realized in f. Withholding-mode g₁ × suppressed H_a = 0 — experienced not as "low signal" but as **active disqualification: "impressive but not for me."** The coupling rule itself is owned by `21`; its H_a definition by `11`. The **approach act** is a multi-variable signal source: selective initiation contributes at once to f_Charm (initiation confidence), H_a (specific attention offered), and g₁ (acting from own evaluation) — all scaled by specificity; this is the Double-Collapse seen from the sender's side.

**Pre-condition, outside the formula:** BDA operates *given* a receiver who is processing the interaction. **Receiver availability** (openness to attaching at all) is not a variable — explained by the belief-frame: it is a belief about *self*, and BDA's scope is sender-directed beliefs. Placed in `50`.

## 7 · Where BDA sits (one paragraph; interface details in `31`)

PRAGMA measures conversations (7 dimensions per message) → composes a per-segment **Dynamics Profile** (description) → the **Interpretation Layer** reads tensions (interpretation) → **APT Inference** reads those into directional per-pair attachment readings → **APT Profiling** aggregates readings into per-person bearings. Causal order: Self-Positioning → attachment (in them) and presentation (by you); analytical order is the reverse — displays are read first, attachment inferred over time, with g readable in seconds and f accumulating (temporal precedence, `21` §6).

The prescriptive layer is not architecture: `60`, behind its banner.
