# 21 · Self-Positioning — The Largest Element, In Full

> **Current as of:** 2026-08-25 · **Sources:** iterations 1–3.1 (the founding sequence), 3.3 (display modes), plus `leadership_and_inexhaustibility` (the object column), `artifacts_of_the_attention_species` (predication), `belief_attributes` and `glue_versus_leader` (backing) · **Validation:** structural-only; the 12 metrics' empirical independence unvalidated; backing-observables an open gate (`80`).

Self-Positioning is the first modulator (g₁) and the theory's biggest element. Its belief statement, under the belief-frame: *"this person acts from their own evaluation."* Its failure signature is **devaluation**: when g₁ collapses, even high Charm/Hope/Fear get discounted post-hoc ("this guy doesn't deserve this") — Charm reads shiny-but-hollow, Hope reads desperate-to-please, Fear reads bluster.

## 1 · The genus: Non-Extractive Attention

**Non-Extractive Attention** — attention that is NOT oriented toward harvesting approval / validation / **response** from the current counterparty. (The word *response* is in the definition and is load-bearing: an angry outburst demanding a reaction is extractive — harvesting a response — even though it seeks no approval.)

**The central property: outcome-independence** *(this file owns the definition; `60` owns its prescription).*

> Attention **whose resolution doesn't depend on the counterparty's response.**

This single property distinguishes every productive species from the two look-alikes:
- **Needy self-attention** — self-directed in content ("tell me I'm okay") but other-dependent for resolution → fits the Supplication pole mechanically. Both productive Self-Focus and neediness attend to self; only the first is outcome-independent.
- **Supplication** (the negative pole) — attention whose resolution IS dependent on the counterparty's response: hedging, over-explaining, permission-seeking, approval-harvesting. Collapses g₁.

**Two-level naming** (a deliberate convention): *Non-Extractive Attention* at the theoretical level; *Self-Focus* at the user-facing level for value-establishing contexts (precedent: force/push, myocardial infarction/heart attack).

## 2 · The species, with the object column

Every species is defined the same way — **attention on [object], whose resolution doesn't depend on the counterparty's response** — so the object is the individuating slot. The column (from the leadership finding) marks the two properties the followership criterion reads (`40`):

| Species | Rank | Attention on | Object generative? | Outside the receiver? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Self-Focus** | primary | one's own priorities | no (a priority can be completed) | yes |
| **Caregiver-Focus** | primary | one person's wellbeing | yes | **no** — the object IS the receiver |
| **Mission-Focus** | secondary | a larger cause | yes | yes |
| **Task-Focus** | secondary | the craft / work itself | yes | yes |
| **Dialogue-Focus** | secondary | a shared inquiry | yes | yes |
| **Transcendent-Focus** | secondary | an abstract ideal / truth | yes | yes |

Notes the table carries: **self-as-project** is a real subclass — a self treated as a *becoming* is generative and, from the receiver's side, external (the respected-grandparent case) — while a self-as-*position* is exhaustible; the species boundary with Mission-Focus survives on **externality/shareability** (a cause can be shared; a self cannot — devoted individuals vs movements). The primary/secondary **rank measures orthogonality-cleanliness, not what a species produces** — the ranking and object-generativity are anti-correlated, which is an explained oddity, not a contradiction. Mission-Focus's demotion argument (collapse into Self-Focus) was found **invalid** (a personal priority can be completed; a cause cannot) but promotion stays blocked by its own empirical trigger (`80`).

**Species-as-proxy (predication discipline, from the artifacts finding):** the species name *tracks* its object and fails where one name covers two objects — the recorded case is **Scenario 4's teacher** ("Dialogue-Focus … or Caregiver-Focus"), whose "or" was genus-licensed for the scenario's own claim and becomes a live fork for any object-level question; **no document has ever adjudicated it**. Every per-species claim carries an address on the **five-axis scheme** — species / genus / level / object / observer — with instruments per axis (`03` §3.2). Follower-generation is predicated on the **object × observer pair**, not the species (the expert/novice variation; the multi-species convergence).

**Genus-predicated facts** (true of all species, per the all-species check): "any species of non-extractive attention produces g(high); Supplication produces g(low)" — and Scenario 4's own devotion claim.

## 3 · Levels, and the dual character

**Levels:** high / calibrated / low / collapsed — the modulator's read per dyad. **Trait-with-bearings:** individuals have central tendencies (which species, what level, how much outcome-independence) measured *across* dyads; realized Self-Positioning is always **asymmetric/relational** (A-relative-to-B), never an absolute.

**Dual character:** an **internal** layer (where attention genuinely lives — not performable over long exposure) and a **displayed** layer (what the counterparty perceives). **The visibility principle:** internal state without observable manifestation produces no modulator effect — the displayed layer is what observers read, and it is *sufficient* to generate attachment even when the internal state is unknowable (the parasocial celebrity case is the clean proof). The internal layer matters because unbacked displays are detected over time (§5).

## 4 · The display catalog (the five signals) and the 12 metrics

What observers actually read — mostly unconsciously — to judge where attention is:

| Signal | Definition | High-g₁ | Low-g₁ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Withholding** | negative space — what the speaker chose not to say | present: comfortable not-elaborating when invited | absent: over-elaborates everywhere |
| **Premise-Posture** | the opening frame | selecting / offering / asserting | requesting-permission |
| **Self-Justification-Density** | clauses-per-message explaining why speaking / why qualified | low | high |
| **Exit-Willingness** | risk taken in the move | high: disagreement, selectivity, terminable close | low: hedged, safety-optimized, open-ended |
| **Rhythm-Comfort** | comfort with silence / delay | unrushed | rushed, filler-laden |

**Scope-note (Exit-Willingness):** the signal prices **the move, never the sequence** — its unit is risk-within-one-approach (terminable close, composed disagreement, selectivity); nothing in it legislates attempt-counts, and citing it against returning-as-such is a recorded mis-citation (Scenario 7's chaser fails on the *aggressive-follow-up + price-drop + elaboration* bundle, not on persistence). Sequence-effects live elsewhere by design: in the cumulative belief-state (`10` §3) and in Coherence's longitudinal signals (`20` §3) — not in a second g₁ sub-signal (that alternative was evaluated and killed: it would break this catalog's instantaneous character). The insistence phenomenon in full: `41` §7. One rider from that account: because a visible sequence is itself readable (returning leaks the returner's investment), **per-move exit-evidence must grow with sequence length**.

The catalog is a **conjunction detector** (no single signal determinative) and a **detection list, not a property list** — two signals can index one underlying property: deciding-without-needing-to-be-right (Premise-Posture) and abandoning-without-defending (Exit-Willingness + low Self-Justification) are one property, *non-extraction toward one's own prior commitments*, read at two moments. (Re-opens if the two ever dissociate.)

**Withholding is double-edged — the coupling rule (this file owns it):** Self-Positioning has two **display modes**. *Selective-engagement mode* (named-counterparty references, expressed evaluation, specific offers, acting from own evaluation) **realizes Attention-Hope (H_a)** in the receiver. *Withholding mode* (generic content, no acknowledgment, non-selective) **suppresses H_a regardless of other Hope inputs** — producing the **"not for me"** read: high g₁ × 0 = 0 experienced as *active disqualification*, not low signal. So Withholding raises the modulator while suppressing an attachment component — the one catalog entry folk trait-lists cannot hold. (Whether other signals also carry such couplings is open — `80`.) A proposed fourth modulator g₄(Mode) was killed: mode has no value independent of g₁ × context; the coupling rule is its correct home.

**The 12 operational metrics.** Text-mode (6): opening verb class · pronoun ratio · question-to-statement ratio · self-justification clause count · elaboration gradient · closing assumption tone. Multimodal (6): posture · voice cadence/pitch · eye-contact pattern · body alignment · response-timing tempo · exit readiness. Empirical independence unvalidated; consolidation possible.

**The anxious-distracted edge:** anxious self-attention *shows* as distress signals (hedging, apologizing, rushed rhythm, over-explaining) — not as the catalog — so the visibility requirement itself does the disambiguation.

## 5 · Backed versus performed

A display is **backed** when the attention it shows is really where it appears to be; **performed** when put on. Non-extractive attention is a **costly signal** (it requires foregoing optimization of the current exchange); observers read the cost and infer reliability. Performed displays fail two ways: **immediately** (in-the-moment display management is itself readable — the calibrating performer is read as approval-fishing "even if technically competent"; performed anger reads "he's putting it on") or **slowly** (the costly-signal **decay arc**, owned by `30`: the unpaid cost surfaces as inconsistencies; attachment decays toward whatever was actually backed). The narcissist layering (`20` §5) and the priced display-path (`60` §5) both run on this distinction. **What backing observably consists of is the corpus's single most-demanded open question** — "currently unfalsifiable in practice" — and the gate on any display-targeted training (`80`).

## 6 · Temporal precedence

g₁ operates at **instantaneous display-bandwidth** (posture, voice, opening premise, eye contact, response tempo, exit readiness — readable in seconds); f needs content to accumulate. In short windows (the first 5–30 seconds of first contact) **g dominates the attachment-probability because f hasn't developed** — why 5-minute approaches are decided by Self-Positioning display rather than Charm depth. Same formula; different temporal signal-density ratio. (Quantitative bandwidth claims: no data; open.)

## 7 · Oscillation, bearing, and scope

**Oscillation:** healthy interactions alternate species within exchanges; in established relationships sustained pure Self-Focus reads cold, sustained pure Caregiver-Focus reads sycophantic — *that* a misreading occurs is a level×duration fact; *which* misreading is an object fact (a mixed-predication item, per `03` §3.2). Formal rhythm modeling deferred.

**Bearing → character:** across repeated interactions, a person's stable species-tendency plus operating level becomes part of what observers call **character**. Scope honestly held: character in full (values, moral disposition, cognitive style) exceeds BDA; Self-Positioning is disproportionately weighted in character-perception only because every interaction exhibits it.

**Context-scoping (descriptive side of what `60` prescribes):** low Self-Focus is a failure mode in value-establishing contexts and appropriate in genuine-need, transparent-power-asymmetry, intimate, and ceremonial contexts.

## 8 · The standing self-claim (the leadership-layer continuation)

*Source: the ego/self-focus finding (`inquiries/2026-08-31_04-53__ego_selffocus_leadership/finding.md`), continuing this file's lineage. The user's field-word was "ego"; the mapping is a named configuration of committed objects — no new machinery.*

- **The object:** a stance-belief about oneself — "I am someone whose judgment/direction others should take" — held at **standing** claim-scope, enacted as **position-occupancy** (taking offered slots; claiming contested ones; bearing the role's maintenance costs: consistency, cross-context coherence, the inexhaustibility debt). Distinct from this file's gate — the four-cell dissociation proves it: gate+claim = the person-leader · gate+, claim− = the valued peer who declines the advisor slot · gate−, claim+ = the supplicating self-promoter (the insecure narcissist — devalued on contact) · gate−, claim− = the avoider. Leadership of the person-slot is the **composition**: the gate's attention × the standing self-claim × **the grant** (occupancy is bilateral — a slot must be offered and taken; grant-without-claim leaves followership orbiting a vacancy; claim-without-grant is the try-hard).
- **Occurrent vs standing (the wedge):** giving a good answer is an *occurrent* claim ("here is my view"); "I am your advisor" is a *standing* one. A person can supply the first freely and decline the second every time — and the decline of a status-slot is itself evidence of non-extraction (the gate *certified* by the refusal). Read conduct-level only: whether want-but-can't or don't-want, the enacted claim is what the composition consumes.
- **Field-confirmation note:** the §1 disambiguation (Self-Focus ≠ self-as-object awareness) did live work in this finding — the capable "self-observant" non-leader is the excluded look-alike observed in the wild.
- **Backing applies to the claim itself** (§5's machinery): a backed standing self-claim reads without leakage; an inflated one is performed importance — grandiosity (`50`'s adjacency list) — and rides the mimicry arc to decay. The claim's credibility is independent of competence — which is why, at distance (low observation bandwidth), the *backed* claim outreads the *calibrated* expert (the ecology note at `40`).
- **Prescriptive face (owner `60`):** cultivate gate-first (claim-first cultivation manufactures the calibrating performer); hold the claim at **exactly the backed scope** (under-claiming refuses grants; inflation decays); scale past personal cover through the term (`40`'s escapes).

**Formation (developed — the claim_formation_dynamics finding, `inquiries/2026-08-31_13-32__claim_formation_dynamics/finding.md`; discharges `80` T12):**

- **The reflexive transfer:** the self-frame question ("am I someone whose direction others should take, in domain D?") crystallizes by the committed machinery — **self-verdicts** (a grant is a verdict-demand on the self; acceptance adopts the claim in one recorded step; declining is a micro-declaration of "not me") · **micro-declarations** (everyday conduct acting on a self-label, grained into one's own and the audience's ledgers) · **the silent threshold** (accumulation-led claims cross without an event — the claim is *discovered* when a challenge samples it) · **default crystallization + liveness** (never claiming while occasions pass settles "not-me"; the self-question runs open → closed → no longer a question; the first-raise is reflexive too — a grant can *raise* a never-live self-question). **One disanalogy, one signed parameter:** the reflexive reader holds stakes (committed motivated-blindness) — deflationary or inflationary; correctives at the instrument layer (conduct-anchored self-reads; the outside-stream as calibration; the scope-pin).
- **Three paths (evidence-source profiles):** **grant-led** — event-shaped, front-loaded (the claim rides term-cover while the ledger fills; revocation-fragile until converted; de-formation real: release / revocation / decay; **scope-migration** is its trap — a craft-record converted to a direction-grant with no cross-domain ledger: the Peter Principle, mechanized) · **accumulation-led** — the silent-threshold path, self-backed by construction; the presence-path's stance-sibling · **asserted-unbacked** — display-first, under `60` §5's own "priced, not banned": assertion can *generate occasions*; either they convert to record or the arc collapses the claim. The **inherited** case is an early-timing profile of grant+accumulation, with one general law: **early grants scope which claim forms.** (Any application to a real person: "consistent with," never "explained by.")
- **The flywheel and its brakes:** grant → role → occasions → record → deference → larger grants. Brakes: grant-without-conversion · accumulation-without-grant (a legibility failure — T5's territory; credentials are the portability-instrument) · **the deflationary reader** (the impostor-shape: full ledger, discounting self-read; honest exit = *accept-then-convert*) · **the inflationary reader** (grandiosity's formation-story; corrective = the scope-pin). Two per-domain spirals; maintenance is the same metabolism (a standing claim without continued occasions fades).
- **Non-formation reframed:** the question is *which* claim formed, never whether — the self-frame reaches settled / stalled (the identity-crisis as the self-frame's non-convergent orbit) / forced (**the premature self-verdict**: demanding one's own verdict on a sub-threshold ledger manufactures a self-defending no; the scheduled self-review is the defer-with-a-date form, reflexive). The gate+/claim− cell's wording stands verbatim: a standing *peer*-claim (about oneself) is not a *covering* claim (over others).
