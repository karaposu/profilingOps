## 1 · Anger (the worked template)

Three cases, four discriminating coordinates — each modulator decisive for exactly one case:

| | **Deployed** | **Leaked** | **Performed** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-Positioning | Mission/Task-Focus — attention on the violated standard | **Supplication** — demanding a reaction (harvesting *response*) | ambiguous (recorded, not guessed) |
| Coherence (alignment) | intact — the anger is real | intact | **FAILS** — surface ≠ depth |
| Emotional Composure | intact — proportionate, aimed, terminates, fast recovery | **COLLAPSES** — Contagion-Drain | intact — the performer is in control |
| Fear × Specificity | high — aimed at *this, now* | **low** if angry at everything | variable |
| Observer report | standing, deference | "exhausting to be around" | "he's putting it on" |

**The bluster rule** (April's line, translated): *fear + Supplication → bluster; fear + Task-Focus → respect.* **The angry-at-everything person earns contempt** via Specificity, before any modulator is consulted: identical outbursts at everyone are a *generic signal* by the theory's own definition (maximal template-match) — the one result no modulator account predicts. **"Correct aggressiveness" = deployed** — *correct* is descriptive (a coordinate signature), not moral. Performed-vs-leaked distinctness is derived, not yet observed (EC opposition is the decisive test; `80`).

## 2 · The folk-attribute bridge (trait-language → mechanism)

| Folk attribute | Lands on | Kind |
|---|---|---|

**The derivation folk lists can't make:** decisiveness is read at the modulator layer, which represents attention-direction only — and the theory has **no outcome term** — so deciding from your own premise displays non-extraction *whether or not the decision is good*. **Bridge directions matter:** the 2×2 runs mechanism→folk (a naming device); the species-context-fit table and this index run folk→mechanism — the direction a person with a trait-list needs.

## 7 · Insistence and persistence (the sequence phenomenon)

*Source: `inquiries/2026-08-25_10-32__insistence_versus_exit_willingness/finding.md` (the fundamentals account) + its same-day case-round. Status: structural, case-round-exemplified (analyzed instances, not observations).*

**The verdict (quotable only as the composite):** Exit-Willingness prices **the move**, never the sequence — each return is priced by its *manner* (own-premise, composed, terminable, specific) **×** the sequence is bounded by a *content-budget* (return only with something new or a changed condition) **×** a natural *horizon* (out of new things = the stopping rule). The folk reading "willing-to-walk means don't come back" was never in the corpus: Scenario 7's chaser fails on a **bundle** (*aggressive* follow-up + price-dropping + incessant elaboration), and every positive exemplar is **persistent toward its object, non-extractive toward its approvers**.

**Five mechanisms of genuine insistence-success** (capitulation excluded by signature): *no-display pair* — **availability-resampling** (the first no was an availability-no; no belief consulted) and **threshold-crossing** (early attempts never registered; θ falls with contact history); *display-borne trio* — **evidence-delivery** (cumulative f; unchanged repeats hit the correlated-evidence discount — prediction-grade), **consistency-accrual** (follow-through credit; supporting-never-sufficient — a reliable pest is a reliably-avoidable pest), **conviction-display** (sustained specific composed terminable pursuit read as backed belief in the fit; a share may ride the adjacent norm-violation mechanism — open).

**The discriminating test:** *does the yes survive the insister easing off?* Capitulation reverses **promptly, with relief** (the Coerced signature, extended one flagged analogical step from "threat removal" to "pressure easing"); genuine success persists — cleanest positive signature: **unprompted re-engagement**. Distinguish reversal from ordinary decay (gradual, relief-free).

**The reading procedure** *(operationalized 2026-08-25; structural, unobserved — the T9 field-window exercises it)*:
1. **Mark the easing** — ease deliberately, or note when pressure lapses naturally (every real pipeline eases). The artifact staying live is not pressure.
2. **Wait one natural contact-cycle** — the window scales to the relationship's established rhythm (weekly-visit B2B ≈ 1–3 weeks; daily contact ≈ days). No reading before one full cycle: a quiet week in a weekly rhythm is no data. Beyond ~3 cycles the hinge blurs and only decay-vs-persistence remains readable.
3. **Read the window by the hinge-test:** is the change **hinged at the easing moment**, or does it **track general contact-distance**? Reversal hinges; decay tracks.
   - **Relief-reversal signs** (capitulation): avoidance appears where pressure used to get answers (calls deflected, meetings dodged); the engagement's artifacts are *abandoned mid-air* rather than wound down (codes never printed, balance never loaded); visible distance or mood-lift at the next encounter; **no counter-offer** — someone who valued the thing renegotiates, someone relieved lets it die; third-party "he kept pushing" reports.
   - **Ordinary decay signs** (lawful fading, not test-failure): slow slope with no inflection at the easing; warmth persists without motion ("we should get back to that"); artifacts kept; responses friendly but slower. Decay is the normal fate of un-maintained belief — the maintenance answer is the zero-display channels, never more pressure. *(Read by its own signs — warmth persists, motion fades, artifacts kept: the belief's content persists; its currency and expression decay. The charm_persistence finding, 2026-08-31.)*
   - **Persistence / the positive pole:** the receiver initiates — calls back, asks a question, references it, forwards it, loads the balance unprompted. **Type the attachment by what they re-engage about:** price → transactional Hope; the product's fit and ideas → belief-change; you-as-partner → Resonance-side.
4. **Log it** (the no-ledger; the evening review's sequence question). Channel caveat, flagged not solved: the test is weakest where θ is highest — cold-channel silence is ambiguous in a way in-person rhythm is not.

**The case-table** (three *poles*; the **mixed-cases rule** from the case-round: real cases classify per-coordinate, and the outcome follows the failing legs):

| | **Deployed** | **Leaked** | **Performed** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-move manner | own-premise · composed · terminable · **specific** | need-shaped pressing | polished pressure-theater |
| Content per return | new material or changed condition, every time | unchanged repeats | manufactured "news," fake deadlines |
| Termination | respects a **reasoned no**; retries only availability-/never-registered-no's; exits at the horizon | can't stop | stops when the tactic stops paying |
| Result | registration-success or belief-change (survives easing) | compounding devaluation (the irritation mechanics) | recruits, then Alignment-failure when caught; decays |

**Consent-gate before the table:** a *licensed* return ("come back in September") is not insistence. **The no-typology the table consumes:** reasoned-no (belief-verdict — respected) · not-now-no (availability — retry) · never-registered-no (sub-θ — a later attempt is a first attempt). The strongest derived move: **ask the no its type**, with the no offered as the easy option — the answer converts the sequence into a licensed one.

**Case-round results:** the *persistent suitor* splits cleanly (deployed sub-case via availability/evidence/conviction vs wear-down sub-case whose "settled-for" yes shows the relief-reversal — folklore's own data) · the *returning founder* = the deployed pole · the *negotiation re-approach* = consent-gate + changed-conditions ("talks about talks" is consent-negotiation) · the *door-to-door repeat* = availability-resampling at population scale (volume strategies work through the no-display pair across many receivers, not belief-change in any dyad) · the **composed pest** (clean manner × dead content) and the **needy innovator** (new content × needy manner) are the worked **mixed cases** — the first also revealed that **the sequence-shape is itself a display**: unexplained persistent returning leaks outcome-attachment even when every move's manner is clean, which is the ground of the asymmetry rider (per-move exit-evidence must grow with sequence length). The needy innovator's rescue is the artifact-channel: route the content through a standing demo, keep the personal moves minimal.

**Irritation, mechanically:** an unchanged need-shaped repeat carries no new content, so its only information is extraction-shaped conduct — gates multiply, devaluation compounds per return, and the pressure itself is Contagion-load. The failure branch is as lawful as the success branches.

## 8 · Verdict-forcing and stance-crystallization (the confrontation/milestone phenomenon)

*Source: the confrontation-milestones finding (`inquiries/2026-08-26_11-28__confrontation_milestones/finding.md`), which supplies the mechanism under `60` §10's pre-existing "soft-no-crystallization avoided" design rule. Builds on the backing-observables finding (concluded 2026-08-25; its own homing pending). The user's field-word for the phenomenon was "confrontation" — not conflict: the forcing of an explicit verdict-moment.*

**The move:** a **verdict-forcing move** demands a stance-response *now* — an explicit offer ("want to use our product?"), an intent-declaration ("I like you"), a status-question ("are we exclusive?"). **Situations force too** (an expiring context; a third party's "so are you two together?") — no asker needed, which is why this is belief-dynamics, not tactics.

**The pre-verdict state:** additive accumulation — property-beliefs build freely because no stance is demanded, so no gate filters the evidence.

**The mechanism (what an ask does, in order of primacy):**
1. **Samples** — reads the accumulation *at this moment*, collapsing a trajectory into a point-verdict; below-threshold accumulation returns "no" even on a rising curve (the sample cannot tell "not yet" from "no").
2. **Gates** — demands a stance, and stances gate: post-verdict evidence arrives **pre-filtered** through the declared category ("someone I turned down" processes new displays differently). The ask converts an additive process into a gated one — *this operates whether or not contact continues*; where the no also ends contact, **evidential starvation** compounds it (the no shuts off the input stream that could have revised it).
3. **Records** — spoken aloud, the verdict enters the declarer's self-consistency ledger *and* the social record; audience multiplies stickiness (a no declared before a room is maximally crystallized).

**The no→yes / yes→no asymmetry, grounded:** a yes stays under test (the ongoing relationship keeps re-pricing it); a no seals itself (filters what arrives; often stops arrival).

**What is irreversible (three objects):** the **pre-ask ambiguity** — fully (the question cannot be unasked); the **declared stance** — sticky, revisable only through *question-change* (the Return Doctrine's mechanism: a genuinely new question gives the receiver a consistency-preserving path **they could articulate themselves** — the individuation criterion; cosmetic reframes are unchanged repeats and the correlated-evidence discount prices them); the **frame** — damaged by the ask's *pressure*, not its existence.

**Crystallization sources (four):** an ask · a forcing situation · spontaneous formation · **default crystallization** — sustained non-asking is itself conduct, and conduct is evidence; under long observation the frame settles into a category with no ask anywhere ("we're friends" — the folk friend-zone, mechanized). Near capacity-peak, forcing-sources multiply (the unresolved frame grows salient; anything can demand the stance) — so late in the window the deliberate ask beats waiting.

**Window-close dynamics (developed — the window_close_dynamics finding, `inquiries/2026-08-26_13-54__window_close_dynamics/finding.md`; discharges `80` T11):**

- **The hardening triple:** (1) *fit-selection* — the label absorbing the record without auxiliary hypotheses gains per cycle, and **non-asking is conduct the default label predicts** (positive confirmation, not absent evidence); the confirmation is *cumulative-comparative* — any single quiet cycle is cheap to excuse, but auxiliaries bill per use while the default runs auxiliary-free. (2) *Deceleration* — same-shaped cycles are correlated evidence and update less; settling stabilizes itself (fast early zone → hardening middle → plateau). (3) *Conduct-recording* — acting on the leading label (confiding accordingly, "my friend" in introductions, planning around it) records it in **micro-declarations**: grains in the self- and social ledgers; grains are weaker than spoken verdicts and *sum*, never equal. The threshold crossing is **silent** — which is why the close is discovered (by an attempted ask), never announced.
- **Liveness (the rider):** the pair-question's *consultedness* is a separate axis from any label's confidence — grounded in the no-typology's own never-registered category (an unprocessed question is a liveness-object, not a low-confidence one). Readout bands: open question → closed question → **no longer a question**. The settled end-state is "the question left the active set," not "a no was decided" — the deep difference from a declared no. Liveness and level can dissociate *reflexively* (one's own question can die of inattention while the underlying state grows sub-threshold).
- **The attractor-field:** contexts arrive pre-labeled with **nulls** of varying enforcement (workplace polices "colleagues"; a friend-group defaults to "friend"; a dating app's null is *prospects* — where non-asking settles "dead prospect" in two-three cycles, the field's fastest close). Context-dependence = which label is the null × how strongly enforced. Labels are surface; the settled object is a stabilized attachment-form with capped claim-scope. **Three ledgers** (hers/his/the audience's) settle at different rates; under frame-typical conduct **the audience settles first** (it sees only typical public conduct) and its label feeds back as third-party recording — dense mutual circles settle pairs faster. (Bi-typical public conduct instead keeps audiences unsettled — that is a stall being watched.)
- **Rate-factors (shape-level):** contact-frequency (settling runs per cycle) · context-breadth (single-context pairs settle fast toward its null; multi-context pairs resist) · perturbation-rate (frame-discrepant events reset deceleration) · the null's ambient strength · conduct's frame-typicality · competing claimants. **Forcing-multiplication derived:** forcing-sources scale with the pair's *contact-surface* (more contexts → more allocation decisions; more third parties → more label-demands; rising internal salience → the receiver's own planning needs the label), and contact-surface grows with the same accumulation that raises capacity — the opening and the forcing grow together (the `60` §11 near-peak line's derivation). Uncontrolled closes are worse mechanically: third-party forcing extracts the verdict publicly, at maximum recording, with no manner-control.
- **Three outcomes:** **converge** (default) · **stall** (bi-typical conduct — neither label out-absorbs; the situationship as non-convergent orbit, usually with a *maintainer* deflecting verdict-moments; chronic Hope + chronic closure-pressure, the anxious adjacency) · **forced**.
- **Reopening (two ordered problems, three cost-legs):** first **revive** the dead question — in-frame conduct cannot (absorbed); it takes frame-discrepant evidence, cleanest **new-context evidence**; then **re-answer** against (1) the revival itself, (2) the recording-defense graded by accumulated micro-declarations and third-party adoption — never "cheap because unspoken," (3) **incumbent value**: the settled frame's standing attachment-value at risk ("I'd lose my best friend" is a real cost-term; grounded F1-neutrally as attachment-value-at-risk). **Audience-drag** persists (the audience's ledger keeps re-recording) — successful reopenings disproportionately involve leaving the shared context (a mechanism-fact; *deliberately* isolating someone from their audience is the abuser's tool, `50`'s isolation row). **Raise = re-raise:** question-raising via frame-discrepant evidence is one direction-neutral operation (a never-live question can be first-raised the same way, with no defense legs). **The no-seeded frame:** a premature ask's declared no becomes the settlement's *seed-label* ("the guy I turned down") — post-no frames settle fast and hard; the worst-trade claim deepens (the premature ask seeds the attractor it must later fight).
- **Boundary objects:** **engineered liveness** — rationed frame-discrepant conduct (cheap, timed, unilateral) keeping the other's question alive while blocking settlement = breadcrumbing, intermittent reinforcement's frame-level face; its honest twin is *real joint novelty* (jointly produced, cost-bearing). **Functional nulls** — strong defaults are social infrastructure, not pathology; the close is the belief-system working. *(Presentation-vocabulary flags: "nucleation," "hysteresis," "prediction-error" are illustrative imports; the machinery is native — Coherence-longitudinal, the correlated-evidence discount, M4, threshold mechanics, the no-typology's liveness axis.)*

**The yes side:** premature yeses crystallize too (the under-carried compliance-/politeness-yes books a forecast that delivery re-prices into a harder no later) — and deliberate **maximal crystallization of a fully-carried yes** (public, witnessed, recorded, identity-framed) is a legitimate terminal instrument: structurally, that is what a wedding vow is.

**Conduct corollary (mechanism-grounded):** arguing with a no forces its giver to *defend* it — rehearsal deepens consistency and seals it; the graceful exit leaves the no undefended and therefore soft.
