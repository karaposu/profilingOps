# 03 · Methods — The Theory's Own Epistemic Instruments

> **Current as of:** 2026-08-25 · **Sources:** iteration-3.2 (`apt_modulator_landscape`), iteration-3.4 (`apt_as_belief_theory`), iteration-3.4.1/3.4.2, and the August findings `never_named_layer`, `model_completeness_fifth_category`, `forms_of_attachment`, `artifacts_of_the_attention_species` · **Validation:** these are the instruments the corpus actually uses; each is stated with its scope and its known misuses.

Everything here is stated once, and every other file points here. A reader deciding "does X belong in the theory, and where?" should be able to answer from this file alone.

## 1 · Admission tests (what gets something INTO the theory)

### 1.1 The variable test — generate-alone (multiplicative-vs-additive)

> **Can the candidate generate attachment on its own, with every other variable at zero?**

If **yes** → it is a **variable** (additive, inside f). This is how Resonance was admitted (the shared-niche-interest case: two strangers, an unusual shared interest, attachment with Charm/Hope/Fear ≈ 0). If **no** → it is not a variable, whatever else it is (Signal Specificity failed it — specificity about nothing generates nothing).

**Known misuse (corrected on the record):** generate-alone is **not** a modulator criterion. A modulator is *supposed* not to generate alone — being a multiplicative gate is what that means. One finding briefly cited it as a modulator-entry ground; the correction is logged (`90` entry 18, `81`).

### 1.2 The modulator criteria — eight, conjunctive

Owned in full by `20_MODULATORS.md`; listed here for the audit trail: multiplicative on attachment · dual character (internal + displayed) · orthogonal to each existing modulator (pairwise) · not reducible to a Self-Positioning species · not reducible to an adjacent mechanism · observable signals · a distinct failure signature · **smooth multiplicativity across the range** (the criterion that excluded Vitality — threshold-switching does not qualify, and loosening it "would open criterion-loosening paths without principled gating").

One failure disqualifies. The corpus ran all eight on the candidates it admitted ("All criteria passed") — the standard any future candidate should receive. **Gap on record:** proposals framed as *sub-flavours* bypass generate-alone by construction; the sub-flavour test (1.3) carries the load for them.

### 1.3 The sub-flavour test

A sub-flavour of a variable needs: the variable's **structural unifier** satisfied (for Hope: forward-oriented + positive-valenced + sender-mediated + receiver-affecting), a **distinct value on the discriminating axis** (for Hope: Object-of-Hope), and orthogonality against the *other variables* (so it isn't a recategorization). Sub-flavours are a **basis, not a partition** — a concrete instance carries several at once, as weights. Killed by this test: Status-Hope (collapses into Charm + specialized H_e), Pleasure-Hope (a cross-variable **affective** specification — and note the kill's own scope: its author bounded the pattern to affective cases; applying it to epistemic candidates is argument-by-analogy, carried at medium confidence).

### 1.4 The sub-kind criterion (species vs aspects) — and its undeclared type

> **Species require distinct mechanisms.** Same failure signature through the same mechanism → **aspects**, not species.

This is why Self-Positioning has species (genuinely different attention-directions) while Coherence has aspects (Stability and Alignment both fail as Model-Collapse). It is also why the five attachment **types** are not a taxonomy: "TYPE reads directly from the variable-weight distribution — no separate mechanism is needed" — a weight configuration is not a mechanism.

**Declared limitation:** this criterion is **causal** (what produces the thing). The corpus has **no relational criterion** (the kind that individuates biological species by non-interbreeding despite identical machinery). Whether a relational test would reclassify the attachment types turns on the open type-interconversion question (`80`). Every future "is X a kind of Y?" adjudication faces this fork and should say which criterion it is using.

## 2 · The element-ontology (what KINDS of thing the theory contains)

Settled across the never-named-layer and model-completeness findings:

- **Categories** — have admission tests; things are argued in and out. The theory has **at most two**: modulators (certainly — eight criteria, enforced) and variables (arguably — the generate-alone test).
- **Members** — admitted items.
- **Properties** — **had, not admitted**. No gate is owed to a property. Failure signatures are properties of modulators; persistence-under-change is a property of attachment types. Asking "what admits something to the signature category?" is a type error.
- **Projections / derived views** — determined by something else; nothing is admitted to them (TYPE; the engagement-state names).
- **Spaces / residuals** — things are *left over* into them; they admit nothing and state no criteria (the outside-the-formula factors in `50`). The corpus's own words: "a residual placement rather than a category assignment."
- **Instruments** — tests and procedures (this file).

**The recurring failure this prevents:** three consecutive August inquiries proposed a structural slot for something that already had a home (compliance as a species; the persistence "layer"; model-completeness as a fifth category). The mechanism was an *invisible home* — placements queued in unexecuted action lists. This registry-and-ontology pair turns that inference into a lookup.

- **"Layer" discipline:** in this corpus the word means a **stage** (consumes from below, feeds above) or a **part** (of a decomposition). Terminal producers are neither; call nothing a layer that has nothing above it. A section heading does not confer element-standing.

## 3 · Dissociation and predication

### 3.1 The two-way dissociation test

> To show X is independent of Y: exhibit **X without Y** and **Y without X**.

Run four times on elements (Coherence vs SP; EC vs SP; Coherence vs EC; Resonance vs the rest) — all returned independent. **Extension on record (artifacts finding):** the same instrument aimed at **predication** — is an *outcome* predicated on the species, or on something the species tracks? Both legs passed against the species (outcome varies with species fixed; species varies with outcome fixed) → the bearer is the **attention-object × observer pair**; the species is a **proxy** (named after its object; fails where one name covers two objects — Scenario 4's teacher).

**Calibration caution (the test's own):** four-for-four "independent" is a streak; a test that only ever returns one verdict carries decreasing information. The census (3.3) is its first non-selected application — if the census returns uniformly, suspect the instrument's sensitivity before celebrating.

### 3.2 The five-axis predication scheme + the writing-time question

Every per-species claim has an address on one (or more) of: **species / genus / level / object / observer**. Instruments are matched to axes, never conflated: species-vs-object → the dissociation test (traction only at variation points: forks, observer-variation, receiver-position, cross-species); genus → the **all-species check** (true of all six species = genus-predicated); level → duration-and-purity variation; followership-support → the **two-clause criterion** (a *local* instrument owned by `40`; stretching it across the pool is a category error).

The reusable habit, one question asked of every new per-species claim at writing time: **"Which axis is this claim predicated on?"** It would have caught the two known mis-addressings (the Hope-amplification cell; Scenario 4's fork) at authoring time.

### 3.3 The census (specified, unrun)

The standing worklist: ~two dozen per-species attributions across four sources (the April amplification and oscillation sections; the fifteen scenarios; iteration-3's species correlates; the leadership finding's object-derived consequences) **plus the nine adjacent mechanisms** — the latter being the determination procedure for **Cluster 4's third reopening condition**, written once and never fired. First item in the queue: **shareability** (the one named shape that could put a member in the species column). Tracked in `80`.

## 4 · The bearer discipline

**Bearers are tested, never asserted.** The corpus's repeated result is that properties land on *pairs*: generativity on **object × observer** ("a topic exhausted for an expert is not exhausted for a novice"); glue-ness on **person × group** (the same Sid is vital in a taciturn group, noise in a talkative one); model-completeness's quantity on the **receiver's model** with its ceiling on the **sender's attention-object**. Relocations land on the bearer the evidence names — and that bearer can be two-place. When a claim's bearer is unstated, state it before using the claim.

## 5 · External anchoring — the vocabulary-only convention

Imports from other fields bring **vocabulary, never apparatus**. Each import carries an explicit non-commitment to the source tradition's full machinery; compatibility with several traditions is a feature. Apparatus-level commitment requires empirical validation *in BDA's domain* first (the canonical-Bayesian proposal was killed exactly there).

Precedent list (all on record): Signal Detection Theory (for θ(context)) · Bayesian belief-update, schema theory, attribution theory, Bowlby's working models (the belief-frame's anchors) · factor-analysis vocabulary (the sub-flavour basis) · neuroscience / attachment theory / decision theory (Pattern A's corroboration) · agency-communion (the softness narrowing) · instrumental-vs-reactive aggression (anger criteria vocabulary) · behaviour-vs-bond (engagement vocabulary) · work/expression/manifestation from cataloguing (corroborating the property reading) · Stoic dichotomy of control, performance-psychology task-focus, Rogers' three conditions, Stanislavski (practice **references**, `60`) · BATNA (outcome-independence operationalized) · indicator/construct ("indicator reification" for species-as-proxy).

## 6 · Cluster 4 — the standing substrate question

**The question:** should attachment be re-grounded in attention as its substrate (Charm/Hope/Fear re-derived as attention-allocation patterns of the observer)?

**Status: never triggered**, across ~ten reviews (ledger `90` §4), including the two direct tests: iteration-3.2 (two new modulators have attention-*aspects*, but dissociation shows the load-bearing aspects are identity and physiology — close-to-line, flagged) and iteration-3.4.2 (three substrate candidates killed on independent grounds).

**Reopening conditions (verbatim commitments):** attention-direction metrics fail to distinguish productive from anxious self-focus in use · "genuine vs evaluative" emerges as the practical decisive factor · multiple additional modulators surface and all are attention-based · the adjacent mechanisms reveal themselves as attention-pattern variants (**the census is this condition's determination procedure**) · a phenomenon appears that no clarification + parameter + coupling rule can absorb.

**Discipline:** Cluster 4 triggers **structurally, not heuristically** — counting refinements never satisfies it (killed four times). **Two recorded reframes** short of triggering: the substrate question may dissolve into a *dynamics* question (the theory is static; trajectories keep arriving disguised as missing modulators), and the relocations may be answering it *piecewise* (each moves an item off the person/species axes; the census's aggregate is the honest read).

## 7 · Procedural norms (earned, not decorative)

- **Read quotes to their end.** Two confirmed instances of a load-bearing citation reversing in its own next sentence (the stable-hypocrite passage; Scenario 4's second sentence). Any quoted ground must be checked against its full passage.
- **Count, don't argue**, for questions about what happened: the three-versions miscount, the eight-months error, and the "space already exists" claim were all overturned by counting (timestamps, action lists, exact-case zero-counts). Four consecutive inquiries' decisive moves were checks, not arguments.
- **Counts are resolution-indexed.** "Four variables" and "seven sub-flavours of Hope" are one enumeration at two grains; report a count only with its resolution (the levels are real — they are gated differently — the *count* is the choice).
- **Shared-origin detection.** Multiple appearances of one idea are not convergence if they descend from one source (the legibility guard). Declare origins before crediting convergence; likewise a claimed "two independent derivations" must actually have independent objects (the two-family reading's support was downgraded to internal consistency on exactly this).
- **No retroactive editing** of finalized findings (killed four times as a move). Corrections land forward: in later findings, in `81`, and in owning files here.
- **Naming criteria** (followed in practice, stated once): the referent exists · it fits an existing kind (per §2) · the label matches the definition · not redundant · structurally symmetric with siblings. Labels are revisable; usage is evidence of meaning, not authority over it.
- **The iteration-label convention** (four categories) and its collision status: owned by `90` §4, tracked in `80`.
- **No outcome term, anywhere.** A recurring *check* as much as a claim: any proposed content mentioning results, success, or company outcomes is outside the formula by architecture (`10` owns the statement; `40`/`41` apply it).
